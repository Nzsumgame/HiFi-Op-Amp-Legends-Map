# AD825

**High-speed JFET-input single op-amp, SOIC only** · Analog Devices (1990s–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*A cheap, fast single JFET op-amp (41 MHz, 125 V/µs, 12 nV/√Hz) that DIY rollers call punchy and rich.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 nV/√Hz | 41 MHz (−3 dB bandwidth) | 125 V/µs; settling 80 ns | Fully specified at ±5 V and ±15 V | 6.5 mA (7.2 mA also reported, unresolved) |

## Why it's rated

A niche cult part from the mid-2000s rolling era. Head-Fi users call it punchy and intimate, rich and thick, good for female vocals and strings. Others find it flat in some DACs, or more coloured and closed-in than the AD797. LC Audio sold AD825 upgrade parts. No objective measurements; all sonic claims are forum opinion.

**Described as:** punchy, intimate, rich timbre, thick texture, flat in some DACs, closed-in versus AD797, less smooth than LME49990

**Used in:** DAC / CD-player rolling on SOIC-to-DIP adapters (two per socket), Upgrade for AD711/AD712, e.g. Parasound HCA-2200 MK-I input, Headphone amplifiers (Tangentsoft / Head-Fi), LC Audio Technology upgrade kits

## Watch out

- Single-channel, SMD only (SOIC-8 / SOIC-16); a DIP dual socket needs a two-chip adapter.
- 41 MHz part: adapter wiring and decoupling matter (no AD825-specific oscillation reports).
- Tangentsoft test: 8.6 V supply for 2 V into 330 Ω (AD823: 6.1 V); 4.7 V for 0.5 V/33 Ω.
- Praise is subjective forum opinion; no published THD+N measurements.
- LME49990 reported smoother with firmer low end; one fan says 'AD825 is the best'.
- **Counterfeits:** No AD825-specific counterfeit reports. Known orderables are SOIC-8 and wide SOIC-16 only, so a bare DIP-8 chip marked 'AD825' is suspect. Buy from ADI-authorised distributors (DigiKey 505-AD825ARZ-ND, Mouser, Farnell); check logo, date/lot code and marking.

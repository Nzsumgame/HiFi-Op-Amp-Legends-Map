# AD825 (single high-speed JFET-input op amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*A cheap, fast single JFET op amp (41 MHz, 125 V/us, 12 nV/rtHz). DIY op-amp rollers call it punchy and rich, and LC Audio sold it as an upgrade part.*

**Technology:** A single JFET-input, high-speed, voltage-feedback op amp from Analog Devices. ADI's SPICE macro-model (JCH / ADI Central Apps, 1999) uses a P-channel JFET differential input pair. The datasheet specifies it fully at +/-5 V and +/-15 V. The orderables found are SOIC-8 and a 16-lead wide SOIC (R-16); the R-16 was added in datasheet Rev. F (10/04). No dual or quad version exists.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche cult part from the mid-2000s op-amp-rolling era. Head-Fi users call it punchy and intimate, with a rich timbre and thick texture that suits female vocals and strings; one fan of 2x AD825 on an adapter says 'AD825 is the best'. Others hear it as flat or unemotional in some DACs, or as more coloured and a bit closed-in than the AD797. The LME49990 was reported as smoother, with a firmer low end. LC Audio Technology sold AD825 upgrade parts. Audio Asylum and diyAudio threads reportedly suggest it as a better replacement for the AD712 in the Parasound HCA-2200 MK-I input stage. No objective audio measurements were found. All sonic claims are forum opinion.

**How people describe the sound:** punchy, intimate, rich timbre, thick texture, good for female voices and strings, flat / unemotional in some DACs (dissenting view), coloured and a bit closed-in compared with the AD797 (dissenting view), less smooth, lighter low end than the LME49990 (comparison)

**Typical uses:** Op-amp rolling in DAC and CD-player analog stages, on SOIC-to-DIP adapters (two AD825s per dual socket), Drop-in upgrade for AD711/AD712-class JFET op amps (forum discussion, e.g. Parasound HCA-2200 MK-I), Headphone amplifiers (Tangentsoft / Head-Fi), LC Audio Technology upgrade kits

**Caveats:**

- Single-channel and surface-mount only (SOIC-8 / SOIC-16). Replacing a DIP dual needs an adapter carrying two chips.
- It is a 41 MHz part, so adapter wiring and local decoupling matter (general high-speed practice; no AD825-specific oscillation reports were found).
- It needs more supply headroom to swing 2 V into 330 ohm than parts such as the AD823 (8.6 V vs 6.1 V minimum in the Tangentsoft test).
- The praise is subjective forum opinion. No published THD+N measurements were found. The forum sources were not re-checked in this pass.

## Other change notes (lifecycle, packaging, successors, lore)

### 1. Package / assembly: Analog Devices, 4 Aug 2010 (PCN 10_0117 publication date per search…

PCN 10_0117, 'Halogen Free Material Change for SOIC_W Products at Carsem', moves SOIC_W assembly from Carsem S (Ablestik 84-1LMISR4 or 8290 die attach; Sumitomo 6600H or G700 mold compound) to Carsem M (Henkel QMI-519 die attach; Hitachi CEL8240F10 mold compound). It was part of ADI's move to halogen-free 'green' packages and was qualified per AEC-Q100. The AD825 has a 16-lead SOIC_W option (AD825ARZ-16), which is the version plausibly affected. The die does not change.

- **When:** 4 Aug 2010 (PCN 10_0117 publication date per search summary); form at Rev. A
- **Affected:** AD825AR-16 / AD825ARZ-16 (16-lead SOIC_W; inferred from the PCN's SOIC_W scope), AD825AR / AD825ARZ (named by an earlier search summary; doubtful, since these are 8-lead SOIC_N)
- **How to tell old from new:** Only the SOIC_W package (the -16 suffix) is in scope. Distinguish by date code after the PCN effective date and by the Carsem M assembly site.
- **Audio impact:** None expected (package materials only).
- **Drop-in risk:** low - package materials and assembly site only; same die, pinout and data sheet
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Verification:** Three searches showed that PCN 10_0117 is a halogen-free die-attach and mold-compound change for SOIC_W at Carsem, not a die change. They also confirmed that the AD825 has a 16-lead SOIC_W variant (AD825ARZ-16). The earlier listing of the 8-lead AD825AR/ARZ conflicts with the SOIC_W scope and is flagged as doubtful.

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| SOIC_W assembly site / materials | Carsem S; Ablestik 84-1LMISR4 or 8290 die attach; Sumitomo 6600H or G700 mold | Carsem M; Henkel QMI-519 die attach; Hitachi CEL8240F10 mold (halogen-free) |

## Counterfeits

No AD825-specific counterfeit reports were found. The known orderables are SOIC-8 (AD825AR/ARZ) and SOIC-16 wide (AD825ARZ-16). No DIP-8 or dual version was found, though the ordering guides of Rev. A and Rev. C were not read. A bare DIP-8 chip marked 'AD825', as opposed to a SOIC chip on a DIP adapter, should be treated as suspect (inference). Buy from ADI-authorised distributors (DigiKey 505-AD825ARZ-ND, Mouser, Farnell), and check the ADI logo, the date and lot code, and consistent laser marking.

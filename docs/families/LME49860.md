# LME49860 / LME49870 (±22 V, 44 V bipolar dual / single)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*National's 44 V member of the LM4562/LME49720 audio series: 2.7 nV/√Hz, 0.00003% THD+N, ±22 V rails. Used in Sony PHA-2/PHA-3 and Gustard DACs and preamps.*

**Technology:** Bipolar voltage-feedback op-amp, unity-gain stable. LME49860 is the dual. LME49870 is the single: distributor and datasheet mirrors title it '44V Single High Performance, High Fidelity Audio Operational Amplifier' (National, later TI). Whether it uses the same die as the LME49860 is not confirmed. Supply is ±2.5 V to ±22 V, with a 46 V absolute maximum. Inputs are bipolar: IB 10 nA typ, differential input impedance 30 kΩ. A TI E2E thread titled 'LME49710, LME49720 and LME49860 Differential Input Diodes' covers the input clamp diodes. The datasheet lists SOIC-8 (D) and PDIP-8 (P) packages; TI's Nov-2025 addendum lists only the SOIC. SNAS389C does not use National's 'Overture' brand for this part.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Seen as the high-voltage (±22 V) member of National's LM4562/LME49720 audio series, with the same headline numbers (2.7 nV/√Hz, 0.00003% THD+N, 55 MHz) and more rail headroom. Forum summaries describe it as refined, detailed and wide-staged: in a head-fi round-up it sat between OPA2604 and AD823 in character, with a better soundstage than either. On diyAudio it is a 'very good all-rounder' with slightly 'synthetic' highs. In a VentureCraft Vantam swap test, Soomal found it firmer and more tense than MUSES02 while still warm, with springier bass, no muddy mid-bass, a wider upper-mid stage and more 3D vocals, and recommended it for speaker systems. Japanese blog summaries praise its clarity across the whole band and its refined highs; others find it plain. Opinion is split on whether it differs audibly from LME49720/LM4562. Nearly all of this is forum and blog opinion, not controlled listening tests.

**How people describe the sound:** between OPA2604 and AD823 in character, more refined and detailed, better soundstage than either (head-fi round-up, via search summary), very good all-rounder: powerful bass, good soundstage, detailed highs (diyAudio 'Best Sounding DUAL opamps', via search summary), slightly 'synthetic' highs (same diyAudio thread), 更硬朗，有张力 (firmer, more tension) but still warm; springier bass and no muddy mid-bass vs MUSES02; wider upper-mid stage, more 3D vocals (Soomal Vantam review), clarity across the whole band, good coherence and resolution, refined highs that are never unpleasant (JP blogs, via search summary), flat or plain with decent resolution; 'lacking' to some (JP, via search summary), less coloured than NJM2114DD; one JP review heard little difference from LME49990 (JP search summaries), some rank it LM4562 < LME49720HA < LME49860; others hear no difference (head-fi)

**Typical uses:** DAC output and filter stages (Gustard A18: four LME49860 duals, per the Frieve-A review), Balanced preamps (Gustard P26: two socketed duals, moved to SO-8 on a socket adapter after DIP-8 was discontinued), Portable DAC/amp voltage-gain stages: Sony PHA-2 (final analog output) and Sony PHA-3 (voltage gain and gain switch), per Soomal, Socketed pre stage in VentureCraft SoundDroid Vantam (stock LME49860; a MUSES02 version was also offered), per Soomal, Input op-amp in the Luxman/Stereo LXA-OT1 kit (low confidence: the sara-mac page title reads as a swap to LME49860NA), Op-amp rolling in DIP-8 sockets; pro-audio line drivers and receivers on ±18 to ±22 V rails; RIAA phono preamps and active filters (datasheet applications)

**Caveats:**

- 55 MHz GBW: capacitive loads above 100 pF must be isolated with a series resistor (datasheet). DIY notes flag socket parasitics, decoupling and CL peaking.
- The 1.6 pA/√Hz current noise means the 2.7 nV/√Hz advantage is lost in high-impedance (≈20 kΩ) networks (i_n × R ≈ 32 nV/√Hz), so it needs low-impedance feedback. IB of 10 nA typ / 72 nA max also adds DC offset there.
- Quiescent current of about 10.5 mA per package at ±22 V means heat and battery drain in portables.
- DIP-8 (NA/NOPB) and SOIC tube (MA/NOPB) went obsolete after a 28-Sep-2016 LTB (per E2E summary). Only SOIC tape-and-reel remains, and DIP sockets need an adapter.
- Differential input diodes (TI E2E 337068) conduct under large differential input, so it is not suitable as a comparator.
- Popcorn/low-frequency noise (TI E2E 415907) and EM-field sensitivity reports concern the sibling LM4562/LME49720, not specifically the LME49860.
- Sound reports are subjective and often whole-device impressions. Soomal found the PHA-3 soft and restrained despite its LME49860s.

### Revision chain status

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit report specific to the LME49860 was found, so the risk here is inferred. LME49860NA/NOPB (PDIP), LME49860MA/NOPB and LME49870MA/NOPB are obsolete: DigiKey lists them so, and a TI E2E 456802 summary gives an LTB of 28 Sep 2016. Any 'new' DIP LME49860NA or LME49870 is therefore old stock at best. Fakes of sibling parts are documented: a fake 'LME49720NA' (diyAudio 386852) and a fake LME49710 (tinytransistors.net, 2022). The discovery note that remarked LME49860s have been reported was not confirmed. How to check: (1) Genuine current SOIC parts are marked 'L49860' over 'MA' (TI addendum) with a logo and lot code. National-logo parts are older stock. (2) Total Iq should be about 10.2-10.5 mA per package at ±18-22 V. (3) THD+N should sit near analyzer residual, e_n about 2.7 nV/√Hz and GBW about 55 MHz. (4) Do not first power-test suspect parts at ±22 V: a remarked 36 V-class part would exceed its absolute maximum. (5) Buy SOIC LME49860MAX/NOPB from authorized distributors.

## Related parts and alternatives

LME49720 / LM4562 (same National series and headline specs, but ±17 V max; the LME49720 MA/NA also went EOL in 2016), OPA1602 (TI's suggested replacement for LME49720 in the EOL discussion; OPA1611 was suggested for LME49710). ±18 V class, so it does not suit ±22 V rails., OPA1612 (dual of OPA1611; a distributor cross-reference names OPA1612AID as the LME49860MA/NOPB replacement; ±18 V class), OPA2227P (a distributor cross-reference names it as the LME49860NA/NOPB PDIP replacement; ±18 V class), OPA2604 (FET-input dual rated to about ±24 V, one of the few rolling candidates that tolerates ±22 V rails), OPA1656 (modern CMOS-input low-distortion dual, ±18 V class), LME49870 (the 44 V single sibling; obsolete since the 2016 LTB), NE5532 (the classic it replaces in sockets; ±22 V is its absolute maximum, not its operating rating)

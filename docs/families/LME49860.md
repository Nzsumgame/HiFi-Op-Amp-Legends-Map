# LME49860 / LME49870

**Bipolar audio op-amp, ±22 V, dual / single** · National Semiconductor (2007–2011) → Texas Instruments (2011–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*The 44 V member of National's LM4562/LME49720 series: 2.7 nV/√Hz, 0.00003% THD+N, ±22 V rails. In Sony PHA-2/PHA-3.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.7 nV/√Hz typ, 4.7 max; 0.34 µVrms (20 Hz–20 kHz) | 55 MHz typ, 45 MHz min; FPBW 10 MHz | ±20 V/µs typ, ±15 V/µs min | 0.00003% typ, 0.00009% max (600 Ω); IMD 0.00005% | ±2.5 V to ±22 V (46 V absolute max) | 10.2 mA typ (±18 V); 10.5 typ / 13 max mA (±22 V) |

## Why it's rated

The ±22 V member of the LM4562/LME49720 series with the same headline numbers. Forums call it refined and detailed with a wide stage; diyAudio a 'very good all-rounder' with slightly 'synthetic' highs. Soomal found it firmer than MUSES02 yet warm. Japanese blogs praise its clarity; some find it plain. Opinion splits on whether it differs from the LME49720.

**Described as:** refined, detailed, wide soundstage, powerful bass, slightly synthetic highs, firmer, more tension (更硬朗，有张力), springy bass, 3D vocals, clear across the band, plain to some

**Used in:** DAC output and filter stages (Gustard A18), Balanced preamps (Gustard P26, now SO-8 on adapter), Sony PHA-2/PHA-3 and VentureCraft Vantam gain stages, Luxman LXA-OT1 input op-amp (low confidence), Op-amp rolling; ±22 V line drivers, RIAA preamps, filters

## Watch out

- Isolate capacitive loads above 100 pF with a series resistor; mind socket parasitics and decoupling.
- 1.6 pA/√Hz current noise erases its voltage-noise edge in ≈20 kΩ networks; use low-impedance feedback.
- About 10.5 mA per package at ±22 V: heat and battery drain in portables.
- DIP-8 and SOIC tube obsolete since 2016; DIP sockets now need an SO-8 adapter.
- Input diodes conduct on large differential input, so not a comparator. Popcorn-noise reports concern LM4562/LME49720.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific reports, but NA/NOPB, MA/NOPB and LME49870 are obsolete, so 'new' DIPs are old stock; sibling fakes exist. Genuine SOIC reads 'L49860 / MA'. Check Iq (~10.5 mA), noise and GBW; don't first power suspects at ±22 V.

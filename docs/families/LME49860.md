# LME49860 / LME49870 (±22 V, 44 V bipolar dual / single)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*National's 44 V member of the LM4562/LME49720 audio series: 2.7 nV/√Hz, 0.00003% THD+N, ±22 V rails. Used in Sony PHA-2/PHA-3 and Gustard DACs and preamps.*

**Technology:** Bipolar voltage-feedback op-amp, unity-gain stable. LME49860 is the dual; LME49870 is reported to be the single, but that is unverified. Supply is ±2.5 V to ±22 V, with a 46 V absolute maximum. Inputs are bipolar: IB 10 nA typ, differential input impedance 30 kΩ. A TI E2E thread titled 'LME49710, LME49720 and LME49860 Differential Input Diodes' covers the input clamp diodes. The datasheet lists SOIC-8 (D) and PDIP-8 (P) packages; TI's Nov-2025 addendum lists only the SOIC. SNAS389C does not use National's 'Overture' brand for this part.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Seen as the high-voltage (±22 V) member of National's LM4562/LME49720 audio series, with the same headline numbers (2.7 nV/√Hz, 0.00003% THD+N, 55 MHz) and more rail headroom. Forum summaries describe it as refined, detailed and wide-staged: in a head-fi round-up it sat between OPA2604 and AD823 in character, with a better soundstage than either. On diyAudio it is a 'very good all-rounder' with slightly 'synthetic' highs. In a VentureCraft Vantam swap test, Soomal found it firmer and more tense than MUSES02 while still warm, with springier bass, no muddy mid-bass, a wider upper-mid stage and more 3D vocals, and recommended it for speaker systems. Japanese blog summaries praise its clarity across the whole band and its refined highs; others find it plain. Opinion is split on whether it differs audibly from LME49720/LM4562. Some say it 'is just a LME49720 that passed a higher voltage test'; others rank LM4562 < LME49720HA < LME49860. Nearly all of this is forum and blog opinion, not controlled listening tests.

**How people describe the sound:** between OPA2604 and AD823 in character, more refined and detailed, better soundstage than either (head-fi round-up, via search summary), very good all-rounder: powerful bass, good soundstage, detailed highs (diyAudio 'Best Sounding DUAL opamps', via search summary), slightly 'synthetic' highs (same diyAudio thread), 更硬朗，有张力 (firmer, more tension) but still warm; springier bass and no muddy mid-bass vs MUSES02; wider upper-mid stage, more 3D vocals (Soomal Vantam review, verified), clarity across the whole band, good coherence and resolution, refined highs that are never unpleasant (JP blogs, via search summary), flat or plain with decent resolution; 'lacking' to some (JP, via search summary), less coloured than NJM2114DD; one JP review heard little difference from LME49990 (JP search summaries), some rank it LM4562 < LME49720HA < LME49860; others hear no difference (head-fi)

**Typical uses:** DAC output and filter stages (Gustard A18: four LME49860 duals, per the Frieve-A review), Balanced preamps (Gustard P26: two socketed duals, moved to SO-8 on a socket adapter after DIP-8 was discontinued), Portable DAC/amp voltage-gain stages: Sony PHA-2 (one per channel at the final analog output) and Sony PHA-3 (one dual per channel for voltage gain plus one at the gain switch), per Soomal, Socketed pre stage in VentureCraft SoundDroid Vantam (stock LME49860; a MUSES02 version was also offered), per Soomal, Input op-amp in the Luxman/Stereo LXA-OT1 kit (a JP search summary says LME49860NA is the default part; the sara-mac page title 'LXA-OT1 オペアンプ交換 LME49860NA編' is ambiguous; low confidence), Op-amp rolling in DIP-8 sockets; pro-audio line drivers and receivers on ±18 to ±22 V rails; RIAA phono preamps and active filters (datasheet applications)

**Caveats:**

- 55 MHz GBW: capacitive loads above 100 pF must be isolated with a series resistor (datasheet). DIY notes flag socket parasitics, decoupling and CL peaking.
- The 1.6 pA/√Hz current noise means the 2.7 nV/√Hz advantage is lost in high-impedance (≈20 kΩ) networks (i_n × R ≈ 32 nV/√Hz), so it needs low-impedance feedback. IB of 10 nA typ / 72 nA max also adds DC offset there.
- Quiescent current of about 10.5 mA per package at ±22 V means heat and battery drain in portables.
- DIP-8 (NA) is discontinued. Current parts are SOIC only and need an adapter for DIP sockets.
- Differential input diodes (TI E2E 337068) conduct under large differential input, so it is not suitable as a comparator.
- Popcorn/low-frequency noise (TI E2E 415907) and air-coupled EM-field sensitivity (ASR thread on the LM4562/LME497X0 family) are reported for the sibling LM4562/LME49720, not specifically for the LME49860.
- Sound reports are subjective and are often whole-device impressions. Soomal found the PHA-3 soft and restrained despite its LME49860s.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit report specific to the LME49860 was found, so the risk here is inferred. First, the PDIP LME49860NA is discontinued: it is absent from TI's Nov-2025 addendum, Gustard says DIP8 was discontinued, and an E2E/Gearspace EOL summary says it is 'no longer available in the N PDIP package, only SMT'. Second, fakes of sibling parts are documented: a fake 'LME49720NA' (diyAudio thread 386852) and a fake LME49710 (tinytransistors.net, 2022). A discovery note that 'remarked parts have been reported' for the LME49860 itself was not confirmed. Treat new-looking DIP LME49860NA from marketplaces as suspect. How to check: (1) Genuine current SOIC parts are marked 'L49860' over 'MA' (TI addendum) with a logo and lot code. National-logo parts are older, pre-TI or early-TI-era stock. (2) Total Iq should be about 10.2-10.5 mA per package at ±18-22 V. (3) THD+N should sit near analyzer residual, e_n about 2.7 nV/√Hz and GBW about 55 MHz. (4) Do not first power-test suspect parts at ±22 V: a remarked 36 V-class part would exceed its absolute maximum. (5) Buy SOIC LME49860MAX/NOPB from authorized distributors.

## Related parts and alternatives

LME49720 / LM4562 (same National series and headline specs, but ±17 V max; SOIC), OPA1602 (TI's suggested replacement for LME49720 in the LME49710/LME49720 EOL discussion; OPA1611 was suggested for LME49710). Supply is ±18 V class, so it does not suit ±22 V rails., OPA1612 (dual of OPA1611; bipolar ultra-low-noise, ±18 V class), OPA2604 (FET-input dual rated to about ±24 V, one of the few rolling candidates that tolerates ±22 V rails), OPA1656 (modern CMOS-input low-distortion dual, ±18 V class), LME49870 (the reported single 44 V sibling; existence and availability not verified), NE5532 (the classic it replaces in sockets; ±22 V is its absolute maximum, not its operating rating)

## Open questions

- The WebSearch budget (200/200) was exhausted in this run too, so none of the 15+ requested fact-check searches ran. Verification used four things: the full SNAS389C text and PDF metadata (GitHub copy of the TI symlink); GitHub code search (Soomal posts, the Gustard A18/P26 texts, indare notes, a KiCad schematic and PCB); WebSearch result titles and summaries that sibling agents cached earlier in this session; and nothing else.
- Inferred and never seen: the TI revision-specific URLs snas389/snas389a/snas389b/snas389c, the lme49870.pdf symlink, and every national.com URL in archive_seed_urls.
- Do TI revisions SNAS389 (-), A and B exist? The rev C change log lists only 1.0, 1.1 and C. The sibling LME49720 had a TI-headered SNAS393B dated October 2007, so an SNAS389B may exist.
- LME49870: existence, literature number, specs and TI status are all unverified (0 GitHub hits). It is not confirmed to share the LME49860 die.
- Same die as LME49720? Forum summaries say it 'is just a LME49720 that passed a higher voltage test'. Another summary says the LME49720 output stage is optimized for under ±17 V and the LME49860 for ±14.5 to ±22 V. The datasheet AC typicals are essentially identical; supply range, absolute max and output specs differ. Neither National nor TI has confirmed either view.
- Discontinuation dates and PCN numbers for LME49860NA (PDIP) and LME49860MA (SOIC tube) were not found. TI fab-migration PCNs affecting the LME49860 were not checked.
- LXA-OT1 stock op-amp: one JP search summary says LME49860NA is the default part, but the sara-mac post is titled as a swap to LME49860NA ('オペアンプ交換 LME49860NA編'). This needs a primary source such as Luxman, Stereo magazine or the kit BOM.
- No direct bench measurement of the LME49860 alone (ASR, Groner, NwAvGuy) was found. The head-fi round-up (thread 870832) reportedly includes measurements, but its content was not read.

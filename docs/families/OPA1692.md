# OPA1692 (TI SoundPlus low-power, low-noise dual bipolar-input audio op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

**Technology:** Bipolar-input, low-power SoundPlus audio dual. Search results give 4.2 nV/√Hz, -127 dB distortion at 1 kHz, 650 µA per channel, ±50 mA output, 3.5 V to 36 V supply and rail-to-rail output.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Cycfi Research's blog 'OPA1692: The perfect Op Amp for low power audio?' reports that it 'sounds fast, dynamic and had good timing on most CDs', though the LME49723 separated instruments better in one track. In Head-Fi's 'The Opamp thread' it is ranked among the three best on specs (with the OPA2209 and OPA2189), and posters prefer it over the OPA1678.

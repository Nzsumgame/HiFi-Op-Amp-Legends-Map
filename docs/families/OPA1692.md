# OPA1692 (TI SoundPlus low-power, low-noise dual bipolar-input audio op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*Draws only 650 µA per channel yet gives 4.2 nV/√Hz and -127 dB distortion. Low-power audio builders and Head-Fi op-amp rollers rate it highly.*

**Technology:** Bipolar-input, low-power SoundPlus dual audio op-amp (TI, 2017). Rail-to-rail output swings to within 200 mV of the supplies into 2 kΩ. 4.2 nV/√Hz at 1 kHz, -127 dB distortion at 1 kHz, 650 µA per channel. Web search summaries give 5.1 MHz GBW and 23 V/µs slew. The earlier sweep gave a 3.5 V to 36 V supply and ±50 mA output; this pass did not confirm those two.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Datasheet respec).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Cycfi Research's 2017 blog post 'OPA1692: The perfect Op Amp for low power audio?' says it 'sounds fast, dynamic and had good timing on most CDs', though the LME49723 separated instruments better on one track. In Head-Fi's 'The Opamp thread', posters rank it among the three best on specs (with the OPA2209 and OPA2189) and prefer it to the OPA1678. The praise comes from a few listener reports, not from measurements.

**How people describe the sound:** fast (Cycfi), dynamic (Cycfi), good timing (Cycfi)

**Typical uses:** low-power / battery audio stages (Cycfi's framing), op-amp rolling in DIP-8 sockets using SOIC-to-DIP adapters, portable preamp and filter stages

**Caveats:**

- SMD only (SOIC-8, VSSOP-8), so DIP sockets need an adapter
- Cycfi preferred the LME49723 for instrument separation on one track
- Praise rests on a small number of subjective reports

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Datasheet respec: Texas Instruments, Datasheet SBOS566C October 2018; notification 5 Nov 2018

No silicon change is known. The only TI notice found is a datasheet-only specification change (SBOS566B to SBOS566C) to 'accurately reflect device characteristics', with no change to the device. Which parameters changed was not retrieved, so specs quoted from rev B or earlier copies may differ from rev C.

- **When:** Datasheet SBOS566C October 2018; notification 5 Nov 2018
- **Affected:** OPA1692ID, OPA1692IDR, OPA1692IDGKR, OPA1692IDGKT
- **How to tell old from new:** None needed. The silicon did not change, so parts built before and after Nov 2018 are the same device. Only the datasheet limits and typicals differ between SBOS566B and SBOS566C.
- **Audio impact:** None expected, since the die is the same. When comparing specs, use rev C values.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - datasheet-only change; the notice says there is no device change
- **Confidence:** high

### Revision chain status

No research was done. The first WebSearch call came back with "web search budget exhausted (200 of 200)", so I stopped as instructed. I made no other searches, used no other tools and added no new data. The chain below comes entirely from the existing OPA1692.json. Texas Instruments SBOS566 is the only vendor document; there are no second sources and no acquirer re-issues. (1) SBOS566 original, June 2017: Advance Information, covering the OPA1692 dual and the OPA1694 quad. No copy is confirmed as holding it. The alldatasheet copy 954302 is only a guess (low confidence). (2) SBOS566A, about December 2017 (medium confidence, from TI E2E): moved to Production Data and dropped the OPA1694. No copy confirmed. (3) SBOS566B, date unknown, sometime between December 2017 and October 2018: it is known only as the 'before' version in TI's 5 Nov 2018 change notice. Contents and copies were not found. (4) SBOS566C, October 2018: specification changes to reflect device characteristics, with no silicon change. This is the latest known revision, held by ti.com/lit/gpn/OPA1692 and the Scribd copy sbos566c. Still missing: the exact date and a copy for rev A; the date, change summary and a copy for rev B; a copy confirmed to hold the June 2017 original; the parameter-level changes in rev C; the revision held by the JA translation; whether any revision after C exists; and legacy TI archive links for these revisions (for example focus.ti.com or revision-specific ti.com/lit/ds/sbos566a URLs). These need another search pass once the search budget is raised.

## Counterfeits

No specific counterfeit reports found. The top-side marking is 'OP1692' (TI package marking, mirrored on alldatasheet.net). The part is SMD only (SOIC-8, VSSOP-8). Marketplace sellers (e.g. eBay) sell 'OPA1692IDR' pre-soldered on DIP-8 adapters for op-amp rolling. Buy from TI or authorized distributors (Mouser, Digi-Key, Farnell) and check the OP1692 marking and TI logo/date code.

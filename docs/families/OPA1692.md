# OPA1692 / OPA1694

**Bipolar-input low-power audio dual (SoundPlus)** · Texas Instruments (2017–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** No silicon change is known. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*Only 650 µA per channel, yet 4.2 nV/√Hz and −127 dB distortion; a favourite for low-power audio builds.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.2 nV/√Hz | 5.1 MHz typ | 23 V/µs typ | −127 dB | 3.5 V to 36 V (±1.75 V to ±18 V) | 650 µA per channel typ |

## Why it's rated

Cycfi Research (2017) found it fast, dynamic and well-timed on most CDs, though the LME49723 separated instruments better on one track. Head-Fi's Opamp thread ranks it among the best on specs with the OPA2209 and OPA2189, and preferred to the OPA1678. Praise rests on a few listener reports, not measurements.

**Described as:** fast, dynamic, good timing

**Used in:** Low-power / battery audio stages, Op-amp rolling in DIP-8 sockets via SOIC adapters, Portable preamp and filter stages

## Watch out

- SMD only (SOIC-8, VSSOP-8); DIP sockets need an adapter.
- Cycfi preferred the LME49723 for instrument separation on one track.
- Praise rests on a small number of subjective reports.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit reports found. Genuine top marking is 'OP1692'. eBay sellers offer OPA1692IDR pre-soldered on DIP-8 adapters. Buy from TI, Mouser, Digi-Key or Farnell and check the marking, TI logo and date code.

## Silicon changes

### Datasheet respec: Texas Instruments, 2018-10 · SBOS566C; notice 2018-11-05

No silicon change is known. TI's only notice is a datasheet-only spec change (SBOS566B to SBOS566C) 'to accurately reflect device characteristics'. Changed parameters were not retrieved, so rev B or earlier figures may differ from rev C.

- **Affected:** OPA1692ID, OPA1692IDR, OPA1692IDGKR, OPA1692IDGKT
- **How to tell:** None needed: the silicon did not change. Only datasheet limits and typicals differ between SBOS566B and SBOS566C.
- **Audio impact:** None expected; same die. Use rev C values when comparing specs.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: datasheet-only change; the notice says the device is unchanged.
- **Confidence:** high

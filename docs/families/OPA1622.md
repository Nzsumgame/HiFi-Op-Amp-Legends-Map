# OPA1622 (dual bipolar-input, high-output-current headphone-driver op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

*TI's 2015 dual bipolar headphone driver: 2.8 nV/rtHz, -119 dB THD+N at 100 mW into 32 ohms and +145/-130 mA drive. It became the default driver in Chinese DAPs and phones.*

**Technology:** Dual bipolar-input SoundPlus audio op-amp, with "Burr-Brown Audio" branding on the datasheet. It uses a single gain stage: a high-gain transconductance input stage feeding a unity-gain high-current output stage. It has a dedicated GND reference pin, to which the compensation capacitors and the enable logic are referenced, plus an EN/shutdown pin (5 uA per channel) and thermal shutdown at 175 C. It comes only in a VSON-10 (DRC) 3x3 mm package with a copper leadframe and an exposed pad that must be tied to V-.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

OPA1622 is a purpose-built headphone driver. It combines ultra-low THD+N into 16-32 ohm loads, 2.8 nV/rtHz noise and high current on 2.6 mA/ch, which suits battery gear. Portable-audio makers adopted it widely: FiiO (X7 AM3 module, X3 III, M9), Lotoo (PAW S1, Paw Gold Touch 4.4 mm output, PAW6000), HiBy (R3 Pro II and R6 Pro Max, four each), vivo Xplay6 (three), Smartisan M1L (two), and Xiaomi MSM8996 phones, whose kernels carry a 'ti,opa1622' driver. DIYers built the AmpCasq integrated headamp around it (diyAudio / headphones.com). Japanese blogs call its sound clear and fast, with tidy contours, tight bass and good rhythmic attack, and use it to add 'tightness and speed'. The community praise comes from discovery notes and was not re-verified this run.

**How people describe the sound:** clear, fast, tidy/defined contours, tight bass, good rhythmic attack, adds tightness and speed (JP blogs)

**Typical uses:** headphone output driver in DAPs, USB dongles and phones, DAC differential-to-single-ended converter plus headphone driver (TI reference design TIDUAW1), balanced (4.4 mm / 2.5 mm) headphone outputs, one device per leg, DIY integrated headamp (AmpCasq), high-current line/buffer stage after a DAC low-pass filter, op-amp rolling on VSON-10-to-DIP-8 adapter boards

**Caveats:**

- Only available in VSON-10 3x3 mm with an exposed pad that must be soldered to V-, never ground. There is no DIP/SOIC version, so socket use needs an adapter.
- Non-standard pinout with GND (pin 3) and EN (pin 8) pins, so it is not drop-in for standard dual op-amp footprints. A DIP-8 socket has no ground pin, so adapters must synthesize GND/EN. PSRR may suffer because the compensation capacitors are referenced to GND (inference from the datasheet).
- EN must not float or sit on a high-Z GPIO, or the amp stays disabled. EN thresholds (0.78/0.82 V) are referenced to the GND pin. The output is NOT high-impedance in shutdown.
- Output drive is limited to about +145/-130 mA typical short-circuit current. In TI's circuit that gives 150 mW into 32 ohm and 95 mW into 16 ohm before clipping, so one device per leg cannot deliver watt-class power.
- Thermal: RthetaJA is 47.6 C/W and the part shuts down at 175 C, then cycles on and off while the fault persists. High rails driving low-ohm loads need copper under the pad.
- Asymmetric supply ramp-up can cause audible pops. TI advises holding EN low while the rails ramp.
- Minimum supply is +/-2 V (rev A).
- Differential input clamp diodes (+/-0.5 V) can conduct in G=+1 circuits with fast inputs, so limit input current to 10 mA.

## Counterfeits

No specific counterfeit or remarked-OPA1622 reports were found this run. Genuine parts are VSON-10 (DRC) with the top marking 'O1622', per the TI package addendum, plus TI logo and lot-trace code. The part is sold on 250- or 3000-unit reels. In the hobby op-amp-rolling market it is usually pre-soldered on VSON-10-to-DIP-8 adapter boards (e.g. the iancanada OPA1622 adapter kit), where the chip cannot be inspected and GND/EN wiring varies. Buy bare parts from authorized distributors and inspect adapter boards for the O1622 marking. In 2021-era shortages OPA1622IDRCR was scarce enough that people wrote TI-store stock monitors (noxue/ticom), a typical setting for grey-market substitutes.

## Related parts and alternatives

TI INA1620: dual high-fidelity audio amp with integrated matched resistors, also with a GND pin and a V- thermal pad; relation to the OPA1622 core not verified, TI TPA6120A2: current-feedback headphone amplifier used in FiiO X7 AM5, OPA1612 or OPA1656 with a buffer (LME49600 or BUF634A) for higher current, e.g. OPA1612+LME49600 in Lotoo Paw Gold Touch 3.5 mm, ADI AD8397: high-current rail-to-rail driver used in FiiO X7 AM1, Maxim MAX97220: low-power headphone driver used in DIY boards

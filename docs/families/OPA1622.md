# OPA1622

**Bipolar-input headphone-driver op-amp, dual** · Texas Instruments (2015-11–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*TI's 2015 dual headphone driver: 2.8 nV/√Hz, −119.2 dB THD+N at 100 mW/32 Ω, +145/−130 mA. Common in DAPs and phones.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.8 nV/√Hz (4 at 100 Hz, 10 at 10 Hz); 2.1 µVpp | 32 MHz (G = +1000); 8 MHz (G = 1) | 10 V/µs | −119.2 dB @100 mW/32 Ω; −116 dB @10 mW/32 Ω; −132 dB @2 kΩ | ±2 V to ±18 V (4–36 V); abs max 40 V; unequal rails OK | 2.6 mA/ch typ, 3.3 max (4.2 max −40–125 °C); 5 µA off |

## Why it's rated

A purpose-built headphone driver: very low THD+N into 16–32 Ω, 2.8 nV/√Hz and high current on 2.6 mA/ch suit battery gear. Reportedly used in FiiO (X7 AM3, X3 III, M9), Lotoo (PAW S1, Gold Touch, PAW6000) and HiBy (R3 Pro II, R6 Pro Max) players, vivo Xplay6, Smartisan M1L and Xiaomi MSM8996 phones, and the DIY AmpCasq headamp.

**Described as:** clear, fast, defined contours, tight bass, good rhythmic attack, adds tightness and speed

**Used in:** Headphone output in DAPs, USB dongles and phones, DAC diff-to-SE converter plus driver (TIDUAW1, unconfirmed), Balanced 4.4/2.5 mm outputs, one device per leg, DIY integrated headamp (AmpCasq); post-DAC line buffer, Rolling on VSON-10-to-DIP-8 adapters (Audiophonics DIP8 unit)

## Watch out

- VSON-10 only; exposed pad must go to V−, never ground. Sockets need an adapter.
- Non-standard pinout with GND and EN pins; DIP adapters must synthesize them, possibly hurting PSRR.
- EN must not float (thresholds 0.78/0.82 V vs GND); output is not high-Z in shutdown. Hold EN low during ramp-up.
- About +145/−130 mA: TI's circuit gives 150 mW/32 Ω, 95 mW/16 Ω before clipping.
- 47.6 °C/W, shutdown at 175 °C; needs copper under the pad. Minimum ±2 V; input clamps ±0.5 V.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit reports found. Genuine parts are VSON-10 (DRC) marked 'O1622' with TI logo and lot code; '.B' orderables are identical. Adapter boards hide the chip and vary GND/EN wiring. 2021 shortages made it scarce; buy from authorized distributors.

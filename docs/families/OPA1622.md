# OPA1622 (dual bipolar-input, high-output-current headphone-driver op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

*TI's 2015 dual bipolar headphone driver: 2.8 nV/rtHz, -119.2 dB THD+N at 100 mW into 32 ohms, +145/-130 mA drive. Widely used in Chinese DAPs and phones.*

**Technology:** Dual bipolar-input SoundPlus audio op-amp, with "Burr-Brown Audio" branding on the datasheet. It uses a single gain stage: a high-gain transconductance input stage feeding a unity-gain high-current output stage. It has a dedicated GND reference pin, to which the compensation capacitors and the enable logic are referenced, plus an EN/shutdown pin (5 uA per channel) and thermal shutdown at 175 C. It comes only in a VSON-10 (DRC) 3x3 mm package with a copper leadframe and an exposed pad that must be tied to V-.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

OPA1622 is a purpose-built headphone driver. It combines very low THD+N into 16-32 ohm loads, 2.8 nV/rtHz noise and high output current on 2.6 mA/ch, which suits battery gear. Teardown and review notes list it in FiiO (X7 AM3 module, X3 III, M9), Lotoo (PAW S1, Paw Gold Touch 4.4 mm output, PAW6000) and HiBy (R3 Pro II, R6 Pro Max) players, vivo Xplay6, Smartisan M1L, and Xiaomi MSM8996 phones, whose kernels carry a 'ti,opa1622' driver. DIYers built the AmpCasq integrated headamp around it (diyAudio / headphones.com). Some Japanese hobby blogs describe its sound as clear and fast, with tight bass. All community praise and device lists come from discovery notes and GitHub mirrors and were not re-verified by web search.

**How people describe the sound:** clear, fast, tidy/defined contours, tight bass, good rhythmic attack, adds tightness and speed (JP blogs)

**Typical uses:** headphone output driver in DAPs, USB dongles and phones, DAC differential-to-single-ended converter plus headphone driver (TI reference design TIDUAW1, unconfirmed), balanced (4.4 mm / 2.5 mm) headphone outputs, one device per leg, DIY integrated headamp (AmpCasq), high-current line/buffer stage after a DAC low-pass filter, op-amp rolling on VSON-10-to-DIP-8 adapter boards (e.g. Audiophonics OPA1622 DIP8 unit)

**Caveats:**

- Only available in VSON-10 3x3 mm with an exposed pad that must be soldered to V-, never ground. There is no DIP/SOIC version, so socket use needs an adapter.
- Non-standard pinout with GND (pin 3) and EN (pin 8) pins, so it is not drop-in for standard dual op-amp footprints. A DIP-8 socket has no ground pin, so adapters must synthesize GND/EN. PSRR may suffer because the compensation capacitors are referenced to GND (inference from the datasheet).
- EN must not float or sit on a high-Z GPIO, or the amp stays disabled. EN thresholds (0.78/0.82 V) are referenced to the GND pin. The output is NOT high-impedance in shutdown.
- Output drive is limited to about +145/-130 mA. In TI's circuit that gives 150 mW into 32 ohm and 95 mW into 16 ohm before clipping, so one device per leg cannot deliver watt-class power.
- Thermal: RthetaJA is 47.6 C/W and the part shuts down at 175 C, then cycles on and off while the fault persists. High rails driving low-ohm loads need copper under the pad.
- Asymmetric supply ramp-up can cause audible pops. TI advises holding EN low while the rails ramp.
- Minimum supply is +/-2 V.
- Differential input clamp diodes (+/-0.5 V) can conduct in G=+1 circuits with fast inputs, so limit input current to 10 mA.

## Counterfeits

No specific counterfeit or remarked-OPA1622 reports were found. Genuine parts are VSON-10 (DRC) with the top marking 'O1622', per the TI package addendum and alldatasheet marking page, plus the TI logo and a lot-trace code. The part ships on 250- or 3000-unit reels. The '.B' orderable suffix (OPA1622IDRCR.B) that appears in newer TI listings is a variant TI calls identical to OPA1622IDRCR, not a different die. In the hobby op-amp-rolling market it is usually pre-soldered on VSON-10-to-DIP-8 adapter boards (e.g. the iancanada adapter kit, Audiophonics 'OPA1622 Dual OPA DIP8'), where the chip cannot be inspected and GND/EN wiring varies. Buy bare parts from authorized distributors and inspect adapter boards for the O1622 marking. In 2021-era shortages OPA1622IDRCR was scarce enough that people wrote TI-store stock monitors (noxue/ticom), a typical setting for grey-market substitutes.

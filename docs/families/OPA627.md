# OPA627 / OPA637

**Difet JFET-input precision single (OPA637: G ≥ 5)** · Burr-Brown (1989–2000) → Texas Instruments (2000–present) → Rochester Electronics (post-discontinuation) · Tier A (legend / revision hazard)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** TI's 2024–2025 rewrite deleted 'Difet', added the OPA627BU and made molded trim pins NC. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*Burr-Brown's Difet JFET single, long the 'king' of op-amp rolling: detailed, smooth, pA bias, 4.5 nV/√Hz.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5 nV/√Hz (10 kHz; 1 kHz value not captured) | 16 MHz (OPA627); 80 MHz (OPA637, G = 10) | 55 V/µs (OPA627); 135 V/µs (OPA637) | ±4.5 V to ±18 V | 7.5 mA max per amp (~7–7.5 mA observed) |

## Why it's rated

The benchmark audiophile JFET single: the 'king' on English forums, 'legendary' in Japan. Soomal hears it warm-leaning with full mids, strong dynamics and space, resolving HF better than OPA111/OPA2107, and rates the OPA637 slightly higher. Leckerton notes blind tests show no clear win over cheaper parts like the AD8610.

**Described as:** detailed, high resolution, warm-leaning, full mids, dynamic, spacious, easy to listen to, smooth, dark (folklore)

**Used in:** DAC I/V and post-filter stages (HiFiMAN HM-901), Headphone amp cards with BUF634 (HiFiMAN IEM, Minibox), Line buffers, preamps, phono and high-impedance inputs, Op-amp rolling in sound cards and DACs via adapters, Photodiode transimpedance and instrumentation front ends

## Watch out

- Single only; dual sockets need an adapter. Up to ~7.5 mA per amp means heat and battery drain.
- OPA637 is unstable below a noise gain of about 5; never use it as a unity-gain buffer.
- One of the most counterfeited audiophile op-amps; DIP versions are obsolete.
- Current molded parts have pins 1/5 NC; offset trim works on TO-99 only.
- Origin folklore (US > Taiwan > SE Asia) and grade differences are unmeasured; A/B grades reportedly inaudible.
- **Counterfeits:** Heavily faked: ZeptoBars (2014) found an AD744 die in a $2.7 eBay 'OPA627'. Check Iq (~7–7.5 mA), slew, square waves; trim-pin tests fail on new parts. Cheap TO-99 OPA637BM and 'custom metal-can' parts are fake. Buy AU/BU authorized.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments, 2024-04 / 2025-01 · SBOS165B/C

TI's 2024–2025 rewrite deleted 'Difet', added the OPA627BU and made molded trim pins NC. For the sister OPA132, TI cites a 'new FAB change and design'. This suggests new silicon under the same part numbers, but no PCN or OPA627-specific confirmation exists. Rev A was still cited in June 2022.

- **Affected:** OPA627AU, OPA627BU, OPA637AU
- **How to tell:** SBOS165B/C titled 'OPA6x7 Precision, High-Speed JFET'; molded pins 1/5 NC; OPA627BU only from 2024–2025. No PCN or date-code cut-over; BB-logo and pre-2024 parts keep working trim pins.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown; no before/after measurements. Pin 1/5 offset nulling is lost on new molded parts; Burr-Brown-era impressions may not carry over.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: offset-trim circuits fail on new molded parts; Rev A to C electrical deltas not captured.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Pins 1/5 (SOIC/DIP) | Offset Trim (SBOS165A) | NC; trim documented for TO-99 only (SBOS165B/C) |
| Process description | Precision High-Speed Difet | Precision, High-Speed JFET (Difet references deleted) |
| SOIC grades | AU only | AU and BU (BU production data in Rev C, Jan 2025) |

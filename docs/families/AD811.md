# AD811

**Current-feedback video op-amp, single** · Analog Devices (before 2004–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*ADI current-feedback video amp (2500 V/µs, 25 ns settling) that diyAudio builders use as a very fast DAC I/V stage.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.9 nV/√Hz | −3 dB BW 140 MHz (G = +1), 120 MHz (G = +2) | 2500 V/µs | ±4.5 V to ±18 V |

## Why it's rated

diyAudio posters rate it highly as an I/V converter for current-output DACs, in a dedicated thread, a build thread and the long 'Best opamp for I/V conversion?' thread. They say it beats other op-amps they tried, including an OPA861 alternative, crediting 2500 V/µs and 25 ns settling (vs ~550 ns quoted for OPA627). Forum opinion, not measurements.

**Used in:** I/V after current-output DACs (PCM61, PCM1702), Video line driver / broadcast video, ADC/DAC buffer

## Watch out

- Current feedback: the feedback resistor sets bandwidth and stability; a capacitor across it needs care.
- Runs hot; diyAudio posters suggest a small heatsink at ±14 V.
- AD811AN DIP-8 is obsolete; newer stock is SOIC (incl. 16-lead wide), so DIP sockets need adapters.
- Praise comes from a few diyAudio threads; linking slew or settling to sound is the posters' reasoning.
- **Counterfeits:** No AD811 fake reports found. AD811AN (PDIP-8) is obsolete, so cheap DIP-8 AD811s for DAC rolling are old stock at best and may be relabelled. Check date codes and markings, and confirm current-feedback behaviour and very fast edges.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

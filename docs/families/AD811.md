# AD811 (Analog Devices single high-speed current-feedback op-amp, used as a DAC I/V stage)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

**Technology:** Single bipolar current-feedback video op-amp: about 2500 V/us slew, about 25 ns settling, high output current

## Why enthusiasts rate it

diyAudio has a dedicated thread, 'AD811 as I/V stage for current DACs (and test some other opamps including Burson Audio opamps as I/V)', and a build thread, 'AD811 IV completed and tested'. The long 'Best opamp for I/V conversion?' thread discusses it too. In these threads posters say it sounds much better than other op-amps in a classic I/V stage, and better than an OPA861 alternative. They credit its 2500 V/us slew rate and 25 ns settling, against 550 ns for the OPA627. It is often paired with PCM61, PCM1702 and similar current-output DACs. Posters warn that it needs a small heatsink at +/-14 V.

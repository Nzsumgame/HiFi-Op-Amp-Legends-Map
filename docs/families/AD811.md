# AD811 (Analog Devices single high-speed current-feedback op-amp, used as a DAC I/V stage)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*Analog Devices current-feedback video amp (2500 V/us, 25 ns settling) that diyAudio builders use as a very fast I/V stage for current-output DACs.*

**Technology:** Single-channel bipolar wideband current-feedback op-amp, designed for broadcast video. Rated 2500 V/us slew, 25 ns settling to 0.1%, 1.9 nV/rtHz voltage noise and ±4.5 V to ±18 V supplies.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

diyAudio posters rate it highly as an I/V converter for current-output DACs. There is a dedicated thread ('AD811 as I/V stage for current DACs...'), a build thread ('AD811 IV completed and tested') and posts in the long 'Best opamp for I/V conversion?' thread. Those posters say it sounds better than other op-amps they tried in a classic I/V stage, including an OPA861 alternative. They credit its 2500 V/us slew and 25 ns settling (versus about 550 ns quoted for the OPA627). This is enthusiast forum opinion, not measured audio data.

**Typical uses:** I/V converter after current-output audio DACs (posters mention PCM61, PCM1702 and similar), Video line driver / broadcast video (datasheet), ADC/DAC buffer (datasheet application list)

**Caveats:**

- Current-feedback amplifier: the feedback resistor value sets bandwidth and stability. The usual voltage-feedback I/V topology with a capacitor directly across the feedback resistor needs care with a CFA.
- Runs hot: diyAudio posters recommend a small heatsink at ±14 V supplies.
- AD811AN (DIP-8) is listed obsolete at DigiKey. Newer stock is SOIC (including the 16-lead wide SOIC AD811ARZ-16), so DIP sockets need an adapter.
- The praise comes from a handful of diyAudio threads. It is subjective, and treating slew rate or settling time as proof of sound quality is the posters' reasoning, not ADI's.

## Counterfeits

No AD811-specific counterfeit reports were found in this pass. AD811AN (PDIP-8) is listed obsolete at DigiKey, so DIP-8 AD811s sold cheaply on marketplaces for DAC op-amp rolling are old stock at best and may be relabelled. Check date codes and markings, and check that the part shows current-feedback behaviour and very fast edges.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA861 (compared against the AD811 in the diyAudio I/V thread), AD844 (another ADI current-feedback amp often used for DAC I/V), OPA627 (voltage-feedback I/V favourite; diyAudio posters contrast its 550 ns settling)

# AD711 / AD712 / AD713

**BiFET (JFET-input) precision op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · Analog Devices (1986–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** Camas, WA wafer fab qualified for ADI's bipolar process 'to ensure reliable and continuous supply'. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*ADI's laser-trimmed 1986 BiFET family (0.0003% THD, 20 V/µs, pA bias): the classic 1990s TL072/LF412 upgrade.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 nV/√Hz (10 Hz: 45, 100 Hz: 22, 10 kHz: 16) | 4.0 MHz typ; 3.0 MHz min (J/A/S), 3.4 MHz min (K) | 20 V/µs typ; 16 min (J/A/S), 18 min (K) | 0.0003% THD; crosstalk 120 dB @1 kHz, 90 dB @100 kHz | ±4.5 V to ±18 V; rated ±15 V; abs. max ±18 V | 5.0 mA typ both amps; max 6.8 (J/A/S), 6.0 (K) |

## Why it's rated

A mainstream 'better' audio dual of the late 1980s and 1990s: the 1993 rec.audio FAQ lists the AD712 with NE5532, LM833, AD827 and OPA2604. Marketed as an LF411/TL081 and LF412/TL082 upgrade, it became a drop-in TL072 swap. Low THD, 120 dB crosstalk and pA bias suit high-impedance line stages and filters. Now a legacy part; no sonic descriptors collected.

**Used in:** drop-in TL072/TL082/LF412 upgrade in CD and DAC output stages, high-impedance line buffers and active filters, DAC/ADC buffers needing 1.0 µs settling to 0.01%, photodiode and electrometer preamps (pA bias, guarding)

## Watch out

- 18 nV/√Hz is noisy next to NE5532, OP27, OPA1642 or OPA827; poor for low-impedance or MM-phono inputs.
- Output phase reversal below about −14.1 V input at ±15 V; CM range only to −11.5 V.
- Needs ±4.5 V minimum, not rail-to-rail; ~25 mA output is no headphone driver.
- Loads above ~1500 pF need a 100 Ω isolation resistor (Figure 46).
- Bias current doubles every 10 °C, raising offset errors in hot DC-coupled designs.
- **Counterfeits:** None documented. The risk is fake OPA627s: ZeptoBars found an AD744 die; Head-Fi reports remarked AD711s (~18 kΩ between pins). Tell-tales: ~20 vs ~55 V/µs slew, lower supply current. Pre-Rev. D datasheets show the die photo.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2025-06 (eff. 2025-09) · PCN 25_0091

Camas, WA wafer fab qualified for ADI's bipolar process 'to ensure reliable and continuous supply'. Published 6 Jun 2025, effective 8 Sep 2025; Rev. A form and report followed. Lists multiple AD711/AD712/AD713 variants. Separate from PCN 26_0029 (Camas complementary bipolar: AD797, AD823).

- **Affected:** AD711, AD712, AD713
- **How to tell:** None given. Standard lot traceability; no marking or date-code change mentioned; previous fab not named.
- **Audio impact:** None documented; ADI expects no change to fit, form, function or reliability. Typical values may shift within limits.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same part numbers and datasheet; drop-in per ADI. Only lot provenance changes.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab site | previous ADI bipolar fab (not named in the sources seen) | ADI Camas, WA qualified |

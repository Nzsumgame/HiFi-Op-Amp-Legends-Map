# OPA1611 / OPA1612

**Bipolar-input low-noise audio op-amp, single / dual** · Texas Instruments (2009–present) → Texas Instruments (2015–present) · Tier A (legend / revision hazard)

*TI's 1.1 nV/√Hz, −136 dB THD+N bipolar op-amp, the go-to I/V and buffer part in modern DACs, DAPs and phones.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 nV/√Hz typ, 1.5 max (1.5 @100 Hz, 2 @10 Hz) | 40 MHz (G = 1); 80 MHz (G = 100) | 27 V/µs; FPBW 4 MHz; overload recovery 500 ns | 0.000015% (−136 dB); IMD −136 to −142 dB | ±2.25 V to ±18 V (4.5–36 V); 40 V absolute max | 3.6 mA typ, 4.5 mA max per channel (5.5 mA max hot) |

## Why it's rated

Measured noise and distortion are exceptional, and measurement-minded users treat it as transparent. audioXpress found it beat NE5532 with shunt feedback and as a follower, equal with series feedback. Soomal tracks it in Chinese phones and DAPs from 2014 and, by 2022, in most I/V stages. Subjective views split: 'reference sound' in Japan, 'hard, not pleasing' (偏硬) at Soomal.

**Described as:** neutral 'reference sound', transparent, flat, genre-neutral, musical, delicate, warm (OPA1611), hard (偏硬), not pleasing, better dynamics than NE5534, worse than DiFET types

**Used in:** DAC I/V conversion (Sabaj A20d, Topping DX7 Pro), Output LPF or post-I/V buffer in DAPs and Hi-Fi phones, Input stage of composite headphone amps with TPA6120 / THS6012, Pro audio, mic preamps, consoles, broadcast, test gear, Op-amp rolling via DIP-8 adapter boards (NFJ, AliExpress)

## Watch out

- High current noise (1.7 pA/√Hz) loses its edge with 10 kΩ+ source or feedback networks.
- Input range stops 2 V from each rail; clamp diodes conduct on fast edges, limit input to 10 mA.
- Capacitive loads at low gain can oscillate; use ~50 Ω isolation and careful adapter layout.
- 3.6 mA/ch with 150 °C/W SOIC-8: solder rather than socket. NFJ 'new lot' reportedly means boards, not silicon.
- Subjective descriptors conflict; Soomal 2016 mislabels it 'Bipolar FET'. No controlled listening test.
- **Counterfeits:** Relabelled TL082 fakes confirmed by decap (diyAudio 332536, 10 pcs €6.80 from a Chinese eBay seller). Cheap AliExpress parts can be genuine (diyAudio 399994). No DIP-8 exists; real marks 'OPA 1611A/1612A', DRG 'OVII'. Expect ~3.6 mA/ch, tens-of-nA bias, ~1.1 nV/√Hz.

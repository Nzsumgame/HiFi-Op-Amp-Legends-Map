# NJM4558 / JRC4558D / RC4558 / uPC4558 / MC4558

**Bipolar dual, general-purpose ("dual 741")** · Raytheon (1971–c. 1997) → Fairchild (after c. 1997) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2022–present) · second sources: Texas Instruments, New Japan Radio (JRC, later NJR), NEC/Renesas, STMicroelectronics, Mitsubishi, Toshiba, Samsung/Fairchild · Tier B (widely praised)

## Why it's rated

Not a hi-fi favourite. Its fame comes from guitar pedals: the JRC4558D in the Ibanez TS-808 Tube Screamer (1979) and BOSS SD-1, credited with warm, mid-forward overdrive. In hi-fi it is the cheap default in 1980s–90s budget gear; Soomal ranks it below the NE5532 and NJM4580. Jazzcaster found glossy and matte JRC4558D near-identical in tone, glossy quieter.

**Described as:** warm, creamy mids (pedals), mid-hump (Tube Screamer), less clean than NE5532, plain budget baseline

**Used in:** Tube Screamer, TS9 and SD-1 overdrive pedals, Budget cassette decks, mini-systems, sound-card outputs, General filters and buffers on ±15 V or 9 V, Synth, Eurorack and retro-console audio stages

## Watch out

- Slow: 0.8–1.7 V/µs gives only about 13–27 kHz full-power bandwidth at 20 Vpp.
- Phase reversal when the input comes within about 1 V of V− (CM range ±12 V min).
- Rated only into 2 kΩ, so not a headphone driver; 25–150 nA bias adds offset with high-impedance sources.
- Vendor dies differ (Raytheon, TI, JRC, NJM4558C), so 'a 4558' is not one sound or spec.
- Vintage JRC NOS premiums attract fakes and relabels.
- **Counterfeits:** Premium 'NOS vintage JRC4558D' listings, glossy (艶あり) ones included, are often fakes. 4558s are also remarked as JRC4580; a slew (~1 V/µs) or GBW (~3 MHz) check exposes them. Genuine TI markings: RC4558P, R4558, R4558I, YRP/YRS/YRU. Buy from authorized distributors.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments (RC4558), 2024-10 · PCN 20240723003

TI moved RC4558 from SFAB 150 mm to RFAB 300 mm (PCN 20240723003, first ship 21 Oct 2024) and re-specified it in Rev H: 4 MHz, 6.5 nV/√Hz, 2.2 V/µs, 0.0001% THD+N, 10–30 V. Shifts this large imply a revised die. It does not match TI's shared NE5532-class die (inference).

- **Affected:** RC4558P, RC4558DR, RC4558PWR, RC4558DGKR, RC4558IDR, RC4558IP, RC4558IPWR, RC4558IDGKR, RC4558D, RC4558PW, RC4558ID, RC4559P / RC4559DR (sibling part in the same PCNs)
- **How to tell:** Datasheet G → H. Datasheet PCN 20241230001.1 (2 Jan 2025, first ship 2 Apr 2025) cites PCN 20240723003. Lots after Oct 2024 likely new silicon; no marking rule.
- **Audio impact:** On paper quieter (~1.8 dB) and faster. Overdrive character, clipping and recovery may differ; 9 V pedals sit below the 10 V minimum.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium – same pinout; 33% more GBW may cut phase margin, and 9 V pedals are below spec.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Gain-bandwidth (typ) | 3 MHz (SLOS073G, Oct 2014) | 4 MHz (SLOS073H, Oct 2024) |
| Input voltage noise (typ) | 8 nV/√Hz (Rev G / old product-page headline) | 6.5 nV/√Hz at 10 kHz (Rev H) |
| Slew rate (typ) | 1.7 V/µs | 2.2 V/µs |
| THD+N | not specified in Rev G | 0.0001% (headline) |
| Supply voltage range | not verified for Rev G | 10 V to 30 V |
| Datasheet revision | SLOS073G (Oct 2014) | SLOS073H (Oct 2024) |

### Second-source difference: Raytheon vs Texas Instruments vs JRC (independent second-source dies), 1971 Raytheon; 1976 TI; late-1970s JRC

One generic number, separate vendor designs. The Raytheon, TI and JRC datasheets differ materially in typical figures, and Raytheon's schematic differs from JRC's equivalent circuit, pointing to independent dies. TI's RC4558 was later revised again (see the 2024 entry).

- **Affected:** RC4558, RC4558P, NJM4558D, JRC4558D
- **How to tell:** Logo and prefix: Raytheon RC4558N/M; TI RC4558P/DR marked 'RC4558P' or 'R4558'; JRC/NJR NJM4558D marked 'JRC4558D'.
- **Audio impact:** Small: all are 3 MHz 741-class parts. Pedal users report TI RC4558P vs JRC4558D tonal differences (folklore). All trail 5532 and 4580 in hi-fi.
- **Drop-in risk:** low – same pinout, supply range and compensation.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Slew rate typ | Raytheon 0.8 V/µs | TI 1.7 V/µs (pre-2024); JRC 1 V/µs |
| Input bias current typ | Raytheon 40 nA | TI 150 nA; JRC 50 nA (old) / 25 nA (2013) |
| Input resistance typ | Raytheon 1.0 MΩ | TI / JRC 5 MΩ |
| Vos typ | Raytheon RC 2.0 mV | TI / JRC 0.5 mV |
| Overshoot (20 mV step, 100 pF) | Raytheon 35 % | TI 5 % |

### Datasheet respec: New JRC / Nisshinbo (NJM4558), Old databook → Ver.2013-11-05

JRC re-characterised the NJM4558 in Ver.2013-11-05: the operating range widened from −20/+75 °C to −40/+85 °C and typical bias is quoted as 25 nA instead of 50 nA. Ver.2019-02-21 (NJM4558 only) followed. No die change is documented.

- **Affected:** NJM4558, NJM4558D, NJM4558M, JRC4558D
- **How to tell:** Datasheet version (Ver.2013-11-05 or later vs the old databook). No marking or suffix change.
- **Audio impact:** None expected; paper re-characterisation.
- **Drop-in risk:** low – same part, wider rated temperature range.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Operating temperature | -20 to +75 °C (old databook) | -40 to +85 °C (Ver.2013) |
| Input bias current typ | 50 nA (old databook) | 25 nA (Ver.2013) |

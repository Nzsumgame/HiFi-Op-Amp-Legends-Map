# LMH6642 / LMH6643 / LMH6644 (National/TI 130 MHz high-output-current rail-to-rail-output op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

**Technology:** Low-power bipolar voltage-feedback op-amp with rail-to-rail output. 130 MHz, about 75 mA output current; a video-class high-speed part.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

FiiO and iBasso used it as the output buffer or headphone driver. FiiO used it in the E10K, where it replaced the AD8397 and FiiO advertised better transient response and drive, and in the X3-II, X5 and E18. It was a common stock buffer in iBasso portable amps; one account says units shipped with either the THS4032 or the LMH6643. In the Head-Fi iBasso D1/D2/D10 rolling threads it was part of the recommended combination (ADA4841-2 L/R + LMH6643 buffers + LT6234 DAC), which users described as having 'very good tonal balance, soundstage and detail with a clean, open sound'. Users called it warm and relaxed and noted 'an intimate detail in the upper register that makes some music sound very good'. It was also rated, with the AD8397, as 'hard to beat for buffers' for driving low-impedance headphones, and as working 'very well as a ground opamp'. Some comments were mixed: as a buffer it loses 'a bit of impact and boldness', and some users preferred the LME49721 or OPA2727.

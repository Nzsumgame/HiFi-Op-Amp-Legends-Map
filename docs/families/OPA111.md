# OPA111 / OPA2111 (Burr-Brown Difet low-noise precision, single/dual)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** JFET-input

*Burr-Brown's low-noise Difet (8 nV/√Hz max at 10 kHz, pA bias). TI's OPA627 datasheet calls it 'the popular OPA111', and Japanese rollers prize the metal-can OPA2111AM.*

**Technology:** Burr-Brown Difet (dielectrically isolated JFET-input) precision op-amp. Datasheet titles: OPA111 'Low Noise Precision Difet Operational Amplifier' (SBOS138 / PDS-526K) and OPA2111 'Dual Low Noise Precision Difet Operational Amplifier' (SBOS140 / PDS-540E). Front-page headlines for OPA111: noise 8 nV/√Hz max at 10 kHz (100% tested), bias current 1 pA max, offset 250 µV max, drift 1 µV/°C max, open-loop gain 120 dB min and CMRR 100 dB min. For OPA2111: 8 nV/√Hz max at 10 kHz and bias current 4 pA max. Burr-Brown's 1990 PSpice macromodels give GBW ≈ 2.0 MHz, SR 2 V/µs and Avol ≈ 125 dB for both parts.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche cult part among vintage-op-amp rollers, and a well-known pA-bias, low-noise instrumentation front end. Vendor context: TI's OPA627/637 datasheet benchmarks against 'the popular OPA111'. Japan: hobby blogs praise the metal-can OPA2111AM. One reported that a swap from OPA827 gave less bass emphasis, clearer vocals and a wider soundstage; others describe a warm, analog sound. A Zigsow reviewer found the plastic OPA2111KP harder-sounding and more fatiguing than the OPA2134. China: Soomal groups OPA111 with the BB Difet parts and says OPA627/637 resolve treble much better; its 'warm, full' description applies to Difet op-amps as a class. On Head-Fi, one poster lists OPA2111KP among favourite duals. No ASR or Samuel Groner measurements were found.

**How people describe the sound:** warm, analog (JP blogs), wider soundstage / 'veil lifted' (OPA2111AM, JP blogs), less bass emphasis, clearer vocals than OPA827 (one JP swap report), warm, full, relaxed (Soomal, said of Difet op-amps as a class), less treble resolution than OPA627/OPA637 (Soomal), harder-sounding, slightly fatiguing vs OPA2134 (OPA2111KP, one Zigsow review)

**Typical uses:** Op-amp rolling in headphone amps (JP/CN hobbyists), High-impedance, low-bias front ends: EEG/biopotential (the brain-duino shield uses OPA2111KP), Current-to-voltage converters for sensors (CBA 2010 paper), DC servo/integrator in low-noise physics preamps (arXiv physics/9902042), Vintage preamp/phono stages (discovery note, not verified)

**Caveats:**

- OPA2111 is NRND at TI, OPA2111KP is obsolete, and OPA111 is no longer available. Supply is NOS or broker stock, so remarked parts are a risk.
- Slow by modern standards (about 2 MHz GBW, 2 V/µs).
- TO-99 metal-can (M-suffix) parts need lead forming or an adapter to fit a DIP-8 socket.
- Differences between the metal-can AM and plastic KP are grade/package differences plus forum folklore. No die change is documented.
- Soomal's descriptors apply to Difet op-amps as a class.
- Quiescent current, output drive and maximum supply were not verified against the datasheet.

## Other change notes (lifecycle, packaging, successors, lore)

### 1. Lifecycle: Texas Instruments, OPA2111: about 2019 (TI E2E thread 808890, 'OPA2111:…

OPA2111 was discontinued after about 26 years of production, and the OPA111 single is also obsolete. No die-revision or fab-transfer PCN for OPA111/OPA2111 was found. Generic RFAB/die-revision PCNs that appeared in searches (for example, 20220328001.1 and 20210811000.1A) were not shown to include OPA111-family parts.

- **When:** OPA2111: about 2019 (TI E2E thread 808890, 'OPA2111: amplifier end of life'). OPA111AM/BM: obsolete, date not established.
- **Affected:** OPA2111KP, OPA2111AM, OPA111AM, OPA111BM
- **How to tell old from new:** Lifecycle status only. DigiKey lists OPA2111KP and OPA2111AM as obsolete, and Octopart lists OPA111AM/OPA111BM as obsolete.
- **Audio impact:** None to the silicon. Parts on sale now are old stock from surplus brokers or possibly counterfeit.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - discontinuance only; no same-part-number silicon change
- **Confidence:** high
- **Verification:** WebSearch confirmed the E2E EOL thread, OPA2111AM obsolete (DigiKey) and OPA111AM/BM obsolete (Octopart). No PDN number or exact date was found.

### Revision chain status

OPA111, Burr-Brown PDS-526: first edition about 1984 (from the copyright year in the search summary; letter not seen) -> letters A to J (1984 to about 1994) all MISSING, with candidate sources in the 1984 BB Product Data Book (archive.org bitsavers item 111008507), the 1986 and 1989 BB IC Data Books (already listed), and the 1995 BB Linear Products book on rsp-italy.it -> PDS-526K, August 1995 (latest BB letter; octopart and TI copies already listed), plus a NEW Japanese-language rev K edition at lam29/www.lebra.nihon-u.ac.jp (OPA111revK.pdf, undated). TI SBOS138: legacy re-issue of PDS-526K with a TI cover page. No TI lettered revision (SBOS138A and so on) turned up in the searches. OPA2111, Burr-Brown PDS-540: first edition date unknown (in the 1989 BB IC Data Book at the latest; possibly 1986) -> letters A to D MISSING -> PDS-540E, October 1993 (latest). TI SBOS140: legacy re-issue of PDS-540E. No lettered TI revision found. A NEW archive.org manuallib OCR copy of the TI OPA2111 datasheet was added (revision not shown). No second-source vendor (National, ADI, Motorola, Philips, JRC, ST) turned up. The OPA111/OPA2111 still appear to be Burr-Brown/TI only. Still missing: every PDS-526 letter before K with its date, every PDS-540 letter before E, and any SBOS138/SBOS140 revision letters and dates. Most new copies (alldatasheet viewers, chipfind, digchip, datasheetcatalog, datasheet4u alternates, UCSD lab copy) do not show their revision in the search summaries. The focus.ti.com and ti.com symlink seed URLs were built from TI's standard URL pattern rather than seen in results. Treat them as Wayback lookup seeds only. WebSearch calls used: 6 of 6.

## Counterfeits

No documented counterfeit reports were found. The risk is structural: OPA2111 is NRND at TI, OPA2111KP is obsolete, OPA111 is no longer available, and supply is NOS or broker stock (a 2023 broker bought KP at €18 and resold at €35). eBay 'Audio Grade' marketing and cult prices for the metal-can AM invite remarks. Practical checks (general advice, not vendor guidance): (1) Burr-Brown logo and a plausible pre-2000 date code, with marking that matches grade and package (M = TO-99, P = plastic DIP). (2) Input bias current should be low-pA (datasheet max 1 pA for OPA111 best grade, 4 pA for OPA2111). A bipolar remark (nA) or generic JFET part (tens of pA) stands out. (3) GBW should be about 2 MHz and slew about 2 V/µs, so a faster slew suggests an NE5532/TL07x-class remark.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA627 / OPA637 (TI/BB Difet singles; the TI datasheet positions them as lower-noise, lower-offset, faster successors to OPA111), OPA196 (suggested by a TI E2E engineer as comparable to OPA111: lower offset/drift, slightly higher Ib), OPA827 (modern TI low-noise JFET single), OPA2107 (Burr-Brown Difet dual of the same era), OPA2604 / OPA2134 (Burr-Brown/TI FET-input audio duals), OPA2140 / OPA1642 (modern TI JFET-input duals with pA-level bias), OPA129 / OPA128 (Difet ultra-low-bias singles)

## Open questions

- No TI revision letter (for example SBOS138A or SBOS140A) was seen. The TI copies appear to be the unchanged BB PDS-526K and PDS-540E with a legacy cover. Check the footers.
- Earlier PDS-526 and PDS-540 letters and their dates: the 1986 and 1989 BB IC Data Books (archive.org) probably hold them, but they were not seen.
- Launch year is unverified. OPA111 is probably in the 1986 data book, and it may be in the 1984 Product Data Book.
- The exact TI lifecycle code for OPA111 was not seen ('no longer available' per E2E). For OPA2111 (NRND), it is not known which orderables are still NRND.
- The AM (metal can) vs KP (plastic) sonic split is not a documented silicon change. No PCN or die-revision evidence was found.
- Per-grade datasheet maxima and typicals (Vos/Ib by grade, Iq, output current, supply range, GBW/SR as printed) were not checked. The ±18 V maximum supply and 0.8 fA/√Hz i_n remain unconfirmed.
- The EWB model files disagree on OPA2111KP (100 µV/2 pA vs 300 µV/3 pA) and OPA2111BM Vos (100 vs 50 µV).

# OPA111 / OPA2111 (Burr-Brown Difet low-noise precision, single/dual)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** JFET-input

*Burr-Brown low-noise Difet precision op-amp. TI's OPA627 datasheet measures itself against 'the popular OPA111', and Japanese hobbyists prize the metal-can OPA2111AM.*

**Technology:** Burr-Brown Difet (dielectrically isolated JFET-input) precision op-amp. TI product titles, as captured in a 2013 model index: OPA111 'Low Noise Precision Difet Operational Amplifier' and OPA2111 'Dual Low-Noise Difet Operational Amplifier'. Burr-Brown's own 1990 PSpice macromodels (OPA111 REV.A 10/12/90, OPA2111 REV.A 10/15/90) give the same values for both parts: GBW = 1/(2π·RD1·C2) ≈ 2.0 MHz, SR = ISS/C2 = 2 V/µs and Avol ≈ 1.78e6 (125 dB). A third-party aggregated SPICE library labels it 'JFET, Low Noise 8nV/RTHz'. Input bias current is in the low-pA range.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche cult part among vintage-op-amp rollers, and a well-known pA-bias, low-noise instrumentation front end. Vendor context: TI's OPA627/637 datasheet benchmarks against 'the popular OPA111'. Japan: hobby blogs praise the metal-can OPA2111AM. One reported a swap from OPA827 that gave less bass emphasis, clearer vocals and a wider soundstage; others describe a warm, analog sound and a 'veil lifted'. A Zigsow reviewer found the plastic OPA2111KP harder-sounding and slightly more fatiguing than the OPA2134. China: in a 2016 HiFiMAN review, Soomal groups OPA111 with OPA627/OPA637 as BB Difet parts. It says OPA627/637 have much better treble resolution than the older OPA111/OPA2107, and that Difet op-amps in general sound warm, full and relaxed. That is a class generalisation, not an OPA111 listening test. On Head-Fi, a poster lists OPA2111KP among favourite duals (and wrongly calls it CMOS). No Audio Science Review or Samuel Groner measurements were found.

**How people describe the sound:** warm, analog (JP blogs), wider soundstage / 'veil lifted' (OPA2111AM, JP blogs), less bass emphasis, clearer vocals than OPA827 (one JP swap report), warm, solid full midrange, relaxed (Soomal, said of Difet op-amps as a class), less treble resolution than OPA627/OPA637 (Soomal), harder-sounding, slightly fatiguing vs OPA2134 (OPA2111KP, one Zigsow review)

**Typical uses:** Op-amp rolling in headphone amps (JP/CN hobbyists), High-impedance, low-bias front ends: EEG/biopotential (brain-duino shield uses OPA2111KP), Current-to-voltage converters for sensors (e.g., a pyroelectric radiation monitor, CBA 2010 paper), DC-offset servo/integrator in low-noise physics preamps (arXiv physics/9902042), Vintage preamp/phono stages (discovery note, not verified)

**Caveats:**

- TI's Japan page reportedly shows OPA2111 in discontinuation. Supply is NOS or broker stock (2023: bought at €18, resold at €35), so remarked parts are a risk.
- Slow by modern standards (about 2 MHz GBW, 2 V/µs), and the 0.1–10 Hz noise (1.5 µVpp, OPA2111) is not outstanding.
- TO-99 metal-can (M-suffix) parts need lead forming or an adapter to fit a DIP-8 socket.
- Sound differences between the metal-can AM and plastic KP are forum folklore, not documented silicon differences. Grades differ in guaranteed Vos/Ib.
- Soomal's 'warm, full' descriptors apply to Difet op-amps as a class, not specifically to the OPA111.
- Quiescent current and output drive were not verified. Check the datasheet before swapping into low-voltage or high-current sockets (the EWB tables suggest a ±18 V maximum).

## Counterfeits

No documented counterfeit reports for OPA111/OPA2111 were found. The risk is structural: TI reportedly lists OPA2111 as in discontinuation, and supply is NOS or broker stock. A 2023 Italian broker record shows 50 OPA2111KP bought at €18 each and resold at €35. eBay listings market the KP as 'Audio Grade', and metal-can AM parts carry cult prices. These conditions invite remarked cheaper duals. Practical checks (general advice, not vendor guidance): (1) look for a Burr-Brown logo, a plausible pre-2000 or early TI-era date code, and marking that matches the grade and package (M = TO-99 metal can, P = plastic DIP); (2) measure input bias current. Genuine Difet parts are in the low-pA range per the model tables (0.5–3 pA typ), so a remarked bipolar op-amp (nA-level Ib) or a generic JFET part (tens of pA) is easy to spot; (3) GBW should be about 2 MHz and slew rate about 2 V/µs. An NE5532-class or TL07x-class remark would slew much faster.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA627 / OPA637 (TI/BB Difet singles; the TI datasheet positions them as lower-noise, lower-offset, faster successors to OPA111), OPA827 (modern TI low-noise JFET single), OPA2107 (Burr-Brown Difet dual of the same era), OPA2604 / OPA2134 (Burr-Brown/TI FET-input audio duals), OPA2140 / OPA1642 (modern TI JFET-input duals with pA-level bias), OPA129 / OPA128 (Difet ultra-low-bias singles, for electrometer uses)

# AD8597 / AD8599

**Bipolar ultralow-noise, low-distortion op-amp, 1/2 ch** · Analog Devices (2007–present) → Analog Devices (2015–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** 'Qualification of ADI Camas Wafer Fab Bipolar Process': Camas, WA fab qualified for ADI bipolar products. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*ADI's 1.1 nV/√Hz, −120 dB THD bipolar pair; Head-Fi rollers called the AD8599 'bloody good': dynamic and sparkling.*

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 nV/√Hz (Rev. F); 1 nV/√Hz (Rev. A) | 10 MHz | 14 V/µs | −120 dB THD at 1 kHz (Rev. F); −105 dB at 20 kHz (Rev. A) | ±4.5 V min, ±15 V typ; ±18 V abs. max | 4.8 mA per amplifier |

## Why it's rated

Best known from Head-Fi's 'AD8599 is bloody good' thread, where rollers call it dynamic, vivacious, sparkling and 'alive'; another thread finds it warmer than other ADI op-amps. Engineers use it for MDAC volume attenuators, Elecraft KX3/KX2 and T41 SDR I/Q stages, and low-noise instrumentation. Often listed as an OPA1612 / LME49720 alternative. No ASR or Groner measurements.

**Described as:** dynamic, vivacious, sparkling, alive, warmer than ADI siblings, accurate soundstage

**Used in:** op-amp rolling in DACs and headphone amps (SOIC-to-DIP adapter), output amp for MDAC volume attenuators, low-noise line and headphone preamps, low source impedance, SDR I/Q and RX AF preamps (Elecraft KX3/KX2, T41), precision low-noise instrumentation and ADC drivers

## Watch out

- SOIC-8 only; rolling needs an adapter, whose layout and decoupling affect stability.
- Needs at least ±4.5 V rails, ±18 V abs. max; unsuited to low-voltage portable designs.
- Low noise helps only at low source impedance; i_n and I_B figures not verified.
- Model shows input clamp diodes; check differential input limits before large input steps.
- Sound descriptors are subjective forum impressions.
- **Counterfeits:** No AD8597/AD8599 counterfeit reports. Sold in SOIC-8/LFCSP via authorised ADI channels, so treat any DIP-8 'AD8599' as a relabel or adapter. Bench check: about 1.1 nV/√Hz and very low THD; a relabelled 5532/4580-class die measures clearly worse.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2025-06 (eff. 2025-09) · PCN 25_0091

'Qualification of ADI Camas Wafer Fab Bipolar Process': Camas, WA fab qualified for ADI bipolar products. Published 6 Jun 2025, effective 8 Sep 2025; Rev. A and a qualification report followed. Confirmed listings are AD711/AD712/AD713; AD8597/AD8599 inclusion unconfirmed.

- **Affected:** AD8597 (unconfirmed), AD8599 (unconfirmed)
- **How to tell:** Not established. Check the affected-parts lists of PCN 25_0091 and Rev. A; if covered, date codes after Sep 2025 may be Camas-built.
- **Audio impact:** ADI says same flows, materials and controls, 'ensuring no degradation of quality and reliability performance'. No Camas-part measurements known.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same process, datasheet unchanged; applicability to AD8597/AD8599 unconfirmed.
- **Confidence:** low

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab site | existing ADI bipolar fab (site not confirmed) | ADI Camas, WA qualified |

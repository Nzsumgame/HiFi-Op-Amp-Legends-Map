# OPA2107 (Burr-Brown/TI precision Difet JFET-input dual)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** JFET-input

*Burr-Brown Difet JFET dual (about 4.5 MHz, 18 V/us) that op-amp rollers call warm and full. HiFiMAN paired it with the OPA627 in its HM-801 and HM-901 players.*

**Technology:** Dual JFET-input op-amp on Burr-Brown's Difet (dielectrically isolated FET) process. The Burr-Brown macromodel (Rev A, 10/17/90) uses a P-channel JFET input pair (.MODEL JX PJF). Its elements give a GBW of 4.5 MHz (GA/(2*pi*C2)), a slew rate of 18 V/us (ISS/C2), CMRR of 100 dB and an Aol of about 100 dB. It is the faster sibling of the OPA2111 Difet dual: the same 1996 EWB library gives the OPA2111 2 MHz and 2 V/us against 4-4.5 MHz and 18 V/us for the OPA2107.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A cult favourite from the Head-Fi op-amp-rolling era. Head-Fi and diyAudio threads, Japanese blogs and Zigsow reviews reportedly call it warm, full and 'tube-like', with a detailed midrange and non-fatiguing highs; those forum pages were not re-checked in this pass. Soomal (2016) groups it with the warm, full, spacious 'DiFET' sound (a group where Soomal also puts the OPA2134 and OPA2604) and says the OPA627/637 clearly beat the older OPA111 and OPA2107 on treble resolution. Soomal's 2013 HM-901 review admits its reviewers had not heard the OPA2107 in any other device. HiFiMAN used it in the HM-801 (with OPA627), HM-602 (headphone stage, with an OP275/OPA2604 LPF), HM-601LE (OP275, OPA2604, OPA2107) and HM-901 (OPA627 + OPA2107 in the LPF). c't Lab (Heise, 2007) specifies it, or an AD712, as the input amplifier of its AD16-8 ADC card, and calls it 'weit besser' (far better) than a TL072 for the 16-bit DA12-8 variant. Tangent's bench notes give only supply headroom and price for it, with no listening verdict. No independent measurements (ASR, Samuel Groner) were found.

**How people describe the sound:** warm, full, tube-like (forum), wide soundstage (forum), good imaging (forum), detailed, nuanced midrange (JP blogs), clear, non-fatiguing highs (JP blogs), spacious DiFET-family sound (Soomal), less treble resolution than OPA627/OPA637 (Soomal)

**Typical uses:** DAC low-pass filter (HiFiMAN HM-901, with OPA627), DAP analogue stage (HiFiMAN HM-801, with OPA627), Portable headphone-amp stage (HiFiMAN HM-602 and HM-601LE), Op-amp rolling in DACs and preamps (DIP-8 OPA2107AP), Precision high-impedance instrumentation (c't Lab AD16-8 ADC input amp; recommended over TL072 in the 16-bit DA12-8)

**Caveats:**

- Needs a fairly high supply: Tangent measured a 10.6 V minimum to meet his headphone-drive tests (OPA627: 6.8/9.8 V), so it is a poor choice for low-voltage battery CMoy builds.
- Modest output current (about 40 mA limit per the models), so buffer it for low-impedance headphones.
- It was expensive even when current ($12.25 per dual at Digi-Key in the Tangent notes). It is now largely secondary-market stock, and its obsolete status is unverified.
- Most sonic praise is forum or blog impression. Soomal says it had not heard the part outside the HM-901, and its 'DiFET sound' grouping also covers non-Difet parts (OPA2134).
- Counterfeit and remark risk on NOS or pulled parts (flagged by the discovery lenses, unverified).
- Soomal's folklore that fab origin (Taiwan vs Southeast Asia) changes the sound refers to OPA627/637, not the OPA2107.

## Counterfeits

No OPA2107-specific counterfeit report was found or verified. The discovery lenses flag a high risk of remarked fakes because supply is now NOS or pulled stock (low confidence). General checks: see whether the logo era (BB vs TI) matches the date code, and look at marking quality and package moulding. On the bench, a genuine part should show pA-level input bias current (JFET input), about 18 V/us slew and about 4.5 MHz GBW (model values). A remarked bipolar part will show nA-level or higher bias current. A remarked generic JFET dual will usually be noisier and may slew or settle differently.

## Open questions

- WebSearch was unavailable for this verification pass too: the session budget was already 200/200, so none of the 15+ required searches ran. GitHub code search was rate-limited and only one query succeeded (EWB model hits only). The verification is limited to direct reads of the GitHub-hosted sources cited, all of which were confirmed.
- The TI datasheet literature number (SBOSxxx) and its revision letters are unknown. The only TI number confirmed is SBOM045 (PSpice model zip). It is deliberately kept out of ti_literature_numbers because it is not a datasheet.
- The archive seeds ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/opa2107.pdf, focus.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/opa2107.pdf and www-s.ti.com/sc/ds/opa2107.pdf are inferred from standard TI URL patterns and were not seen in any result. ti.com/product/OPA2107 is also inferred.
- The Burr-Brown PDS number and first-release date are unknown. The macromodel date of 10/17/90 only shows the part existed by 1990. Frieve-A's 'early-to-mid 1990s design' phrasing is looser than that model evidence.
- Current TI lifecycle status (Active, NRND or Obsolete) and the last-time-buy date are unverified.
- Which revision the alldatasheet copy (ID 177039) holds is unknown.
- When the TO-99 grades (AM, BM, SM) were dropped, and whether TI ever catalogued them, is unknown. The TO-99 package for M-suffix parts is inferred from convention.
- Datasheet values are still needed for e_n at 1 kHz, i_n, THD+N, supply range, Iq and guaranteed min/max limits. Current specs come from EWB and SPICE models plus one Soomal quote of 8 nV/rtHz at 10 kHz.
- No die, fab or process change or PCN is known for OPA2107, and none could be searched for in this pass, so a TI-era fab transfer cannot be ruled out.
- The Head-Fi, diyAudio, Zigsow, Japanese blog and tangentsoft.net pages could not be fetched or searched. The sonic descriptors drawn from them rest on the discovery notes alone.
- Whether the original HM-601 (not LE) used the OPA2107 is unverified. Only the HM-601LE is confirmed (smzdm, 2014).
- OPA2107E and OPA2107B are SPICE model names (E revised 3/21/92; B is the plain model's subcircuit name in BBOPA.LIB), not confirmed orderable grades. The Eagle device name OPA2107D is a library package name.

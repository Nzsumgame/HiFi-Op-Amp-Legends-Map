# OPA2107 / OPA2107AP / OPA2107AU

**Difet JFET-input precision dual op-amp** · Burr-Brown (1989–2000) → Texas Instruments (2000–obsolete) · Tier B (widely praised)

*Burr-Brown Difet JFET dual (4.5 MHz, 18 V/µs) rollers call warm and full; HiFiMAN paired it with OPA627 in HM-801/901.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 nV/√Hz (at 10 kHz; 1 kHz value not seen) | 4.5 MHz typ | 18 V/µs typ | ±4.5 V to ±18 V (9 V to 36 V) | <2.5 mA per amp (a listing says 4.5 mA/ch) |

## Why it's rated

A cult favourite of the Head-Fi rolling era. Forums, Japanese blogs and Zigsow call it warm, full and tube-like, between OPA2604 and OPA627. Soomal groups it in a warm, spacious 'DiFET' sound but says OPA627/637 resolve treble better. HiFiMAN used it in several players; c't Lab calls it 'weit besser' than a TL072. No independent measurements.

**Described as:** warm, full, tube-like, wide soundstage, good imaging, detailed midrange, non-fatiguing highs, less treble resolution

**Used in:** DAC low-pass filter: HiFiMAN HM-901 (2013), with OPA627, DAP analogue stage: HiFiMAN HM-801 (2009), with OPA627, Portable amp stage: HiFiMAN HM-602 (2010), HM-601LE (2014), DIP-8 op-amp rolling in DACs and preamps, Precision ADC/DAC input amp: c't Lab AD16-8, DA12-8

## Watch out

- Obsolete at TI (OPA2107AP per DigiKey); supply is NOS or pulled stock.
- Needs a fairly high supply: Tangent measured 10.6 V minimum (OPA627: 6.8/9.8 V); Digi-Key price then $12.25.
- Modest output current (about 40 mA per models); buffer it for low-impedance headphones.
- Praise is mostly forum impression; Soomal's 'DiFET' group includes non-Difet OPA2134, and its HM-901 reviewers hadn't heard it elsewhere.
- NOS remark risk is a general concern for obsolete audio op-amps; no OPA2107-specific report.
- **Counterfeits:** No OPA2107-specific fake reports. Stock is NOS, pulled or broker parts, even die. Check logo era (BB vs TI) against date code and marking. Genuine parts show pA bias, ~18 V/µs and ~4.5 MHz; remarked bipolar parts show nA bias.

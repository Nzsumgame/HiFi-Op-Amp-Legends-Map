# OPA2107 (Burr-Brown/TI precision Difet JFET-input dual)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** JFET-input

*Burr-Brown Difet JFET dual (4.5 MHz, 18 V/us) that op-amp rollers call warm and full; HiFiMAN paired it with the OPA627 in its HM-801 and HM-901.*

**Technology:** Dual FET-input op-amp on Burr-Brown's proprietary dielectrically isolated (Difet) process. The datasheet says Difet keeps input bias current very low (10 pA max) without giving up offset, drift or noise. The Burr-Brown macromodel (Rev A, 10/17/90) uses a P-channel JFET input pair (.MODEL JX PJF). The datasheet and TI parametrics give 4.5 MHz GBW, 18 V/us slew and unity-gain stability. It is the faster sibling of the OPA2111 Difet dual: the 1996 EWB library gives the OPA2111 2 MHz and 2 V/us.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A cult favourite from the Head-Fi op-amp-rolling era. Forum threads (Head-Fi 'OPA2107 WOW', diyAudio), Japanese blogs and Zigsow reviews reportedly call it warm, full and tube-like, placing it between the OPA2604 and OPA627. Those pages were not re-checked. Soomal (2016) puts it in a warm, full, spacious 'DiFET' sound group, a group that also covers the OPA2134 and OPA2604, and says the OPA627/637 beat it on treble resolution. Soomal's 2013 HM-901 review admits its reviewers had not heard the OPA2107 in any other device. HiFiMAN used it in the HM-801 (with OPA627), HM-602, HM-601LE and HM-901 (OPA627 + OPA2107 in the LPF). c't Lab (2007) specifies it, or an AD712, as the input amplifier of its AD16-8 ADC card, and calls it 'weit besser' (far better) than a TL072 for the 16-bit DA12-8. No independent measurements were found.

**How people describe the sound:** warm, full, tube-like (forum), wide soundstage (forum), good imaging (forum), detailed midrange (JP blogs), non-fatiguing highs (JP blogs), spacious DiFET-family sound (Soomal), less treble resolution than OPA627/OPA637 (Soomal)

**Typical uses:** DAC low-pass filter (HiFiMAN HM-901, with OPA627), DAP analogue stage (HiFiMAN HM-801, with OPA627), Portable headphone-amp stage (HiFiMAN HM-602, HM-601LE), Op-amp rolling in DACs and preamps (DIP-8 OPA2107AP), Precision high-impedance instrumentation (c't Lab AD16-8 ADC input amp)

**Caveats:**

- Obsolete at TI (OPA2107AP per DigiKey), so supply is now NOS or pulled stock.
- Needs a fairly high supply: Tangent measured a 10.6 V minimum (OPA627: 6.8/9.8 V). The rated range is +/-4.5 V to +/-18 V.
- Modest output current (about 40 mA per the models); buffer it for low-impedance headphones.
- Most sonic praise is forum or blog impression, and Soomal's 'DiFET sound' group also includes non-Difet parts (OPA2134).
- Counterfeit or remark risk on NOS parts is a general concern for obsolete audio op-amps; no OPA2107-specific report was verified.

## Counterfeits

No OPA2107-specific counterfeit report was found. The part is obsolete (OPA2107AP per DigiKey), so what is sold now is NOS, pulled or broker stock, including bare die from die brokers. General checks: see whether the logo era (BB vs TI) matches the date code, and look at marking quality and package moulding. On the bench, a genuine part should show pA-level input bias current (10 pA max), about 18 V/us slew and about 4.5 MHz GBW. A remarked bipolar part will show nA-level bias current. A remarked generic JFET dual will usually be noisier than 8 nV/rtHz at 10 kHz.

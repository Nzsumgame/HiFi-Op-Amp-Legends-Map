# TLE2081 / TLE2082 / TLE2084 (TI Excalibur high-speed JFET-input)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*A fast (about 40 V/us, 10 MHz) TI JFET-input op-amp that Head-Fi CMoy builders call a lively, clean, detailed budget pick.*

**Technology:** BiFET. The input is a P-channel JFET differential pair: both TI PSpice macromodels (level 1 and level 2) use PJF devices J1/J2. TI's macromodel catalogue lists it as 'high speed, biFET'. It is part of TI's 'Excalibur' TLE20xx generation, and the sister TLE2071 family is labelled 'Excalibur ... JFET-Input' in the MicroSim/TI symbol library. Single (TLE2081), dual (TLE2082) and quad (TLE2084), each also in a tighter-offset A grade.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This is a niche, budget-tier favourite rather than a legend. Head-Fi CMoy/portable-amp threads (c. 2007–2009) group it with its sister TLE2072 among the best cheap op-amps for 9 V CMoys. Users say the TLE2072 has the better DC specs and the TLE2082 the better AC specs. They describe the TLE2082 as 'lively, clean, detailed' with 'great highs and lows' and a good midrange, and say it 'shines for budget-fi'. No Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner or other published distortion measurement was located, because the web search budget ran out before a dedicated search.

**How people describe the sound:** lively, clean, detailed, great highs and lows, good midrange, fast

**Typical uses:** 9 V battery CMoy headphone amplifiers (op-amp rolling), Input/gain stage and driver for discrete-transistor headphone output stages. Example: the ohdsp AmpTwo (2017) uses TLE2082 input stages and TLE2081s driving BCP53/BCP56 output devices., General fast JFET-input buffering and filtering, e.g. oscilloscope front ends (the SampleScope project), Proposed as a replacement for the MC33182D in the Gameboy DSO project

**Caveats:**

- The evidence base is small: praise comes from a handful of Head-Fi CMoy threads. Measurements are not documented.
- JFET-input noise (11.6 nV/rtHz typ) is well above bipolar audio op-amps such as the NE5532 (about 5 nV/rtHz). This matters in low-impedance or high-gain phono/mic stages.
- Output current is about 25 mA (LISO library). Low-impedance headphones need a buffer or a discrete output stage, as the AmpTwo design uses.
- It is a fast (10 MHz, 40 V/us) amplifier. This is general engineering caution, not a documented report: use short decoupling and watch capacitive loads and long leads when socket-rolling it into circuits designed for slower parts such as the TL07x.

## Counterfeits

No TLE208x-specific counterfeit or remarking reports were found, though the web search budget was exhausted before a dedicated search. It is a low-volume, inexpensive TI part and an unlikely main counterfeit target. Treat 'TLE2082' DIPs from marketplace sellers as unverified and prefer authorized distributors. A quick bench check: slew rate should be about 40 V/us typ and input bias current tens of pA at room temperature. A remarked TL072/TL082 would slew only about 13 V/us.

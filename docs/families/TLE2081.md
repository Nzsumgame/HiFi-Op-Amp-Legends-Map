# TLE2081 / TLE2082 / TLE2084

**JFET-input high-speed op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · Texas Instruments (1991–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** PCN 20231219013.1 (19 Dec 2023; Mouser copy 22 Dec 2023) moves TLE208x to RFAB with a die revision and assembly change. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*TI's fast Excalibur JFET op-amp (40 V/µs, 10 MHz, 38 V), a lively, clean budget pick among Head-Fi CMoy builders.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11.6 nV/√Hz typ | 10 MHz typ (8 MHz min) | 40 V/µs typ (28 V/µs min, SLOS105A) | ±2.25 V to ±19 V (38 V total) | 1.5 mA typ per channel |

## Why it's rated

A budget favourite, not a legend. Head-Fi CMoy threads (c. 2007–2009) group it with its sister TLE2072 as a good cheap op-amp for 9 V CMoys: the TLE2072 has better DC specs, the TLE2082 better AC specs. Users call the TLE2082 'lively, clean, detailed'. No published distortion or noise measurements found.

**Described as:** lively, clean, detailed, great highs and lows, good midrange, fast

**Used in:** 9 V battery CMoy headphone amps, Input/driver stage for discrete headphone outputs (ohdsp AmpTwo), Fast JFET buffering and filtering, e.g. SampleScope, Proposed MC33182D replacement in the Gameboy DSO

## Watch out

- Small evidence base: a few Head-Fi CMoy threads, no measurements.
- Noise (about 11.6 nV/√Hz) is well above bipolar audio parts like NE5532 (about 5 nV/√Hz).
- About 25 mA output: low-impedance headphones need a buffer or discrete stage.
- Fast (10 MHz, 40 V/µs): decouple closely and watch capacitive loads in TL07x-era circuits.
- Praise predates the 2023 RFAB die revision; new-production parts may differ.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific reports. A low-volume, cheap part and unlikely target, but treat marketplace 'TLE2082' DIPs as unverified. Bench check: about 40 V/µs slew and tens of pA bias; a remarked TL072/TL082 slews only about 13 V/µs.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments, 2023-12 · PCN 20231219013.1

PCN 20231219013.1 (19 Dec 2023; Mouser copy 22 Dec 2023) moves TLE208x to RFAB with a die revision and assembly change. Datasheet Rev C (March 2026) then dropped 'BiFET', slew-rate and offset-trim features and 'JFET-Input' from the title, as TI did after TL07x die changes. Linking the two is inferred.

- **Affected:** TLE2081IDR, TLE2082IDR, TLE2082CP, TLE2082ACP, TLE2082ACDR, TLE2081 / TLE2081A / TLE2082 / TLE2082A / TLE2084 / TLE2084A (datasheet Rev C scope; PCN coverage of other orderables not confirmed)
- **How to tell:** Same part number; PCN die-revision field becomes A, chip site RFAB, new assembly site. Identify by date code versus the (unknown) first-ship date.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. A changed input stage, trim or process could alter noise, offset, slew, stability and sound versus parts reviewed c. 2007–09.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: die revision is PCN-documented and the datasheet rewritten, but no electrical deltas retrieved.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab | legacy fab (not named in the retrieved summary) | RFAB, Richardson USA |
| Die revision (PCN field) | A, B, C, H (as summarised for the PCN) | A |
| Features list | BiFET technology, slew-rate figure and on-chip offset voltage trimming listed (Rev B) | these items deleted (Rev C) |
| Document title | TLE208x, TLE208xA, TLE208xY EXCALIBUR HIGH-SPEED JFET-INPUT OPERATIONAL AMPLIFIERS | TLE208x and TLE208xA Excalibur High-Speed Operational Amplifiers |

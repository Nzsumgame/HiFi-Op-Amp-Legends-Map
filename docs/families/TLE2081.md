# TLE2081 / TLE2082 / TLE2084 (TI Excalibur high-speed JFET-input)

Second sources: Texas Instruments · Tier C (niche / baseline)

## Why it's rated

This is a niche, budget-tier favourite rather than a legend. Head-Fi CMoy/portable-amp threads (c. 2007–2009) group it with its sister TLE2072 among good cheap op-amps for 9 V CMoys. Users there say the TLE2072 has the better DC specs and the TLE2082 the better AC specs, and describe the TLE2082 as 'lively, clean, detailed' and good for budget builds. No published distortion or noise measurement (ASR, Samuel Groner, diyAudio) was found, and none was searched for in this session.

**Described as:** lively, clean, detailed, great highs and lows, good midrange, fast

**Used in:** 9 V battery CMoy headphone amplifiers (op-amp rolling), Input/gain stage and driver for discrete-transistor headphone output stages, e.g. the ohdsp AmpTwo (2017): TLE2082 input stage, TLE2081 driving BCP53/BCP56, General fast JFET-input buffering and filtering, e.g. the SampleScope oscilloscope front end, Proposed as a replacement for the MC33182D in the Gameboy DSO project

## Watch out

- The evidence base is small: a handful of Head-Fi CMoy threads, with no documented measurements.
- JFET-input noise (about 11.6 nV/rtHz typ, per a model library) is well above bipolar audio op-amps such as the NE5532 (about 5 nV/rtHz). This matters in low-impedance or high-gain stages.
- Output current is about 25 mA (LISO library), so low-impedance headphones need a buffer or discrete output stage.
- It is fast (10 MHz, 40 V/us). As general engineering caution, not a documented report: use short decoupling and watch capacitive loads when rolling it into circuits designed for TL07x-class parts.
- The praise dates from c.2007–09. If TI changed the die around the 2026 datasheet revision (unconfirmed), new-production parts may not match those reports.
- **Counterfeits:** No TLE208x-specific counterfeit or remarking reports were found, and no dedicated search was run. It is a low-volume, inexpensive TI part and an unlikely main counterfeit target. Treat 'TLE2082' DIPs from marketplace sellers as unverified and prefer authorized distributors. Quick bench check: slew rate should be about 40 V/us typ and input bias current tens of pA at room temperature. A remarked TL072/TL082 would slew only about 13 V/us.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments, PCN 20231219013.1 (19 Dec 2023; the Mouser copy is dated 22 Dec 2023); datasheet SLOS182 Rev C, March 2026

TI PCN 20231219013.1 moves TLE208x orderables to RFAB with a die revision and an assembly-site change; the die was changed as a result of the process change. Datasheet Rev C (March 2026) later removed 'BiFET', the slew-rate bullet and 'On-chip offset voltage trimming' from Features, rewrote the Description and dropped 'JFET-Input' from the title. TI made the same kind of edits in the refreshes that followed new dies on the TL07x. Linking the Rev C edits to the PCN die is an inference, and no electrical deltas were retrieved.

- **Affected:** TLE2081IDR, TLE2082IDR, TLE2082CP, TLE2082ACP, TLE2082ACDR, TLE2081 / TLE2081A / TLE2082 / TLE2082A / TLE2084 / TLE2084A (datasheet Rev C scope; PCN coverage of other orderables not confirmed)
- **How to tell:** The part number is unchanged. Per summaries of the same PCN as the TLE207x, the die-revision field changes to A and RFAB becomes the chip site, with an assembly-site change. Identification is by date code relative to the (unretrieved) first-ship date. Datasheet Rev C (March 2026) is retitled 'TLE208x and TLE208xA Excalibur High-Speed Operational Amplifiers'; Rev B (June 2001) was titled '...EXCALIBUR HIGH-SPEED JFET-INPUT...'.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. If the input stage, trim or process changed, noise, offset, slew, stability margin and sound may differ from the parts Head-Fi reviewed c. 2007-09.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: the die revision is PCN-documented and the datasheet was rewritten, but no electrical deltas were retrieved.
- **Confidence:** medium

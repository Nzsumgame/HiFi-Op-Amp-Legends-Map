# TLE2081 / TLE2082 / TLE2084 (TI Excalibur high-speed JFET-input)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*A fast (40 V/us, 10 MHz, up to 38 V supply) TI JFET-input op-amp that Head-Fi CMoy builders called a lively, clean, detailed budget pick.*

**Technology:** BiFET. The input is a P-channel JFET differential pair: both TI PSpice macromodels (level 1 and level 2) use PJF devices J1/J2, and TI's product folders call it a 'JFET-input operational amplifier'. Part of TI's 'Excalibur' TLE20xx generation. Rev B of the datasheet said the family has more than twice the bandwidth and three times the slew rate of the TL07x/TL08x, with supply rails up to ±19 V. Rev C (Mar 2026) dropped 'JFET-Input' from the document title and removed 'BiFET' from the Features list; see silicon_changes. Made as a single (TLE2081), dual (TLE2082) and quad (TLE2084), each also in a tighter-offset A grade. Older datasheets also list Y chip (die) forms.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (2 recorded).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This is a niche, budget-tier favourite rather than a legend. Head-Fi CMoy/portable-amp threads (c. 2007–2009) group it with its sister TLE2072 among good cheap op-amps for 9 V CMoys. Users there say the TLE2072 has the better DC specs and the TLE2082 the better AC specs, and describe the TLE2082 as 'lively, clean, detailed' and good for budget builds. No published distortion or noise measurement (ASR, Samuel Groner, diyAudio) was found, and none was searched for in this session.

**How people describe the sound:** lively, clean, detailed, great highs and lows, good midrange, fast

**Typical uses:** 9 V battery CMoy headphone amplifiers (op-amp rolling), Input/gain stage and driver for discrete-transistor headphone output stages, e.g. the ohdsp AmpTwo (2017): TLE2082 input stage, TLE2081 driving BCP53/BCP56, General fast JFET-input buffering and filtering, e.g. the SampleScope oscilloscope front end, Proposed as a replacement for the MC33182D in the Gameboy DSO project

**Caveats:**

- The evidence base is small: a handful of Head-Fi CMoy threads, with no documented measurements.
- JFET-input noise (about 11.6 nV/rtHz typ, per a model library) is well above bipolar audio op-amps such as the NE5532 (about 5 nV/rtHz). This matters in low-impedance or high-gain stages.
- Output current is about 25 mA (LISO library), so low-impedance headphones need a buffer or discrete output stage.
- It is fast (10 MHz, 40 V/us). As general engineering caution, not a documented report: use short decoupling and watch capacitive loads when rolling it into circuits designed for TL07x-class parts.
- The praise dates from c.2007–09. If TI changed the die around the 2026 datasheet revision (unconfirmed), new-production parts may not match those reports.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Texas Instruments: Datasheet SLOS182 rev C, March 2026 (a die change is NOT…

This is a possible hazard only. Rev C removed 'BiFET', the slew-rate bullet and 'On-chip offset voltage trimming' from Features, rewrote the Description and dropped 'JFET-Input' from the title. TI made similar edits in datasheet refreshes that accompanied new dies on other legacy parts, but no PCN, new-die statement or electrical delta for the TLE208x was found.

- **When:** Datasheet SLOS182 rev C, March 2026 (a die change is NOT confirmed)
- **Affected:** TLE2081, TLE2081A, TLE2082, TLE2082A, TLE2084, TLE2084A
- **How to tell old from new:** No PCN found. Compare the rev C electrical tables against rev B (June 2001). If TI issued a new die, parts would be told apart by date code or lot and PCN, not by part number.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. If the input stage, trim or process changed, noise, offset, slew and sound may differ from the parts Head-Fi reviewed c.2007–09.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - unconfirmed; only datasheet text edits were seen, with no electrical deltas or PCN
- **Confidence:** low

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Features list | BiFET technology, slew-rate figure, on-chip offset voltage trimming listed (rev B) | these items deleted (rev C) |
| Document title | TLE208x, TLE208xA, TLE208xY EXCALIBUR HIGH-SPEED JFET-INPUT OPERATIONAL AMPLIFIERS | TLE208x and TLE208xA Excalibur High-Speed Operational Amplifiers |

### 2. Texas Instruments: PCN 20231219013.1 (19 December 2023)

Search summaries name TLE2081IDR, TLE2082IDR, TLE2082ACP and TLE2082ACDR in the same TI PCN 20231219013.1 as the TLE207x. The notice covers a wafer fab site change to RFAB, a die revision and an assembly site change. The die was changed as a result of the process change.

- **When:** PCN 20231219013.1 (19 December 2023)
- **Affected:** TLE2081IDR, TLE2082IDR, TLE2082ACP, TLE2082ACDR
- **How to tell old from new:** The part number is unchanged. Per the search summary, the die-revision field changes to A and RFAB becomes the chip site. Identification is by date code relative to the first-ship date.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. The same new-die caveats apply as for the TLE207x: possible changes in slew, bandwidth and stability margin under the same part number.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: the die change is PCN-documented, but no electrical deltas were retrieved.
- **Confidence:** medium

## Counterfeits

No TLE208x-specific counterfeit or remarking reports were found, and no dedicated search was run. It is a low-volume, inexpensive TI part and an unlikely main counterfeit target. Treat 'TLE2082' DIPs from marketplace sellers as unverified and prefer authorized distributors. Quick bench check: slew rate should be about 40 V/us typ and input bias current tens of pA at room temperature. A remarked TL072/TL082 would slew only about 13 V/us.

## Related parts and alternatives

TLE2072 / TLE2071 / TLE2074 (sister TI Excalibur JFET-input family; Head-Fi rates the TLE2072 highly for 9 V CMoys), OPA1642 (modern TI JFET-input audio op-amp), OPA1656 (modern TI low-noise CMOS audio op-amp), OPA2134 / OPA2132 (JFET-input audio parts it was compared against in CMoy threads), TL072 / TL082 (pin-compatible but slower legacy BiFETs)

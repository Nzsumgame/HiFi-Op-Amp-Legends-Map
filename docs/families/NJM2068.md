# NJM2068 / JRC2068

**Bipolar low-noise dual, 4558 pinout** · New Japan Radio (JRC/NJR) (by 2003 (intro year unknown)–2021) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2022–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*A cheap, quiet JRC bipolar dual: NwAvGuy's O2 gain stage, and common in E-MU interfaces and Japanese CD/AV gear.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not specified; 0.44 µVrms typ / 0.56 max (weighted) | 27 MHz at 10 kHz, 19 MHz at 100 kHz; fT 5.5 MHz | 6 V/µs typ | 0.001% typ (THD) | ±4 V to ±18 V (abs. max ±18 V) | 5 mA typ / 8 mA max per package (≈2.5 mA/ch) |

## Why it's rated

NwAvGuy measured it for the O2 and chose it for the gain stage: quieter than NE5532, LM4562 and OPA2134 at lower current, for under a dollar. Soomal found it in E-MU interfaces, Chinese sound cards and many CD players, calling it very low noise, high resolution, neutral. Japanese swappers know it as the classic quiet JRC dual.

**Described as:** very low noise (噪声很小), high resolution (解析力高), neutral, plain (中性平白), relaxed, ample bass, dense, mellow (unverified)

**Used in:** O2 (Objective2) and SmartStand headphone amp gain stages, E-MU 0202/0204/0404/1212m; Musiland X-Sword II, ooAoo SQ710 II/SQ717a, Japanese CD, AV (Yamaha YSP-600) and car (Pioneer KEX-M8406ZT) units, MM phono preamps (FX-Audio BOX-01/BOX-02), Roland VG-99/GK-3 inputs; ICON PX-T6A active monitors

## Watch out

- Capacitive loads can oscillate: add about 50 Ω in series (vendor). One note.com user reports NJM2068D oscillating in a preamp.
- Needs ±4 V (8 V) minimum: unsuitable for 5 V or USB-powered designs.
- '27 MHz' is GB at 10 kHz; unity-gain frequency is only 5.5 MHz.
- No nV/√Hz or current-noise spec; bias current up to 1 µA. Keep source and feedback impedances low.
- Not a headphone driver: output current unspecified, loads ≥2 kΩ. O2 pairs it with NJM4556A.
- **Counterfeits:** No NJM2068-specific fakes found. Marketplace 'NJM2068M SOP8 JRC2068' lots carry general remark risk; buy from Mouser, DigiKey or Akizuki. 'DD'/'MD' only means noise-selected, so doubt big premiums. Genuine DMP8 is 5.3 mm wide, not 3.9 mm SOIC.

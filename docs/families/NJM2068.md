# NJM2068 / JRC2068 (low-noise bipolar dual, New JRC → Nisshinbo)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*A cheap, quiet JRC bipolar dual. NwAvGuy used it for the O2's gain stage, and it is common in E-MU audio interfaces and in Japanese CD and AV gear.*

**Technology:** Bipolar dual op-amp with the standard 4558 dual pinout. NJR's own SPICE macro-model uses a PNP input pair. Internally compensated: GB is 27 MHz at 10 kHz, but the unity-gain frequency is only 5.5 MHz. The noise-selected D-rank grades (DD/MD/LD) have a guaranteed maximum RIAA noise.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

NwAvGuy measured the NJM2068 for the Objective2 (O2) and chose it for the gain stage. He found it quieter than the NE5532, LM4562 and OPA2134 at lower supply current, and it cost well under a dollar. That made it a favourite with the measurement-minded DIY community. The DIY SmartStand amp also copies the O2's NJM2068 gain stage with an NJM4556A output. In China, Soomal reviews found it in E-MU interfaces (0202/0204/0404/1212m), the Musiland X-Sword II and ooAoo sound cards, and many mid-range CD players. Soomal describes it as very low noise and high resolution, with a neutral, plain sound. Japanese op-amp swappers treat it as the classic quiet JRC dual, calling it relaxed with ample bass, and compare the NJM2114 against it as the 'higher-performance 2068'. Pro and MI gear uses it too: the Roland VG-99 input stage and GK-3 pickup (per h-milz), Yamaha and Pioneer AV and car units, and cheap MM phono preamps such as FX-Audio.

**How people describe the sound:** very low noise (噪声很小), high resolution (解析力高), neutral, plain (中性平白), relaxed, ample bass (JP community; folklore), dense/mellow (JP community; unverified)

**Typical uses:** O2 (Objective2) headphone amp gain stage, E-MU 0202/0204/0404/1212m audio interfaces, Chinese PC sound cards (Musiland X-Sword II, ooAoo SQ710 II/SQ717a), mid-range Japanese CD players and AV receivers (e.g. Yamaha YSP-600 uses NJM2068MD-TE2), car audio (Pioneer KEX-M8406ZT, NJM2068MD), MM phono / RIAA preamps (FX-Audio BOX-01/BOX-02), guitar-synth/pro-audio input stages (Roland VG-99, GK-3 per h-milz), active monitor speakers (ICON PX-T6A per Soomal)

**Caveats:**

- Vendor notice: capacitive loads can cause oscillation. Add about 50 Ohm in series with the output (Ver.2011-03-18 p.2).
- Minimum supply is +/-4 V (8 V total), so it is unsuitable for 5 V single-supply or USB-powered designs.
- The '27 MHz' headline figure is GB at 10 kHz. The unity-gain frequency is only 5.5 MHz, so it is not comparable with other vendors' GBW numbers.
- No noise-current or nV/rtHz spec, and input bias current is relatively high (150 nA typ, 1 uA max). Keep source and feedback impedances low.
- Output current is not specified, and specs are given for loads of 2 kOhm and above. It is not a headphone driver: the O2 pairs it with NJM4556A outputs.
- jh4vaj measured more turn-on pop on a DD (noise-selected) sample than on a standard D (single blog report, not verified this session).
- One Japanese blog reports NJM2068D oscillating in a preamp (folklore, not verified).

## Counterfeits

No NJM2068-specific counterfeit or remarking reports were found. This session could not run web searches, so that is a gap, not a clean result. As with other popular 'JRC' audio duals, marketplace listings sold as 'JRC2068' (AliExpress/Taobao boards and loose chips) carry a general risk of remarked parts. Buy from authorized distributors (e.g. Mouser 513-NJM2068D / 513-NJM2068M). A 'DD' marking only means noise-selected, not a different die, so a big price premium for 'DD' from unknown sellers deserves suspicion. Check the logo style, lot codes and package (DMP8 is a 5.3 mm-wide body, not a 3.9 mm JEDEC SOIC).

## Related parts and alternatives

NJM4580 (Nisshinbo): similar noise floor and slightly better THD per h-milz; drop-in dual, NJM2114 (Nisshinbo): JRC's faster, 'higher-performance 2068' per the JP community, NJM5532 / NE5532: classic low-noise bipolar dual (check vendor die revisions), LM4562 / LME49720 (TI): lower distortion, higher drive, OPA1612 / OPA1656 (TI): modern low-noise successors, MUSES8820 (Nisshinbo): premium JRC audio dual

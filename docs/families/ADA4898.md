# ADA4898-1 / ADA4898-2

**Bipolar high-voltage low-noise op-amp, 1/2 channels** · Analog Devices (2008–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** PCN 22_0142 (Rev. -, 4 Apr 2023; later Revs. A and C) adds ADI Wilmington, MA (ADWL) as an alternate wafer fab to Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) for high-voltage bipolar products, for… [Details](#silicon-changes)

*0.9 nV/√Hz, 55 V/µs bipolar op-amp for ±5 V to ±16 V rails; a diyAudio favourite for DAC I/V stages.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.9 nV/√Hz typ (≈3.9 at 1 Hz, ≈2.8 at 2 Hz) | 50 MHz GBW; 65 MHz −3 dB bandwidth | 55 V/µs | better than −90 dBc to 600 kHz | ±5 V to ±16 V | 15.8 mA per dual (≈7.9 mA/amp); ≈8 mA/amp |

## Why it's rated

A niche part in DIY DAC circles. diyAudio builders pick it for I/V stages after current-output DACs, citing very low noise, bandwidth and ±16 V headroom. ADI pitches it for 16/18-bit systems. It also appears in instrument LNAs and photodetectors, such as Gerhard Hoffmann's 'lono' parallel LNA. An audio review site calls the ADA4898-1 a cheap LT1115 equivalent.

**Described as:** deep, tight bass (reportedly), impactful bass, excellent in I/V stages

**Used in:** I/V converter after current-output DACs, DAC output buffer or reconstruction filter, Low-noise LNA front ends, e.g. parallel arrays, Photodiode transimpedance and servo loops, 16-bit/18-bit ADC drivers

## Watch out

- SOIC-8 with exposed pad only; rolling needs an adapter. Tie the pad to −VS or leave floating, never ground/+VS.
- About 7.9 mA per amplifier: a dual dissipates ≈0.47 W at ±15 V and runs warm on padless adapters.
- Needs at least ±5 V; unsuitable for low-voltage portable designs.
- High current noise (2.4 pA/√Hz); noise-optimal source impedance is about 375 Ω.
- With 50–65 MHz bandwidth, layout, decoupling and capacitive loading matter; sockets can cause peaking or oscillation.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit or remarking reports found, though forums were not specifically checked. Cheap, SOIC-EP-only parts make poor fake targets. Pre-built DIP-8 adapters are unchecked. Buy from ADI-authorised distributors (DigiKey, Mouser); check for a real exposed pad and ADI marking.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2023-04 · PCN 22_0142 (eff. 2023-07-07)

PCN 22_0142 (Rev. -, 4 Apr 2023; later Revs. A and C) adds ADI Wilmington, MA (ADWL) as an alternate wafer fab to Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) for high-voltage bipolar products, for supply continuity. ADA4898-2YRDZ is listed; ADA4898-1 coverage is unconfirmed. No fit, form, function or reliability impact.

- **Affected:** ADA4898-2YRDZ, ADA4898-2YRDZ-R7, ADA4898-1 (not confirmed)
- **How to tell:** No marking difference stated. Parts dated after mid-2023 may come from Limerick (ADLK) or Wilmington (ADWL); trace by ADI lot or date code.
- **Audio impact:** None documented. Same process at a second fab; no data-sheet spec changed.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same design and data sheet; only fab-to-fab variation
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab site | ADI Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) | ADLK or ADI Wilmington, MA (ADWL) |

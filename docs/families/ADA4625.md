# ADA4625-1 / ADA4625-2 (ADI 36 V low-noise JFET-input, rail-to-rail output)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

**Technology:** JFET-input precision op-amp, 36 V single or split supply, 3.3 nV/rtHz, 18 MHz GBW, rail-to-rail output

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A diyAudio member tried it as a DAC I/V converter and was 'amazed by the sound quality, almost like a CFA op amp' (in the OPA828 vs OPA627 thread). A Head-Fi Opamp-thread poster put it in a headphone amp, 'really liked it', and described a 'great clean sound' that 'seems to help low end a lot'. diyAudio also has dedicated threads on it: 'ADA4625 -- 3.3nV/rtHz JFET opamp' and 'ADA4625-2 for phono preamp use'. One rolling comment calls it a good JFET option but 'somewhat cold' in tone.

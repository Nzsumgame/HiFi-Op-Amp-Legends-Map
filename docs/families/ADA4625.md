# ADA4625-1 / ADA4625-2

**36 V JFET-input op-amp, RRO, 1/2 channels** · Analog Devices (2017–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*ADI's 36 V JFET op-amp with 3.3 nV/√Hz, 18 MHz and rail-to-rail output, liked for I/V, headphone and phono stages.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.3 nV/√Hz typ | 18 MHz typ | 48 V/µs typ | +5 V to +36 V (±2.5 V to ±18 V) | 4 mA per amplifier typ |

## Why it's rated

A niche modern pick with a few forum reports. A diyAudio member used it for DAC I/V and was "amazed… almost like a CFA op amp". A Head-Fi poster liked its "great clean sound" that "seems to help low end" in a headphone amp. diyAudio has threads on phono use. One rolling comment calls it "somewhat cold". Impressions, not measurements.

**Described as:** clean, helps low end, almost like a CFA, somewhat cold (one comment)

**Used in:** DAC I/V converter, Headphone amplifier, Phono (RIAA) preamp, Low-noise high-impedance front ends

## Watch out

- Exposed-pad SOIC-8 only; DIP-8 rolling needs an adapter that handles the EPAD as the datasheet directs.
- At 18 MHz it can oscillate on long adapter leads or with poor decoupling.
- About 4 mA per amplifier, so it runs warmer than low-power JFET parts.
- Praise comes from a few forum posts, and the tonal descriptions contradict each other.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit or remarking reports found. Single-source niche ADI part in an exposed-pad SOIC: buy from ADI, DigiKey, Mouser or Arrow/Verical. Treat cheap marketplace DIP-adapter "upgrade" modules with caution.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

# ADA4625-1 / ADA4625-2 (ADI 36 V low-noise JFET-input, rail-to-rail output)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*ADI's 36 V JFET-input op-amp: 3.3 nV/rtHz, 18 MHz GBW and rail-to-rail output. diyAudio and Head-Fi users like it for I/V, headphone and phono stages.*

**Technology:** Precision JFET-input op-amp. Runs from a single +5 V to +36 V supply (±2.5 V to ±18 V). Input common-mode range includes the negative supply, and the output swings rail to rail. 3.3 nV/rtHz at 1 kHz, 18 MHz GBW, 48 V/us, 4 mA per amplifier. 8-lead SOIC with exposed pad (EPAD).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche modern pick with a small base of forum reports. On diyAudio, one member used it as a DAC I/V converter and was 'amazed by the sound quality, almost like a CFA op amp' (OPA828 vs OPA627 thread). On Head-Fi, a poster in The Opamp Thread 'really liked it' in a headphone amp, citing a 'great clean sound' that 'seems to help low end a lot'. diyAudio also has dedicated threads on its 3.3 nV/rtHz spec and on phono-preamp use. One rolling comment calls it a good JFET option but 'somewhat cold'. All of these are individual listener impressions, not measurements.

**How people describe the sound:** clean (Head-Fi user), helps low end (Head-Fi user), 'almost like a CFA op amp' (diyAudio I/V user), 'somewhat cold' (one rolling comment)

**Typical uses:** DAC I/V converter, headphone amplifier, phono (RIAA) preamp, low-noise high-impedance front ends

**Caveats:**

- Only sold in an exposed-pad SOIC-8. Rolling it into DIP-8 sockets needs an adapter, and the adapter should handle the EPAD thermally and electrically as the datasheet directs.
- The 18 MHz GBW, fast part can oscillate on long adapter leads or with poor decoupling.
- It draws about 4 mA per amplifier, so it runs warmer than low-power JFET parts.
- The praise comes from a few individual forum posts, and the tonal descriptors contradict each other.

- **When:** 2025 (datasheet Rev B 03/2025; Mouser lists a PCN dated 2025-06-26)
- **Affected:** ADA4625-1, ADA4625-2
- **How to tell old from new:** ADI PCN 25_0070 compares the old and new package outline drawings. Check the date code against the PCN effective date, and check the package drawing in datasheet Rev B against Rev A.
- **Audio impact:** None expected, because this is a package or documentation change with no die change indicated.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - package outline change only. If the design depends on the exposed-pad land pattern or the fit of a DIP adapter, check the footprint.

No counterfeit or remarking reports were found. It is a single-source, niche ADI part in an exposed-pad SOIC, so buy from authorized distributors (ADI, DigiKey, Mouser, Arrow/Verical). Treat cheap DIP-adapter 'upgrade' modules from marketplaces with caution.

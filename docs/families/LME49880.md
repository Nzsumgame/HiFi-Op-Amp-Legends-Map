# LME49880 (National / TI dual JFET-input audio op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*A niche National LME-series JFET-input audio dual. A few Japanese op-amp swappers praise it as tight and clean, and shops sell it pre-mounted on DIP-8 adapter boards.*

**Technology:** JFET-input dual audio operational amplifier from National Semiconductor's LME audio op-amp series (siblings: LME49720, LME49860, LME49990). The JFET input and the rest of the electrical architecture were not checked against a vendor datasheet in either research session.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche part, discussed mainly in the Japanese op-amp-rolling community. Users there reportedly describe it as tight, clean and high-resolution, with clean mids and highs and a 'rich NatSemi tone'. Because it is surface-mount only, Japanese retailers (Kyoritsu/eleshop, Digit, Bispa) reportedly sell it pre-mounted on DIP-8 adapter boards. A Zigsow user review covered it as a DAC op-amp candidate. A Yahoo Chiebukuro thread reportedly compares its sound with OPA1612, OPA2211, OPA627 and OPA1622. No objective measurements (Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner) were located. All of this comes from one discovery lens, and none of it could be re-checked in either session, so treat it as low-confidence folklore.

**How people describe the sound:** tight, clean, high-resolution, rich 'NatSemi tone', clean mids and highs

**Typical uses:** Op-amp swapping in DACs (I/V and output/LPF stages) via DIP-8 adapter board, Op-amp swapping in headphone amps and preamps with DIP-8 sockets

**Caveats:**

- Surface-mount only: socketed swapping needs a third-party DIP adapter board, and the adapter's layout and decoupling can affect results. If the package turns out to be an exposed-pad PSOP, the pad connection and heat path on the adapter also matter (unverified).
- The praise is subjective opinion from a small Japanese community (low confidence), and the source pages were not re-read. No independent measurements were found.
- Lifecycle status at TI is unverified, so availability may be limited and old stock or grey-market parts are likely.

## Counterfeits

No LME49880-specific counterfeit reports were found, and no counterfeit searches could run in either session. Enthusiast supply is mostly pre-mounted on third-party DIP adapter boards, so the chip's top marking is often the only check possible. Inspect it, check the date code against the National/TI era, and buy from reputable shops or authorized distributors. If TI has discontinued the part (unverified), expect a higher share of broker and grey-market stock.

# LME49713 (National / TI high-fidelity current-feedback audio op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

**Technology:** Single bipolar current-feedback audio op-amp on National's LME high-voltage process

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Head-Fi has a thread titled 'LME49713, could be a killer opamp'. Someone who worked on the chip called it the best-sounding op-amp made on the LME high-voltage process. Extended listening sessions preferred this current-feedback part over voltage-feedback op-amps in most applications. A user said a simple headphone amp built with it was 'quite amazing - dead silent and with real drive', and that it easily drives 150-ohm loads. Caveat: it is not a drop-in replacement for standard duals, and it is very fast and tricky to stabilise.

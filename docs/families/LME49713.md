# LME49713 (National / TI high-fidelity current-feedback audio op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

*Very fast single current-feedback part from National's LME audio line. Head-Fi and diyAudio users praise it as 'dead silent' with 'real drive' in headphone and line stages.*

**Technology:** Single-channel bipolar current-feedback audio op-amp on National's LME high-voltage process. Process detail comes from a forum post and is unconfirmed.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Head-Fi has a thread titled 'LME49713, could be a killer opamp'. On diyAudio, a poster described as having worked on the chip called it the best-sounding op-amp made on the LME high-voltage process. The same source says long listening sessions favoured this current-feedback part over voltage-feedback op-amps in most uses. A Head-Fi user called a simple headphone amp built with it 'quite amazing - dead silent and with real drive' and said it easily drives 150-ohm loads. All of this is anecdotal forum opinion.

**How people describe the sound:** dead silent (forum user), real drive (forum user)

**Typical uses:** Headphone amplifier output or gain stage (drives 150-ohm headphones, per forum report), DIY line stages and preamps

**Caveats:**

- Single channel and current-feedback, so it is not a drop-in replacement for standard dual op-amps such as NE5532 or OPA2134 sockets.
- Very fast and tricky to stabilise. A current-feedback amp needs the feedback resistor value the datasheet specifies, and must not have a capacitor directly in the feedback path.
- The praise comes from a small number of enthusiast forum posts. Treat it as anecdotal.

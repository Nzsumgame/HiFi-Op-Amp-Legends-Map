# LME49713 (National / TI high-fidelity current-feedback audio op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

*National's current-feedback single: 1.9 nV/√Hz, 0.00036% THD+N, 1900 V/µs, ±100 mA. Head-Fi and diyAudio builders praise it for quiet headphone and buffer stages with strong drive.*

**Technology:** Single-channel bipolar current-feedback (CFB) audio op-amp from National's LME49xxx high-performance audio line. Someone who worked on the chip said it was made on National's LME high-voltage process (forum attribution). Packages: SOIC-8 and 8-lead TO-99 metal can; no DIP-8. Supply ±5 V to ±18 V.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Head-Fi has a thread titled 'LME49713, could be a killer opamp'. On diyAudio, someone who worked on the chip called it the best-sounding op-amp made on National's LME high-voltage process, and said that in extended listening sessions it was preferred over voltage-feedback parts in most applications. One Head-Fi user called a simple headphone amp built with it 'quite amazing - dead silent and with real drive' and said it drives 150-ohm loads easily. It is a niche, enthusiast-praised part rather than a mainstream standard. It is not a drop-in replacement for standard duals, and it is fast and tricky to stabilise.

**How people describe the sound:** dead silent (one Head-Fi user), real drive / effortless load driving (one Head-Fi user)

**Typical uses:** Headphone amplifier / output buffer (drives 150 Ω loads), DAC output / I/V stages (on SOIC-to-DIP adapters), Make-up gain stage for passive summing mixers (Gearspace RMS Folcrom thread)

**Caveats:**

- Single channel, SOIC-8 or TO-99 only: needs an adapter for DIP-8 sockets and cannot directly replace dual NE5532/LME49720 parts.
- Current-feedback part: the feedback resistor sets bandwidth and stability, and a capacitor must not go across the feedback resistor. Voltage-feedback circuits can oscillate.
- diyAudio report: it ran very hot/oscillated in the NE5534 sockets of a Sony CDP-557ESD, where an AD844 worked instead.
- Input bias current of about 1.8 µA typ can cause large offsets with high-value bias resistors.
- Obsolete since the 2017 last-time buy, so sourcing is limited to old stock and brokers.

## Counterfeits

No LME49713-specific counterfeit reports were found. Fakes of its siblings (LME49720, LM4562) are well documented on diyAudio. The part has been obsolete since the 2017 last-time buy, and SOIC and TO-99 units on eBay, AliExpress or broker sites carry elevated remark/fake risk. Check marking, date code and the metal-can finish, and bench-test bias current, slew rate and distortion.

## Related parts and alternatives

AD844 (Analog Devices current-feedback, DIP-8 available; one diyAudio user found it stable where LME49713 oscillated), LME49710 (National/TI voltage-feedback single from the same family; easier to stabilise; status not verified), OPA1611 (TI low-noise voltage-feedback audio single), THS3091 / THS3491 (TI high-voltage current-feedback amplifiers; not audio-specified)

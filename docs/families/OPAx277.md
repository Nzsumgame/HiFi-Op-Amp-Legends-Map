# OPA277 / OPA2277 / OPA4277 (Burr-Brown high-precision low-power bipolar)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Bipolar classics

**Technology:** Precision bipolar (10 uV offset, 0.1 uV/C drift), low power; single / dual / quad

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A zigsow review titled '低電力でクリアな音質' ('Low power, clear sound') praises the OPA2277P for audio. Silicon House (Digit), a Japanese DIY parts shop, ran a blog post titled '意外! それはOPA2277！' ('Surprise! It's the OPA2277!') about its surprisingly good audio use. Other Japanese users compare it with the OPA2134, calling it slightly veiled but clear, and some prefer its balanced, vocal-forward sound to the OPA2604. Other impressions are mixed (strong bass, forward, can be harsh).

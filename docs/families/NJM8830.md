# NJM8830 (Nisshinbo ultra-low-distortion, ultra-low-noise full-swing audio dual)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

**Technology:** Audio dual with full-swing output. Vendor figures: 0.000012% THD, 2.5 nV/√Hz, ±100 mA output. The search did not confirm the input type.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Nisshinbo launched it in 2022 (vendor news 2022-06-07; PHILE WEB and AV Watch covered it), and Akizuki sells it to hobbyists. Japanese enthusiasts who swapped it in report positively: a Threads user who put it in an FX-Audio amp calls the result 'メッチャ高音質' (extremely high sound quality), another swap in place of a MUSES8920E reportedly gave stronger bass and punch, and there is a Minkara parts review of a DIY install.

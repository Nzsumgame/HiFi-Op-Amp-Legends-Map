# NJM8830 / NJM8830GM1

**Bipolar-input audio dual, rail-to-rail output** · JRC/Nisshinbo (2022–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Nisshinbo's 2022 low-voltage audio dual: 0.000012% THD, 2.5 nV/√Hz and 100 mA output for headphone and DAC stages.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.5 nV/√Hz typ | 90 MHz typ | 30 V/µs typ | 0.000012% typ | ±2.0 V to ±5.25 V (4–10.5 V) | 6.5 mA (launch coverage); ~6 mA per diyAudio |

## Why it's rated

A 2022 launch covered by PHILE WEB, Nikkan Kogyo and PR Times, sold to hobbyists by Akizuki with a DIP8 adapter. Japanese swappers report strong results. diyAudio bench posts show it beating the OPA1642A but not the OPA1612A, with low overshoot and fast settling. Seen as an IEM or low-impedance headphone driver.

**Described as:** very high sound quality, stronger bass and punch, low overshoot, fast settling

**Used in:** IEM / low-impedance headphone driver, Preamp and active-filter stages on low-voltage rails, DAC output / buffer stages in 5 V-class gear, Op-amp rolling via Akizuki HSOP8-to-DIP8 adapter

## Watch out

- Max supply is only ±5.25 V (10.5 V total); never fit it in ±12 V or ±15 V sockets.
- SMD only (HSOP8-M1 / DFN8-W1); socket swaps need an adapter board.
- About 6–6.5 mA supply current; a diyAudio poster calls it unsuitable for portable gear.
- 90 MHz GBW: adapter installs need short, well-decoupled layouts to stay stable.
- Praise is mostly Japanese anecdote plus one diyAudio thread, where it measured below the OPA1612A.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit reports. Recent single-source part sold via Mouser, LCSC and Akizuki. It has no DIP package, so any 'DIP-8 NJM8830' is an adapter board (such as Akizuki's kit) or a fake.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

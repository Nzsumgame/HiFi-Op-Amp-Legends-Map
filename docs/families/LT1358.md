# LT1357 / LT1358 / LT1359 (single/dual/quad 25 MHz, 600 V/µs C-Load bipolar)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*Lower-bias, 2 mA/amp sibling of the LT1364, rated 25 MHz and 600 V/µs. A niche sound-card buffer pick that listeners call warm, bright and tonally rich.*

**Technology:** Bipolar, built on Linear Technology's complementary bipolar process. It uses the single-stage topology of the LT1354–LT1365 family: matched high-impedance voltage-feedback inputs with current-feedback-like slewing. It is sold as a C-Load part, stable with any capacitive load. Linear's LT1364 datasheet calls the LT1358/LT1359 the 'Lower Power Version of LT1364/LT1365' at 2 mA per amplifier.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche, cult pick from the op-amp-rolling era of sound cards (Asus Xonar, Audiotrak Prodigy HD2). The LT1358 is described as 'warm, bright, tonal rich and sounds forward'. Some listeners prefer it to the faster LT1364 for sound because it is the same circuit run at a lower bias and a slower speed. With the LT1364 and LT1361 it is named among the preferred parts for buffer duty. The evidence is subjective forum impressions only: no Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner or other measurement data was found for the LT1357/8/9.

**How people describe the sound:** warm, bright, tonally rich, forward

**Typical uses:** Sound-card op-amp swaps (Asus Xonar, Audiotrak Prodigy HD2 buffer/output stage), Buffer and I/V stages in DIY DACs, Wideband amplifiers, buffers, active filters, video/cable drivers (the family's datasheet application list)

**Caveats:**

- The praise rests on a few subjective forum posts (one discovery lens); confidence is low.
- This is a 25 MHz / 600 V/µs part: keep the layout tight and decouple locally. C-Load stability helps with capacitive loads but not with poor bypassing or long socket leads.
- This is a low-power (2 mA/amp) version of the LT1364. Output current was not verified, so check it before using the part to drive headphones or low impedances directly.
- Bipolar inputs: input bias current is not negligible (value not verified). Watch high source impedances.
- Supply range quoted by a third party is ±2.5 V to ±15 V. Check the datasheet before running it at the ±15 V limit.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remark reports specific to the LT1357/LT1358/LT1359 were found; web search was unavailable, so this is not a clean bill of health. General advice: buy from authorized ADI distributors. Treat marketplace 'LT1358' DIP-8s with suspicion, especially leaded (non-#PBF) parts sold as new. A quick functional check is the slew rate or small-signal bandwidth on a bench: a slow part remarked as an LT1358 cannot reach roughly 600 V/µs or 25 MHz GBW. Also compare the laser marking and date code against known-genuine Linear/ADI parts.

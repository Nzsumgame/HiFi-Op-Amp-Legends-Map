# LT1357 / LT1358 / LT1359 (single/dual/quad 25 MHz, 600 V/µs C-Load bipolar)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*Lower-power (2 mA/amp) sibling of the LT1364, rated 25 MHz and 600 V/µs. A niche sound-card buffer pick that forum listeners call warm, bright and tonally rich.*

**Technology:** Bipolar, built on Linear Technology's complementary bipolar process. It is a single-stage voltage-feedback amplifier with matched high-impedance inputs and the slewing performance of a current-feedback amplifier, and it is stable with any capacitive load (C-Load). Per the ADI/Linear datasheets, the single-stage design settles well. The family comes in 25, 50 and 70 MHz versions at about 2, 4 and 6 mA per amplifier, each as a single, dual and quad. The LT1357 datasheet's front-page typical application is a DAC I-to-V converter.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche pick from the op-amp-rolling era of sound cards (Asus Xonar, Audiotrak Prodigy HD2). Forum posts describe the LT1358 as 'warm, bright, tonal rich and sounds forward'. Some posters prefer it to the faster, higher-current LT1364 in buffer duty, and it is named alongside the LT1364 and LT1361 for buffer positions. The evidence is subjective forum impressions only. No Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner or other measurement data was found for the LT1357/8/9.

**How people describe the sound:** warm, bright, tonally rich, forward

**Typical uses:** Sound-card op-amp swaps (Asus Xonar, Audiotrak Prodigy HD2 buffer/output stage), Buffer and I/V stages in DIY DACs (the LT1357 datasheet's front-page application is a DAC I-to-V converter), Wideband amplifiers, buffers, active filters, data acquisition and video/cable drivers (datasheet application list)

**Caveats:**

- The praise rests on a few subjective forum posts from one discovery lens, and those posts were not re-checked in this pass, so confidence is low.
- This is a 25 MHz / 600 V/µs part: keep the layout tight and decouple locally. C-Load stability helps with capacitive loads but not with poor bypassing or long socket leads.
- This is a low-power (2.5 mA max per amplifier) part. The datasheet guarantees ±12.5 V into 500 Ω, but output current was not verified, so check it before driving headphones or low impedances directly.
- Bipolar inputs: input bias current is up to 500 nA max. Watch for offset with high source impedances.
- The supply range is ±2.5 V to ±15 V, so ±15 V is the top of the range and a ±18 V rail is out of spec.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remark reports specific to the LT1357/LT1358/LT1359 were found. Web search this pass was limited to five queries, so this is not a clean bill of health. Buy from authorized ADI distributors, and treat marketplace 'LT1358' DIP-8s with suspicion, especially leaded (non-#PBF) parts sold as new. A bench check helps: a slow part remarked as an LT1358 cannot reach about 600 V/µs slew or 25 MHz GBW, and supply current should be at most 2.5 mA per amplifier. Also compare the laser marking and date code against known-genuine Linear/ADI parts.

## Related parts and alternatives

# OPA1652 / OPA1654 (TI SoundPlus dual/quad FET-input audio op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

*Cheap SMD-only TI FET-input dual/quad (4.5 nV/√Hz, 0.00005% THD+N, rail-to-rail out), used as an OPA2134-class workhorse for I/V and LPF stages in portable DAPs.*

**Technology:** FET-input, per TI's datasheet and product page. Low-noise folded-cascode input stage, class-AB rail-to-rail output, phase-reversal protection, back-to-back diodes across the inputs, copper leadframe. TI does not say in the datasheet whether the input is JFET or CMOS. A TI E2E engineer groups it with OPA2134 as a 'JFET' audio amp, and the discovery notes call it CMOS. Unity-gain stable. Supply ±2.25 V to ±18 V (4.5 V to 36 V).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A tier-C 'workhorse' rather than a cult part. Makers treat it as the inexpensive surface-mount successor to OPA2134: FET input, rail-to-rail output, very low THD, 2 mA per channel. A TI E2E engineer says the two have very similar specs and swap in SO-8, but OPA1652 drives much less capacitance (about 230 pF vs 1 nF at G=−1) and has no PDIP. A Japanese zigsow review is titled 'OPA2134の後継 – OPA1652AID' (per an earlier search summary). Head-Fi 'Opamp thread' posters, as summarised by search, call it very good for a JFET-type part and under $2. One poster called it the best-sounding OPA16x2, with a forward, full-bodied presentation that is less detailed than LME49720. Another heard it as close to OPA1656, with the OPA1656 more neutral. A TI designer is quoted there as a fan of its noise performance for the price. Its real footprint is OEM portable audio. Soomal teardowns show it doing I/V and/or LPF in the FiiO X5K, VentureCraft SoundDroid Vantam and Cayin N5 MKII. It also appears in HiBy R6 PRO II / R6 III, HiBy R4 and Cayin BTC-2. The OPA1654 quad appears in Eurorack/synth designs. No independent bench measurements (ASR, Groner) were found. Most enthusiasts now point to the OPA1656 as the higher-performance OPA165x.

**How people describe the sound:** Forward, 'spectator-like' soundstage with adequate body and thickness; detailed, but less so than LME49720 (single Head-Fi poster, per search summary), Similar to OPA1656; OPA1656 a bit more linear (Head-Fi poster, per search summary), Described as an improved OPA2134 successor (zigsow JP review, per earlier search summary)

**Typical uses:** DAC current-to-voltage (I/V) conversion in portable players (FiiO X5K, Cayin N5 MKII, VentureCraft Vantam), Post-DAC low-pass filter and line-out stages, Headphone amp front end/driver ahead of discrete transistor output stages (HiBy R6 PRO II / R6 III), SO-8 replacement for OPA2134 where the capacitive load is small, Unity-gain buffers with high-impedance sources (FET input, low current noise), Mixers, effects, musical instruments, Eurorack (OPA1654 quad), guitar pedal output buffers, Op-amp rolling via SOIC-to-DIP adapter modules

**Caveats:**

- SMD only (SOIC-8, VSSOP-8, WSON-8; quad in SOIC-14/TSSOP-14); no PDIP. Rolling needs a DIP adapter, and adapter/socket parasitics add capacitance.
- Weak capacitive-load drive: 100 pF per the datasheet, and about 230 pF max at G=−1 per a TI E2E engineer, versus 1 nF for OPA2134. It is not a blind drop-in where the old circuit relied on OPA2134's cap-load tolerance. Use a series resistor of about 50 Ω.
- Output drive is ±30 mA (ISC ±50 mA): not a stand-alone low-impedance headphone driver. OEMs add discrete or buffer stages.
- Input common-mode range ends 2 V below V+, so it is not rail-to-rail input. Output swings to within 800 mV of each rail at 2 kΩ.
- The sound impressions above come from individual forum posts and are subjective.
- Noisier (4.5 vs 2.9 nV/√Hz) and slower (18 MHz and 10 V/µs vs 53 MHz and 24 V/µs) than the later OPA1656.

## Counterfeits

No OPA1652/OPA1654-specific counterfeit reports were found. Counterfeits of sibling TI audio parts are documented: an 'OPA1612' that was a TL082 die (TI E2E, diyAudio) and suspected fake OPA1656 from AliExpress. Hobbyists mostly buy OPA1652 as DIP-adapter modules (AliExpress; Kyoritsu Q5M411 'OPA1652AID-DIP'), where the chip's origin cannot be verified. How to check: (1) TI never made a PDIP OPA1652, which a TI E2E engineer confirms, so a DIP-bodied 'OPA1652' chip is fake. (2) Genuine top marks per TI's 2026 addendum are 'OP1652' (SOIC-8 D and WSON DRG), 'OUPI' (VSSOP-8 DGK) and 'OPA1654' (SOIC-14/TSSOP-14), plus the TI logo and a lot code. (3) Iq should be about 2 mA per channel; input bias should be in the pA range (±100 pA max), and a bias current in the nA range points to a bipolar remark; GBW should be about 18 MHz. (4) Buy from authorised distributors.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA1656 (dual) / OPA1655 (single): TI's next-generation OPA165x (SBOS901); 2.9 nV/√Hz @10 kHz, 53 MHz, 24 V/µs, 100 mA output. Closest upgrade on the same SOIC-8 pinout., OPA1642 / OPA1641 / OPA1644: TI JFET-input SoundPlus parts, 5.1 nV/√Hz, THD+N 0.00005%. TI lists them with OPA2134 and OPA1652 as its FET-input audio duals., OPA1678 / OPA1679: lower-cost TI CMOS audio dual/quad, 4.5 nV/√Hz. Discussed on modwiggler as an alternative to the OPA1654., OPA1688: 36 V, 10 MHz, high-drive RRO audio op-amp., OPA2134 (and OPA134/OPA4134): the legacy Burr-Brown FET audio op-amp OPA1652 often replaces; still available in DIP and better with capacitive loads (about 1 nF at G=−1)., OPA1612 / OPA1602 / OPA1662: bipolar alternatives when source impedance is low (lower e_n, higher i_n).

- No web copy of SBOS477A (Aug 2016) was found. Try the Wayback Machine at ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/opa1652.pdf with snapshots between Aug and Dec 2016.
- Which revision do the Mouser mirror opa1654-443342.pdf and the RadioLocman di=161021 / di=305473 / di=305475 copies hold?
- Silicon: no OPA1652/OPA1654-specific PCN was found. TI has generic RFAB/FFAB fab-qualification PCNs on Mouser (e.g. PCN20221219006, PCN20240116000, PCN20220127001), but search did not show whether they cover OPA1652/OPA1654. Check their device lists before declaring 'no silicon change'. The meaning of the '.A'/'.B' sub-rows and multiple DGKR lead finishes in the 2026 addendum is also unresolved.
- Technology label: TI says 'FET-input'; a TI E2E engineer calls it a JFET-type amp; the discovery notes say CMOS. TI's parametric 'architecture' field was not seen.
- The inferred TI URLs (sbos477*/…pdf, ti.com/lit/gpn/opa1652, ti.com/lit/pdf/sbot007) and the SBOT007 literature number were not seen in search. Check via the Wayback Machine.
- No independent bench measurements (Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner, diyAudio) of OPA1652 were found.
- TI calling OPA1652 an official 'OPA2134 successor' is not confirmed. TI E2E material only says it is swappable in SO-8 with similar specs, and lists it with OPA2134 and OPA1642 as FET-input audio duals.

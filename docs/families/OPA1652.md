# OPA1652 / OPA1654

**FET-input audio op-amp, 2/4 channels, SMD only** · Texas Instruments (2011–present) → Texas Instruments (2019–) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Cheap TI SoundPlus FET dual/quad (4.5 nV/√Hz, 0.00005% THD+N, RRO); an OPA2134-class workhorse in portable DAPs.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5 nV/√Hz (3.8 at 10 kHz; 4.0 µVpp 20 Hz–20 kHz) | 18 MHz | 10 V/µs | 0.00005% (−126 dB); IMD (SMPTE, DIM30, CCIF) same | ±2.25 V to ±18 V (4.5–36 V); abs max 40 V | 2 mA typ, 2.5 mA max per channel (2.8 mA max hot) |

## Why it's rated

A workhorse, not a cult part: makers and a zigsow review call it the cheap SMD OPA2134 successor; TI says swappable in SO-8. Head-Fi posters call it very good for under $2 and close to OPA1656. Soomal teardowns show it in FiiO X5K, VentureCraft Vantam, Cayin N5 MKII; also HiBy R6 PRO II/III, R4, Cayin BTC-2. No bench measurements found.

**Described as:** forward soundstage, full-bodied, detailed, less than LME49720, close to OPA1656

**Used in:** DAC I/V conversion in portable players, Post-DAC low-pass filter and line-out stages, Headphone amp front end before discrete output stages, SO-8 OPA2134 replacement with light capacitive load, Mixers, pedals, Eurorack (OPA1654 quad)

## Watch out

- SMD only, no PDIP: rolling needs a DIP adapter, which adds capacitance.
- Weak cap-load drive: 100 pF (datasheet), ~230 pF at G=−1 (TI E2E) vs 1 nF for OPA2134; add ~50 Ω.
- ±30 mA output (ISC ±50 mA): not a stand-alone low-impedance headphone driver.
- Not rail-to-rail input: common-mode range ends 2 V below V+.
- Noisier and slower than OPA1656 (4.5 vs 2.9 nV/√Hz; 18 vs 53 MHz; 10 vs 24 V/µs).
- **Counterfeits:** No OPA1652 fakes reported, but fake OPA1612 (TL082 die) and OPA1656 exist. TI never made a DIP OPA1652. Genuine marks: OP1652 (D/DRG), OUPI (DGK), OPA1654. Check ~2 mA/channel Iq, pA-range bias, ~18 MHz GBW; buy authorised.

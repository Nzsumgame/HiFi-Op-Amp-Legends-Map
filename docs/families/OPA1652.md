# OPA1652 / OPA1654 (TI SoundPlus dual/quad FET-input audio op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

*Cheap, SMD-only TI FET-input audio dual/quad (4.5 nV/√Hz, 0.00005% THD+N, rail-to-rail output). Widely used for I/V and LPF stages in portable DAPs; treated as a modern OPA2134 replacement.*

**Technology:** FET-input (datasheet wording). Folded-cascode low-noise input stage, class-AB rail-to-rail output, phase-reversal protection, back-to-back diodes across the inputs, copper leadframe. Discovery notes class it as CMOS; TI's parametric label was not re-verified this run. Unity-gain stable; ±2.25 V to ±18 V supply (4.5 V to 36 V).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A tier-C 'workhorse' rather than a cult part. Enthusiasts and makers treat it as the inexpensive surface-mount successor to OPA2134: a FET input with rail-to-rail output, very low THD and 2 mA per channel. A Japanese zigsow review is titled 'OPA2134の後継 – OPA1652AID' and calls it the better part, partly because of its rail-to-rail output (per search summary). Its real footprint is OEM portable audio. Soomal teardowns show it doing I/V and/or LPF in the FiiO X5K (after a PCM1792, 2015), the VentureCraft SoundDroid Vantam (four OPA1652 for I/V and LPF, 2015) and the Cayin N5 MKII (I/V and line-out LPF, 2018). It is also in the HiBy R6 PRO II headphone amp (OPA1652 plus 16 discrete transistors, 2023), the HiBy R6 III 2025, the HiBy R4 (OPA1652/OPA1612) and the Cayin BTC-2. The quad OPA1654 appears in Eurorack/synth and guitar-pedal designs. No independent distortion measurements (ASR, Groner) were found this run. Most enthusiasts now point to the OPA1656 as the higher-performance OPA165x.

**How people describe the sound:** No distinctive sonic signature was found in sources this run., Described as an improved OPA2134 successor (zigsow JP review, per search summary).

**Typical uses:** DAC current-to-voltage (I/V) conversion in portable players (FiiO X5K, Cayin N5 MKII, VentureCraft Vantam), Post-DAC low-pass filter and line-out stages, Headphone amp front end/driver ahead of discrete transistor output stages (HiBy R6 PRO II / R6 III), Unity-gain buffers with high-impedance sources (FET input, low current noise), Mixers, effects, musical instruments, Eurorack (OPA1654 quad), guitar pedal output buffers, Op-amp rolling via SOIC-to-DIP adapter modules

**Caveats:**

- SMD only (SOIC-8, VSSOP-8, WSON-8; quad in SOIC-14/TSSOP-14). Rolling needs a DIP adapter, and adapter/socket parasitics add capacitance.
- Capacitive load spec is only 100 pF. The datasheet warns that low gain plus high capacitive load can cause peaking or oscillation and recommends a series resistor of about 50 Ω.
- Output drive is ±30 mA (ISC ±50 mA): not a stand-alone low-impedance headphone driver. OEMs add discrete or buffer stages.
- Input common-mode range ends 2 V below V+, so it is not rail-to-rail input. Output swings to within 0.8 V of each rail at 2 kΩ.
- The datasheet says soldering rather than socketing minimises temperature rise at high rails. It is rated to 36 V total (40 V abs. max).
- Noisier (4.5 vs 2.9 nV/√Hz) and slower (18 MHz and 10 V/µs vs 53 MHz and 24 V/µs) than the later OPA1656.

## Counterfeits

No OPA1652/OPA1654-specific counterfeit reports were found this run. Counterfeits of sibling TI audio parts are documented: an 'OPA1612' that turned out to be a TL082 die (TI E2E and diyAudio), and suspected fake OPA1656 from AliExpress. OPA1652 is mostly sold to hobbyists as DIP-adapter modules (AliExpress 'AkLIAM OPA1612 OPA1652 OPA1656' adapters; Kyoritsu Q5M411 'OPA1652AID-DIP' in Japan), where the chip's origin is unverifiable. How to check: (1) TI never made a DIP OPA1652, so a DIP-8-bodied 'OPA1652' chip is fake; only SOIC-on-adapter modules exist. (2) Genuine top marks per TI's 2026 addendum are 'OP1652' (SOIC-8 D and WSON DRG), 'OUPI' (VSSOP-8 DGK) and 'OPA1654' (SOIC-14 and TSSOP-14), plus TI logo and lot-trace code. (3) Iq should be about 2 mA per channel (4 mA per dual). Input bias should be in the pA range (±100 pA max), so a bias current in the nA range points to a bipolar remark. GBW should be about 18 MHz. (4) Buy from authorised distributors.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA1656 (dual) / OPA1655 (single): TI's next-generation OPA165x (SBOS901); 2.9 nV/√Hz @10 kHz, 53 MHz, 24 V/µs, 100 mA output. Closest drop-in upgrade on the same SOIC-8 pinout., OPA1642 / OPA1641 / OPA1644: TI JFET-input SoundPlus parts, 5.1 nV/√Hz, THD+N 0.00005%., OPA1678 / OPA1679: lower-cost TI CMOS audio dual/quad, 4.5 nV/√Hz. Discussed as an alternative to the OPA1654 on modwiggler., OPA1688: 36 V, 10 MHz, high-drive RRO audio op-amp, positioned as a general-purpose successor., OPA2134 (and OPA134/OPA4134): the legacy Burr-Brown JFET audio op-amp OPA1652 is commonly substituted for; still available in DIP., OPA1612 / OPA1602: bipolar alternatives when source impedance is low (lower e_n, higher i_n).

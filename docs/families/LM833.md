# LM833 / LM833-N (LM837 quad)

**Tier** A: legend / hazard · **Category** Bipolar classics

*Cheap, quiet (4.5 nV/rtHz, ~15 MHz, 7 V/us) bipolar audio dual found throughout consumer and pro gear; the budget sibling of the NE5532.*

**Technology:** Bipolar dual op-amp, internally compensated. Several different dies are sold under the one generic number. National's LM833 (TI LM833-N today) has a complementary emitter-follower output that uses a vertical PNP. TI's own LM833 (SLOS481, July 2010) has a quasi-complementary output made from two NPNs. Per a search summary of TI E2E thread 1443925, a TI engineer said TI's MC33078 and LM833 appear to be the same die because their specs and graphs are identical; the headline feature lists do match. Motorola/onsemi and ST have their own versions. TI later confirmed on E2E (widely reported June 2026) that its NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 now share one die.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (2 recorded).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A budget, low-noise (4.5 nV/rtHz) bipolar audio dual. Its datasheets aim it at 'all preamp and high level stages in PCM and HiFi systems'. It is common in CD players, portable players, graphic equalizers and DIY mic preamps, and hobbyists usually weigh it against the NE5532. A search summary attributes to Mithat Konar's 2026 blog the view that, after TI's NE5532 change, the LM833 (still made by TI, onsemi and ST) has better specs than the revised TI NE5532 in most respects. No verified community sound descriptors or published measurements (ASR, Self, Groner) were found, so treat this entry as thin.

**Typical uses:** Preamp and line-level stages in PCM/CD and hi-fi equipment (per datasheet), CD players: Philips CD614 with a Motorola LM833N (Soomal teardown photo), Portable players: HiFiMan HM-601 used a TI LM833 as the I/V converter after a TDA1543 DAC (Soomal), Graphic equalizers and tone-control stages, Mic preamps (as an NE5532 alternative), Consumer-audio op-amp sockets / op-amp rolling baseline

**Caveats:**

- TI 'LM833' and TI 'LM833-N' are different dies with different output stages. Check the logo and exact orderable.
- TI moved LM833 onto one die shared with NE5532/MC33078/RC4580 (TI E2E confirmation, reported 2026). New-date-code TI parts may behave differently from older ones; LM833-specific deltas are not documented in sources found.
- Some search summaries say TI's LM833 went from ±22 V to ±18 V and from 9 to 5 V/us. Those are the NE5532 (SLOS075K) deltas. TI's LM833 was always specified at ±18 V and 7 V/us.
- Capacitive loads above 50 pF must be isolated from the output (LM833-N datasheet).
- TI LM833 is specified only to ±18 V supplies.
- Some onsemi DIP/SOIC orderables (LM833NG/LM833DG) are reported discontinued; LM833DR2G/NCV833DR2G appear active.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Texas Instruments: July 2010 (TI's own LM833, SLOS481) / after the 2011…

TI sells two different LM833 dies. National's original LM833 has a standard complementary (vertical-PNP) emitter-follower output. For its own second-source LM833 (2010), TI used an NPN quasi-complementary output. After buying National, TI renamed National's part LM833-N. A TI E2E answer says they are 'two different devices'. Separately, a TI engineer on E2E (thread 1443925, search-summary level) said TI's LM833 and MC33078 appear to be the same die because their specs and graphs are identical.

- **When:** July 2010 (TI's own LM833, SLOS481) / after the 2011 National acquisition, by May 2012 (ex-National part appears as LM833-N in SNOSBD8E)
- **Affected:** LM833 (TI: LM833P, LM833DR), LM833-N (LM833M/NOPB, LM833MX/NOPB, LM833N/NOPB)
- **How to tell old from new:** Orderable and datasheet. TI's own design is LM833P/LM833DR, datasheet SLOS481. The ex-National die is LM833-N (LM833M, LM833MX, LM833N with /NOPB), datasheet SNOSBD8. Parts with a National logo, and older Motorola/onsemi/ST parts, are other dies.
- **Audio impact:** A different output-stage topology can change crossover and load-driving behaviour. No published audio measurements comparing the two were found.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - same pinout and similar headline specs, but a different die and output stage; circuits tuned to the National part may behave differently.
- **Confidence:** high

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Output stage topology | Complementary emitter-follower pair (NPN + vertical PNP), National LM833 / LM833-N | Quasi-complementary, two NPN devices, TI LM833 (SLOS481) |

### 2. Texas Instruments: 2024-2025 (PCN activity from April 2024;

A TI E2E thread titled 'NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 all now the same die' carries TI's confirmation that these parts were merged onto one die; Hackaday (3 June 2026) and Headphonesty (July 2026) reported it. Search summaries tie the merge to moving production from SFAB (Sherman) to RFAB (Richardson). A commentator (attribution unclear) speculated the old parts ran on 150 mm wafers in Sherman/Dallas fabs closed in late 2025. For NE5532 the documented deltas are large (±22 V to ±18 V, 9 to 5 V/us; SLOS075K, Dec 2025). LM833-specific before/after datasheet deltas are not verified. TI's LM833 was already rated ±5 to ±18 V and may already have shared the MC33078 die, so its change may be smaller than NE5532's (inference).

- **When:** 2024-2025 (PCN activity from April 2024; TI confirmation on E2E and Hackaday coverage in June 2026)
- **Affected:** LM833 (TI SLOS481 orderables, e.g. LM833P, LM833DR), NE5532, MC33078, RC4580
- **How to tell old from new:** TI parts with 2024+ date codes (exact cut-over not documented). A TI datasheet revision after SLOS481B may document the change (not verified). PCN 20240429005.1 and/or 20231114002.1 (LM833 membership unconfirmed). No top-marking difference is documented in sources found.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown for LM833 specifically; no old-vs-new LM833 measurements were found. Reports say the merged die derives from RC4580, described as having NPN inputs and a complementary output stage. TI's old LM833 had a two-NPN quasi-complementary output, so its output stage may have changed (inference, unverified). For the NE5532 in the same merge, users report different behaviour and boards failing QA.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - same part number, new silicon. The supply rating was already ±18 V for TI LM833, but input stage, output stage, stability and protection details may differ. Unverified.
- **Confidence:** medium

## Counterfeits

No LM833-specific counterfeit reports were found. Generic unbranded 'LM833 LM833N' DIP listings circulate on eBay. Buy from authorised distributors and check that the logo, orderable and date code agree (TI LM833P/LM833DR vs LM833-N LM833M/LM833N vs onsemi LM833DR2G/NCV833 vs ST). Relabelled-die fakes are well documented for the related NE5532 family.

## Related parts and alternatives

NE5532 (onsemi or pre-2024 TI stock; higher output drive), MC33078 (onsemi, the original Motorola design), LM4562 / LME49720 (National/TI high-performance audio dual), OPA1612 / OPA1602 (TI modern low-noise bipolar audio dual), onsemi LM833DR2G or ST LM833 (to avoid TI's shared die), TI LM833-N (ex-National die, if still available)

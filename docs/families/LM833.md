# LM833 / LM833-N (LM837 quad)

**Tier** A: legend / hazard · **Category** Bipolar classics

*Cheap, quiet (4.5 nV/rtHz, 15-16 MHz, 7 V/us) bipolar audio dual found throughout consumer and pro gear; the budget sibling of the NE5532.*

**Technology:** Bipolar dual op-amp, internally compensated. Several different dies are sold under the one generic number. National's LM833 (TI LM833-N today) has a complementary emitter-follower output that uses a vertical PNP. TI's own LM833 (SLOS481, July 2010) has a quasi-complementary output made from two NPNs (TI E2E 430833). In E2E thread 1443925 a responder said TI's MC33078 and LM833 appear to be the same die because all specs and graphs are identical. Motorola/onsemi and ST have their own versions. TI confirmed on E2E (thread 1652528; reported by Hackaday 3 June 2026 and Headphonesty July 2026) that its NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 now share one die.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (3 recorded: Die redesign, Fab / process transfer, Second-source difference).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A budget, low-noise (4.5 nV/rtHz) bipolar audio dual that datasheets aim at 'all preamp and high level stages in PCM and HiFi systems'. Common in CD players, portable players, graphic equalizers and DIY mic preamps, and usually weighed against the NE5532. GroupDIY forum posters (search-summary level) describe it as having lower distortion but lower output drive than the 5532, suited to high-gain/low-distortion work where drive is not needed, and as National's attempt to rival the 5532 that did not beat it. A search summary attributes to Mithat Konar's 2026 blog the view that the LM833 now out-specs the revised TI NE5532 in most respects. No verified sound descriptors or published measurements (Self, Groner, ASR) were found; treat as thin.

**Typical uses:** Preamp and line-level stages in PCM/CD and hi-fi equipment (per datasheet), CD players: Philips CD614 with a Motorola LM833N (Soomal teardown photo), Portable players: HiFiMan HM-601 used a TI LM833 as the I/V converter after a TDA1543 DAC (Soomal), Graphic equalizers and tone-control stages, Mic preamps (as an NE5532 alternative), Consumer-audio op-amp sockets / op-amp rolling baseline

**Caveats:**

- TI 'LM833' and TI 'LM833-N' are two different devices with different output stages (TI E2E 430833). Check the logo and exact orderable.
- TI merged LM833 onto one die shared with NE5532/MC33078/RC4580 (TI E2E 1652528). New-date-code TI parts may behave differently; LM833-specific deltas and the cut-over date are not documented in sources found.
- Some search summaries say TI's LM833 went from ±22 V to ±18 V and from 9 to 5 V/us. Those are NE5532 (SLOS075K) deltas; TI's LM833 was always specified at ±18 V and 7 V/us.
- Lower output drive than NE5532 per forum reports; capacitive loads above 50 pF must be isolated (LM833-N datasheet).
- TI LM833 is specified only to ±18 V supplies.
- onsemi LM833NG/LM833DG are discontinued; LM833DR2G/NCV833DR2G appear active.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Second-source difference: Texas Instruments (own LM833 design)…, TI's own LM833 datasheet SLOS481 dates from July 2010.

TI sells two different LM833 devices; TI E2E thread 430833 calls them 'two different devices'. National's original LM833 has a complementary emitter-follower output (NPN plus vertical PNP). TI's own second-source LM833 (2010) uses a quasi-complementary output made of two NPN devices. In E2E thread 1443925 a TI engineer said TI's LM833 and MC33078 appear to be the same die, because all specs and graphs are identical. onsemi keeps Motorola's design.

- **When:** TI's own LM833 datasheet SLOS481 dates from July 2010. After the 2011 National acquisition, TI sold both dies; the National part is documented as LM833-N (SNOSBD8E, revised May 2012).
- **Affected:** LM833 (TI own design: LM833P, LM833DR), LM833-N (ex-National: LM833M/NOPB, LM833MX/NOPB, LM833N/NOPB), LM833 (onsemi/Motorola design, e.g. LM833DR2G)
- **How to tell old from new:** Check the orderable code and datasheet. TI's own design is LM833P/LM833DR (datasheet SLOS481). The ex-National die is LM833M, LM833MX or LM833N with /NOPB (datasheet SNOSBD8, LM833-N). National-logo parts are the National die; Motorola/onsemi and ST parts are other dies.
- **Audio impact:** A different output-stage topology can change crossover and load-driving behaviour. No published audio measurements comparing the two were found.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - same pinout and similar headline specs, but a different die and output stage. Circuits tuned on the National part may behave differently.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Verification:** Not re-searched: high-confidence entry of a kind outside the priority list. The National-to-LM833-N rename was moved into its own entry. The LM833-N datasheet SNOSBD8E (revised May 2012) was seen in search results.

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Output stage topology | Complementary emitter-follower pair (NPN + vertical PNP), National LM833 / LM833-N | Quasi-complementary, two NPN devices, TI LM833 (SLOS481) |
| Gain-bandwidth (typ, headline) | 15 MHz (National LM833 / LM833-N) | 16 MHz (TI LM833, SLOS481B) |

### 2. Die redesign: Texas Instruments, Process-migration PCN 20231114002.1 dated 15 Nov 2023;…

TI confirmed on its E2E forum (thread 'NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 all now the same die') that these four parts now share one die, with part numbers unchanged. Reporting describes a move from SFAB (Sherman, 150 mm wafers) to RFAB (Richardson) on a newer process with a die-shrink redesign. The shared die is RC4580-based (Hackaday quotes Rich Cabot: 'they just relabeled a 4580'), and Headphonesty describes it as having NPN inputs and a complementary output. The LM833DR appears in the same November 2023 PCN as the NE5532DR. For NE5532 the documented deltas are large (±22 V to ±18 V; 9 to 5 V/µs; SLOS075K). No LM833-specific before/after deltas are documented. TI's LM833 was already rated ±18 V and 7 V/µs and was reportedly already the same die as MC33078, so its change may be smaller (inference).

- **When:** Process-migration PCN 20231114002.1 dated 15 Nov 2023; search summaries say it lists LM833DR. SFAB closure was planned for 2024-2025. TI confirmed the die merge on E2E (thread 1652528), and it was publicised in June-July 2026 (Hackaday 3 Jun 2026, Headphonesty Jul 2026).
- **Affected:** LM833DR (listed in PCN 20231114002.1), LM833P and other TI SLOS481 LM833 orderables (covered by the E2E die-merge statement; PCN listing not seen)
- **How to tell old from new:** No LM833 datasheet revision after SLOS481B and no LM833 datasheet PCN were found, and no marking change is documented. PCN 20231114002.1 (15 Nov 2023) lists LM833DR among devices for process migration and BOM-option qualification (Group 1, per search summary). Whether LM833 is in PCN 20240429005.1 is unconfirmed. TI LM833 parts with 2024-or-later date codes are probably the merged die (inference). The ex-National LM833-N is not named in the merge.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown for LM833 specifically; no old-versus-new LM833 measurements were found. If the merged die is RC4580-derived with a complementary output, the former two-NPN quasi-complementary output of TI's LM833 may have changed (inference). For NE5532 in the same merge, users report changed behaviour and boards failing QA (Headphonesty).
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - same part number, new silicon on a new process. TI's LM833 supply rating was already ±18 V, but the input and output stages, stability and protection details may differ. None of this is verified for LM833.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Verification:** Searched the PCN 20231114002 device list: results put LM833DR in its process-migration/BOM group, alongside NE5532DR/SA5532ADR (22 to 18 V, 2 to 1 kV ESD). The E2E 1652528 title and the Hackaday/Cabot coverage were confirmed. No LM833 datasheet PCN was found. LM833 membership in PCN 20240429005.1 is still unconfirmed.

### 3. Fab / process transfer: onsemi (ON Semiconductor, ex-Motorola), Initial PCN 11528 issued 19 July 2001

ON Semiconductor PCN 11528 lists the LM833 for transfer from the Motorola BMC wafer fab in Mesa, AZ to ON's Tesla wafer fab in Roznov, Czech Republic. This is a same-part-number fab move of the Motorola-design LM833, separate from the TI die issues. A later onsemi Roznov conversion from 150 mm to 200 mm wafers (IPCN/FPCN23597 series) exists, but no source ties it to LM833.

- **When:** Initial PCN 11528 issued 19 July 2001
- **Affected:** LM833D, LM833DR2, LM833N
- **How to tell old from new:** No marking rule was found to tell Mesa wafers from Roznov wafers. Pre-2001/2002 date codes would be Mesa BMC material (inference). The later Pb-free orderables are LM833DG, LM833DR2G and LM833NG.
- **Audio impact:** None documented. The same design moved to another fab.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - same design and part numbers; any difference would be process spread within datasheet limits.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Verification:** Search confirmed that onsemi PCN 11528 (issued 19 July 2001) names LM833D and LM833N for transfer to the Tesla wafer fab in Roznov. LM833DR2 comes from the completeness sweep and was not separately seen.

## Other change notes (lifecycle, packaging, successors, lore)

### 1. Renumbering / successor: Texas Instruments (ex-National…, After TI acquired National in 2011.

TI renamed National's LM833 to LM833-N to separate it from TI's own LM833 (SLOS481), which is a different die. A buyer who orders the generic 'LM833' from TI gets the TI design, not the National part the audio reputation was built on.

- **When:** After TI acquired National in 2011. The LM833-N datasheet SNOSBD8E was revised in May 2012.
- **Affected:** LM833-N, LM833M/NOPB, LM833MX/NOPB, LM833N/NOPB
- **How to tell old from new:** The product and datasheet name is LM833-N (SNOSBD8). The orderable codes keep National's format (LM833M, LM833MX, LM833N plus /NOPB). A plain 'LM833' at TI means TI's own design (LM833P, LM833DR; SLOS481).
- **Audio impact:** The rename changes nothing electrically. The risk is receiving TI's different die when ordering the generic 'LM833' (see the second-source entry).
- **Drop-in risk:** low - naming change only. The die difference is covered in the second-source entry.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Verification:** Searched LM833-N status. Found the TI LM833-N product page and SNOSBD8E ('May 2004 - revised May 2012') listing the LM833M/LM833MX/LM833N /NOPB orderables. No NRND or obsolete status was found.

### Revision chain status

TI SLOS481 (TI's own LM833): original JULY 2010 -> A (REVISED AUGUST 2010) -> B (REVISED OCTOBER 2014). Searches for SLOS481C/SLOS481D found nothing, so there is no evidence of a Rev C+. The original July 2010 edition is still missing as a confirmed copy. The best candidate is the already-listed Octopart LM833P ...-10089682.pdf, which uses the old lowercase TI-style title 'dual high-speed audio operational amplifier'; its revision is unconfirmed.  National LM833: DS number found. National's datasheet is DS005218 (pdf.datasheetcatalog.com/.../DS005218.PDF; a search summary gives (c) 2004). Individual National edition dates (pre-2004 editions) are still missing. Copies whose edition is not shown: Jameco 120942 and 1309253, sm5cbw.se, and Octopart 9811680, 8191293 and 7578376. No legacy national.com URL came up in any search.  TI SNOSBD8 (ex-National): A, B and C are still missing, and no search result mentioned them. D is confirmed (new copies: Scribd 102660047, Octopart LM833M-National ...-10846346); its date is still unknown. E is MAY 2004 - REVISED MAY 2012 (new copy: Octopart ...-28360528). Related document found: JAJSB49, the Japanese TI edition of the National LM833 datasheet (kyohritsu copy); its date is not shown. The 'MAY 2004' origin date in SNOSBD8 matches the (c) 2004 DS005218 edition.  Motorola LM833/D: one search summary says the eletrodex copy is '1996 Rev 0' (low confidence, footer not shown). Other Motorola revisions and dates are still missing. New copies with no revision shown: alldatasheet 3070, datasheetspdf 54287, datasheetq (2 entries), chipfind.  onsemi LM833/D: Rev. 6 (September 2011) -> [Rev. 7 missing] -> Rev. 8 (July 2025). Rev. 6 is now also confirmed for the already-listed mouser LM833-D-93350.pdf (search-title footer) and for a new copy at media.digikey.com (LM833,NCV833.pdf). Rev. 0-5 (ON Semi era, around 2000-2010) are still missing. The Scribd 'lm833-rev2-pdf' copy may be an early revision, but neither its vendor nor its revision is confirmed.  ST Doc ID 2169: only Rev 3 (August 2009) is confirmed. Rev 1, Rev 2 and anything after Rev 3 are still missing. The datasheetq entry 'LM833_05' may be a 2005 edition (unverified). A second alldatasheet entry (527770, LM833DT) may be a newer upload. New copies with no revision shown: datasheet4u 1394947, radiolocman di=147172, datasheetdir, allaboutcircuits.  Side notes: the already-listed docs.lightningmaps.org lm833.pdf showed up once with the title 'LM833-N Dual Audio Operational Amplifier (Rev. E)'. ## Counterfeits

No LM833-specific counterfeit reports found. Generic unbranded 'LM833 LM833N' DIP listings circulate on eBay/Amazon (e.g. listings mislabelling onsemi 'LM833NG/NOPB'). Buy from authorised distributors and check that logo, orderable and date code agree (TI LM833P/LM833DR vs LM833-N LM833M/LM833N vs onsemi LM833DR2G/NCV833 vs ST). Note onsemi LM833NG/LM833DG are discontinued, so 'new' stock of them is surplus or suspect. Relabelled-die fakes are well documented for the related NE5532 family.

## Related parts and alternatives

NE5532 (onsemi or older-date-code TI stock; higher output drive), MC33078 (onsemi, the original Motorola design), LM4562 / LME49720 (National/TI high-performance audio dual), OPA1612 / OPA1602 (TI modern low-noise bipolar audio dual), onsemi LM833DR2G or ST LM833 (to avoid TI's shared die), TI LM833-N (ex-National die, if still available)

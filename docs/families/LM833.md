# LM833 / LM833-N (LM837 quad)

**Tier** A: legend / hazard · **Category** Bipolar classics

*Cheap, quiet (4.5 nV/rtHz, 15-16 MHz, 7 V/us) bipolar audio dual found throughout consumer and pro gear; the budget sibling of the NE5532.*

**Technology:** Bipolar dual op-amp, internally compensated. Several different dies are sold under the one generic number. National's LM833 (TI LM833-N today) has a complementary emitter-follower output that uses a vertical PNP. TI's own LM833 (SLOS481, July 2010) has a quasi-complementary output made from two NPNs (TI E2E 430833). In E2E thread 1443925 a responder said TI's MC33078 and LM833 appear to be the same die because all specs and graphs are identical. Motorola/onsemi and ST have their own versions. TI confirmed on E2E (thread 1652528; reported by Hackaday 3 June 2026 and Headphonesty July 2026) that its NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 now share one die.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (3 recorded: Unclassified).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A budget, low-noise (4.5 nV/rtHz) bipolar audio dual that datasheets aim at 'all preamp and high level stages in PCM and HiFi systems'. Common in CD players, portable players, graphic equalizers and DIY mic preamps, and usually weighed against the NE5532. GroupDIY forum posters (search-summary level) describe it as having lower distortion but lower output drive than the 5532, suited to high-gain/low-distortion work where drive is not needed, and as National's attempt to rival the 5532 that did not beat it. A search summary attributes to Mithat Konar's 2026 blog the view that the LM833 now out-specs the revised TI NE5532 in most respects. No verified sound descriptors or published measurements (Self, Groner, ASR) were found; treat as thin.

**Typical uses:** Preamp and line-level stages in PCM/CD and hi-fi equipment (per datasheet), CD players: Philips CD614 with a Motorola LM833N (Soomal teardown photo), Portable players: HiFiMan HM-601 used a TI LM833 as the I/V converter after a TDA1543 DAC (Soomal), Graphic equalizers and tone-control stages, Mic preamps (as an NE5532 alternative), Consumer-audio op-amp sockets / op-amp rolling baseline

**Caveats:**

- TI 'LM833' and TI 'LM833-N' are two different devices with different output stages (TI E2E 430833). Check the logo and exact orderable.
- TI merged LM833 onto one die shared with NE5532/MC33078/RC4580 (TI E2E 1652528). New-date-code TI parts may behave differently; LM833-specific deltas and the cut-over date are not documented in sources found.
- Some search summaries say TI's LM833 went from ±22 V to ±18 V and from 9 to 5 V/us. Those are NE5532 (SLOS075K) deltas; TI's LM833 was always specified at ±18 V and 7 V/us.
- Lower output drive than NE5532 per forum reports; capacitive loads above 50 pF must be isolated (LM833-N datasheet).
- TI LM833 is specified only to ±18 V supplies.
- onsemi LM833NG/LM833DG are discontinued; LM833DR2G/NCV833DR2G appear active.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Unclassified: Texas Instruments, July 2010 (TI's own LM833, SLOS481) / after the 2011…

TI sells two different LM833 devices (TI E2E 430833: 'two different devices'). National's original LM833 has a complementary (vertical-PNP) emitter-follower output; TI's own second-source LM833 (2010) changed to an NPN (quasi-complementary) output. After buying National, TI renamed National's part LM833-N. Separately, an E2E 1443925 reply said TI's LM833 and MC33078 appear to be the same die because all specs and graphs are identical.

- **When:** July 2010 (TI's own LM833, SLOS481) / after the 2011 National acquisition, by May 2012 (ex-National part appears as LM833-N in SNOSBD8E)
- **Affected:** LM833 (TI: LM833P, LM833DR), LM833-N (LM833M/NOPB, LM833MX/NOPB, LM833N/NOPB)
- **How to tell old from new:** Orderable and datasheet. TI's own design is LM833P/LM833DR, datasheet SLOS481. The ex-National die is LM833-N (LM833M, LM833MX, LM833N with /NOPB), datasheet SNOSBD8. National-logo parts and Motorola/onsemi/ST parts are other dies.
- **Audio impact:** Different output-stage topology can change crossover and load-driving behaviour. No published audio measurements comparing the two were found.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - same pinout and similar headline specs, but a different die and output stage; circuits tuned to the National part may behave differently.
- **Confidence:** high

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Output stage topology | Complementary emitter-follower pair (NPN + vertical PNP), National LM833 / LM833-N | Quasi-complementary, two NPN devices, TI LM833 (SLOS481) |
| Gain-bandwidth (typ, headline) | 15 MHz (National LM833 / LM833-N) | 16 MHz (TI LM833, SLOS481B) |

### 2. Unclassified: Texas Instruments, Merge confirmed by TI on E2E (thread 1652528) and…

TI confirmed on its E2E forum (thread titled 'NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 all now the same die') that these parts now share one die; part numbers are unchanged. Hackaday (3 June 2026) and Headphonesty (July 2026) reported it, the latter describing the merged design as RC4580-based with NPN inputs and a complementary output stage. Earlier search summaries tie the merge to an SFAB (Sherman) to RFAB (Richardson) move (not re-confirmed). For NE5532 the documented deltas are large (±22 V to ±18 V, 9 to 5 V/us; SLOS075K). LM833-specific before/after deltas are not documented; since TI's LM833 was already ±18 V/7 V/us and reportedly already the MC33078 die, its change may be smaller than NE5532's (inference).

- **When:** Merge confirmed by TI on E2E (thread 1652528) and publicized June-July 2026; production cut-over date not documented (PCN activity 2023-2025 suspected)
- **Affected:** LM833 (TI SLOS481 orderables, e.g. LM833P, LM833DR), NE5532, MC33078, RC4580
- **How to tell old from new:** Not documented. Likely recent TI date codes; no top-marking difference or LM833 datasheet revision after SLOS481B has been confirmed. PCN 20240429005.1 / 20231114002.1 may cover it (LM833 membership unconfirmed).
- **Audio impact:** Unknown for LM833 specifically; no old-vs-new LM833 measurements found. If the merged die is RC4580-derived with a complementary output, TI LM833's former two-NPN quasi-complementary output may have changed (inference). For NE5532 in the same merge, users report changed behaviour and boards failing QA (Headphonesty).
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - same part number, new silicon; TI LM833 supply rating was already ±18 V, but input/output stage, stability and protection details may differ. Unverified for LM833.
- **Confidence:** high

### 3. Unclassified: onsemi (ON Semiconductor, ex-Motorola), Initial PCN 11528 issued 19 July 2001

The same ON Semiconductor PCN 11528 lists LM833D, LM833DR2 and LM833N for transfer from the Motorola BMC wafer fab in Mesa, AZ to ON's Tesla wafer fab in Roznov, Czech Republic. This is a same-part-number fab move of the Motorola-design LM833, distinct from the known TI-internal LM833 die issues. A later onsemi Roznov 150 mm to 200 mm wafer conversion (FPCN23597 series) was found, but no retrieved source ties it to LM833 or MC33078.

- **When:** Initial PCN 11528 issued 19 July 2001
- **Affected:** LM833D, LM833DR2, LM833N
- **How to tell old from new:** Not stated in the retrieved summary. The later Pb-free orderables are LM833DG, LM833DR2G and LM833NG. No marking rule to tell Mesa wafers from Roznov wafers was found.
- **Audio impact:** None documented. It was a same-design fab transfer.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - same design and part numbers.
- **Confidence:** medium

## Counterfeits

No LM833-specific counterfeit reports found. Generic unbranded 'LM833 LM833N' DIP listings circulate on eBay/Amazon (e.g. listings mislabelling onsemi 'LM833NG/NOPB'). Buy from authorised distributors and check that logo, orderable and date code agree (TI LM833P/LM833DR vs LM833-N LM833M/LM833N vs onsemi LM833DR2G/NCV833 vs ST). Note onsemi LM833NG/LM833DG are discontinued, so 'new' stock of them is surplus or suspect. Relabelled-die fakes are well documented for the related NE5532 family.

## Related parts and alternatives

NE5532 (onsemi or older-date-code TI stock; higher output drive), MC33078 (onsemi, the original Motorola design), LM4562 / LME49720 (National/TI high-performance audio dual), OPA1612 / OPA1602 (TI modern low-noise bipolar audio dual), onsemi LM833DR2G or ST LM833 (to avoid TI's shared die), TI LM833-N (ex-National die, if still available)

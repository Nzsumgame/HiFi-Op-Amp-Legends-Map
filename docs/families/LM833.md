# LM833 / LM833-N / LM837

**Bipolar low-noise audio dual (LM837 quad)** · National Semiconductor (by 1990s–2011) → Texas Instruments (2011–present) → Texas Instruments (cut-over undocumented; public 2026) · second sources: Motorola → onsemi, STMicroelectronics, Texas Instruments · Tier A (legend / revision hazard)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** TI confirmed on E2E (1652528) that NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 now share one RC4580-based die, part numbers unchanged, after a reported SFAB-to-RFAB move with die shrink… (+2 more) [Details](#silicon-changes)

*Cheap, quiet (4.5 nV/√Hz, 15–16 MHz, 7 V/µs) audio dual found throughout consumer and pro gear; the NE5532's sibling.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5 nV/√Hz typ | 16 MHz (TI LM833); 15 MHz typ / 10 min (National, onsemi) | 7 V/µs typ (5 V/µs min, National) | 0.002% typ | ±5 V to ±18 V (10–36 V) |

## Why it's rated

A cheap, quiet audio dual aimed at 'all preamp and high level stages in PCM and HiFi systems', common in CD players, equalizers and mic preamps. GroupDIY posters find lower distortion but less drive than the NE5532; Mithat Konar (2026) reportedly says it now out-specs TI's revised NE5532. No verified sound descriptors or measurements (Self, Groner, ASR).

**Used in:** Preamp and line stages in CD and hi-fi gear, Philips CD614 CD player (Motorola LM833N, Soomal), HiFiMan HM-601 I/V after TDA1543 DAC (TI LM833), Graphic equalizers, tone controls and mic preamps, Op-amp rolling baseline in consumer sockets

## Watch out

- TI 'LM833' and 'LM833-N' are different devices with different output stages; check logo and exact orderable.
- TI's LM833 now shares the NE5532/MC33078/RC4580 die; newer date codes may behave differently. Cut-over undocumented.
- Reported ±22→±18 V and 9→5 V/µs changes are NE5532 (SLOS075K) deltas; TI's LM833 was always ±18 V, 7 V/µs.
- Lower output drive than NE5532 (forum reports); isolate capacitive loads above 50 pF (LM833-N datasheet).
- onsemi LM833NG/LM833DG discontinued; LM833DR2G and NCV833DR2G appear active.
- **Counterfeits:** No LM833-specific counterfeit reports; unbranded DIP listings circulate (some mislabel onsemi 'LM833NG/NOPB'). Buy authorised; check logo, orderable and date code agree. 'New' onsemi LM833NG/DG stock is surplus or suspect. NE5532-family relabelled fakes are well documented.

## Silicon changes

### Second-source difference: Texas Instruments (own LM833 design) vs National Semiconductor (now sold by TI as LM833-N); onsemi (Motorola design) and other vendors also make their own LM833, 2010-07 · SLOS481 vs SNOSBD8

TI sells two different LM833 devices (E2E 430833). National's original has a complementary NPN + vertical-PNP output; TI's own 2010 LM833 uses a quasi-complementary two-NPN output and reportedly shares the MC33078 die (E2E 1443925). onsemi keeps Motorola's design. TI has sold both since acquiring National in 2011.

- **Affected:** LM833 (TI own design: LM833P, LM833DR), LM833-N (ex-National: LM833M/NOPB, LM833MX/NOPB, LM833N/NOPB), LM833 (onsemi/Motorola design, e.g. LM833DR2G)
- **How to tell:** TI design: LM833P/LM833DR (SLOS481). Ex-National: LM833M/MX/N with /NOPB (SNOSBD8, LM833-N). National, Motorola/onsemi and ST logos mark other dies.
- **Audio impact:** Different output topology can change crossover and load-driving behaviour. No published comparison measurements.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: same pinout and similar specs, but different die and output stage.
- **Confidence:** high

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Output stage topology | Complementary emitter-follower pair (NPN + vertical PNP), National LM833 / LM833-N | Quasi-complementary, two NPN devices, TI LM833 (SLOS481) |
| Gain-bandwidth (typ, headline) | 15 MHz (National LM833 / LM833-N) | 16 MHz (TI LM833, SLOS481B) |

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments, 2023-11-15 · PCN 20231114002.1

TI confirmed on E2E (1652528) that NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 now share one RC4580-based die, part numbers unchanged, after a reported SFAB-to-RFAB move with die shrink (SFAB closure 2024–2025). Publicised by Hackaday (3 Jun 2026) and Headphonesty (Jul 2026). No LM833-specific deltas documented.

- **Affected:** LM833DR (listed in PCN 20231114002.1), LM833P and other TI SLOS481 LM833 orderables (covered by the E2E die-merge statement; PCN listing not seen)
- **How to tell:** No datasheet revision after SLOS481B or marking change. PCN 20231114002.1 lists LM833DR in Group 1 (process migration, BOM options). 2024+ date codes probably merged die.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown for LM833. The two-NPN output may now be complementary (inference). NE5532 users in the same merge report changed behaviour.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: same part number, new silicon and process; stages and protection may differ (unverified for LM833).
- **Confidence:** high

### Fab / process transfer: onsemi (ON Semiconductor, ex-Motorola), 2001-07-19 · onsemi PCN 11528

ON Semiconductor PCN 11528 moves the Motorola-design LM833 from the BMC fab in Mesa, AZ to ON's Tesla fab in Roznov, Czech Republic. A later Roznov 150→200 mm conversion (IPCN/FPCN23597) is not tied to LM833.

- **Affected:** LM833D, LM833DR2, LM833N
- **How to tell:** No marking rule separates Mesa from Roznov wafers; pre-2001/2002 date codes are probably Mesa. Later Pb-free codes: LM833DG, LM833DR2G, LM833NG.
- **Audio impact:** None documented; same design moved fabs.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same design and part numbers; only process spread within limits.
- **Confidence:** high

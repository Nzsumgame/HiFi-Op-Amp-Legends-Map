# AD843

**JFET-input (CBFET) fast-settling op-amp, single** · Analog Devices (c. 1990–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*ADI's 34 MHz, 250 V/µs CBFET single. A Soomal study called it the only OPA627 rival, and smoother; Head-Fi is split.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 19 nV/√Hz | 34 MHz unity-gain bandwidth | 250 V/µs; settles to 0.01% in 135 ns | Unverified; needs ≥8.2 V (0.5 V/33 Ω), ≥12.2 V (2 V/330 Ω) | 13 mA max (10.7 mA typ in model; 11.7 mA grade models) |

## Why it's rated

A niche cult part with split opinion. A 2005 Soomal/DiyEden translation of an English headphone-amp study named it the only op-amp to rival the OPA627: smoother, slightly less resolving, with 'boldness, charm, vitality and passion', at half the price. It rated AD845 below and AD8610 above it. Head-Fi is reportedly mostly negative: harsh, bad offset, oscillation. No objective measurements found.

**Described as:** smoother than OPA627, bold, energetic (气魄、神韵、活力和热情), less resolving than OPA627, warm (2× AD843JN adapter), harsh (Head-Fi dissent)

**Used in:** Headphone amp op-amp rolling (CMoy-style single sockets), DAC output stages, two on a dual adapter, OPA627 substitute in single sockets, Maverick TubeMagic D1 op-amp rolling

## Watch out

- High quiescent current (13 mA max, about 10.7 mA typ): runs warm, poor for battery amps.
- Needs high supply (hobbyist minimums 8.2 V and 12.2 V); sound degrades as voltage drops.
- Head-Fi reports bad offset and oscillation; a 34 MHz, 250 V/µs part is layout- and decoupling-sensitive.
- Single only: dual sockets need two chips on an adapter.
- SMD version is 16-pin SOIC (R-16), not SO-8; standard SO-8 adapters do not fit.
- **Counterfeits:** No AD843 fakes reported (not specifically searched). Marketplace DIP JN/KN may be relabelled pulls. Iq should be 10–13 mA; much lower suggests a TL071-class die. Offset within 2 mV (J) or 1 mV (K). 8-pin SOIC 'AD843' is suspect.

# OPA2156 / OPA156

**36 V CMOS precision RRIO op-amp, dual** · Texas Instruments (2018–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*TI's 36 V CMOS rail-to-rail I/O precision dual: 3 nV/√Hz, 25 MHz, 40 V/µs, ±25 µV and 100 mA drive.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 nV/√Hz | 25 MHz typ | 40 V/µs typ | 36 V total |

## Why it's rated

Thin, anecdotal praise. diyAudio posters in the OPA1656 thread call its distortion "amazingly good" and better than the OPA1656 in some respects. No ASR or other published measurements. Used mostly in instrumentation (patch-clamp, DAQ) and one DIY preamp output stage. Not a higher-rail OPA1656: both are 36 V; it adds RRI, precision offset and 100 mA drive.

**Described as:** amazingly good distortion

**Used in:** Precision and instrumentation front ends (patch-clamp, DAQ), DIY audio preamp output or buffer stage, OPA1656-class swap needing RRI, low offset or 100 mA

## Watch out

- Not audio-branded; no audio THD+N measurements found.
- SMD only (SOIC-8, VSSOP-8); DIP socket rolling needs an adapter.
- Same 36 V limit as the OPA1656, so no supply-headroom advantage.
- Praise is a handful of forum remarks; tier C rests mostly on specs.
- **Counterfeits:** No reports. Modern SMD-only TI part without the cult following that drives relabeling, so risk is presumably low; buy authorized. OPA2156ID top marking is 'OP2156'. 'OPA156' hits on digchip and datasheetarchive are an unrelated JFET part.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

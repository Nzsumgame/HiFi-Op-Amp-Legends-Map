# OPA2156 / OPA156 (TI 36 V ultra-low-noise CMOS rail-to-rail I/O precision; single OPA156 unconfirmed)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

*TI 36 V CMOS rail-to-rail I/O precision dual: 3 nV/rtHz, 25 MHz, 40 V/us, ±25 uV. A few diyAudio posts rate its distortion alongside the OPA1656.*

**Technology:** CMOS-input precision op amp. The TI document title calls it 'CMOS' and DigiKey's product highlight says 'CMOS precision rail-to-rail'. It runs on 36 V total supply with rail-to-rail input and output and a 100 mA output drive. TI describes the OPA2156 as 'the first in a planned new generation of 36-V, rail-to-rail operational amplifiers'. As indexed, the datasheet title is 'OPA2156 36-V, Ultra-Low Noise, Wide-Bandwidth, Precision Rail-to-Rail Operational Amplifier'. It is not an audio-branded (Burr-Brown/SoundPlus) part.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Praise is thin, and it is attributed forum comment rather than measurement. In the diyAudio thread 'OPA1656: High-Performance CMOS Audio Op Amp', posters said the OPA2156 has 'amazingly good distortion' and looks better than the OPA1656 in certain respects. No ASR or other published measurements were found. On GitHub it shows up mostly in instrumentation (a patch-clamp amplifier and a DAQ board, where the covg_clamp BOM lists OPA1656ID as the alternate) and in one DIY audio preamp output stage (bengineer91/Preamp). The discovery note calls it a 'higher-rail alternative to the OPA1656', which is wrong. Both are 36 V parts. What the OPA2156 adds is rail-to-rail input, precision offset (±25 uV) and 100 mA drive.

**How people describe the sound:** 'amazingly good distortion' (diyAudio forum comment, unmeasured)

**Typical uses:** Precision / instrumentation front ends (patch-clamp amplifier, DAQ), DIY audio preamp output/buffer stage (one GitHub project), Candidate in place of OPA1656-class duals where rail-to-rail input, low offset or 100 mA drive is wanted

**Caveats:**

- Not an audio-branded part. No audio THD+N measurements were found.
- Only SMD packages (SOIC-8, VSSOP-8), so DIP socket rolling needs an adapter
- Same 36 V supply limit as the OPA1656 (no supply-headroom advantage)
- The praise is a handful of forum remarks. Tier C rests mostly on specs.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit reports found. It is a modern SMD-only TI part (SOIC-8/VSSOP-8) without the cult following that drives relabeling of DIP audio op-amps, so risk is presumably low. Buy from authorized distribution. alldatasheet gives the OPA2156ID top marking as 'OP2156'. Watch for the part-number collision: 'OPA156' hits on digchip and datasheetarchive point to an unrelated JFET-input op-amp document, not a single version of this TI CMOS part.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA1656 / OPA1655 (TI Burr-Brown audio CMOS; 4.5-36 V; rail-to-rail output only; 2.9 nV/rtHz at 10 kHz, 53 MHz GBW, 24 V/us, 3.9 mA/ch per SBOS901C). Pin-compatible SOIC-8 dual, listed as the OPA2156IDR alternate in the covg_clamp BOM., OPA1688 (listed as the second alternate to OPA2156IDR in the covg_clamp BOM), OPA828 / OPA2828 (TI JFET-input low-noise 36 V precision)

## Open questions

- Does the single OPA156 exist? No TI product page or orderable code was found, and the indexed datasheet title names only OPA2156.
- SBOS900 change logs for A (Dec 2018) and B (June 2019) were not retrieved. Was the original Sept 2018 issue a Product Preview?
- Which alldatasheet copy (1082239 vs 1154466) holds rev A and which holds rev B? The DigiKey HTML and datasheet4u copies have unknown revisions.
- Datasheet quiescent current per channel, supply minimum, current noise and THD+N were not retrieved. The 4.4 mA covg_clamp figure is ambiguous.
- VSSOP-8 orderable codes (OPA2156IDGKR/IDGKT?) are unconfirmed.
- No PCN or silicon-change information found. The TI PCN database was not searched directly.
- Identity of the legacy 'OPA156 JFET Input Operational Amplifiers' document on digchip/datasheetarchive is unconfirmed.
- Whether OPA2156 and OPA165x share a process platform: the consecutive literature numbers SBOS900/SBOS901 are the only hint.

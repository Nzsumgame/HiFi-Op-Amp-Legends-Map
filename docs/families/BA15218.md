# BA15218 (ROHM dual low-noise, high-slew-rate bipolar)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*ROHM's M5218-class dual op-amp (10 MHz GBW, 3 V/us). Japanese op-amp rollers say its highs sound cleaner and more extended than the M5218 or NJM4580.*

**Technology:** Monolithic dual op-amp with internal phase compensation, listed by ROHM as a low-noise, low-distortion, high-slew-rate part (bipolar; ROHM does not state the process in the search summaries). Runs from single 4-32 V or dual supplies. It is a consumer/audio catalogue part and is now end-of-life: the DIP8 version is obsolete, and the BA15218F-E2 had a Last Time Buy date of 2026-03-31.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This is a niche Japanese op-amp-rolling favourite, not a widely known audiophile legend. The C-Area wiki (atwiki) has a dedicated BA15218 listening page. In a comparison with the M5218 and NJM4580, the BA15218 was heard as having clean, open, extended highs, while the M5218 sounded slightly veiled and the NJM4580 had sluggish bass. A note.com headphone-amp write-up reports a firm, low-noise sound with solid lows. Another hobbyist comparison surfaced by search found it somewhat hard and less spacious than dedicated audio op-amps. All of these are single-listener subjective reports.

**How people describe the sound:** clear, extended highs, clean / natural, not harsh, firm, solid lows, low noise, somewhat hard, less spacious than audio-grade op-amps (one hobbyist comparison)

**Typical uses:** Japanese consumer audio equipment (M5218-class sockets), phono/RIAA and line stages (datasheet quotes RIAA noise), op-amp rolling in headphone amps and DIY kits

**Caveats:**

- EOL: the DIP8 BA15218 is obsolete, and the BA15218F-E2 LTB was 2026-03-31. Remaining supply is old stock or marketplace lots.
- The BA15218N is SIP8 and the BA15218F is SOP8. Only the plain BA15218 drops into a DIP8 socket without an adapter.
- The praise comes from a few Japanese hobbyist pages and is subjective. There are no measured audio comparisons.

## Counterfeits

No documented counterfeit reports were found. Because the part is EOL, marketplace lots (e.g. Amazon '5PCS 15218 BA15218 BA15218F BA15218F-T1 BA15218F-E2 SOP8') are of unknown provenance, and remarked or reclaimed parts are a generic risk. Buy from authorised distributors while old stock lasts.

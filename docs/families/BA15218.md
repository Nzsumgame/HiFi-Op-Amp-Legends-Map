# BA15218 / BA15218F / BA15218N

**Bipolar dual, low-noise, high slew rate** · Mitsubishi Electric (unknown) · second sources: ROHM · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*ROHM's M5218-class dual (10 MHz, 3 V/µs); Japanese rollers hear cleaner, more extended highs than M5218 or NJM4580.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 µVrms typ (RIAA-weighted) | 10 MHz typ | 3 V/µs typ | 4–32 V single; ±3 to ±16 V dual (dual limit unconfirmed) |

## Why it's rated

A niche Japanese op-amp-rolling favourite rather than a legend. The C-Area wiki heard clean, open, extended highs versus a slightly veiled M5218 and sluggish-bass NJM4580. A note.com headphone-amp test reports firm, low-noise sound. Another hobbyist found it somewhat hard and less spacious. All single-listener impressions.

**Described as:** clear, extended highs, clean, natural, firm, solid lows, low noise, somewhat hard (one report)

**Used in:** Japanese consumer audio (M5218-class sockets), Phono/RIAA and line stages, Op-amp rolling in headphone amps and kits

## Watch out

- EOL: DIP8 BA15218 obsolete, BA15218F-E2 last-time buy 2026-03-31. Remaining supply is old stock.
- BA15218N is SIP8 and BA15218F is SOP8; only the plain BA15218 fits a DIP8 socket directly.
- Praise comes from a few Japanese hobbyist pages; no measured audio comparisons exist.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit reports found. As an EOL part, marketplace lots (e.g. Amazon '5PCS 15218 BA15218 BA15218F BA15218F-T1 BA15218F-E2 SOP8') are of unknown provenance. Buy from authorised distributors while stock lasts.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

# BA15218 (ROHM dual low-noise, high-slew-rate bipolar)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

**Technology:** Bipolar dual op-amp, low noise and high slew rate, for dual supplies (ROHM audio/consumer part, now end-of-life)

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The C-Area wiki has a dedicated BA15218 listening page. It describes good high-frequency extension and a clear, natural sound without harshness. In a separate comparison against the M5218 and NJM4580, a user found the BA15218 clean with clear highs, while the M5218 sounded slightly cloudy and the NJM4580 had sluggish bass. A note.com headphone-amp write-up (ヘッドホンアンプba15218オペアンプを試しました) reports a firm, low-noise sound with solid lows.

# MUSES8820

**Bipolar-input dual audio op-amp (mass-production MUSES)** · New JRC (2010–2021) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2022–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*The 'budget MUSES02': praised for deep bass and wide stage, a common upgrade over NJM4580 and NE5532.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5 nV/√Hz typ | 11 MHz (unconfirmed) | 5 V/µs typ | ±3.5 V to ±16 V | 8 mA (unconfirmed) |

## Why it's rated

Nisshinbo says it carries the MUSES01/02 sound concept, with layout and lead frame tuned for mass production. Japanese reviewers hear MUSES02-like sound with thinner lines, deep bass and softened top treble. Head-Fi posters call it the 'least opamp-sounding', tube-like and wide-staged. Rankings place it above NJM4580/NJM8801, below newer or JFET parts.

**Described as:** deep, full bass, wide soundstage, crisp, forward, MUSES02-like, thinner lines, least op-amp-sounding, tube-like, softened extreme treble, warm (vs LT1364)

**Used in:** Drop-in upgrade for NJM4580/NE5532 sockets in DACs and preamps, Op-amp rolling in socketed headphone amps and DACs, DIY preamp, line and LPF stages, OEM: TEAC AP-507, Ibanez BT Mini, VentureCraft VALOQ

## Watch out

- Praise is subjective: no ASR or Samuel Groner bench data, and Head-Fi round-up figures not captured.
- The 'least opamp-sounding / almost OPA627' quotes may come from the Opamp thread, not the Burson round-up.
- Bipolar input: 100 nA typ / 500 nA max bias, far above FET parts; mind high source impedances.
- Output drive and stability not yet checked; read the datasheet before driving headphones directly.
- One Head-Fi poster heard no difference from MUSES02 in an LPF position.
- **Counterfeits:** No MUSES8820 fake documented yet, but MUSES01/02 and MUSES8920D fakes exist. Buy from DigiKey, Mouser, Akizuki, Kyohritsu or Marutsu; distrust prices far below ~¥1,100 (MUSES8820D); compare marking and lot code; measure bias or supply current.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

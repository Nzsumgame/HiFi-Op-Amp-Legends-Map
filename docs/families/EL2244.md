# EL2244 / EL2444 (Elantec/Intersil dual/quad high-speed video op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

**Technology:** Bipolar voltage-feedback, unity-gain-stable low-power high-speed video op-amp in dual (EL2244) and quad (EL2444) versions. Exact GBW and slew rate need datasheet confirmation; recalled as a roughly 60 MHz class part.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A fixture of Chinese sound-card and car-head-unit 'polishing' (打磨/摩机) guides. It is described with the AD827 as among the top voltage-feedback dual op-amps then on the market, with a neutral tone, a wide soundstage (slightly wider than the OP275) and strong high frequencies and resolution. The same guides criticise a thin midrange, light bass and a bland musicality, and call it hard to use well. It appears in the Baidu Tieba '40款音频运算放大器主观分析评价' list, the PCPOP modding guide, a 360doc 各款运放音质比较 comparison and a sound-card op-amp tutorial. It also appears in the Korean digipine 'OP Amp의 음색 정리 자료' tone summary, so the evidence spans CN and KR.

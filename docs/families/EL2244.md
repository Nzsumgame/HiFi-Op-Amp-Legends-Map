# EL2244 / EL2444 (Elantec/Intersil dual/quad high-speed video op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*Elantec's dual 120 MHz, 325 V/us video op-amp that Chinese and Korean op-amp-swap guides rank beside the AD827 for neutral, wide, detailed sound.*

**Technology:** Voltage-feedback, unity-gain-stable, low-power high-speed op-amp built on Elantec's proprietary complementary bipolar process. The EL2244 (dual) and EL2444 (quad) are multi-channel versions of the single EL2044. They have a 120 MHz GBW, 325 V/us slew rate and 5.2 mA per amplifier.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This summary comes from the earlier discovery sweep and was not re-checked in this pass. Chinese sound-card and car-head-unit modding (摩机) guides, including the Baidu Tieba 40-op-amp list, PCPOP, 360doc and book118, rank it with the AD827 among the best voltage-feedback duals. They describe a neutral tone, a soundstage slightly wider than the OP275, and strong treble and resolution. The same guides criticise a thin midrange, light bass and bland musicality, and call it hard to use well. A Korean digipine tone summary also lists it. All of this is subjective forum and guide opinion.

**How people describe the sound:** neutral, wide soundstage, strong treble / high resolution, thin midrange (criticism), light bass (criticism), bland musicality (criticism)

**Typical uses:** op-amp swaps in PC sound cards, car head-unit modding (摩机), DIY I/V and buffer stages in CN/KR hobby builds

**Caveats:**

- It is a 120 MHz, 325 V/us video op-amp, so tight decoupling and a careful layout are needed. Oscillation risk in sockets and adapters fits the guides' 'hard to use well' remark.
- Input noise of 15 nV/rtHz is high compared with dedicated audio op-amps.
- The praise is subjective and comes from CN/KR modding sources only.
- The part is probably obsolete, so it is supplied mainly from old or broker stock.

## Counterfeits

This check found no specific counterfeit or remarking reports. The part is old and probably obsolete, and CN audio-mod buyers get it mainly from brokers and the secondary market. Treat DIP and SO stock with unverified provenance, including Elantec-marked parts, as at risk of being remarked or relabelled. Pb-free 'Z' parts are a separate part number, not a die change.

## Related parts and alternatives

EL2044 (single-channel member of the same family), AD827 (dual high-speed video op-amp the guides rank alongside it), OP275 (the soundstage reference in the CN guides)

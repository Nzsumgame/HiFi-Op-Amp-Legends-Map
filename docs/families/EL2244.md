# EL2244 / EL2444 (Elantec/Intersil dual/quad high-speed video op-amp)

Elantec Semiconductor (by 1995 (Rev C dated Dec 1995) to 2002) → Renesas Electronics (2017-present) · second sources: Intersil · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Elantec's dual 120 MHz, 325 V/us video op-amp that Chinese and Korean op-amp-swap guides rank beside the AD827 for neutral, wide, detailed sound.*

| Slew rate | Supply current |
|---|---|
| 325 V/us | 5.2 mA per amplifier |

## Why it's rated

This summary comes from the earlier discovery sweep and was not re-checked in this pass. Chinese sound-card and car-head-unit modding (摩机) guides, including the Baidu Tieba 40-op-amp list, PCPOP, 360doc and book118, rank it with the AD827 among the best voltage-feedback duals. They describe a neutral tone, a soundstage slightly wider than the OP275, and strong treble and resolution. The same guides criticise a thin midrange, light bass and bland musicality, and call it hard to use well. A Korean digipine tone summary also lists it. All of this is subjective forum and guide opinion.

**Described as:** neutral, wide soundstage, strong treble / high resolution, thin midrange (criticism), light bass (criticism), bland musicality (criticism)

**Used in:** op-amp swaps in PC sound cards, car head-unit modding (摩机), DIY I/V and buffer stages in CN/KR hobby builds

## Watch out

- It is a 120 MHz, 325 V/us video op-amp, so tight decoupling and a careful layout are needed. Oscillation risk in sockets and adapters fits the guides' 'hard to use well' remark.
- Input noise of 15 nV/rtHz is high compared with dedicated audio op-amps.
- The praise is subjective and comes from CN/KR modding sources only.
- The part is probably obsolete, so it is supplied mainly from old or broker stock.
- **Counterfeits:** This check found no specific counterfeit or remarking reports. The part is old and probably obsolete, and CN audio-mod buyers get it mainly from brokers and the secondary market. Treat DIP and SO stock with unverified provenance, including Elantec-marked parts, as at risk of being remarked or relabelled. Pb-free 'Z' parts are a separate part number, not a die change.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

<summary>Verification and open questions</summary>

Status: **verified-with-corrections**, 5 web searches.

**Confirmed:**

- The Intersil datasheet is FN7059, titled 'EL2244, EL2444 Dual/Quad Low-Power 120MHz Unity-Gain Stable Op Amp'; a FN7059.2 copy exists (http://www.manuallib.com/download/pdf14/INTER-IL-EL2244-EL2444-DATA-SHEET.PDF)
- FN7059.1 is dated July 14, 2004 with copyright Intersil 2002-2004 (https://datasheetspdf.com/pdf/1437145/Intersil/EL2244/1)
- It is the dual/quad version of the EL2044 on Elantec's complementary bipolar process: 325 V/us, 120 MHz GBW, 5.2 mA per amplifier (https://datasheetspdf.com/pdf/1437145/Intersil/EL2244/1)
- It runs on a single supply from 2.5 V to 36 V (https://datasheetspdf.com/pdf/1437145/Intersil/EL2244/1)
- An Elantec EL2244C/EL2444C datasheet Rev C dated December 1995 exists (https://www.sm0vpo.com/_pdf/amplifiers/EL2244.PDF)
- Input noise is 15 nV/rtHz at 10 kHz, 25 C (https://www.sm0vpo.com/_pdf/amplifiers/EL2244.PDF)
- FN7059.2 lists the Pb-free EL2244CSZ, EL2244CSZ-T13 and EL2444CSZ (http://www.manuallib.com/download/pdf14/INTER-IL-EL2244-EL2444-DATA-SHEET.PDF)
- The Elantec ordering codes EL2244CN, EL2244CS, EL2444CS and EL2444CN exist (https://www.datasheetq.com/EL2244CN-doc-Elantec, https://www.alldatasheet.com/datasheet-pdf/pdf/59896/ELANTEC/EL2244CS.html, https://pdf.datasheet.live/69509328/intersil.com/EL2444CN.html)

- Added the Pb-free part numbers EL2244CSZ, EL2244CSZ-T13 and EL2444CSZ, plus EL2444CN and EL2444CS.

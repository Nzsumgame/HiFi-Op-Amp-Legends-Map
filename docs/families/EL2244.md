# EL2244 / EL2444

**High-speed VFB video op-amp, 2/4 channels** · Elantec (by 1995–2002) → Intersil (2002–2017) → Renesas (2017–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Elantec's 120 MHz, 325 V/µs video dual, ranked beside the AD827 by CN/KR swap guides for neutral, detailed sound.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 nV/√Hz (at 10 kHz) | 120 MHz typ | 325 V/µs typ | 2.5 V to 36 V single (about ±1.25 V to ±18 V) | 5.2 mA per amplifier typ |

## Why it's rated

Chinese sound-card and car head-unit modding (摩机) guides (Baidu Tieba's 40-op-amp list, PCPOP, 360doc, book118) rank it with the AD827 among the best voltage-feedback duals: neutral, a soundstage slightly wider than OP275, strong treble and resolution. They fault its thin mids, light bass and bland musicality, and call it hard to use well. Korea's digipine also lists it.

**Described as:** neutral, wide soundstage, strong treble, high resolution, thin midrange, light bass, bland musicality

**Used in:** Op-amp swaps in PC sound cards, Car head-unit modding (摩机), DIY I/V and buffer stages (CN/KR builds)

## Watch out

- 120 MHz, 325 V/µs video part: needs tight decoupling and layout; may oscillate in sockets.
- 15 nV/√Hz input noise is high next to dedicated audio op-amps.
- Praise is subjective and comes only from CN/KR modding sources.
- Probably obsolete; supply is mainly old or broker stock.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit reports found. Old and probably obsolete, it reaches CN mod buyers via brokers, so treat DIP and SO stock of unverified provenance, Elantec-marked included, as possibly remarked. Pb-free 'Z' parts are a new number, not a die change.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

# NJM8901 / NJM8901E (New JRC entry-tier JFET-input audio dual)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*Entry-tier New JRC JFET-input audio dual (13 nV/√Hz, 20 V/µs) that applies MUSES know-how to mass-production process; one blog review calls it easy to listen to, with light bass.*

**Technology:** JFET-input (BiFET) dual audio op-amp. Distributor and vendor copy says it balances MUSES-series technology against normal mass-production techniques, reaching audio quality at controlled cost through 'original process tuning and assembly technology'. stereo.jp's report on a visit to JRC puts it in the entry tier with NJM8801 and NJM4582, below the mass-production MUSES (MUSES8820 / 8920 / 8832) and the flagship MUSES01 / 02 / 03. The low input bias current of the JFET inputs suits it to I/V (transimpedance) converters. Compensation, lead-frame and die details are not documented in any source that was seen.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche Japanese-market part with little coverage, not a recognised 'legend'. Japanese retailers market it as a Hi-Fi op-amp (Akizuki: '2回路入HiFiオペアンプ'), and one auction seller calls it 'MUSESの量産型'. The only listening report is the guiblo blog comparison (2023). It describes NJM8901E as easy to listen to with light bass, and MUSES8820D as giving noticeably deeper, weightier bass. A Japanese YouTube op-amp comparison reportedly includes it, but its content was not verified. No bench measurements were found.

**How people describe the sound:** light / lean bass (低音があっさり目), from guiblo only, easy to listen to (聴きやすい), from guiblo only, less bass weight than MUSES8820D, from guiblo only

**Typical uses:** op-amp rolling in DACs and headphone amplifiers, the guiblo context (needs an SOP8-to-DIP8 adapter), I/V converter, preamp, active-filter and line-amp stages (the application list in the vendor and retailer copy), budget step up from generic NJM4580 / NJM4558-class duals (inferred from the entry-tier positioning, not from a user report)

**Caveats:**

- Evidence is thin: one subjective, unblinded blog comparison and a YouTube shoot-out that was not checked. No measurements were found.
- The sound descriptors come from a single listener.
- NJM8901E is SMD (EMP8 = SOP8 150 mil), so DIP8 sockets need an adapter.
- Typical offset is 2 mV and the maximum is 10 mV. That is high for DC-coupled stages, so use coupling capacitors or a DC servo where needed.
- Supply is ±4 V minimum, so the part is not suited to low-voltage single-cell rails.
- Lifecycle is uncertain: one distributor (Profusion) flags NJM8901E-TE2 as obsolete.

## Counterfeits

No NJM8901-specific counterfeit reports were found, but no search targeted counterfeits directly. It is a low-volume Japanese-market part. It turns up at broker and secondary-market sites (veswin, censtry, ovaga), and a distributor flags the reel code as obsolete, so buy from authorised channels (Mouser, chip1stop, Akizuki, Kyohritsu eleshop) where possible. Treat the absence of reports as unknown, not as evidence the part is clean.

## Open questions

- The datasheet version history is unknown. No copy's 'Ver.' date was seen. Check the Mouser mirror (filename 'DataSheetE3'), the sekorm and datasheet4u copies, and the Nisshinbo PDF to build the revision list and to see whether any version changes specs or package.
- The official Nisshinbo datasheet and product-page URLs were not seen in the search results. Every Nisshinbo, njr.co.jp and Kyohritsu eclib URL in archive_seed_urls is inferred from sibling-part patterns.
- Lifecycle: Profusion flags NJM8901E-TE2 as Obsolete. A Japanese blog (foxtango101) reports that several ex-JRC op-amps were designated 保守品 (maintenance / planned discontinuation), but whether NJM8901 is among them was not confirmed. Check Nisshinbo's discontinued / maintenance list.
- Launch date is unknown. An onlinecomponents copy of the 2011 NJR short-form catalogue appeared in one search, but whether it lists NJM8901 was not confirmed.
- Does a DIP8 NJM8901D, or any other package, exist? Only NJM8901E (EMP8) was seen.
- Is the 4 / 6 mA supply current per device or per channel, and what are the test conditions (e.g. ±15 V, f = 1 kHz) for noise and THD?
- No PCN, die change or marking change was found. Did the JRC-to-Nisshinbo transition bring a fab move for this part?
- Is NL8902 positioned as NJM8901's replacement? Do MUSES8920A and MUSES8921 exist as described?

# NJM8901 / NJM8901E

**JFET-input (BiFET) audio op-amp, dual** · New Japan Radio (JRC/NJR) (launch date unknown–2021) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2022–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Entry-tier JRC JFET dual (13 nV/√Hz, 20 V/µs) using MUSES know-how; reportedly easy-listening with light bass.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 nV/√Hz | 5 MHz typ (open-loop gain 86 dB min / 110 dB typ) | 20 V/µs | 0.003% (THD) | ±4.0 V to ±18 V | 4 mA typ / 6 mA max |

## Why it's rated

A niche Japanese-market part, not a legend. Akizuki sells it as a 'HiFi op-amp'; one auction seller calls it 'mass-production MUSES'. The only listening report, a 2023 guiblo blog comparison, finds it easy to listen to with light bass, while MUSES8820D gives deeper, weightier bass. A Japanese YouTube comparison reportedly includes it. No measurements found.

**Described as:** light bass (低音があっさり目), easy listening (聴きやすい), less weight than MUSES8820D

**Used in:** Op-amp rolling in DACs and headphone amps (needs adapter), I/V converters, thanks to low JFET bias current, Preamp, active-filter and line-amp stages, Budget step up from NJM4580/NJM4558-class duals (inferred)

## Watch out

- Thin evidence: one unblinded blog comparison and an unchecked YouTube shoot-out; descriptors come from a single listener.
- NJM8901E is SMD (EMP8 = SOP8 150 mil); DIP8 sockets need an adapter.
- Offset 2 mV typ, 10 mV max: use coupling capacitors or a DC servo in DC-coupled stages.
- Needs at least ±4 V supply; unsuited to low-voltage rails.
- Lifecycle uncertain: Profusion flags NJM8901E-TE2 obsolete.
- **Counterfeits:** No NJM8901-specific counterfeit reports found, though none were sought directly. Low-volume part seen at brokers (veswin, censtry, ovaga); the reel code is flagged obsolete. Buy from Mouser, chip1stop, Akizuki or Kyohritsu eleshop.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

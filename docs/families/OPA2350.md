# OPA350 / OPA2350 / OPA4350 (Burr-Brown CMOS rail-to-rail single-supply)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

**Technology:** CMOS, rail-to-rail input and output, 2.7-5.5 V single supply, 38 MHz, 22 V/us, 5 nV/rtHz

## Why enthusiasts rate it

In nabe's Japanese Chu-Moy and low-voltage headphone-amp writing (オペアンプの仕様と音質の謎, 高品質な低電圧ヘッドホンアンプ), the OPA2350 is described as having a thick sound (音に厚みがあり) with solid bass (低音もしっかりしてる) and a 'BB-like' character. It is said to have more body than the AD822, to be usable even without an output buffer, and to be a recommended DIP option for 5 V CMoy builds. It also comes up in the kakaku.com mini headphone-amp thread (ミニミニHPAと3Vのミニアンプ). Its sibling OPA2353 was praised for soundstage and imaging in a Japanese CMoy-like build.

# OPA350 / OPA2350 / OPA4350

**CMOS rail-to-rail single-supply op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · Burr-Brown (1998–2000) → Texas Instruments (2000–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Burr-Brown 38 MHz rail-to-rail CMOS amp for 5 V rails; Japanese CMoy builders call it thick and 'BB-like'.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 nV/√Hz HF spot (reportedly 100 kHz); 1 kHz likely higher | 38 MHz | 22 V/µs | 0.0006% | 2.7–5.5 V single supply (fully specified) | ~5.2 mA per amplifier (unconfirmed) |

## Why it's rated

Praise comes mainly from Japanese hobbyists. nabe's blog calls the OPA2350 thick (音に厚み), with solid bass and a 'BB-like' character, fuller than the AD822, and a recommended DIP pick for 5 V CMoy builds, even without an output buffer. It also appears in a kakaku.com thread on 3 V headphone amps. Western audiophile coverage is thin.

**Described as:** thick (音に厚み), full-bodied, solid bass, 'BB-like' character

**Used in:** 5 V and battery CMoy-style headphone amplifiers, Low-voltage portable headphone amps, even without output buffer, Single-supply audio processing and ADC driving (TI)

## Watch out

- Absolute maximum supply is about 5.5 V: a ±12 V or ±15 V socket will destroy it.
- Praise is subjective and comes from a few Japanese hobbyist sources.
- The 5 nV/√Hz headline is a high-frequency figure; CMOS audio-band noise is likely higher.
- Rail-to-rail input uses complementary pairs; behaviour can shift in the input crossover region.
- Fairly high quiescent current for battery use (about 5 mA per amplifier, unconfirmed).
- **Counterfeits:** No family-specific counterfeit reports. Take usual care with DIP-8 OPA2350PA from marketplace sellers: a remarked non-rail-to-rail part shows poor output swing near the rails at 5 V.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

# OPA350 / OPA2350 / OPA4350 (Burr-Brown CMOS rail-to-rail single-supply)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

*Burr-Brown 38 MHz rail-to-rail CMOS op-amp for 5 V rails. Japanese low-voltage CMoy builders say the OPA2350 sounds thick and 'BB-like', with solid bass.*

**Technology:** CMOS, rail-to-rail input and output (output within 10 mV of the rails), unity-gain stable, 2.7-5.5 V single supply, 38 MHz GBW, 22 V/us, 5 nV/rtHz (high-frequency spot figure)

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Praise for this family comes mainly from Japanese hobbyist writing. On nabe's blog (nabe.adiary.jp), in the op-amp spec/sound list and the low-voltage headphone-amp articles, the OPA2350 is said to sound thick (音に厚みがあり), with solid bass and a 'BB-like' character. The blog says it has more body than the AD822, works even without an output buffer, and is a recommended DIP choice for 5 V CMoy-style builds. It also comes up in a kakaku.com thread on small 3 V headphone amps. The OPA2353 praise cited here is for the sibling part, not the OPA2350. Western audiophile coverage is thin.

**How people describe the sound:** thick / full-bodied (音に厚み), solid bass, 'BB-like' character

**Typical uses:** 5 V / battery CMoy-style headphone amplifiers, low-voltage portable headphone amps (per nabe, can run without an output buffer), single-supply audio processing and ADC driving (TI's stated applications)

**Caveats:**

- The absolute maximum supply is only about 5.5 V. Do not drop it into a +/-12 V or +/-15 V op-amp socket, where it will be destroyed.
- The praise is subjective and comes from a few Japanese hobbyist sources.
- The 5 nV/rtHz headline is a high-frequency figure. Being CMOS, noise in the audio band is likely higher.
- Rail-to-rail input uses complementary input pairs, so behavior can change in the input crossover region. See the datasheet.
- Quiescent current is relatively high for battery use (about 5 mA per amplifier, unconfirmed).

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit reports specific to this family were found. Take generic care with DIP-8 OPA2350PA bought from marketplace sellers. A remarked non-rail-to-rail part would show up as poor output swing near the rails at 5 V.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA2353 (lower-power CMOS rail-to-rail sibling, also praised in Japanese CMoy builds), OPA2354 (TI 100 MHz rail-to-rail CMOS, 2.5-5.5 V), AD822 (JFET-input single-supply dual; nabe's comparison point)

## Open questions

- Introduction year of the OPA350 family and any pre-September-2000 Burr-Brown datasheet (pre-SBOS numbering) were not found.
- The date of SBOS099B and the change lists for revs A-D were not found. It is also unknown which mirror holds rev A (July 2001).
- It is not established whether any TI RFAB fab-transfer or die-revision PCN covers OPA350/OPA2350/OPA4350. Such PCNs exist for other TI amplifiers (2021-2023), but none was linked to this family.
- Lifecycle status of OPA350, OPA4350 and of the DIP OPA2350PA is not confirmed; only the OPA2350 base part was confirmed ACTIVE.
- Check the frequency behind the 5 nV/rtHz figure (believed 100 kHz), the noise at 1 kHz, and quiescent current against SBOS099D.

# NJM2043 / NJM2043DD (New JRC low-noise dual, NJM4558 lineage)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** General-purpose baselines

*Low-noise NJM4558 derivative with twice the output current. The DD rank is noise-screened for RIAA use, and one Japanese listener praised its clear treble.*

**Technology:** Bipolar dual op-amp built on New JRC's planar epitaxial process. It is an NJM4558 derivative with improved input and output stages, giving lower noise, about 2x the output current (25 mA), 6 V/us slew rate and 14 MHz unity-gain bandwidth. Marketed as a 'Low-noise dual pre-amplifier'.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The praise comes mainly from one Japanese enthusiast listening comparison (C-Area @WIKI). It rated the NJM2043DD the most pleasing of the NJM4558-lineage op-amps tested, noting unusually clear treble for a 4558-type and a fairly flat balance that suited classical music. Akizuki markets it as a low-noise-selected, improved 4558. Other opinions differ: a Zigsow user review is titled 'コレジャナイ音質' ('not the sound I wanted').

**How people describe the sound:** clear treble, unusual for a 4558-type (one reviewer), relatively flat balance, suited to classical music (one reviewer)

**Typical uses:** Phono/RIAA and other pre-amplifier stages (the DD rank is noise-graded under RIAA conditions), Tone-control and line stages in legacy Japanese audio gear, DIP8 op-amp rolling in DIY audio

**Caveats:**

- The praise rests mostly on one hobbyist listening test; others disagree (Zigsow review)
- Discontinued; buy from reputable stock (e.g. Akizuki) rather than anonymous marketplaces
- The DD rank differs from the D only by noise screening, not by design

## Counterfeits

No part-specific counterfeit reports were found. The part is discontinued (DIP8 NJM2043D/DD still circulate on eBay, Reverb and brokers), so relabelled 4558-type parts are a plausible risk. The DD low-noise rank uses the same die as the D and can only be checked by measuring noise, so a remarked D sold as DD would be undetectable by inspection. Prefer known retail stock such as Akizuki.

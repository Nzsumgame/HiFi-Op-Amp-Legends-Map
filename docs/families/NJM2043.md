# NJM2043 / NJM2043DD (New JRC low-noise dual, NJM4558 lineage)

New Japan Radio (New JRC) (unknown introduction (datasheet Ver.2003-03-17 is the latest dated version found) to 2021) → New Japan Radio (New JRC) (notice dated 2018-12-20) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2022-) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Low-noise NJM4558 derivative with twice the output current. The DD rank is noise-screened for RIAA use, and one Japanese listener praised its clear treble.*

| Slew rate |
|---|
| 6 V/us typ |

## Why it's rated

The praise comes mainly from one Japanese enthusiast listening comparison (C-Area @WIKI). It rated the NJM2043DD the most pleasing of the NJM4558-lineage op-amps tested, noting unusually clear treble for a 4558-type and a fairly flat balance that suited classical music. Akizuki markets it as a low-noise-selected, improved 4558. Other opinions differ: a Zigsow user review is titled 'コレジャナイ音質' ('not the sound I wanted').

**Described as:** clear treble, unusual for a 4558-type (one reviewer), relatively flat balance, suited to classical music (one reviewer)

**Used in:** Phono/RIAA and other pre-amplifier stages (the DD rank is noise-graded under RIAA conditions), Tone-control and line stages in legacy Japanese audio gear, DIP8 op-amp rolling in DIY audio

## Watch out

- The praise rests mostly on one hobbyist listening test; others disagree (Zigsow review)
- Discontinued; buy from reputable stock (e.g. Akizuki) rather than anonymous marketplaces
- The DD rank differs from the D only by noise screening, not by design
- **Counterfeits:** No part-specific counterfeit reports were found. The part is discontinued (DIP8 NJM2043D/DD still circulate on eBay, Reverb and brokers), so relabelled 4558-type parts are a plausible risk. The DD low-noise rank uses the same die as the D and can only be checked by measuring noise, so a remarked D sold as DD would be undetectable by inspection. Prefer known retail stock such as Akizuki.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

<summary>Verification and open questions</summary>

Status: **verified-with-corrections**, 6 web searches.

**Confirmed:**

- The English datasheet is 'NJM2043 LOW-NOISE DUAL PRE-AMPLIFIER', Ver.2003-03-17 (https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/294/NJM2043_E-218482.pdf)
- It is a bipolar low-noise version of the NJM4558, with 6 V/us slew rate and 14 MHz unity-gain bandwidth, on New JRC's planar epitaxial process (https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/294/NJM2043_E-218482.pdf)
- Operating voltage is +/-4 V to +/-22 V and output current is 25 mA (https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/294/NJM2043_E-218482.pdf)
- Packages are DIP8/DMP8/SIP8 as NJM2043D/M/L (https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/294/NJM2043_E-218482.pdf)
- NJM2043DD is the low-noise selected version, with VNI <= 1.4 uV (Rs=2.2 kOhm, RIAA); the NJM4558 input/output stages were improved and output current is about 2x (https://akizukidenshi.com/catalog/g/g102369/)
- The NJM2043D and NJM2043DD are listed as discontinued under Nisshinbo Micro Devices (https://octopart.com/part/nisshinbo-micro-devices-inc/NJM2043DD, https://www.onlinecomponents.com/en/productdetail/nisshinbo-micro-devices-inc/njm2043d-11971622.html)
- New JRC issued an EOL notice dated 2018-12-20 on terminating its 4-inch wafer line (https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/739/181220_EOL.pdf). Whether NJM2043 is on its list was not directly confirmed.
- The C-Area @WIKI review praises the NJM2043DD for clear treble and a flat balance suited to classical music (https://w.atwiki.jp/higemouse/pages/50.html)
- A dissenting Zigsow user review exists, titled 'コレジャナイ音質' (https://zigsow.jp/item/326108/review/325414)

**Corrections:**

- Channels changed from 0 to 2 for all part numbers (dual op-amp).
- Statuses changed from 'unknown' to discontinued. Distributors list the NJM2043D and NJM2043DD as discontinued.
- NJM2043DD is the D-rank low-noise selected DIP8 version (VNI <= 1.4 uV, Rs=2.2 kOhm, RIAA), not just a package code.
- Added part numbers NJM2043D (DIP8), NJM2043M (DMP8) and NJM2043L (SIP8), plus NJM2043S (unconfirmed broker listing).
- Filled in the technology, key specs, datasheet (NJM2043_E Ver.2003-03-17), lineage and alternatives, which were empty in the draft.
- Named the manufacturers separately: New Japan Radio as originator, Nisshinbo Micro Devices as successor.

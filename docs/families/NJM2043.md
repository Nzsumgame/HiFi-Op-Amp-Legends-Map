# NJM2043 / NJM2043DD

**Bipolar dual, low-noise preamp (NJM4558 derivative)** · New JRC (unknown–2021) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2022–) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Low-noise NJM4558 derivative with twice the output current; the noise-screened DD rank targets RIAA preamps.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ 1.4 µV max equivalent input noise (DD rank) | 14 MHz typ (unity-gain bandwidth) | 6 V/µs typ | ±4 V to ±22 V |

## Why it's rated

Praise comes mainly from one Japanese listening comparison (C-Area @WIKI), which rated the NJM2043DD the most pleasing NJM4558-lineage part tested: unusually clear treble for a 4558-type and a fairly flat balance suited to classical. Akizuki sells it as an improved, noise-selected 4558. A Zigsow review disagrees: "コレジャナイ音質" ("not the sound I wanted").

**Described as:** clear treble, relatively flat balance, suits classical music

**Used in:** Phono/RIAA and other preamp stages, Tone-control and line stages in Japanese gear, DIP8 op-amp rolling in DIY audio

## Watch out

- Praise rests mostly on one hobbyist listening test; a Zigsow reviewer disagrees.
- Discontinued: buy reputable stock such as Akizuki, not anonymous marketplaces.
- The DD rank differs from the D only by noise screening, not design.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific reports, but relabelled 4558-type parts are a plausible risk now it is discontinued. DD shares the D's die, so a remarked D sold as DD is only detectable by measuring noise. Prefer known retail stock such as Akizuki.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

# ADA4627-1 / ADA4637-1 (ADI single JFET-input; ADA4637-1 decompensated)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** JFET-input

*ADI's 2009 JFET-input single (6.1 nV/rtHz, 19 MHz), often suggested as an OPA627 alternative. ADI's own AN-1429 names it among op amps 'reputable with music fans'.*

**Technology:** JFET-input single op amp. ADI calls the ADA4637-1 'a decompensated version of the ADA4627-1', stable at a noise gain of 5 or greater. The ADA4627-1 is therefore the unity-gain-stable member (inferred from that wording). Title history: the ADA4627-1 product page is titled '36 V, 19 MHz, Low Noise, Low Bias Current, JFET Op Amp'. An early ADA4637 datasheet copy is titled '36 V, 80 MHz, Low Noise, Low Bias Current, JFET Op Amp'. The current combined datasheet (Rev. F) is titled '30 V, High Speed, Low Noise, Low Bias Current, JFET Operational Amplifier'. The process technology was not verified.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

ADI's application note AN-1429 lists the ADA4627-1, alongside the AD797, OP275 and AD8620, as ADI op amps 'reputable with music fans'. That is vendor marketing, not a measurement. Enthusiast material carried over from the discovery notes was not re-verified: a diyAudio thread titled 'ADA4627 outperforms Burr-Brown's OPA627' calls the sound refined and dynamic, and a Japanese Dr.DAC3 comparison (Shirokuma) reported less treble harshness than the OPA627. The ADA4637-1 is praised for DAC I/V stages. No independent distortion measurements (Groner, ASR) were found.

**How people describe the sound:** more refined (forum), dynamic (forum), 'everything sounds BIGGER' (mic preamp, forum), less treble harshness than the OPA627 (Japanese blog), clean (forum)

**Typical uses:** OPA627 alternative in single-op-amp positions (needs an SOIC-to-DIP adapter), DAC I/V conversion (ADA4637-1 only at a noise gain of 5 or greater), microphone preamplifiers, op-amp rolling in DAC and headphone gear (e.g. Dr.DAC3), low-bias-current transimpedance stages (e.g. open-hardware STM tunnelling amps using the ADA4637-1ARZ)

**Caveats:**

- The ADA4637-1 is decompensated (stable only at a noise gain of 5 or greater). It is not a drop-in for unity-gain buffers or low-gain positions.
- Single channel only; dual sockets need an adapter.
- SOIC-8 and LFCSP-8 only, with no DIP. The LFCSP is impractical for hand-rolling.
- The sonic claims are subjective forum and blog listening and were not re-verified in this pass.
- The AN-1429 praise is vendor marketing.
- No independent measurement data was found.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Analog Devices: PCN 14_0038 Rev. -, 2014-02-13 (date per search summary)

Assembly-site transfer for 3x3 mm LFCSP products to Amkor Philippines. This is an assembly change, not a die change. The search engine surfaced it for an ADA4627-1ARZ/ADA4637-1ARZ query, but inclusion of these parts is not confirmed, and it applies to LFCSP only (not SOIC ARZ/BRZ).

- **When:** PCN 14_0038 Rev. -, 2014-02-13 (date per search summary)
- **Affected:** ADA4627-1ACPZ-R2/-R7/-RL (if listed; unconfirmed), ADA4637-1ACPZ-R2/-R7/-RL (if listed; unconfirmed)
- **How to tell old from new:** After the change, parts carry a laser-marked pin-1 indicator. Country of origin and date code would separate assembly sites. Not verified per part.
- **Audio impact:** None expected: same die, package assembly only.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - assembly-only change, and only on the LFCSP, which is rarely used for rolling.
- **Confidence:** low

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit reports were found; counterfeits were not searched this pass because of the search budget. The claim that the community prefers the part because, unlike the OPA627, it is not faked is low-confidence folklore. Buy from ADI or authorised distributors. Be wary of pre-mounted 'ADA4627 on DIP adapter' marketplace modules whose die provenance cannot be checked.

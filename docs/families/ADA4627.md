# ADA4627-1 / ADA4637-1 (ADI single JFET-input; ADA4637-1 decompensated)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** JFET-input

*ADI's 2009 JFET-input single, often suggested as an OPA627 alternative. ADI's own AN-1429 names the ADA4627-1 among its op amps 'reputable with music fans'.*

**Technology:** JFET-input single op amp. ADI's SPICE macro-models use a JFET input pair. The ADA4627-1 is unity-gain stable (from discovery notes; not re-verified). The ADA4637-1 is decompensated: the ADI model header says 'Operate at a noise gain >5'. The earliest ADI title, as copied into the Eagle library in 2009, was '36 V, 19 MHz, Low Noise, Low Bias Current, JFET Operational Amplifier'. A later title, shown in a KiCad library, is '30 V, High Speed, Low Noise, Low Bias Current, JFET Operational Amplifier'. The ADI model 'Generic Desc' gives '8/30V'. The process technology was not verified.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

ADI's own application note AN-1429 (David Guo) lists the ADA4627-1, alongside the AD797, OP275 and AD8620, as ADI op amps 'reputable with music fans'. That is a vendor marketing statement, not a measurement. Enthusiast material carried over from the discovery notes, not re-verified in this pass, includes a long diyAudio thread titled 'ADA4627 outperforms Burr-Brown's OPA627'. It describes the sound as refined and dynamic; one mic-preamp user said 'everything sounds BIGGER'. A Japanese Dr.DAC3 comparison (Shirokuma) found less treble harshness than the OPA627. The ADA4637-1 is praised for DAC I/V stages. No independent distortion measurements (Groner, ASR) were found.

**How people describe the sound:** more refined (forum), dynamic (forum), 'everything sounds BIGGER' (mic preamp, forum), less treble harshness than the OPA627 (Japanese blog), clean (forum)

**Typical uses:** OPA627 alternative in single-op-amp positions (needs an SOIC-to-DIP adapter), DAC I/V conversion (ADA4637-1 only at noise gain >5), microphone preamplifiers, op-amp rolling in DAC/headphone gear (e.g. Dr.DAC3), low-bias-current transimpedance stages (e.g. open-hardware STM tunnelling amps use ADA4637-1ARZ)

**Caveats:**

- The ADA4637-1 is decompensated; ADI's model says to use a noise gain >5. It is not a drop-in for unity-gain buffers or low-gain positions.
- Single-channel only. Dual-op-amp sockets need a dual-to-single adapter.
- Only SOIC-8 and LFCSP-8 were seen, with no DIP, so socket rolling needs an adapter board. The LFCSP is impractical for hand-rolling.
- The sonic claims are subjective forum and blog listening. The forum quotes were carried over from discovery notes and not re-verified in this pass.
- The AN-1429 praise is vendor marketing, not independent evidence.
- No independent measurement data was found.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit reports were found; this pass could not search the web. The claim that the community prefers it because it is not faked, unlike the OPA627, is low-confidence folklore. Buy from ADI or authorised distributors. Be wary of pre-mounted 'ADA4627 on DIP adapter' marketplace modules whose die provenance cannot be checked; no specific fake reports were found.

## Open questions

- VERIFICATION LIMITED: the WebSearch budget was already exhausted (200/200), so both search attempts in this pass were refused. Verification used only GitHub-hosted files: ADI SPICE macro-models in LTspice libraries, the Autodesk Eagle analog-devices.lbr and its ChangeLog, a KiCad ADI symbol library, a text extraction of ADI AN-1429, and open-hardware BOMs. A full web re-run is still needed.
- Datasheet revision chain: the earliest known file is the standalone ADA4627-1.pdf (before 2009-12-22); a later combined ADA4627-1/ADA4637-1 datasheet is presumed. Find the revision letters and dates, and the revision in which the ADA4637-1 was added (its SPICE model is dated 08/2010). Check Wayback captures of http://www.analog.com/static/imported-files/data_sheets/ADA4627-1.pdf.
- Supply-voltage conflict: the Eagle transcription of the early title says '36 V, 19 MHz'. The ADI SPICE 'Generic Desc' says '8/30V', and the later title says '30 V'. Was '36 V' an Eagle transcription error or the original Rev. 0 title?
- INFERRED URLs, never seen in any source: analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ADA4627-1_4637-1.pdf, analog.com/static/imported-files/data_sheets/ADA4627-1_4637-1.pdf, and the legacy diyaudio.com/forums/solid-state/150222-... thread URL. Archive seeds only.
- Spec values recalled from memory and still NOT verified: ADA4627-1 SR ~40 V/us; ADA4637-1 GBW ~79 MHz and SR ~170 V/us; e_n ~6.1 nV/rtHz at 1 kHz. Only the ADA4627-1 GBW of 19 MHz is corroborated, by the Eagle title and a code comment. Also check i_n, Iq, output current, THD+N, and Vos/Ib by A/B grade.
- Adversarial flag: the OPA627 is commonly specified at about 55 V/us (not verified this run). If the ADA4627-1 is really about 40 V/us, the blog claim of 'higher slew rate than OPA627' is wrong for the ADA4627-1 and would hold only for the ADA4637-1 against the OPA627.
- Ordering codes ADA4627-1BRZ, ADA4637-1BRZ and ADA4637-1ACPZ-R2/-R7/-RL were seen only in a third-party chip-catalogue sitemap. Confirm them in ADI's ordering guide, check whether a B-grade LFCSP exists, and check lifecycle status (NRND?).
- ADI PCNs (fab or assembly transfer, die change) for either part were not searched. None are known.
- The LTspice ChangeLog lists ADA4637 macro-model fixes on 04/17/21 (output-voltage limit) and 05/12/21 (open-loop gain/phase). These are simulation-model changes only and must not be read as silicon changes.
- The SPICE model author tag 'HH / ADSJ' may refer to an ADI Japan design group; this is unverified.
- No independent measurements (Samuel Groner distortion survey, Audio Science Review) have been located.

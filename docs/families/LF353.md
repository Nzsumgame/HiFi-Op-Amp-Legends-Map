# LF351 / LF353 / LF347

**JFET-input (BiFET) op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · National Semiconductor (1977–2011) → Texas Instruments (ex-National) (2011–present) · second sources: Texas Instruments, Motorola / ON Semiconductor, SGS-Thomson / STMicroelectronics, Fairchild Semiconductor · Tier B (widely praised)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** TI moved its own LF353 from Sherman to RFAB with an entirely new design (PCNs 20220615003.1, 20230627002.1). (+5 more) [Details](#silicon-changes)

*National's 1977 BI-FET II dual: a cheap TL072-class part in vintage audio and synth gear, prized for restorations.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 nV/√Hz (TI); 16 (National, LF353-N); 15 (ST) | 3 MHz (TI LF353); 4 MHz, 2.7 min (LF353-N); 4 MHz (ST) | 13 V/µs typ, 8 min (TI, National); 16 V/µs (ST) | <0.02% (National); 0.01% (ST headline); TI not specified | ±3.5 to ±18 V (TI); from ±6 V (National); ST 6–32 V | 3.6 mA typ / 6.5 mA max per LF353 |

## Why it's rated

Not web-verified. Reportedly a vintage BiFET dual in 1980s–90s consumer and synth gear, called smooth or warm by some listeners and ordinary by measurement-minded reviewers. On paper it is TL072-class (16–18 nV/√Hz, 3–4 MHz). TI lists pro audio mixers; National specified THD <0.02%. Its appeal is originality in restorations.

**Described as:** smooth (folklore), warm (folklore)

**Used in:** Pro audio mixers (TI application list), Vintage preamp, tone-control and filter stages (unverified), Synth CV/VCO/VCF circuits ('LF353 or TL072'), Active filters, integrators, sample-and-hold, D/A converters, Budget Chinese DACs, e.g. Xiang Sheng DAC-01A (low confidence)

## Watch out

- Noisier than bipolar duals: 16–18 nV/√Hz vs about 5 for NE5532; matters with low-impedance sources.
- Offset up to 10 mV (13 mV over temperature): trim, servo or AC-couple DC stages.
- Phase reversal below the negative CM limit (National): keep inputs 3–4 V above V−; below V− can destroy it.
- Output specified only into 2 kΩ/10 kΩ: not for 600 Ω or headphones. Commercial 0–70 °C only.
- Specs differ by vendor and TI lineage: LF353P (3 MHz/18 nV) is not LF353M/NOPB (4 MHz/16 nV).
- **Counterfeits:** No LF353-specific reports checked. A cheap commodity part, but recycled DIP pulls are common. Buy TI or ST stock from authorised distributors. 'New' ON-marked stock is old (discontinued c. 1999–2000). LF353AN/BN or TO-99 LF353H parts are pre-2000 National.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments (TI's own LF353, SLOS012 lineage; not the ex-National LF353-N), 2022-06-16 · PCN 20220615003.1

TI moved its own LF353 from Sherman to RFAB with an entirely new design (PCNs 20220615003.1, 20230627002.1). E2E 1512001: guaranteed limits unchanged. E2E 1442987: bandwidth rose to about 4.5 MHz, risking instability. The TL082's new die adds VCC+ clamps (E2E 1428797); unconfirmed for LF353.

- **Affected:** LF353D, LF353DR, LF353DRE4, LF353P / LF353PE4 (likely; not itemised in any retrieved source)
- **How to tell:** Unchanged part number, marking and datasheet (SLOS012C). Identify by date code after first ship 2022-09-16. Die rev C/E/F/H → A. LF353-N (National design) unaffected.
- **Audio impact:** About 50% more real bandwidth changes phase margin in filters, followers and cable drivers. Vintage circuits may oscillate; ~10 pF across 30 kΩ fixed one.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - pin-compatible, same limits, but an entirely new die with ~50% more bandwidth.
- **Confidence:** high

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab | SH-BIP-1 / SFAB, Sherman TX | RFAB, Richardson TX |
| Die revision (PCN fields, reported) | C (20220615003.1); C, E, F, H (20230627002.1) | A |
| Assembly site | existing sites | MLA (Kuala Lumpur) added |
| Gain-bandwidth (typical, actual behaviour) | 3 MHz typ (SLOS012C) | about 4.5 MHz (TI E2E statement; not a datasheet value) |
| Guaranteed datasheet limits | SLOS012C limits | unchanged, per TI E2E |
| Input protection | no clamp to V+ reported (legacy die) | clamp diodes to VCC+ reported for the same PCN's TL082 new die; unconfirmed for LF353 |
| Slew rate / input bias / supply current (typ) | 13 V/us / 50 pA / 3.6 mA (SLOS012C) | no published change found |

### Second-source difference: Texas Instruments / National Semiconductor (two TI product lines), 1987 (TI LF353) / 2011 (TI LF353-N)

TI sells two LF353 products. National's (TI LF353-N): 4 MHz, 16 nV/√Hz, THD <0.02%. TI's own, likely its own die: 3 MHz, 18 nV/√Hz, no THD figure, like its TL07x. DC specs and 13 V/µs match. TI's LF353 is now a new RFAB die, so the gap may have changed.

- **Affected:** LF353, LF353P, LF353DR, LF353D, LF353PE4, LF353N/NOPB, LF353M/NOPB, LF353MX/NOPB
- **How to tell:** TI: LF353P/DR/D/PE4, SLOS012, 3 MHz. National: LF353N/M/MX (/NOPB), DS005649 or SNOSBH3F (revised March 2013), 4 MHz. Pre-2011 National parts carry the NS logo.
- **Audio impact:** Probably small: the National version is specified slightly quieter and wider-band, with a THD guarantee. Both are TL072-class.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same pinout, function and DC specs; only minor AC-spec differences
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| GBW typ (min) | 4 MHz (2.7 MHz min), National DS005649 / TI SNOSBH3F | 3 MHz typ, no min, TI SLOS012C |
| e_n @1 kHz | 16 nV/rtHz, RS = 100 ohm (National) | 18 nV/rtHz, RS = 20 ohm (TI Rev C; RS = 100 ohm in 1988) |
| THD | <0.02% (AV = 10, 20 Vp-p, 10 k; National) | not specified (TI) |
| Minimum supply | normal operation on +/-6 V (National application hints) | +/-3.5 V recommended minimum (TI) |
| ESD HBM | 1700 V (National 2000) | +/-2000 V (TI Rev C) |

### Datasheet respec: National Semiconductor, 1980 databook → Aug 2000 DS005649

- **Affected:** LF353A, LF353B, LF353AN, LF353BN, LF353AH, LF353BH, LF353H, LF351A, LF351B, LF347A
- **How to tell:** Parts marked LF353AN/BN, LF353AH/BH (TO-99 can) or LF351A/B are pre-2000 National. The 2000 sheet lists only LF353M/MX/N.
- **Audio impact:** None documented. The A/B grades differ only in offset.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same die family and pinout; the TO-99 can has a different footprint

### Second-source difference: STMicroelectronics vs Texas Instruments, 2010-03 · ST Doc ID 2153 Rev 3

ST specifies its LF353 as 4 MHz, 16 V/µs, 15 nV/√Hz with a 0.01% THD headline. ST's selector gives its LF35x and TL072 the same 1400 µA/ch, hinting at a shared JFET process (unverified). Mouser's LF353DT figure (3.2 mA per package) differs.

- **Affected:** LF353N, LF353D, LF353DT, LF253, LF351, LF347
- **How to tell:** ST logo and ST orderables (LF353DT, and LF253 industrial grade). One ST datasheet (Doc ID 2153) covers LF253 and LF353.
- **Audio impact:** Marginal. ST's version is slightly quieter and faster on paper.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: drop-in, but ST's LF353 is rated only to 32 V against TI's 36 V
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| GBW typ | 3 MHz (TI SLOS012C) | 4 MHz (ST, via Mouser parametric extraction) |
| Slew rate | 13 V/us typ (TI) | 16 V/us (ST headline) |
| e_n | 18 nV/rtHz @1 kHz (TI) | 15 nV/rtHz (ST headline) |
| Supply current | 3.6 mA typ per dual (TI) | 1.4 mA/ch (ST selector) or 3.2 mA typ per LF353DT (Mouser) |

# LF351 / LF353 / LF347 (single/dual/quad BiFET, JFET-input)

National Semiconductor (by Sep 1977 to 2011) → Texas Instruments (ex-National) (2011 onward) · second sources: Texas Instruments, Motorola to ON Semiconductor, SGS-Thomson / STMicroelectronics, Fairchild Semiconductor · Tier B (widely praised)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** TI moved its own LF353 from Sherman to RFAB on a new die. (+5 more) [Details](#silicon-changes)

*National's 1977 BI-FET II JFET dual: a cheap TL072-class op-amp found in vintage audio and synth gear, valued mostly for period-correct restorations.*

| Slew rate | Supply current |
|---|---|
| 13 V/us typ, 8 V/us min (TI LF353, National / LF353-N). ST LF253/LF353 and LF347: 16 V/us headline. | LF353: 3.6 mA typ / 6.5 mA max per package (TI and National standard grade; National 1980 A/B grades 5.6 mA max). ST: 1.4 mA/ch (selector) or 3.2 mA typ for LF353DT (Mouser). LF347: TI 8/11 mA, National 7.2/11 mA. LF351: TI and National 1.8/3.4 mA, Fairchild 2.3/3.4 mA. |

## Why it's rated

NOT WEB-VERIFIED: this pass spent no searches on listening or measurement reports. The discovery notes describe the LF353 as a vintage BiFET dual in 1980s-90s consumer and synth gear, called smooth or warm by some listeners and ordinary by measurement-minded reviewers. On paper it is a TL072-class part: 16-18 nV/rtHz and 3-4 MHz. TI lists 'Pro Audio Mixers' among its applications, and National specified THD <0.02%. Its appeal is originality in restorations, not performance.

**Described as:** smooth (discovery notes, unverified folklore), warm (discovery notes, unverified folklore)

**Used in:** pro audio mixers (TI SLOS012C application list), preamp, tone-control and filter stages in vintage gear (discovery notes, unverified), synthesizer and modular CV/VCO/VCF circuits (e.g. open4Umodular subosc BOM 'LF353 or TL072'), active filters, integrators, sample-and-hold, D/A converters (TI and National datasheets), budget Chinese DACs (a Xiang Sheng DAC-01A review lists OPA2604 x1 and LF353N x2; low confidence)

## Watch out

- Noisier than bipolar audio duals: 16-18 nV/rtHz against about 5 nV/rtHz for an NE5532. It matters with low-impedance sources.
- Offset up to 10 mV (13 mV over temperature), so DC-coupled stages need trimming, a servo or a coupling capacitor.
- National documents phase reversal: taking either input below the negative common-mode limit forces the output high (no latch). Keep inputs at least 3-4 V above V-. TI SLOS012C does not mention it.
- TI recommends +/-3.5 V minimum, while National says zener biasing gives normal operation from +/-6 V. Commercial 0-70 °C only.
- Output specified only into 2 k/10 k loads, so not for 600-ohm or headphone loads.
- Specs differ by vendor and by TI lineage (TI LF353 3 MHz/18 nV; LF353-N and National 4 MHz/16 nV; ST 4 MHz/15 nV, 16 V/us). An 'LF353' from another maker, or LF353P against LF353M/NOPB, is not the same datasheet.
- Never let an input go below the negative supply: National warns this can destroy the unit.
- **Counterfeits:** No LF353-specific counterfeit reports were checked in this pass. It is a very cheap commodity part, so remarking pays little, but recycled or remarked DIP pulls from marketplace sellers are common for all classic op-amps. Buy TI or ST stock from authorised distributors. Motorola/ON LF35x parts were discontinued around 1999-2000 (ON notice 10109), so 'new' ON-marked stock is old or suspect. Current TI parts are marked LF353P (PDIP) or LF353 (SOIC) per TI's addendum. 'LF353AN/BN' or TO-99 'LF353H' parts are genuine National grades from before 2000; offers of them as new production are suspect. When TI stopped using the NS logo on ex-National (-N, /NOPB) parts was not verified.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments (TI's own LF353, SLOS012 lineage; not the ex-National LF353-N), PCN 20220615003.1, dated 16 Jun 2022 (samples until 16 Jul 2022; proposed first ship 16 Sep 2022). PCN 20230627002.1 is numbered June 2023; one search summary gives its PCN date as 25 Oct 2023 (unverified). TI E2E threads 1442987 and 1512001 (2024-2025) discuss post-PCN parts.

TI moved its own LF353 from Sherman to RFAB on a new die. On E2E (1512001), TI says PCNs 20220615003.1 and 20230627002.1 'introduced an entirely new die' with an entirely new design, and the guaranteed datasheet limits did not change. In E2E 1442987 ('LF353: new part parameter change'), TI says the new part's bandwidth rose to about 4.5 MHz because of the fab change and warns of possible stability issues in existing circuits. The same PCN's new die for the TL082 adds clamp diodes to VCC+ (E2E 1428797); that the LF353 die has them too is likely but not confirmed. No typical-value changes were found for noise, slew, bias or supply current.

- **Affected:** LF353D, LF353DR, LF353DRE4, LF353P / LF353PE4 (likely; not itemised in any retrieved source)
- **How to tell:** The part number and marking are unchanged, and no SLOS012 revision newer than C (Mar 2016) was found. Per search summaries, PCN 20220615003.1 moves the fab from SH-BIP-1 (Sherman, TX) to RFAB (Richardson, TX), changes die rev C to A and adds MLA (Kuala Lumpur) assembly. PCN 20230627002.1 shows the die-revision field going from C/E/F/H to A. The only external clue is the date code or lot relative to the first-ship dates (from about Sep 2022); TI lot/fab trace data is definitive. The National-design LF353-N (LF353N/NOPB, LF353M/NOPB, LF353MX/NOPB) is a separate product and is not covered by these PCNs.
- **Audio impact:** About 50% more real bandwidth under unchanged limits points to different compensation and input stage. This changes phase margin and HF behaviour in active filters, tone stacks, unity-gain followers and buffers driving cable or capacitive loads. Users report stability problems in existing designs; the E2E thread suggests about 10 pF across a 30 kohm feedback resistor as a fix in one case. A new-die LF353 in a vintage design can sound different or oscillate even though the paper specs are the same. Noise and THD differences were not quantified.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: pin-compatible with unchanged guaranteed limits, but TI confirms an entirely new die with about 50% more bandwidth, and users report stability and behaviour differences in existing circuits.
- **Confidence:** high

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab | SH-BIP-1 / SFAB, Sherman TX | RFAB, Richardson TX |
| Die revision (PCN fields, per search summaries) | C (20220615003.1); C, E, F, H (20230627002.1) | A |
| Assembly site | existing sites | MLA (Kuala Lumpur) added |
| Gain-bandwidth (typical, actual behaviour) | 3 MHz typ (SLOS012C) | about 4.5 MHz (TI E2E statement; not a datasheet value) |
| Guaranteed datasheet limits | SLOS012C limits | unchanged, per TI E2E |
| Input protection | no clamp to V+ reported (legacy die) | clamp diodes to VCC+ reported for the same PCN's TL082 new die; unconfirmed for LF353 |
| Slew rate / input bias / supply current (typ) | 13 V/us / 50 pA / 3.6 mA (SLOS012C) | no published change found |

### Fab / process transfer: Texas Instruments (LF347 quad, SLOS013 lineage), PCN 20221219006.1, issued 21 Dec 2022 (samples until 20 Jan 2023; proposed first ship 20 Mar 2023); the same notice as the TL072/TL074 RFAB move

Summaries of the Mouser-hosted PCN 20221219006.1 ('Qualification of new Fab site (RFAB)...') put LF347N and LF347 in the Group 2 device list, for 'RFAB/Process migration and adding MLA Assembly site'. The TL072/TL074 on the same notice received a revised die. Group 2's wording names a process migration, not an explicit die revision, and no LF347 datasheet deltas, die letters or user reports were found. Search summaries also loosely associated LF347 with PCNs 20220615003.1 and 20230627002.1 (the LF353 new-die PCNs); both are unconfirmed.

- **Affected:** LF347N, LF347 (other TI LF347/LF347B orderables not confirmed)
- **How to tell:** The part number is unchanged. No marking or die-revision letter was retrieved, so identification is by date code relative to the 20 Mar 2023 first-ship date. The ex-National LF347-N/LF147 are separate products and are not implicated.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown from sources. If it follows the TL07x and LF353 RFAB dies, expect different real-world bandwidth, slew and input behaviour under the same part number. The quad is common in vintage mixers and synths, so behaviour changes in legacy circuits are the main risk.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: the RFAB process migration is PCN-listed, but the LF347's actual electrical deltas and die status were not retrieved.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab / process | legacy fab (Sherman, per the sibling TL07x/LF353 moves) | RFAB process migration (PCN 20221219006.1 Group 2) |
| Assembly site | existing sites | MLA added as an assembly site option |
| Electrical specs | SLOS013 (not re-read) | no change retrieved |

### Second-source difference: Texas Instruments / National Semiconductor (two TI product lines), TI has published its own LF353 (SLOS012) since March 1987. Since the 2011 acquisition TI has also sold the National design as a separate product, LF353-N (SNOSBH3F, revised March 2013).

TI sells two products under near-identical numbers. National's LF353 (TI LF353-N) is rated 4 MHz and 16 nV/rtHz (RS = 100 ohm), with THD <0.02%. TI's own LF353 is rated 3 MHz and 18 nV/rtHz with no THD figure, the same numbers as its TL07x. DC specs (VOS, IB, ICC, SR 13 V/us) match. Since the 2022-2023 PCNs, TI's own LF353 is itself a new RFAB die (see the die-redesign entry), so the gap between the two lineages may have changed. That TI's 1987 part is its own die is likely but inferred.

- **Affected:** LF353, LF353P, LF353DR, LF353D, LF353PE4, LF353N/NOPB, LF353M/NOPB, LF353MX/NOPB
- **How to tell:** TI lineage: orderables LF353P, LF353DR, LF353D and LF353PE4, datasheet SLOS012 (lf353.pdf, ti.com/product/LF353), 3 MHz. National lineage: orderables LF353N, LF353M and LF353MX (/NOPB under TI), datasheet National DS005649 or TI SNOSBH3 (lf353-n.pdf, ti.com/product/LF353-N), 4 MHz. RS Online lists LF353P at 3 MHz and LF353M/NOPB, LF353MX/NOPB and LF353N/NOPB at 4 MHz. Pre-2011 National parts carry the NS logo. Top-mark details are unverified.
- **Audio impact:** Probably small. The National version is specified slightly quieter and wider-band, with a THD guarantee. Both are TL072-class.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same pinout, function and DC specs; only minor AC-spec differences
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |

### Datasheet respec: National Semiconductor, between the 1980 databook and the August 2000 DS005649 (new-format DS005649 first dated April 1998 per TI SNOSBH3F)

National dropped the tighter-offset A and B grades and the TO-99 can for the LF353 (a lifecycle step) and added SO-8. The 2000 datasheet adds a GBW minimum (2.7 MHz) and an ESD rating, raises Tj(max) from 115 to 150 C and lowers the N-package thetaJA from 160 to 115 C/W. This is a product-line and datasheet change; no die change is documented.

- **Affected:** LF353A, LF353B, LF353AN, LF353BN, LF353AH, LF353BH, LF353H, LF351A, LF351B, LF347A
- **How to tell:** Old stock marked LF353AN/BN, LF353AH/BH (TO-99 metal can) or LF351A/B is pre-2000 National. The 2000 datasheet lists only LF353M/MX/N.
- **Audio impact:** None documented. The A/B grades differ only in offset.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same die family and pinout; the TO-99 can has a different footprint
- **Confidence:** high

### Second-source difference: STMicroelectronics vs Texas Instruments, ongoing (ST Doc ID 2153 Rev 3, March 2010)

ST's selector lists the same 1400 uA/ch for its LF35x and TL072, which hints at a shared ST JFET process (unverified). Mouser data for LF353DT (3.2 mA typ per package) differs from the selector's 1.4 mA/ch.

- **Affected:** LF353N, LF353D, LF353DT, LF253, LF351, LF347
- **How to tell:** ST logo and ST orderables (LF353DT, and LF253 industrial grade). One ST datasheet (Doc ID 2153) covers LF253 and LF353.
- **Audio impact:** Marginal. ST's version is slightly quieter and faster on paper.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: drop-in, but ST's LF353 is rated only to 32 V against TI's 36 V
- **Confidence:** medium

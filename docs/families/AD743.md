# AD743 / AD745 (ultralow-noise BiFET single op amps; AD745 = decompensated high-speed version)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*ADI BiFET single with 2.9 nV/rtHz (10 kHz) voltage noise and just 6.9 fA/rtHz current noise, prized for high-impedance sources such as MM phono and hydrophones.*

**Technology:** BiFET: JFET input differential pair (p-channel JFETs in ADI's macro-models) with bipolar gain and output stages. Single op amp. AD743 is unity-gain stable (4.5 MHz, 2.8 V/us). AD745 shares the same noise and input design, but its internal compensation is optimized for higher gains (20 MHz, 12.5 V/us). In ADI's models its dominant pole is 4.8x higher at the same 132 dB open-loop gain.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

More of an instrumentation legend than a mainstream op-amp-rolling part. It pairs 2.9 nV/rtHz voltage noise with only 6.9 fA/rtHz current noise, so it stays quiet with both low and very high source impedances. That makes it a reference choice for hydrophone, piezo and charge-amplifier front ends and for low-noise measurement. For example, Rubiola chose the AD743 for AM-noise detector amplifiers. Discovery notes report a niche following in MM phono/RIAA stages and high-impedance mic or piezo preamps; this was not web-verified. Community sound descriptors and measurements were not retrieved.

**Typical uses:** MM phono / RIAA preamp input stage (niche following; per discovery notes, not web-verified), Hydrophone and piezo-film charge amplifiers (e.g. AD745 charge amp for PVDF film in a Taiwanese thesis), Low-noise measurement front ends (AM/phase-noise detector amplifiers, Rubiola arXiv:physics/0512082), Cryogenic bolometer readout / precision bias circuits (AD743 in arXiv physics/9902042), High-impedance sensor and accelerometer preamps

**Caveats:**

- Single op amp only: not a drop-in for dual (5532/TL072-style) DIP-8 sockets. It needs two parts per stereo pair plus an adapter.
- The surface-mount part is a 16-lead wide SOIC (R-16), not SOIC-8, so an adapter or custom footprint is needed.
- AD745 compensation is optimized for higher gains (data sheet). It is not a unity-gain buffer. The exact minimum stable gain (believed 5) is unconfirmed.
- Large JFET input capacitance (20 pF in the model) creates a feedback pole with high-value resistors, so a feedback capacitor may be needed.
- About 8 mA quiescent current per amplifier (model), high for a JFET op amp.
- Minimum supply is about +/-4.8 V; not a 5 V single-supply part.
- Several SOIC reel and leaded variants were discontinued via ADI PDNs, so check which orderable part numbers remain active.

## Counterfeits

No AD743/AD745-specific counterfeit reports were found (forums not searched). Generic risk: expensive single JFET op amps are remarking targets. Checks: (1) supply current should be about 8 mA per amp at +/-15 V, while a remarked TL071-class part draws far less and is much noisier. (2) The documented surface-mount package is the 16-lead SOIC (R-16), so treat an 'AD743/AD745' in SOIC-8 with suspicion. (3) Several R-16 reel and leaded variants were discontinued (ADI PDN 14_0014), so a 'new' leaded AD745KR-16 offered today is old stock at best. (4) Buy from ADI or authorised distributors.

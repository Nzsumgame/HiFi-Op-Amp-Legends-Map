# AD743 / AD745

**BiFET (JFET-input) single, ultralow noise** · Analog Devices (by 1992–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*ADI BiFET single with 2.9 nV/√Hz and only 6.9 fA/√Hz: a reference choice for MM phono, piezo and hydrophones.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.9 nV/√Hz typ (at 10 kHz); 0.38 µV p-p 0.1–10 Hz | AD743 4.5 MHz; AD745 20 MHz | AD743 2.8 V/µs; AD745 12.5 V/µs | 9.6 V to 30 V total (±4.8 V to ±15 V) | ~8.1 mA (AD743), ~8.0 mA (AD745) |

## Why it's rated

More an instrumentation legend than a rolling favourite. 2.9 nV/√Hz voltage noise with only 6.9 fA/√Hz current noise keeps it quiet with low and very high source impedances, so it suits hydrophone, piezo and charge amplifiers; Rubiola chose the AD743 for AM-noise detectors. A niche MM phono following is reported, unconfirmed. No community listening reports found.

**Used in:** MM phono / RIAA input stage (reported niche following), Hydrophone and piezo-film charge amps (AD745 with PVDF film), Low-noise measurement front ends (Rubiola AM/phase-noise detectors), Cryogenic bolometer readout and precision bias (arXiv physics/9902042), High-impedance sensor and accelerometer preamps

## Watch out

- Single only, in 16-lead SOIC (R-16) or DIP: dual DIP-8 sockets need two parts and an adapter.
- AD745 is compensated for higher gains (minimum believed 5); not a unity-gain buffer.
- 20 pF input capacitance (model) forms a pole with large feedback resistors; add a feedback capacitor.
- ~8 mA per amplifier and at least ±4.8 V supply; not a 5 V single-supply part.
- Several R-16 reel and leaded variants discontinued via ADI PDNs; check which orderables remain.
- **Counterfeits:** No AD743/AD745 fake reports found. Checks: ~8 mA supply current at ±15 V (a remarked TL071 draws far less and is noisier); SMD should be R-16, not SOIC-8; 'new' leaded AD745KR-16 is old stock at best. Buy from authorised channels.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

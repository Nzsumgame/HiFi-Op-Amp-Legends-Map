# LT1468 / LT1469 / LT1468-2 / LT1469-2

**Bipolar 16-bit-accurate precision op-amp, 1/2 channels** · Linear Technology (1998–2017) → Analog Devices (2017–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*Linear Tech's 16-bit-accurate op-amp for DAC I/V: 5 nV/√Hz, 90 MHz, ±15 V, ultra-low distortion; a measurement-led pick.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 nV/√Hz typ (at 10 kHz; no 1 kHz value given) | 90 MHz typ (60 min) ±15 V; 88 MHz (55 min) ±5 V | 22 V/µs typ (15 min) ±15 V; 17 V/µs (11 min) ±5 V | THD −125 dB at 1 kHz; −96.5 dB at 100 kHz | ±4.5 V min (PSRR-guaranteed; ±2.5 V typ) to 36 V abs max | 4.1 mA typ / 5.2 max per amp ±15 V; 3.8 / 5.0 at ±5 V |

## Why it's rated

Linear calls it 'optimum' for DAC I/V and ADC buffers and shows a 0.00025% THD+N balanced line driver. diyAudio members call it as clean as the OPA627 but quieter and more neutral; one, citing Samuel Groner's shootout, rated it 'best of the bunch overall'. Others found it thin, heard 'metallic' treble, or wanted more output current.

**Described as:** clean, quiet, neutral, thin (one listener), lacks character (some)

**Used in:** I/V conversion for current-output DACs (LTC1597 example), DAC post-filter and active reconstruction filters, ADC input buffer or driver (LTC1604 example), Balanced line driver, paralleled with LT1364 (0.0008% 20 Hz–20 kHz), Op-amp rolling in DAC output stages

## Watch out

- -2 variants (LT1468-2/LT1469-2) need gain ≥ 2 and may oscillate in unity-gain sockets; check the suffix.
- Layout-sensitive: ground plane, short leads, 0.01–0.1 µF plus 1–10 µF bypass; one 'metallic' report blamed bypassing.
- An input within 0.5 V of V+ reverses output phase. Cap load limit 100 pF (G=1), 300 pF (G=−1).
- Add CF ≥ RG·CIN/RF (DACs add tens of pF; LTC1597 example uses 15 pF/12k). Avoid balanced source resistances.
- ±22 mA output and 4.1 mA/amp (6.5 mA hot); needs ±4.5 V min. Old LTspice -2 symbols mislabelled 90 MHz.
- **Counterfeits:** No LT1468/LT1469 fakes reported. Genuine marks: SO-8 '1469' or '1469I'; PDIP 'LT1469CN8'/'LT1469IN8'; DFN '1469'. It is pricey, so buy from authorized distributors and distrust cheap 'LT1469CN8' DIPs. Check you did not get an LT1469-2.

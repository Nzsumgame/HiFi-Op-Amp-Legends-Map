# LM4562 / LME49720 / LME49710 / LME49740

**Bipolar ultra-low-distortion audio op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · National Semiconductor (2006–2011) → Texas Instruments (2011–present) · Tier A (legend / revision hazard)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** PCN 20180308002, 'Transfer of select VIP3 devices from GFAB to DFAB (DL-LIN) Wafer Fab site', moved LM4562/LME49720 wafers from closing TI Greenock to TI Dallas, same VIP3 process. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*National's 2006–07 audio op-amp: 2.7 nV/√Hz, 0.00003% THD+N; the standard NE5532 upgrade, TO-99 cans collectable.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.7 nV/√Hz (LM4562/LME49720/LME49740); 2.5 (LME49710) | 55 MHz typ | ±20 V/µs typ; 15 V/µs min | 0.00003% typ | ±2.5 V to ±17 V; abs max 36 V | 12 mA max (dual, whole package) |

## Why it's rated

The default 'better than NE5532' upgrade, with distortion near test-equipment limits; NwAvGuy's 2011 measurements are often cited for it. Reportedly stock or socketed in Asus Xonar and Creative cards and common in Chinese DACs. Listeners call it neutral, clear and detailed, sometimes clinical. LM4562 vs LME49720 'voicing' is folklore: TI says same silicon.

**Described as:** neutral, clear, transparent, detailed, clean, tight bass, clinical, analytical (some), 'too clear' on violins, cleaner treble than discrete, brighter/bassier marking folklore

**Used in:** NE5532/4580 replacement in CD players, DACs, sound cards, DAC I/V and output stages, Headphone amplifier gain stages, Phono and mic preamps (LME49710HA prized), Pro-audio line stages; SDR audio/IQ stages

## Watch out

- 55 MHz GBW: may oscillate in some sockets and layouts; needs good local decoupling.
- Max supply ±17 V (36 V abs max); use LME49860 for ±18–22 V rails.
- Input protection diodes (E2E 337068); 1/f noise (E2E 415907), start-up (PADF 641), RF susceptibility (ASR 10687) discussed.
- Made in TI Dallas, not Greenock, since 2018 under the same part number; old-vs-new claims unverified.
- Marketplace DIP and TO-99 stock is often fake; prices differ between identical LM4562 and LME49720.
- **Counterfeits:** Marketplace DIP and TO-99 parts are often fake: LME49710 (tinytransistors, 2022-09-24), LME49720NA (diyAudio 386852), LM4562NA (diyAudio 266368; JE6LVE measured ~0.001% vs 0.0006% THD). Buy SOIC from authorised distributors; check THD, 12 mA supply current, era-correct logo. 'JR' lots = Greenock.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Texas Instruments, 2018-03-09 · PCN 20180308002

PCN 20180308002, 'Transfer of select VIP3 devices from GFAB to DFAB (DL-LIN) Wafer Fab site', moved LM4562/LME49720 wafers from closing TI Greenock to TI Dallas, same VIP3 process. First ship 2018-06-09; reissued as -001 (2018-03-26, first ship 2018-09-26). Stock reached distribution early 2019. No die redesign documented.

- **Affected:** LM4562 (SOIC MA/MAX confirmed; full PCN list not seen), LME49720 (SOIC MA/MAX confirmed; full PCN list not seen)
- **How to tell:** Part number and marking unchanged; only lot/date code shows the fab. Forum claims: 2019+ date codes are Dallas; 'JR' lots are Greenock-fab, Malaysia-assembled.
- **Audio impact:** No spec change or old-vs-new data. One diyAudio report: 2019+ parts lack popcorn noise seen in some earlier LM4562 lots (anecdotal).
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same part, pinout, process family and limits; unspecified noise or RF behaviour could shift.
- **Confidence:** high

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab site | GFAB (TI Greenock, Scotland; ex-National) | DFAB / DL-LIN (TI Dallas, TX) |
| Process | VIP3 (National complementary bipolar) | VIP3 (same process family, per the PCN title) |
| Wafer diameter (unresolved) | 150 mm / 6-inch (per diyAudio posters) | 200 mm / 8-inch per diyAudio posters and one search summary of the PCN. However, TI's LM6172 PCN 20241217001.1 describes DL-LIN VIP3 as a 150 mm line, so this is not confirmed. |
| Datasheet electrical limits | SNAS326K (Dec 2013) / SNAS393D (Nov 2016) | No datasheet revision tied to the PCN was found, so there is no published spec delta. |

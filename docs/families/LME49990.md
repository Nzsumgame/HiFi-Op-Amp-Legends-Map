# LME49990 / LME49990MA

**Bipolar single, ultra-low-noise audio (Overture E-Series)** · National Semiconductor (2009–2011) → Texas Instruments (2011–discontinued) · Tier B (widely praised)

*National's 2009 Overture flagship (0.9 nV/√Hz, 0.00001% THD+N), rolled as an AD797/LT1028 rival in phono and DAC stages.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.9 nV/√Hz typ, 1.3 nV/√Hz max (0.88 also quoted) | 110 MHz typ | ±22 V/µs | 0.00001% typ | ±5 V to ±18 V (10 V to 36 V total) | about 9 mA typ (unconfirmed) |

## Why it's rated

A cult single among rollers and low-noise designers. On diyAudio a long-time AD797 fan reportedly called it the 'winner by a mile'. Soomal (AE-9, 2020) found it the most refined single but the LME49710 better value. Zigsow praises its resolution. EEVblog's Dave Jones called the datasheet an 'audiophile wank alert'. Douglas Self covers its common-mode distortion.

**Described as:** very extended bass, clear midrange, sweet top end, refined texture (Soomal), high resolution (Zigsow), harsh if under-bypassed

**Used in:** MC/MM phono preamplifiers (DIY), DAC and sound-card output stages (Creative AE-9), Low-noise preamps and measurement front-ends, Op-amp rolling via SOIC-to-DIP adapters

## Watch out

- Obsolete after TI process EOL; only NOS or grey-market stock remains. SOIC-8 only, so adapters add lead length.
- Japanese users say it needs ≥10 µF local bypass, sounding harsh and thin with 0.1 µF. Folklore, not datasheet.
- EEVblog #541 found noise only slightly better than AD797; don't over-read the 0.88/0.9 nV figure.
- Douglas Self treats its common-mode distortion separately; check non-inverting and follower use.
- OPA1611 replacement's THD rises more at HF (TI E2E). Costs more than the LME49710.
- **Counterfeits:** No documented fakes, but risk is elevated: obsolete, single-source, SOIC-only, often pre-soldered on adapters. Buy traceable stock, check the National or TI logo marking, and measure quiescent current (about 9 mA) and noise; remarks show clearly above 0.9 nV/√Hz.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

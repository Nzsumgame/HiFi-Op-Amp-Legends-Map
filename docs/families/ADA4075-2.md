# ADA4075-2

**Bipolar low-noise, low-power dual (iPOLAR), SMD only** · Analog Devices (2008–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** ADI added its Wilmington, MA fab (ADWL) as an alternate to Limerick (ADLK) for 'High Voltage Bipolar Products'. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*2.8 nV/√Hz at just 1.8 mA per amp; ADI's pick for DAC I/V, used by Matrix Audio for DAC filtering.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.8 nV/√Hz typ | 6.5 MHz typ | 12 V/µs typ | 0.0002% typ | 9 V min total (SPICE: 9/30 V); max unconfirmed, ~±18 V | 1.8 mA typ |

## Why it's rated

An engineer's part rather than an op-amp-rolling favourite. ADI markets it for power-limited hi-fi and instrumentation, and AN-1429 recommends it for DAC I/V stages. Matrix Audio's Mini-i Pro 2S uses it for the DAC low-pass filter and to drive a TPA6120A2. Soomal ranks it with the LME49720 as a 'very high-end low-distortion op amp'.

**Described as:** low distortion, low noise, low current

**Used in:** DAC current-to-voltage (I/V) conversion, DAC post-filter / low-pass stage, Headphone pre-driver, e.g. ahead of a TPA6120A2, Low-power, compact or high-density audio, NE5532 replacement where current is limited (unconfirmed)

## Watch out

- Needs at least 9 V total supply: unsuitable for 5 V or ±2.5 V rails (AN-1429).
- Not rail-to-rail: about 2 V headroom per rail, so 3.0 V peak (2.13 V rms) at ±5 V.
- Bipolar input, so current noise matters; one Japanese ADC designer chose the FET OPA1656 for kilohm networks.
- SMD only (SOIC-8 or 2 × 2 mm LFCSP): socket rolling needs a DIP adapter.
- Confirm the maximum supply before ±18 V NE5532 sockets. No ASR, Head-Fi or diyAudio reports found.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit or remarking reports. As an SMD-only part rarely rolled, fakes seem less likely than for NE5532, OPA2134 or LME49720 (unconfirmed). Buy from authorized ADI distributors; be wary of marketplace DIP-adapter modules.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2023 · PCN 22_0142

ADI added its Wilmington, MA fab (ADWL) as an alternate to Limerick (ADLK) for 'High Voltage Bipolar Products'. Sources conflict on whether ADA4075-2ARZ is listed, and whether its iPOLAR process falls in that scope is not stated. Inclusion is unconfirmed.

- **Affected:** ADA4075-2ARZ (claimed), other ADA4075-2 orderables (unconfirmed)
- **How to tell:** No marking change known; trace by date code or lot after implementation. PCN Rev. - form dated 4 Apr 2023, reportedly effective 7 Jul 2023; Revs. A and C followed.
- **Audio impact:** None expected: ADI states no fit, form, function or reliability impact; same process at an alternate fab.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: alternate-fab qualification with no datasheet change; not a new die.
- **Confidence:** low

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab site | ADI Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) | ADLK or ADI Wilmington, MA (ADWL) |

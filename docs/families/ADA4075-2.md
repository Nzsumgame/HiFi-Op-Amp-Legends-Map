# ADA4075-2 (ADI dual ultralow-noise, low-power bipolar audio op amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*A 2.8 nV/rtHz iPOLAR bipolar dual drawing 1.8 mA per amp. ADI recommends it for DAC I/V stages, and Matrix Audio used it for DAC filtering and a headphone pre-driver.*

**Technology:** Bipolar-input dual voltage-feedback op amp on ADI's iPOLAR process, which uses vertical junction isolation plus lateral trench isolation. ADI calls it an 'Ultralow Noise Amplifier at Lower Power'. The SPICE model header reads '9/30V, BIP, OP, Low Noise, Low THD, 2X'. Output is not rail-to-rail. Packages: SOIC-8 and 8-lead LFCSP (2 mm x 2 mm) only; there is no DIP.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Fab / process transfer).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This is a niche, engineer-oriented part rather than an op-amp-rolling favourite. ADI markets it for high-fidelity audio and instrumentation where power is limited. ADI app note AN-1429 recommends it for the I/V stage of current-output audio DACs. The Matrix Audio Mini-i Pro 2S uses it for the DAC low-pass filter and to drive a TPA6120A2 headphone stage (LME49720 does the I/V). Soomal groups it with the LME49720 as a 'very high-end low-distortion op amp'. The claim that it is a drop-in NE5532 upgrade comes only from the discovery note and was not verified. No ASR, Head-Fi or diyAudio listening or measurement reports were retrieved.

**How people describe the sound:** low distortion (Soomal: 'very high-end low-distortion op amp'; ADI 0.0002% THD+N typ), low noise at low supply current (vendor positioning)

**Typical uses:** DAC current-to-voltage (I/V) conversion, DAC post-filter / low-pass stage, headphone amplifier input or pre-driver stage (e.g. ahead of a TPA6120A2), low-power, small-enclosure or high-density audio, NE5532 replacement where supply current is limited (discovery note, unverified)

**Caveats:**

- The minimum specified supply is 9 V total, so it is unsuitable for a single 5 V or +/-2.5 V rail (AN-1429).
- Output is not rail-to-rail. About 2 V of headroom to each rail limits swing on low supplies (AN-1429).
- Bipolar input means current noise matters. A Japanese measurement-ADC designer chose the FET-input OPA1656 instead because of kilohm-level resistor networks.
- It is SMD-only (SOIC-8 or 2x2 mm LFCSP), so socket rolling needs a DIP adapter.
- Confirm the datasheet maximum supply before putting it in an NE5532 socket at +/-18 V or above.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, PCN 22_0142 (Rev. - form dated 4 Apr 2023 per search…

ADI added its Wilmington, MA fab (ADWL) as an alternate wafer fab to Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) for 'High Voltage Bipolar Products'. Some search summaries say ADA4075-2/ADA4075-2ARZ is on this PCN and another did not name it. The ADA4075-2 is built on ADI's iPolar trench-isolated bipolar process, and whether iPolar falls under this PCN's 'High Voltage Bipolar' scope is not stated in the sources seen. Inclusion is therefore unconfirmed.

- **When:** PCN 22_0142 (Rev. - form dated 4 Apr 2023 per search summaries, effective 7 Jul 2023 per another pass; Revs. A and C followed)
- **Affected:** ADA4075-2ARZ (claimed), other ADA4075-2 orderables (unconfirmed)
- **How to tell old from new:** The available evidence gives no way to tell from the marking. Traceability is by date code or lot after the implementation date. Check the affected-parts list on the ADI PCN form.
- **Audio impact:** None expected. ADI states no impact to fit, form, function or reliability, and the same process is transferred to an alternate fab.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - alternate-fab qualification with no data-sheet change; not a new die design
- **Confidence:** low
- **Verification:** Three searches. One summary said PCN 22_0142 includes ADA4075-2ARZ. Another only inferred applicability from the part's bipolar process. A check of PCN 23_0069 found it covers 0.6 µm CMOS/BiCMOS isolators, not the ADA4075. The listing is neither confirmed nor refuted, so the entry stays at low confidence.

### Revision chain status

Only one vendor made this part: Analog Devices, which originated the ADA4075-2 and is its sole source. It is the dual op amp on ADI's iPolar process. No second source, acquirer re-issue or die change is known, and none of the revision notes mentions a die change. The document is the ADA4075-2 data sheet, oldest to newest: (1) Rev. 0, 10/08 (initial version). Copy: manuallib.com (title shows 'Rev. 0'). (2) Rev. A, 8/09. This adds the 8-lead 2 mm x 2 mm LFCSP.

# ADA4075-2 (ADI dual ultralow-noise, low-power bipolar audio op amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*Low-power bipolar audio dual that ADI recommends for DAC I/V stages. Matrix Audio used it for the DAC low-pass filter and as the headphone pre-driver ahead of a TPA6120A2.*

**Technology:** Bipolar-input dual voltage-feedback op amp; the ADI SPICE model header reads '9/30V, BIP, OP, Low Noise, Low THD, 2X'. ADI's own description is 'Ultralow Noise Amplifier at Lower Power'. Output is not rail-to-rail. Packages: SOIC-8 and LFCSP-8 only, no DIP.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This is a niche, engineer-oriented part rather than a roller's legend. ADI markets it for low-power, low-noise audio. ADI app note AN-1429 recommends it for the I-to-V stage of current-output audio DACs, in both the 'High Performance' and 'Quality performance by low power consumption' columns. Commercial use is documented in the Matrix Audio Mini-i Pro 2S: LME49720 for I/V, ADA4075-2 for the low-pass filter, and ADA4075-2 driving a TPA6120A2 headphone stage. Soomal's review groups it with the LME49720 as a 'very high-end, low-distortion op amp'. The project discovery note calls it a low-power drop-in upgrade for the NE5532, but that was not independently verified. No Audio Science Review or Samuel Groner measurements, and no Head-Fi or diyAudio listening impressions, were retrieved this run.

**How people describe the sound:** low distortion (Soomal: 'very high-end low-distortion op amp'), low noise at low supply current (vendor positioning)

**Typical uses:** DAC current-to-voltage (I/V) conversion, DAC post-filter / low-pass stage, headphone amplifier input or pre-driver stage (e.g. ahead of TPA6120A2), low-power portable and mobile audio on +/-5 V rails, NE5532 replacement where supply current is limited (discovery note, unverified)

**Caveats:**

- Minimum specified supply is 9 V total, so it is unsuitable for single 5 V or +/-2.5 V rails (AN-1429).
- Output is not rail-to-rail: about 2 V headroom to each rail limits swing on low supplies (AN-1429).
- It has bipolar input current noise. A Japanese measurement-ADC designer dropped it for the FET-input OPA1656 because the 3.3k/4.7k resistor network would make current noise significant.
- It is SMD-only (SOIC-8/LFCSP-8), so socket rolling needs a DIP adapter.
- Check the maximum supply before using an NE5532 socket at +/-18 V. The SPICE header lists '9/30V', but the datasheet maximum was not verified.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remarking reports were found in this run. Because the part is SMD-only (SOIC-8/LFCSP-8) and not a common rolling part, fakes seem less likely than for the NE5532, OPA2134 or LME49720, but this is unverified. Buy from authorized ADI distributors. Treat pre-built DIP-adapter modules from marketplaces with the usual caution.

## Related parts and alternatives

ADA4896-2 (ADI low-noise rail-to-rail-output dual; recommended alongside the ADA4075-2 in AN-1429), ADA4807-2 (ADI lowest-Iq rail-to-rail I/O dual; AN-1429), ADA4841-2 (ADI low-cost option in AN-1429), OPA1612 / OPA1602 (TI SoundPlus bipolar low-noise duals), LME49720 / LM4562 (bipolar audio dual, paired with the ADA4075-2 in the Matrix Mini-i Pro 2S), OPA1656 (FET-input choice when source or feedback impedances are a few kilohms or more), NE5532 (baseline the ADA4075-2 is said to upgrade)

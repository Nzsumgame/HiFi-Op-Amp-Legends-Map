# NJM4556 / NJM4556A (dual high-output-current bipolar, 70 mA)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

*JRC's 70 mA dual that drives 150 ohm loads directly. It is the paralleled output stage of NwAvGuy's O2 and a staple of Japanese DIY headphone amps.*

**Technology:** Bipolar dual op-amp with a high-current output stage (+/-70 mA into 150 ohm at +/-10.5 V). The input differential pair is PNP in the datasheet equivalent circuit, in NJR's own PSpice macromodels (Version 1 and 2) and in the 2007 Bee Technologies model. Supply is +/-2 V to +/-18 V. The datasheet says it combines many NJM4558 features with 150 ohm drive capability. The Japanese search summary describes it as a 4558 with a reinforced output stage. On the typical curve at +/-15 V, the output sources about 100 mA and sinks about 200 mA before swing collapses.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (3 recorded: Unclassified).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

It is valued for current drive rather than for a distinctive sound. NwAvGuy used NJM4556A in the current-gain (output) stage of the Objective2 (O2), with both halves paralleled per channel. An earlier search summary of his 'O2 Details' page paraphrased him as saying no other dual DIP8 op-amp delivered as much current at as low distortion ('killed everything else'); this pass did not re-confirm that wording. The O2 made it a default DIY headphone driver, reused in O2/ODA derivatives, Eurorack headphone modules and other projects. Japanese DIY sources call it a standard (定番) headphone-amp chip, and say it sounds surprisingly good for about 30 yen. One Japanese listening comparison (audio-simulation.net, 2019) found it more energetic but rougher in texture than the NJM4580. It also appears in commercial gear: one 2018 owner's Behringer UCA202 had a 4556A, and the Roland AC-90 uses NJM4556AM in its phones amp.

**How people describe the sound:** energetic / lively (vs NJM4580; single Japanese listening test), rougher texture than NJM4580 (same source), surprisingly good for the price (Japanese DIY summary), clean, low-distortion current drive (NwAvGuy O2 testing)

**Typical uses:** O2 / ODA headphone amp output stage (paralleled sections), DIY and kit headphone amps in Japan (e.g. aitendo kits), Eurorack headphone and line-out modules (NJM4556AV), USB audio interface headphone outputs (one 2018 teardown of a Behringer UCA202), instrument-amp phones outputs (Roland AC-90, NJM4556AM), line drivers into 150-600 ohm loads

**Caveats:**

- The DIP8 (NJM4556AD) and SIP8 (AL) versions are NRND per the March 2025 Nisshinbo datasheet. A maintenance-product (保守品) designation was reported in April 2023. The SMD AM and AV versions continue.
- The SMD packages are JRC DMP8 (wider body than a 3.9 mm JEDEC SOIC-8) and SSOP8, so footprints differ.
- Package dissipation limits are low (DMP8 300 mW, SSOP8 250 mW), and quiescent current is 9 mA typ per package. Derate for high rails and low-impedance headphones.
- Speed is modest (3 V/us, 8 MHz). The typical THD at 20 kHz rises steeply with output level (about 0.2% at 10 Vrms into 200 ohm).
- High offset (6 mV max) and bias current (500 nA max) matter in DC-coupled designs like the O2.
- Its reputation rests on drive capability rather than a distinctive sound. The sonic descriptors come from single listening reports.
- A '4556' on vintage boards may be NEC's uPC4556. That part is decompensated (stable only at gain >= 20 dB), so it is not a substitute for an NJM4556 in a unity-gain buffer. It may also be a Yamaha house-marked part.

### 2. Unclassified: NEC (vs JRC), 1980s (uPC4556 on late-1980s Taito boards per MAME notes)

Same '4556' number, different silicon. NEC's uPC4556 is a decompensated high-speed dual derived from its uPC4558 (20 MHz GBW guaranteed at gain > 20 dB, 5 V/us). JRC's NJM4556/NJM4556A is an internally compensated 70 mA / 150 ohm line and headphone driver used at unity gain in the O2 output stage.

- **When:** 1980s (uPC4556 on late-1980s Taito boards per MAME notes)
- **Affected:** uPC4556, uPC4556C, '4556' markings on vintage boards
- **How to tell old from new:** Check the prefix and logo, not the digits. NEC 'uPC4556' (DIP8, C suffix) is a different device from JRC 'NJM4556'/'NJM4556A'.
- **Audio impact:** A uPC4556 placed in an NJM4556 socket at unity or low gain (e.g. the O2 output buffer) risks oscillation, and its heavy-load drive is unknown. An NJM4556A replacing a uPC4556 in a high-gain vintage stage would lose bandwidth and slew.
- **Drop-in risk:** high: a decompensated part is not a drop-in for unity-gain buffer or driver roles, and the pinout is the standard dual but the stability requirements differ
- **Confidence:** medium

|---|---|---|
| Compensation | NJM4556A: internally compensated; used as a unity-gain buffer in the O2 | uPC4556: decompensated; GBW guaranteed only at gain >= 20 dB |

| Slew rate | 3 V/us typ | 5 V/us |
| Output drive | +/-70 mA into 150 ohm (+/-10.5 V min) | not seen in datasheet summaries (no 150 ohm drive claim) |

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remarking reports for NJM4556/NJM4556A were found; search coverage was limited. eBay listings of NJM4556AD advertised as '"Original" JRC' show that DIP stock now circulates on the grey market after the NRND notice; the 2023 保守品 report and the March 2025 datasheet both mark AD/AL as NRND. Remarked 4558/4559-class duals would not deliver 70 mA (unverified risk). Salvaged vintage '4556' parts may be NEC uPC4556, a decompensated part that is unstable at unity gain and not an equivalent, or Yamaha house-marked YM4556 parts. A practical check: at +/-15 V a genuine NJM4556A should hold about +/-11 V into 150 ohm (datasheet min +/-10.5 V), draw about 9 mA per package, and be stable as a unity-gain follower. Buy from authorized Nisshinbo distributors.

## Related parts and alternatives

NJM4556AM / NJM4556AV: the same NJM4556A in DMP8/SSOP8, still active; the direct continuation for new designs, TI OPA1622: high-output-current audio op-amp designed for headphone drive, TI TPA6120A2: current-feedback headphone amplifier with higher output-current capability, TI LME49600 buffer inside the loop of a precision op-amp: for more current than a paralleled NJM4556A, ADI AD8397: high-output-current bipolar dual, NE5532 (SOIC-8): lower-current substitute; the Exits-1U Eurorack module uses it 'in lieu of NJM4556' for the headphone portion

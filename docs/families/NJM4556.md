# NJM4556 / NJM4556A (dual high-output-current bipolar, 70 mA)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

*JRC's 70 mA dual that drives 150 ohm loads directly. It is the paralleled output stage of NwAvGuy's O2 and a staple of Japanese DIY headphone amps.*

**Technology:** Bipolar dual op-amp with a high-current output stage (+/-70 mA into 150 ohm at +/-10.5 V). The input differential pair is PNP in the datasheet equivalent circuit, in NJR's own PSpice macromodels (Version 1 and 2) and in the 2007 Bee Technologies model. Supply is +/-2 V to +/-18 V. The datasheet says it combines many NJM4558 features with 150 ohm drive capability. The Japanese search summary describes it as a 4558 with a reinforced output stage. On the typical curve at +/-15 V, the output sources about 100 mA and sinks about 200 mA before swing collapses.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

It is valued for current drive rather than for a distinctive sound. NwAvGuy chose it for the Objective2 (O2) output stage, with both halves paralleled per channel. A search summary of his 'O2 Details' page says no other dual DIP8 op-amp could deliver as much current at as low distortion, and that for the output stage it 'killed everything else'; it is also made for driving headphones and works well paralleled. The O2 made it a default DIY headphone driver, reused in O2/ODA derivatives (smartstand), Eurorack headphone modules (Forestcaver/AJH, Exits, HeadPho) and other projects. Japanese DIY sources call it a standard (定番) headphone-amp chip: kits use it, and it sounds surprisingly good for about 30 yen. One Japanese listening comparison (audio-simulation.net, 2019) found it more energetic but rougher in texture than the NJM4580. It also appears in commercial gear: a 2018 owner's Behringer UCA202 had a 4556A where NwAvGuy's 2011 unit had a 4558, and the Roland AC-90 uses NJM4556AM for its phones amp.

**How people describe the sound:** energetic / lively (vs NJM4580; single Japanese listening test), rougher texture than NJM4580 (same source), surprisingly good for the price (Japanese DIY summary), clean, low-distortion current drive (NwAvGuy O2 testing)

**Typical uses:** O2 / ODA headphone amp output stage (paralleled sections), DIY and kit headphone amps in Japan (e.g. aitendo kits), Eurorack headphone and line-out modules (NJM4556AV), USB audio interface headphone outputs (one 2018 teardown of a Behringer UCA202), instrument-amp phones outputs (Roland AC-90, NJM4556AM), line drivers into 150-600 ohm loads

**Caveats:**

- The DIP8 (NJM4556AD) and SIP8 (AL) versions are NRND per the March 2025 Nisshinbo datasheet; a maintenance-product designation was reported in April 2023. DIP stock for O2-style builds is winding down. The SMD AM and AV versions continue.
- The SMD packages are JRC DMP8 (wider body than a 3.9 mm JEDEC SOIC-8) and SSOP8, so footprints differ.
- Low package dissipation limits (DMP8 300 mW, SSOP8 250 mW) and 9 mA typ quiescent current per package. Derate for high rails and low-impedance headphones.
- Modest speed (3 V/us, 8 MHz). The typical THD at 20 kHz rises steeply with output level (about 0.2% at 10 Vrms into 200 ohm).
- High offset (6 mV max) and bias current (500 nA max) matter in DC-coupled designs like the O2.
- Its reputation rests on drive capability rather than a distinctive sound. The sonic descriptors come from single listening reports; some listeners find it rough next to the NJM4580 or modern parts.
- A '4556' marking on vintage boards may be a NEC uPC4556 or a Yamaha house-marked part, not a JRC NJM4556.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. JRC / New JRC: before 2003-03 (the NJM4556A datasheet Ver.2003-03-13…

The part number moved from NJM4556 to NJM4556A. No vendor document found says whether this was a new die, a process change or only a re-specification. The non-A datasheet was not read, so no spec comparison is possible.

- **When:** before 2003-03 (the NJM4556A datasheet Ver.2003-03-13 exists; the non-A NJM4556 is in 1980s-1990s equipment)
- **Affected:** NJM4556, NJM4556D, NJM4556S, NJM4556A, NJM4556AD, NJM4556AM, NJM4556AL
- **How to tell old from new:** Part marking: NJM4556 / 4556 with no 'A' (old) versus NJM4556A (new). The SIP suffix changed from S to L.
- **Audio impact:** unknown
- **Drop-in risk:** low (presumed): same function and 8-pin dual op-amp role; but the non-A specs, including output current and minimum supply, were not verified
- **Confidence:** low

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remarking reports for NJM4556/NJM4556A were found in the sources checked; the search coverage was limited. It is a low-cost part, so the incentive is lower than for MUSES or OPA parts. Now that the DIP8 NJM4556AD is NRND (maintenance-product designation reported April 2023; NRND in the March 2025 datasheet), grey-market DIP stock may grow. That could include other 4558/4559-class duals remarked as 4556, which would not deliver 70 mA (unverified risk). Salvaged vintage '4556' parts may be NEC uPC4556 or Yamaha house-marked YM4556, not JRC parts. A practical check: at +/-15 V a genuine NJM4556A should hold about +/-11 V into 150 ohm (datasheet min +/-10.5 V) and draw about 9 mA per package. Buy from authorized Nisshinbo/NJR distributors.

NJM4556AM / NJM4556AV: the same NJM4556A in DMP8/SSOP8, still active; the direct continuation for new designs, TI OPA1622: high-output-current audio op-amp designed for headphone drive, TI TPA6120A2: current-feedback headphone amplifier with higher output-current capability, TI LME49600 buffer inside the loop of a precision op-amp: for more current than a paralleled NJM4556A, ADI AD8397: high-output-current bipolar dual, NE5532 (SOIC-8): lower-current substitute; the Exits-1U Eurorack module uses it 'in lieu of NJM4556' for the headphone portion

- The Ver.2003-03-13 (JA) and Ver.2020-03-26 (EN) editions are known only from search-result titles. Their contents and any spec differences from Ver.2011-06-08 are unknown. Wayback copies of the njr.com URL could fill the 2011-2020 gap.
- The original NJM4556 (non-A) datasheet (alldatasheet id 7450, datasheetbank) was not read. Unknown: its minimum supply voltage, output-current and noise specs, and whether NJM4556A is a new die or a re-specification.
- Is NEC's uPC4556 a licensed or functional equivalent of the NJM4556 (70 mA into 150 ohm), or a different design with the same number? No uPC4556 datasheet was seen.
- Yamaha 'YM4556D' house-marked parts are known only from forum text scraped into an NLP dataset; unconfirmed.
- No PCN or EOL notice number was found for the NRND/maintenance status of NJM4556AD/AL. A blog dated April 2023 reports the maintenance-product designation; the vendor list and final-order date were not seen.
- The JA 20250306 search snippet does not show a 保守品/NRND line on page 1, unlike the EN edition. Check whether the JA edition carries the notice elsewhere.
- Several archive_seed_urls are inferred from other NJR URL patterns and were not seen for this part: http://www.njr.co.jp/products/semicon/PDF/..., http://semicon.njr.co.jp/eng\|jpn/PDF/..., and the non-A NJM4556_E.pdf.
- The NwAvGuy 'killed everything else' wording comes from a search-engine summary of the O2 Details page; the page text itself was not read.
- The Head-Fi op-amp round-up measured the 'JR4556', but its results (THD, output drive) were not extracted.
- No independent bench measurements were found (Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner). The THD figures here are read approximately from the datasheet typical graph.
- The Korg Poly-800 mod-PCB mention of NJM4556 has no traceable source in this record.
- The DMP8 body dimensions come from community footprints only; check them against the Nisshinbo DMP8 package drawing.

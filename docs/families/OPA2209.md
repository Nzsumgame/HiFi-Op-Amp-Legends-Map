# OPA209 / OPA2209 / OPA4209

**Bipolar precision op-amp, 36 V, RRO, 1/2/4 channels** · Texas Instruments (2008–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Low-power 36 V precision part (2.2 nV/√Hz, rail-to-rail output); Head-Fi rollers praise its detail and treble clarity.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.2 nV/√Hz typ | 18 MHz typ | 6.4 V/µs typ | 4.5 V to 36 V (±2.25 V to ±18 V) | 2.5 mA per channel max |

## Why it's rated

A favourite of several rollers in Head-Fi's 'The Opamp thread' and the xDuoo XD-05 threads. One poster calls it their 'favorite bipolar', citing 'amazing resolution and detail level', deep soundstage, air and 'the best treble detail and clarity'. Others found it 'somewhat laid back' like the OP275, or with a brighter, noisier background than the OPA1692. Individual forum impressions.

**Described as:** detailed, high resolution, airy, clear treble, deep soundstage, somewhat laid back (one)

**Used in:** Rolling in xDuoo XD-05 (needs SMD-to-DIP adapter), DAC output and filter stages, Low-noise instrumentation and ADC drivers

## Watch out

- Praise is subjective, from a handful of Head-Fi posters.
- One comparison heard a brighter or noisier background than the OPA1692.
- A precision general-purpose part, not a TI SoundPlus audio part.
- SMD packages only; DIP-8 sockets need an adapter board.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific counterfeit reports found. TI makes the family only in SOT-23-5, MSOP-8, SOIC-8 and TSSOP-14, so any 'DIP-8 OPA2209' is an adapter board or a fake. Check markings; buy from authorised distributors.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

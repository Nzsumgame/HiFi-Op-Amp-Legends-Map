# OPA209 / OPA2209 / OPA4209 (TI 36 V, 2.2 nV/rtHz bipolar, rail-to-rail output)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*A low-power 36 V TI precision op-amp (2.2 nV/rtHz, 2.5 mA/ch max, rail-to-rail output) that Head-Fi op-amp rollers praise for detail and treble clarity.*

**Technology:** Bipolar-input precision op-amp. Supply 4.5 V to 36 V (±2.25 V to ±18 V). 2.2 nV/rtHz at 1 kHz, 18 MHz GBW, 6.4 V/us slew rate, rail-to-rail output, 2.5 mA per channel max quiescent current, 150 uV max offset. SMD packages only: SOT-23-5, MSOP-8, SOIC-8, TSSOP-14.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Several op-amp rollers name it a favourite in Head-Fi's 'The Opamp thread' and in the xDuoo XD-05 op-amp rolling threads. One poster calls it their 'favorite bipolar' and credits it with 'amazing resolution and detail level', 'great soundstage depth', an airy sound and 'the best treble detail and clarity'. Other posters rank it with the OPA2189 and OPA1692 among the top three duals on specs. Some comparisons are more mixed: one found it 'somewhat laid back' like the OP275, and another found its background brighter or noisier than the OPA1692's. These are individual forum impressions and were not re-checked in this pass.

**How people describe the sound:** detailed / high resolution (one poster), airy, clear, detailed treble, deep soundstage, somewhat laid back (one comparison)

**Typical uses:** Op-amp rolling in the xDuoo XD-05 portable DAC/amp and similar socketed gear (needs an SMD-to-DIP adapter), DAC output / filter stages (TI lists high-performance DAC output amplifiers as an application), Low-noise instrumentation and ADC-driver stages

**Caveats:**

- The praise is subjective and comes from a handful of Head-Fi posters.
- One comparison heard a brighter or noisier background than the OPA1692.
- It is a precision general-purpose part, not a TI SoundPlus audio part.
- It is sold in SMD packages only, so DIP-8 sockets need an adapter board.

## Counterfeits

No specific counterfeit reports were found in this pass. TI makes the family only in SMD packages (SOT-23-5, MSOP-8, SOIC-8, TSSOP-14). Any 'DIP-8 OPA2209' must therefore be an SMD die on an adapter board, or a fake. Check the top marking and buy from authorised distributors.

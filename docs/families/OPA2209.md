# OPA209 / OPA2209 / OPA4209 (TI 36 V, 2.2 nV/rtHz bipolar, rail-to-rail output)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

**Technology:** Bipolar-input precision op-amp, 36 V, 2.2 nV/rtHz, 18 MHz GBW, rail-to-rail output, 2.5 mA max supply current

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Several op-amp rollers name it a favourite in Head-Fi's 'The Opamp thread' and in the xDuoo XD-05 op-amp rolling threads. One poster calls it their 'favorite bipolar' and credits it with 'amazing resolution and detail level', 'great soundstage depth', an airy sound and 'the best treble detail and clarity'. Other posters rank it with the OPA2189 and OPA1692 among the top three duals on specs. Some comparisons are more mixed: one found it 'somewhat laid back' like the OP275, and another found its background brighter or noisier than the OPA1692's.

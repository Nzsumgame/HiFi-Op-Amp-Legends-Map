# OPA210 / OPA2210 / OPA4210

**Super-beta bipolar precision op-amp, 36 V, 1/2 channels** · Texas Instruments (before 2018) → Texas Instruments (2018–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*TI's 2018 successor to the OPAx209: 2.2 nV/√Hz, low 1/f noise and µV-level offset on 36 V supplies.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.2 nV/√Hz | 18 MHz | 6.4 V/µs | ±2.25 V to ±18 V (4.5 V to 36 V) | 2.2 mA typ / 2.5 mA max |

## Why it's rated

Liked in phono and DIY circles. Hagerman sells an OPA2210 upgrade for the Bugle MC phono preamp promising about 6 dB less noise, and a TNT-Audio reviewer was surprised by the change. Audiophonics buyers report more clarity and better bass. Head-Fi reports are mixed. The datasheet confirms rail-to-rail output.

**Described as:** more clarity, better bass, low noise (MC phono)

**Used in:** MC phono preamp gain stage (Hagerman Bugle MC), I/V stage after a current-output DAC, op-amp rolling via DIP8 adapter

## Watch out

- DIP8 versions are SOIC-on-adapter builds; TI makes no DIP package.
- Head-Fi: one user got hum after a swap; another heard no difference from LM833 or OPA2107.
- Praise rests on a small number of sources.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit reports. TI makes no DIP-8 (only SOIC-8, VSSOP-8, SON and SOT-23-5 for OPA210), so any 'OPA2210 DIP8' is an SOIC on an adapter. Check the SOIC marking.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

# OPA210 / OPA2210 / OPA4210 (TI 36 V super-beta precision bipolar, 2.2 nV/rtHz)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

**Technology:** Super-beta complementary-bipolar precision op-amp, 36 V, 2.2 nV/rtHz, 0.3 nA bias current, 5 uV offset, 6.4 V/us, rail-to-rail output

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Jim Hagerman (Hagerman Audio Labs) sells an OPA2210 op-amp upgrade for his Bugle MC phono preamp. TNT-Audio published a listening test of the Bugle MC fitted with OPA2210s: it reports a promised 6 dB noise reduction and says the reviewer was surprised how different the Bugle sounded. Audiophonics sells the OPA2210 as a DIP8 rolling unit, and its user reviews report more clarity and better bass, with some caveats. The part also comes up in diyAudio's 'What's the best dual I/V opamp these days???' thread. The evidence is mixed: in Head-Fi's Opamp thread one user reported hum after a swap and another heard no difference from the LM833 or OPA2107.

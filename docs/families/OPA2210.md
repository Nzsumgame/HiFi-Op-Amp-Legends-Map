# OPA210 / OPA2210 / OPA4210 (TI 36 V super-beta precision bipolar, 2.2 nV/rtHz)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*TI's 2018 super-beta bipolar successor to the OPAx209: 2.2 nV/rtHz, low 1/f noise and uV-level offset on 36 V. Sold as an upgrade for the Hagerman Bugle MC phono stage.*

**Technology:** Super-beta complementary-bipolar precision op-amp on TI's precision process. It runs from up to 36 V (+/-18 V) and has 2.2 nV/rtHz noise, 18 MHz GBW, 6.4 V/us slew, 5 uV typ / 35 uV max offset, 300 pA input bias and 2.2 mA typ supply current per amplifier. The draft's rail-to-rail output claim is not confirmed by web search.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Praise comes mainly from the phono and DIY scene. Jim Hagerman (Hagerman Audio Labs) sells an OPA2210 upgrade for his Bugle MC phono preamp, which promises about 6 dB less noise. A TNT-Audio reviewer who tested the upgraded Bugle MC wrote that he was surprised how different it sounded. Audiophonics sells the OPA2210 as a DIP8 rolling unit, and its user reviews report more clarity and better bass, with some caveats. The part also comes up in diyAudio's I/V op-amp discussions. Head-Fi reports are mixed: one user got hum after a swap, and another heard no difference from the LM833 or OPA2107.

**How people describe the sound:** more clarity (Audiophonics user reviews), better bass (Audiophonics user reviews), lower noise in MC phono use (Hagerman/TNT-Audio)

**Typical uses:** MC phono preamp gain stage (Hagerman Bugle MC upgrade), I/V stage after a current-output DAC (diyAudio discussion), op-amp rolling via DIP8 adapter units

**Caveats:**

- The DIP8 versions are SOIC-on-adapter builds; TI makes no DIP package.
- Head-Fi: one user reported hum after a swap, and another heard no difference from the LM833 or OPA2107.
- The praise rests on a small number of sources.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit reports found. TI does not make a DIP-8 package (only SOIC-8, VSSOP-8, SON and, for the OPA210, SOT-23-5), so any 'OPA2210 DIP8' is an SOIC chip on an adapter board. Check the SOIC marking on those.

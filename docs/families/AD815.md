# AD815 (Analog Devices dual high-output-current current-feedback driver op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

*Dual 500 mA current-feedback line driver with 1.85 nV/rtHz noise. DIY builders use it for headphone amps and preamps that can drive almost any load.*

**Technology:** Dual bipolar current-feedback amplifier in a power package. Each amp drives at least 500 mA. It can be wired as one differential driver (40 V p-p on +/-15 V) or as two single-ended drivers. It was designed for xDSL/HDSL line, video and coil/transformer drive. The current datasheet (Rev. D) lists only the 24-lead thermally enhanced SOIC (RB-24); the 15-lead SIP (Y-15, YS-15) and DDPAK (VR-15) packages were deleted. Obsolete.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This is a niche DIY favourite, not a mainstream audio op-amp. Reports come from Head-Fi threads (including a portable OPA132 + AD815 design), s3t.it's 'The Buffa' headphone amp, a long pinkfishmedia AD815 preamp build thread and a Linear Audio 'AD815 Power Amp' article. Head-Fi users say it sounds great as a headphone amp. Pinkfishmedia preamp builders report deep, punchy, well-controlled bass, nice mids and detailed but smooth treble. All of these are subjective forum impressions. Forum users cite TI's THS6012 as a near-equivalent, and some find it slightly cleaner in the treble.

**How people describe the sound:** deep, punchy, well-controlled bass (pinkfishmedia builders), really nice mids (pinkfishmedia builders), detailed but smooth treble (pinkfishmedia builders)

**Typical uses:** DIY headphone amplifier output stage (Head-Fi, s3t.it 'The Buffa'), Line preamp / buffer (pinkfishmedia build thread), Small power amplifier (Linear Audio article), Original target: xDSL/HDSL differential line driver, video distribution, coil and transformer drive

**Caveats:**

- Obsolete. Only the RB-24 SOIC stayed in the datasheet, and the SIP/DDPAK versions that DIY builds used were deleted at Rev. D, so parts are scarce and broker-sourced
- Forum reports say DC offset can be high (up to about 50 mV); datasheet offset specs not checked
- Current-feedback part: it needs the feedback resistor the datasheet specifies (spec'd at RFB = 1 kohm) and is not a drop-in for voltage-feedback op-amp sockets
- The power package needs heat-sinking at high output currents

## Counterfeits

No specific counterfeit reports found. The part is obsolete and scarce, and the SIP/DDPAK versions DIY builds want were deleted years ago, so stock comes from brokers and surplus. Treat re-marked or suspiciously cheap parts with caution, and check the package and markings against the datasheet.

## Related parts and alternatives

TI THS6012: 500 mA dual differential line driver, cited on forums as a near-equivalent and by some as slightly cleaner in the treble, ADI AD816: sibling 500 mA differential driver plus dual low-noise voltage-feedback amps; also obsolete, ADI AD8016: later high-output-current xDSL line driver (from memory, not verified as an audio substitute)

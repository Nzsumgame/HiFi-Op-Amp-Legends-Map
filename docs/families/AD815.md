# AD815 (Analog Devices dual high-output-current current-feedback driver op-amp)

Analog Devices (Mid-1990s (approximate, unconfirmed) until discontinuance (date unconfirmed; possibly PDN 10_0081)) · Tier B (widely praised)

*Dual 500 mA current-feedback line driver with 1.85 nV/rtHz noise. DIY builders use it for headphone amps and preamps that can drive almost any load.*

| Slew rate |
|---|
| 900 V/us (differential) |

## Why it's rated

This is a niche DIY favourite, not a mainstream audio op-amp. Reports come from Head-Fi threads (including a portable OPA132 + AD815 design), s3t.it's 'The Buffa' headphone amp, a long pinkfishmedia AD815 preamp build thread and a Linear Audio 'AD815 Power Amp' article. Head-Fi users say it sounds great as a headphone amp. Pinkfishmedia preamp builders report deep, punchy, well-controlled bass, nice mids and detailed but smooth treble. All of these are subjective forum impressions. Forum users cite TI's THS6012 as a near-equivalent, and some find it slightly cleaner in the treble.

**Described as:** deep, punchy, well-controlled bass (pinkfishmedia builders), really nice mids (pinkfishmedia builders), detailed but smooth treble (pinkfishmedia builders)

**Used in:** DIY headphone amplifier output stage (Head-Fi, s3t.it 'The Buffa'), Line preamp / buffer (pinkfishmedia build thread), Small power amplifier (Linear Audio article), Original target: xDSL/HDSL differential line driver, video distribution, coil and transformer drive

## Watch out

- Obsolete. Only the RB-24 SOIC stayed in the datasheet, and the SIP/DDPAK versions that DIY builds used were deleted at Rev. D, so parts are scarce and broker-sourced
- Forum reports say DC offset can be high (up to about 50 mV); datasheet offset specs not checked
- Current-feedback part: it needs the feedback resistor the datasheet specifies (spec'd at RFB = 1 kohm) and is not a drop-in for voltage-feedback op-amp sockets
- The power package needs heat-sinking at high output currents
- **Counterfeits:** No specific counterfeit reports found. The part is obsolete and scarce, and the SIP/DDPAK versions DIY builds want were deleted years ago, so stock comes from brokers and surplus. Treat re-marked or suspiciously cheap parts with caution, and check the package and markings against the datasheet.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

<summary>Verification and open questions</summary>

Status: **verified-with-corrections**, 8 web searches.

**Confirmed:**

- The current ADI AD815 datasheet is Rev. D (https://www.radiolocman.com/datasheet/pdf.html?di=170539&p=1; https://manualzz.com/doc/48845416/ad815--rev.-d-).
- Rev. D revision history: deleted the VR-15, Y-15 and YS-15 packages and changed the Ordering Guide (https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/AD815.pdf).
- Two amps, each supplying at least 500 mA; 40 V p-p differential on +/-15 V (https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ad815.pdf).
- 900 V/us differential slew, 120 MHz -3 dB bandwidth, 70 ns settling to 0.1%, 1.85 nV/rtHz at 10 kHz, -66 dB THD at 1 MHz into 200 ohm at 40 V p-p (https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ad815.pdf).
- 0.05%/0.45 deg differential gain/phase into 25 ohm; up to 12 back-terminated loads (https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ad815.pdf).
- The current datasheet lists the 24-pin SOIC (RB) only, industrial -40 to +85 C (https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ad815.pdf).
- AD815ARBZ-24 and AD815ARBZ-24-REEL are listed as Obsolete (https://www.mouser.com/ProductDetail/Analog-Devices/AD815ARBZ-24?qs=Lds8L0UsNW4AveIzWG7liw%3D%3D).
- An older datasheet copy names AD815-EB, AD815AYS, AD815AY and AD815AVR (https://manualmachine.com/analogdevices/ad815ays/3999610-datasheet).
- The sibling AD816 datasheet sits in ADI's obsolete-data-sheets folder (https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/obsolete-data-sheets/AD816.pdf).

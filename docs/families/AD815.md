# AD815 (Analog Devices dual high-output-current current-feedback driver op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

*ADI's brawny dual current-feedback line driver (~500 mA per side) that DIY builders drive headphones and preamp outputs with directly.*

**Technology:** Dual bipolar current-feedback, high-output-current (about 500 mA) differential or dual line driver; EOL

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Niche DIY favourite, not a mainstream 'rolling' op-amp. It shows up in a Head-Fi 'AD815' thread, the s3t.it 'The Buffa' headphone amp, a long pinkfishmedia 'AD815 preamp build thread' and Linear Audio's 'AD815 Power Amp' article. Head-Fi users say it 'sounds great' as a headphone amp. Pinkfishmedia preamp builders describe deep, punchy, well-controlled bass, really nice mids and detailed but smooth treble. TI's THS6012 is cited as a near-equivalent, and some builders find it a bit cleaner in the treble. These are subjective forum impressions.

**How people describe the sound:** deep, punchy, well-controlled bass (pinkfishmedia preamp builders), really nice mids (pinkfishmedia), detailed but smooth treble (pinkfishmedia), 'sounds great' as a headphone amp (Head-Fi)

**Typical uses:** Headphone amplifier output stage (e.g. s3t.it 'The Buffa'), Line-level preamp / buffer stage (pinkfishmedia build thread), Small power amplifier (Linear Audio 'AD815 Power Amp'), Portable headphone amp paired with OPA132 (Head-Fi development thread)

**Caveats:**

- High output DC offset reported (up to about 50 mV); a DC servo or output coupling may be needed
- EOL, with poor availability of genuine parts
- Power packages (SIP/DDPAK) need proper heatsinking at high output current
- Current-feedback topology: the feedback resistor sets stability, so no capacitors directly in the feedback path and no plain voltage-feedback socket swaps

## Counterfeits

No part-specific counterfeit reports were found (no web search ran). As an EOL, hard-to-find ADI part, marketplace stock (eBay/AliExpress) may be pulls or remarked devices. Check the package style (15-lead power SIP/DDPAK, 24-lead SOIC), the ADI logo and date-code format, and the quiescent current and DC offset before trusting it.

## Related parts and alternatives

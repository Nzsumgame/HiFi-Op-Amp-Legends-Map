# AD815 (Analog Devices dual high-output-current current-feedback driver op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Headphone drivers

**Technology:** Dual bipolar current-feedback, high-output-current (about 500 mA) differential or dual line driver; EOL

## Why enthusiasts rate it

DIY headphone amps and preamps have been built around it: a Head-Fi 'AD815' thread, the s3t.it 'The Buffa' headphone amp, a long pinkfishmedia 'AD815 preamp build thread', and Linear Audio's 'AD815 Power Amp' article. Users say it 'sounds great' as a headphone amp. Preamp builders describe deep, punchy, well-controlled bass, really nice mids and detailed but smooth treble. Caveats: high DC offset (up to about 50 mV), EOL status and poor availability. TI's THS6012 is cited as a near-equivalent, which some find a bit cleaner in the treble.

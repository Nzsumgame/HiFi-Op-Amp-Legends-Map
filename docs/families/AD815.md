# AD815

**Dual current-feedback driver, 500 mA per amp** · Analog Devices (mid-1990s (unconfirmed)–discontinued) · Tier B (widely praised)

*Dual 500 mA current-feedback line driver with 1.85 nV/√Hz noise; a DIY favourite for headphone amps and preamps.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.85 nV/√Hz | 120 MHz −3 dB bandwidth (current feedback) | 900 V/µs (differential) | −66 dB THD | ±15 V (spec conditions) |

## Why it's rated

A niche DIY favourite, not a mainstream audio op-amp. Reports come from Head-Fi (including a portable OPA132 + AD815 design), s3t.it's 'The Buffa', a pinkfishmedia preamp build thread and a Linear Audio power-amp article. Users report great headphone sound, deep punchy bass, nice mids and smooth detailed treble. Forums cite TI's THS6012 as near-equivalent, some say slightly cleaner treble.

**Described as:** deep, punchy bass, well-controlled bass, really nice mids, detailed but smooth treble

**Used in:** DIY headphone amp output stage (Head-Fi, 'The Buffa'), Line preamp / buffer (pinkfishmedia build), Small power amplifier (Linear Audio article), Designed for xDSL/HDSL lines, video, coil/transformer drive

## Watch out

- Obsolete; SIP/DDPAK versions DIY builds used were deleted at Rev. D, so parts are scarce.
- Forums report DC offset up to about 50 mV; datasheet offset specs not checked.
- Current feedback: needs the specified feedback resistor (RFB = 1 kΩ); not a voltage-feedback drop-in.
- Power package needs heat-sinking at high output currents.
- Impressions are subjective forum reports.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific counterfeit reports. Obsolete and scarce; the SIP/DDPAK versions DIY builds want were deleted years ago, so stock comes from brokers and surplus. Distrust re-marked or cheap parts; check package and markings against the datasheet.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

**Confirmed:**

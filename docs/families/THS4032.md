# THS4032 (TI dual high-speed low-noise voltage-feedback op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

**Technology:** High-speed low-noise bipolar dual voltage-feedback op-amp (not designed for audio)

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Head-Fi has a dedicated thread on it, and it appears across the long-running 'The Opamp thread'. Users call it a 'highly recommended opamp for audio although it was not designed with audio in mind', 'bright, detailed, clean, well separated', and say it has 'probably the best imaging' against the AD797. Caveats from the same posts: it runs hot, can oscillate, and one reviewer found it thin in the mids.

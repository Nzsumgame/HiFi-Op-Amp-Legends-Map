# THS4032 (TI dual high-speed low-noise voltage-feedback op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*1.6 nV/rtHz, 100 MHz TI video/comms dual that Head-Fi op-amp rollers praise for detail and imaging, despite heat and oscillation risk.*

**Technology:** Dual high-speed, ultra-low-voltage-noise voltage-feedback op-amp on TI's 30-V dielectrically isolated complementary bipolar process (NPN/PNP fT of several GHz). Made for communications, imaging and ADC buffering, not for audio.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Datasheet respec).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Head-Fi has a dedicated thread on the THS4032, and it comes up across the long-running 'The Opamp thread'. Posters there call it 'highly recommended for audio although it was not designed with audio in mind', 'bright, detailed, clean, well separated', and one says it has 'probably the best imaging' next to the AD797. These are forum impressions from op-amp rolling, not measurements.

**How people describe the sound:** bright, detailed, clean, well separated, strong imaging (per one Head-Fi comparison vs AD797), thin mids (one reviewer)

**Typical uses:** Op-amp rolling in DACs and headphone amps (e.g., Beresford TC7520 DAC thread), I/V and output stages in DIY audio, Designed for: communications, imaging, low-noise ADC buffering

**Caveats:**

- Runs hot (about 8.5 mA per channel quiescent)
- Can oscillate in sockets or poor layouts: 275 MHz unity-gain bandwidth needs tight decoupling
- Surface-mount only (SOIC-8 / MSOP PowerPAD), so rolling needs a DIP adapter
- Datasheet headline specs changed between revisions (settling 60 ns to 70 ns)
- One reviewer found it thin in the mids

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Datasheet respec: Texas Instruments, Between SLOS224C (Apr 2000) and the current new-format…

There is no evidence of a die change; only the datasheet headline specs moved. The current datasheet and product pages quote 70 ns 0.1% settling, -96 dBc THD at 1 MHz and 1.2 nV/rtHz, where older revisions gave 60 ns settling (confirmed). The older -90 dBc THD and 1.6 nV/rtHz figures are unconfirmed. This looks like re-characterisation rather than new silicon.

- **When:** Between SLOS224C (Apr 2000) and the current new-format revision (exact revision unknown)
- **Affected:** THS4031, THS4032
- **How to tell old from new:** None known. No PCN, new-die note or marking change was found; compare the datasheet revision in use.
- **Audio impact:** Likely negligible for audio. Settling in the tens of ns is far beyond audio bandwidth.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: pinout and package unchanged; no PCN found; the differences appear to be datasheet-level only
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Verification:** Search confirmed that the current THS4032 documentation quotes 70 ns settling (0.1%), -96 dBc THD at 1 MHz and 1.2 nV/rtHz, and that older revisions list 60 ns. The older THD and noise values were not confirmed, and no PCN or die-change evidence was found.

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Settling time (0.1%) | 60 ns | 70 ns |
| THD at 1 MHz | -90 dBc (unconfirmed) | -96 dBc |
| Voltage noise headline | 1.6 nV/rtHz (unconfirmed) | 1.2 nV/rtHz |

### Revision chain status

Texas Instruments SLOS224 (THS4031/THS4032; TI is the only vendor, with no second sources or acquirer re-issues). Chain, oldest to newest: - Original: July 1999, known only as the 'JULY 1999' base date on every later header. No copy of it has been found. - A and B: missing; no dates or copies. - C: April 2000, alldatasheet THS4032EVM copy (38 pages). A datasheetarchive SLOS224C listing was added. alldatasheet 28722 and ic37 735153 (38 pages) may also be rev C but do not show it. - D, E, F: missing; not found in the search index (one query for D/E/F/B returned nothing). - G: March 2010. No copy has been matched to it. - H: June 2016. It is quoted by a mirror, but which of RadioLocman or ManualMachine was not stated. - I: May 2018, confirmed header 'SLOS224I – JULY 1999 – REVISED MAY 2018'; the DigiKey HTML copy is likely. - J: February 2024 per earlier summaries; header 'SLOS224J – JULY 1999' confirmed. - K: exists but undated; it must fall between February and July 2024 if the J and L dates are right. - L: July 2024 (new). This is the current ti.com symlink. Still missing: dates and copies for the original, A, B, D, E, F and K; the exact copy that holds G; and which revision changed the 0.1% settling time from 60 ns to 70 ns. Note that the search summaries of the current symlink still quote 60 ns, which conflicts with the earlier note of 70 ns. Separate THS4031-EP/THS4032-EP Enhanced Product datasheet: its literature number (probably SGLSxxx, not confirmed) and revisions are unknown. Copies with no revision shown were added: alldatasheet 463260 and datasheet4u 1488360/1488359. Archive seeds for Wayback have been added to recover early revisions: focus.ti.com symlinks, www-s.ti.com/sc/ds and ti.com/lit/gpn.

## Counterfeits

No THS4032-specific counterfeit reports were found in this pass. It is a surface-mount-only part often bought pre-mounted on DIP adapters from marketplace sellers, so buy from authorized distributors and check TI markings and date codes.

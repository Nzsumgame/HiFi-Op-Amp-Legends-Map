# THS4032 / THS4031 / THS4032-EP

**High-speed low-noise VFB op-amp, 1/2 channels** · Texas Instruments (1999–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** No evidence of a die change; only headline specs moved. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*TI's 1.6 nV/√Hz, 100 MHz comms dual that Head-Fi rollers praise for detail and imaging, despite heat and oscillation.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.6 nV/√Hz (Rev C); current datasheet reportedly 1.2 | 100 MHz −3 dB (G = +2); 275 MHz unity-gain | 100 V/µs typ | −90 dBc (Rev C) → −96 dBc (current, reported) | ±4.5 V to ±16 V | 8.5 mA per channel typ |

## Why it's rated

Head-Fi has a dedicated THS4032 thread, and it recurs in 'The Opamp thread'. Posters call it 'highly recommended for audio although it was not designed with audio in mind' and 'bright, detailed, clean, well separated'; one rates its imaging next to the AD797. These are rolling impressions, not measurements.

**Described as:** bright, detailed, clean, well separated, strong imaging (vs AD797), thin mids (one reviewer)

**Used in:** Op-amp rolling in DACs and headphone amps (Beresford TC7520), I/V and output stages in DIY audio, Designed for communications, imaging, low-noise ADC buffering

## Watch out

- Runs hot: about 8.5 mA per channel quiescent.
- Can oscillate in sockets or poor layouts; 275 MHz needs tight decoupling.
- SMD only (SOIC-8 / MSOP PowerPAD); rolling needs a DIP adapter.
- Headline specs changed between revisions (settling 60 → 70 ns).
- One reviewer found the mids thin.
- **Counterfeits:** No THS4032-specific counterfeit reports found. It is SMD-only and often sold pre-mounted on DIP adapters by marketplace sellers; buy from authorized distributors and check TI markings and date codes.

## Silicon changes

### Datasheet respec: Texas Instruments, Post-SLOS224C (2000-04), rev unknown

No evidence of a die change; only headline specs moved. Current documents quote 70 ns settling, −96 dBc THD at 1 MHz and 1.2 nV/√Hz; older revisions gave 60 ns (confirmed), −90 dBc and 1.6 nV/√Hz (unconfirmed). Looks like re-characterisation, not new silicon.

- **Affected:** THS4031, THS4032
- **How to tell:** None known. No PCN, new-die note or marking change was found; compare the datasheet revision in use.
- **Audio impact:** Likely negligible for audio. Settling in the tens of ns is far beyond audio bandwidth.
- **Drop-in risk:** low – pinout and package unchanged; no PCN; datasheet-level differences only.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Settling time (0.1%) | 60 ns | 70 ns |
| THD at 1 MHz | -90 dBc (unconfirmed) | -96 dBc |
| Voltage noise headline | 1.6 nV/rtHz (unconfirmed) | 1.2 nV/rtHz |

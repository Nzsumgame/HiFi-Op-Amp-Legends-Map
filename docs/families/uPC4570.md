# µPC4570

**Bipolar dual, ultra-low-noise, wideband** · NEC (–2010) → Renesas Electronics (2010–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** Renesas replaced the NEC datasheet with R03DS0135EJ: Rev. 1.00 (17 Jan 2019, 9 pages), Rev. 2.00 (27 Feb 2025, 11 pages). [Details](#silicon-changes)

*NEC's 4.5 nV/√Hz, 15 MHz, 7 V/µs dual, pitched as a 4558 upgrade; Japanese modders hear tighter bass than NJM4580DD.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5 nV/√Hz | 15 MHz | 7 V/µs | ±4 V to ±16 V (unconfirmed) |

## Why it's rated

A cult part among Japanese modders. NEC claims 3× lower noise and 7× the GBW and slew rate of the 4558; the C-Area wiki calls it 4558-derived, like the NJM4580. Versus NJM4580DD it has tighter bass, similar treble, maybe less resolution. Common in Japanese CD players, AV receivers and Tascam multitracks. No independent measurements found.

**Described as:** tight bass (vs NJM4580DD), treble like NJM4580, slightly less resolution

**Used in:** CD player output and filter stages, AV receivers and mini-systems (Yamaha AV-S70, Sony HCD-GRX80), cassette multitrack preamps (Tascam 424 MKI, 238S), preamp and tone-control kits (AIYIMA boards), op-amp rolling as an NJM4580 alternative

## Watch out

- DIP-8 µPC4570C is reportedly a maintenance product; aftermarket supply via Rochester Electronics.
- Needs about ±4 V minimum; unsuited to low-voltage single-rail designs.
- SIP-9 (HA) and SOP-8 (G2) versions do not fit DIP-8 sockets.
- Sound claims are Japanese-community folklore; no independent measurements.
- Tascam 424 MKI sound partly credited to its UPC4570 + NJM4565 (JHS 424 Gain Stage).
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit reports. DIP µPC4570C sells via surplus and hobby channels (Suzusho, AliExpress kits); Rochester Electronics is an authorised aftermarket source. Remarked vintage Japanese op-amps are a general risk. LCSC 'NEC4570G2' origin unverified; top-mark formats not seen.

- **Audio impact:** Unknown; no silicon change documented and datasheet deltas not known.

- **Confidence:** low

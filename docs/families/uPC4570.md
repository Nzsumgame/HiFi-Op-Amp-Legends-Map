# µPC4570 (NEC / Renesas ultra-low-noise dual bipolar)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*NEC's ultra-low-noise dual (4.5 nV/√Hz, 15 MHz, 7 V/µs), pitched as a 4558 upgrade. Japanese modders credit it with tighter bass than the NJM4580DD.*

**Technology:** Internally compensated bipolar dual op-amp for dual supplies. It has ultra-low noise, wide bandwidth and a high slew rate. The datasheet benchmarks it against the 4558: 3x lower input noise, and 7x higher GBW and slew rate. The PNP input pair comes from the OrCAD JOPAMP macromodel, and the 'developed from the 4558' lineage comes from the C-Area wiki. No vendor schematic was seen.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Unclassified).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A cult part among Japanese modders rather than a Western audiophile legend. The C-Area wiki describes it as NEC's audio op-amp developed from the 4558, like the JRC NJM4580. Comparing it with the NJM4580DD, the wiki says it has tighter bass, similar treble and possibly slightly lower resolution, and calls this a matter of taste. It appears widely in Japanese CD players, AV receivers, mini-systems and Tascam multitracks. The Tascam 424 MKI channel sound is partly credited to its UPC4570 + NJM4565 op-amps (JHS 424 Gain Stage). No independent measurements were found.

**How people describe the sound:** tight / firm bass (compared with NJM4580DD, per C-Area wiki), high-frequency extension comparable to NJM4580, possibly slightly less resolution than NJM4580 (subjective)

**Typical uses:** CD player output and filter stages (Japanese domestic players), AV receiver audio paths (Yamaha AV-S70, µPC4570G2), mini-system audio (Sony HCD-GRX80, UPC4570C-1), cassette multitrack channel preamps (Tascam Portastudio 424 MKI; Tascam 238S uses µPC4570HA), preamps and tone-control kits (e.g. AIYIMA boards sold with UPC4570C), op-amp rolling in Japanese DIY as an NJM4580 alternative

**Caveats:**

- The DIP-8 µPC4570C is reportedly a maintenance (保守) product, and aftermarket supply goes through channels such as Rochester Electronics.
- Minimum supply of about ±4 V (not re-confirmed) makes it unsuitable for low-voltage single-rail designs.
- The SIP-9 (HA) and SOP-8 (G2) versions are not drop-in replacements for DIP-8 sockets.
- The sound claims are Japanese-community folklore, and no independent distortion or noise measurements were found.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Unclassified: Renesas Electronics (ex-NEC), New Renesas datasheet R03DS0135EJ0100 Rev.1.00 dated 17…

Renesas replaced the NEC-era µPC4570 datasheet (G10528EJ8V0DS00) with a new Renesas document, R03DS0135EJ: Rev.1.00 in January 2019 and Rev.2.00 in February 2025. The product page now titles it 'High-Voltage, Bipolar Dual Operational Amplifier, Dual-Power Supply, Ultra Low-Noise, High-Speed, Wide Band for Consumer Products'. The rewrite may include respecs, but the spec content of each revision was not retrieved. Separately, Renesas PCN RL-BB-23-0058, 'Addition of back-end production for general-purpose linear IC products (SOP package products)', lists UPC4570 SOP orderables. That is an assembly-site addition, not a die change. New orderables such as UPC4570GR-9LG-E1-A and UPC4570G2-E1-AP appear on the Renesas site. The part remains active.

- **When:** New Renesas datasheet R03DS0135EJ0100 Rev.1.00 dated 17 Jan 2019, and Rev.2.00 dated 27 Feb 2025. PCN RL-BB-23-0058 is from 2023, exact date not captured.
- **Affected:** UPC4570G2-E1-A, UPC4570G2-E2-A, UPC4570AG2-E1-A (as listed in the PCN search summary), UPC4570G2-E1-AP, UPC4570GR-9LG-E1-A, UPC4570C-A
- **How to tell old from new:** Datasheet document number R03DS0135EJ (Rev.1.00 / Rev.2.00) versus the NEC-era G10528EJ8V0DS00. The assembly-site change has no marking rule in the retrieved summary.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. No silicon change is documented, and the datasheet deltas, if any, were not captured.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - same die as far as documented; watch the Rev.2.00 limits and the new orderable suffixes before substituting.
- **Confidence:** low

## Counterfeits

No reports of counterfeit µPC4570 parts were found. The DIP µPC4570C is reportedly a maintenance product and is sold through surplus and hobby channels, such as Suzusho and AliExpress tone-control kits advertised with 'UPC4570C'. DigiKey Marketplace lists UPC4570C-A through Rochester Electronics, an authorized aftermarket source. Remarked vintage Japanese op-amps are a general risk from hobby channels. The LCSC/EasyEDA 'NEC4570G2' part has an unverified origin. The actual top-mark formats were not seen.

## Related parts and alternatives

NJM4580 (JRC / Nisshinbo): the closest functional counterpart, also described as 4558-derived, NJM2114 (JRC / Nisshinbo): low-noise bipolar dual used in similar Japanese equipment, NE5532 / LM833: pin-compatible generic low-noise bipolar duals, OPA1602 (TI): modern low-noise bipolar dual, LM4562 / LME49720 (TI): modern low-distortion bipolar dual

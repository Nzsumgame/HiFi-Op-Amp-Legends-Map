# µPC4570 (NEC / Renesas ultra-low-noise dual bipolar)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*NEC's ultra-low-noise dual (4.5 nV/√Hz, 15 MHz, 7 V/µs), pitched as a 4558 upgrade. Japanese modders credit it with tighter bass than the NJM4580DD.*

**Technology:** Internally compensated bipolar dual op-amp for dual supplies. It has ultra-low noise, wide bandwidth and a high slew rate. The datasheet benchmarks it against the 4558: 3x lower input noise, and 7x higher GBW and slew rate. The PNP input pair comes from the OrCAD JOPAMP macromodel, and the 'developed from the 4558' lineage comes from the C-Area wiki. No vendor schematic was seen.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Datasheet respec).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A cult part among Japanese modders rather than a Western audiophile legend. The C-Area wiki describes it as NEC's audio op-amp developed from the 4558, like the JRC NJM4580. Comparing it with the NJM4580DD, the wiki says it has tighter bass, similar treble and possibly slightly lower resolution, and calls this a matter of taste. It appears widely in Japanese CD players, AV receivers, mini-systems and Tascam multitracks. The Tascam 424 MKI channel sound is partly credited to its UPC4570 + NJM4565 op-amps (JHS 424 Gain Stage). No independent measurements were found.

**How people describe the sound:** tight / firm bass (compared with NJM4580DD, per C-Area wiki), high-frequency extension comparable to NJM4580, possibly slightly less resolution than NJM4580 (subjective)

**Typical uses:** CD player output and filter stages (Japanese domestic players), AV receiver audio paths (Yamaha AV-S70, µPC4570G2), mini-system audio (Sony HCD-GRX80, UPC4570C-1), cassette multitrack channel preamps (Tascam Portastudio 424 MKI; Tascam 238S uses µPC4570HA), preamps and tone-control kits (e.g. AIYIMA boards sold with UPC4570C), op-amp rolling in Japanese DIY as an NJM4580 alternative

**Caveats:**

- The DIP-8 µPC4570C is reportedly a maintenance (保守) product, and aftermarket supply goes through channels such as Rochester Electronics.
- Minimum supply of about ±4 V (not re-confirmed) makes it unsuitable for low-voltage single-rail designs.
- The SIP-9 (HA) and SOP-8 (G2) versions are not drop-in replacements for DIP-8 sockets.
- The sound claims are Japanese-community folklore, and no independent distortion or noise measurements were found.

## Other change notes (lifecycle, packaging, successors, lore)

### 1. Package / assembly: Renesas Electronics (ex-NEC), PCN RL-BB-23-0058 (2023;

Renesas added, then transferred, the back-end factory for its general-purpose linear IC SOP products, including the uPC4570 SOP8. The PCNs say the plating and bond-wire specifications differ at the new back end, so the part numbers change (-A to -AP). Some materials differ but are already in mass production, the outline drawing has minor dimensional differences with the same recommended footprint, and the tape packaging box size differs. This is an assembly change; the die is not reported to change. DIP8 (UPC4570C-A) and TSSOP8 (GR-9LG) orderables are not covered by these SOP PCNs.

- **When:** PCN RL-BB-23-0058 (2023; SOP-8 samples available 25 Aug 2023) added a back-end (assembly/test) factory. PCN RL-BB-24-0007 (published 19 Jan 2024, effective 13 May 2024) then transferred SOP back-end production.
- **Affected:** UPC4570G2-A, UPC4570G2-E1-A, UPC4570G2-AP, UPC4570G2-E1-AP
- **How to tell old from new:** New orderable suffix '-AP' replaces '-A' on SOP8 (G2) parts, e.g. UPC4570G2-E1-A -> UPC4570G2-E1-AP. The '-AP' part-details page exists on renesas.com. No top-marking rule was captured.
- **Audio impact:** None expected. The die is unchanged, and a bond-wire or plating change has no documented electrical effect at the datasheet level.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - same die and footprint. Update BOMs and AVLs for the new -AP suffix and check the minor outline differences for tight mechanical fits.
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Verification:** Four WebSearches returned summaries of RL-BB-23-0058 (back-end addition, plating and wire differ, part numbers change, SOP-8 samples 8/25/2023) and RL-BB-24-0007 (19 Jan 2024 publication, 13 May 2024 effective, UPC4570 listed), plus the -A to -AP mapping for UPC4570G2 and the live Renesas UPC4570G2-E1-AP page. The PCN PDFs were not read directly.

### Revision chain status

The uPC4570 has one vendor document chain: NEC, then Renesas. No second sources were found.  (1) NEC data sheet G10528. English editions: EJ7V0DS00 (7th), then EJ8V0DS00 (8th). The 8th is re-served by Renesas at /en/ and /us/en/ with the 'Old Company Name' cover after 2010-04-01. Japanese edition: JJ8V0DS00. Missing links: the 1st through 6th editions (G10528EJ1V0 to EJ6V0, and the matching JJ editions), plus any pre-G-number NEC IC-/IEM- document for the original introduction. No search summary showed them, and the publication dates of the 7th and 8th editions were not shown either. The early alldatasheet entries 6770 (UPC4570), 6771 (UPC4570C) and 6773 (UPC4570HA), and datasheet4u entries 368239 and 1480871 (UPC4570HA SIP), are the likeliest copies of older NEC editions, but their revisions are unconfirmed.  (2) Renesas R03DS0135. The chain is R03DS0135EJ0100 Rev.1.00 (2019.1.17, 9 pages), then R03DS0135EJ0200 Rev.2.00 (Feb.27.25, 11 pages). Rev.1.00 is confirmed in the known copy at datasheet4u.com/pdf-down/u/P/C/uPC4570-Renesas.pdf, whose header reads 'R03DS0135EJ0100 Rev.1.00 Page 1 of 9 2019.1.17'. The existing entry for that URL can be upgraded from 'revision not seen' to Rev.1.00 with high confidence. Rev.2.00 is at renesas.com/en/document/dst/upc4570-datasheet. Missing: any intermediate Rev.1.xx between 2019 and 2025. None was seen.  No legacy NEC Electronics URLs were found (e.g. necel.com/nesdis/image/G10528...), so none were added as archive seeds.

## Counterfeits

No reports of counterfeit µPC4570 parts were found. The DIP µPC4570C is reportedly a maintenance product and is sold through surplus and hobby channels, such as Suzusho and AliExpress tone-control kits advertised with 'UPC4570C'. DigiKey Marketplace lists UPC4570C-A through Rochester Electronics, an authorized aftermarket source. Remarked vintage Japanese op-amps are a general risk from hobby channels. The LCSC/EasyEDA 'NEC4570G2' part has an unverified origin. The actual top-mark formats were not seen.

## Related parts and alternatives

NJM4580 (JRC / Nisshinbo): the closest functional counterpart, also described as 4558-derived, NJM2114 (JRC / Nisshinbo): low-noise bipolar dual used in similar Japanese equipment, NE5532 / LM833: pin-compatible generic low-noise bipolar duals, OPA1602 (TI): modern low-noise bipolar dual, LM4562 / LME49720 (TI): modern low-distortion bipolar dual

# OPA828 / OPA2828

**JFET-input (SiGe BiFET) op-amp, 1/2 channels, 45 MHz** · Texas Instruments (2018–present) → Burr-Brown (late 1980s–) · Tier B (widely praised)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** 'Same part number, different datasheet', not a die change. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*TI's 2018 'next-generation OPA627': 4 nV/√Hz, 45 MHz, 150 V/µs, −130 dB THD+N. Rollers call it extremely neutral.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 nV/√Hz (100 Hz: 4.8, 10 Hz: 7.5) | 45 MHz; 57° phase margin; 8% overshoot | 150 V/µs; settling 110 ns (12-bit), 120 ns (14-bit) | 0.000028% (−130 dB); IMD CCIF 137 dB, SMPTE 132 dB | ±4 V to ±18 V (8–36 V); abs. max 40 V (±20 V) | 5.5 mA typ / 6.2 mA max per amp (7.9 mA at 125 °C) |

## Why it's rated

Rollers treat it as the modern OPA627, as TI positions it. In diyAudio's 'OPA828 / OPA2828 vs OPA627' thread users call it extremely neutral, very transparent, detailed and fatigue-free, above the OPA1642, with no oscillation even on breadboards; OPA627 fans may find it 'thin'. The Q17 power amp swapped its OPA1641 for OPA828. No ASR or Samuel Groner measurements.

**Described as:** extremely neutral, very wide soundstage, very transparent, lots of micro-detail, no listening fatigue, 'thin' to OPA627 fans, elegant, spacious (JP blogs), low colouration (JP blogs)

**Used in:** DAC I/V and output stages, line buffers, preamps and RIAA phono stages, headphone amps with a separate buffer (OPA828 + LME49600), power-amp input stage (Q17 amplifier), rolling on SOIC-to-DIP adapters (Audiophonics DIP8 unit)

## Watch out

- No DIP; adapters add parasitics. Solder the DGN pad to V− or GND.
- Fast: isolate capacitive loads (e.g. 50 Ω); layout and decoupling matter.
- Slew-boost engages when inputs are driven far apart and 'can distort the signal' in overload.
- Not rail-to-rail despite 'RRO' blurbs: needs ±4 V and 0.9–1.2 V output headroom.
- 5.5 mA per channel heats SOIC ~20 °C; JFET bias rises steeply with temperature.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeits documented. TI makes no DIP: any DIP 'OPA828/OPA2828' is an adapter. OPA2828 exists only as HVSSOP-8 marked '2QGJ'; SOIC OPA828 is marked 'OPA828', DGN '2RAJ'. Check marking, ~150 V/µs slew and pA bias.

## Silicon changes

### Datasheet respec: Texas Instruments, 2022-11-17 · PCN20221117006

'Same part number, different datasheet', not a die change. PCN20221117006 revised SOIC OPA828ID/IDR limits 'to accurately reflect device characteristics', with no device or fit/form/function change. Changed parameters unknown; possibly linked to a 2019 E2E output-swing report. Falls between Rev C (Oct 2022) and Rev D (Dec 2022).

- **Affected:** OPA828ID, OPA828IDR
- **How to tell:** No marking or orderable change; parts are physically identical. Only the datasheet differs: SBOS671B (Dec 2018) old limits, SBOS671C/D revised limits.
- **Audio impact:** None expected: silicon unchanged; noise, GBW, slew and THD+N typicals match Rev B and Rev D.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: spec-only, same device and marking; re-check budgets qualified against Rev B limits.
- **Confidence:** high

# AD8610 / AD8620 (single/dual precision low-noise JFET)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** JFET-input

*ADI's low-noise (about 6 nV/rtHz), 25 MHz trimmed JFET op-amp. Portable-amp makers and op-amp rollers use it as an OPA627-class choice for DAC filters and headphone stages.*

**Technology:** JFET-input precision op-amp from Analog Devices. The ADI Op Amp Applications Handbook calls the AD8610B/AD8620B 'the very best trimmed JFET input types', with about 100 uV offset. The trim method is not confirmed: the handbook names DigiTrim only for CMOS parts, and the process (XFCB or other) was not verified. The single AD8610 comes in SOIC-8 (R) and MSOP-8 (RM); the MSOP orderables seen are all reel-only. The dual AD8620 comes in SOIC-8 only, and neither has a DIP version. ADI's Eagle library draws the AD8610 with offset-trim pins.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Popular with Head-Fi and diyAudio op-amp rollers since the mid-2000s. Tangentsoft's 'Notes on Audio Op-Amps' (Warren Young; Chinese translation on DiyEden/Soomal, 2005) calls it the author's favourite chip, especially for battery amps. It likens the AD8610 to the AD823 without some of the aggressiveness and advises using it in laid-back rather than aggressive systems. ADI's AN-1429 (Rev. 0, 10/2016, David Guo) lists the AD8620 among ADI op-amps 'reputable with music fans', alongside the AD797, OP275 and ADA4627-1. Soomal teardowns confirm commercial use: the Topping NX5 (2016) runs 2x AD8610 plus BUF634, the Xuelin iHiFi990 (2017) uses 2x AD8610 as its DAC LPF, and the FiiO X7 MkII AM3A module (2017) uses an AD8620 preamp. Three sources were cited but not re-checked this session: Head-Fi threads praising the AD8620, a Leckerton blog post (AD8610 as its default output op-amp, OPA627 not clearly better in blind A/B), and a Japanese Zigsow review ('won't lose to the 627').

**How people describe the sound:** clear/detailed (Head-Fi; not re-checked), black background (Head-Fi; not re-checked), tight, controlled bass (Zigsow; not re-checked), AD823-like without some of its aggressiveness (Tangentsoft), more detail and smoothness than AD843 (Tangentsoft via translation; wording ambiguous), somewhat aggressive compared with AD8065/OPA227 (Tangentsoft), lean (some listeners; unverified)

**Typical uses:** DAC output low-pass filter / I/V, headphone-amp gain stage, often with a BUF634 buffer (Topping NX5), battery-powered portable headphone amps, op-amp rolling on SOIC-to-DIP adapters, precision/high-impedance instrumentation and photodiode amps

**Caveats:**

- The operating supply is specified only to +/-13 V, with an absolute max of about 27.3 V total per Tangentsoft (not checked against ADI). It is not a drop-in for +/-15 V or higher rails.
- The single comes in SOIC-8 or MSOP-8 and the dual in SOIC-8 only, so DIP-8 sockets need an adapter board.
- Output current is about +/-30 mA. Designs driving low-impedance headphones often add a buffer (e.g. BUF634 in the Topping NX5), although Tangentsoft rated its drive as good for a battery amp.
- Tangentsoft advises against pairing it with aggressive or bright systems.
- No independent THD+N measurements (ASR, Samuel Groner) were found.
- The Head-Fi, Leckerton, diyAudio and Zigsow reputation sources were not re-verified this session.

## Counterfeits

ADI sells the AD8610 in SOIC-8 or MSOP-8 (the MSOP versions seen are reel-only) and the AD8620 in SOIC-8 only. Eagle's ADI library lists the AD8620 as R/SO8 only, no AD8620ARM orderable was found, and Tangentsoft also notes the part is SOIC-only. So any 'AD8610/AD8620' in a native DIP-8 body, or an 'AD8620' in MSOP, is suspect. Genuine SOIC parts soldered on SOIC-to-DIP adapters are legitimate and are what rollers sell. Marketplace fakes (AliExpress/eBay DIP modules) are reported in the discovery notes but were not verified. Check the chip under the adapter for ADI marking, and buy from authorized distributors. No remarked-die folklore or old-versus-new lot differences were found.

## Related parts and alternatives

ADA4627-1 (ADI JFET; AN-1429 names it next to AD8620 as popular with music fans; specs not verified), ADA4610-1 / ADA4610-2 (newer ADI JFET-input family; specs not verified this session), OPA627 / OPA827 (TI/Burr-Brown JFET, the usual comparison), OPA1641 / OPA1642 (TI JFET audio op-amps), AD8065 / AD8066 (ADI FastFET; Tangentsoft compares it directly with the AD8610), AD823 (ADI FET-input dual; Tangentsoft reference point)

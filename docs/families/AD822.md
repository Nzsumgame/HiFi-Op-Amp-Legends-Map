# AD820 / AD822 / AD824

**JFET-input single-supply, rail-to-rail output; 1/2/4 ch** · Analog Devices (1990s–present) → Analog Devices (current) → arXiv 2409.13509 (academic) (2024) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** PCN 26_0029 lists AD820, AD822 and AD824 variants alongside the AD823 and AD8224 JFET amps. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*Low-power ADI JFET amps with rail-to-rail outputs; a Japanese battery-CMoy pick likened to the OPA2134.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 nV/√Hz (AD820) | 1.8 MHz (AD820, AD822); 2 MHz (AD824) | 3 V/µs (AD820, AD822); 2 V/µs (AD824) | AD820 5–36 V; AD822 5–30 V; AD824 3 V to ±15 V | 800 µA max (AD820; AD822 unconfirmed); 500 µA (AD824) |

## Why it's rated

Modest praise, mostly Japanese DIY. A zigsow review calls the AD822 a single-supply part that holds up well enough for audio. nabe hears a tendency like the OPA2134 with a cool FET character, and finds the OPA2350 fuller. Its very low current suits battery CMoy amps, though nabe notes transient problems into 15 Ω loads.

**Described as:** cool FET-input character, similar to OPA2134, less full than OPA2350

**Used in:** Battery-powered CMoy headphone amplifiers, Single-supply portable audio stages, DIY op-amp rolling (DIP-8 AD822ANZ)

## Watch out

- Designed as a low-power precision/instrumentation part, not for audio.
- Limited drive: nabe reports transient problems into 15 Ω headphone loads.
- Praise is lukewarm ('good enough for audio'), not legendary.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific counterfeit reports. The DIP-8 AD822ANZ circulates widely in the hobby market, so buy from authorized distributors or reputable retailers such as Akizuki.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2026 · PCN 26_0029 (date unconfirmed)

PCN 26_0029 lists AD820, AD822 and AD824 variants alongside the AD823 and AD8224 JFET amps. The nature of the change (fab/process, assembly, test or package) is not yet known; a family-wide PCN may mean a process or fab change.

- **Affected:** AD820, AD822, AD823, AD824, AD8224
- **How to tell:** ADI PCN 26_0029 Rev. -; the PCN should state how to tell pre- and post-change parts apart (date code or marking).
- **Audio impact:** Unknown until the PCN text is read.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: a 2026 PCN covers the whole JFET-RRO family; nature of change unconfirmed.
- **Confidence:** low

# AD820 / AD822 / AD824 (ADI single-supply rail-to-rail-output JFET-input)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

**Technology:** JFET input, single supply (5-30 V) or dual supply, rail-to-rail output, 1.8 MHz, 3 V/us, 800 uA per amplifier

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A zigsow review is titled 'オーディオにも十分通用する単電源用' ('A single-supply part that holds up well enough for audio'). In nabe's spec-vs-sound notes it is described as having a sound tendency similar to the OPA2134 and a cool FET-input character. Its very low current draw makes it a battery CMoy choice, though with some transient issues into 15-ohm loads, and nabe finds the OPA2350 fuller-bodied. Akizuki stocks the DIP version for DIY builders.

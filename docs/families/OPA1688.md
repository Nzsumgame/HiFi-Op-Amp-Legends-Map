# OPA1688 / OPA1689 (TI SoundPlus 36 V, 10 MHz CMOS audio op-amp with rail-to-rail high-drive output; dual / quad)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

*Cheap, rugged 36 V TI SoundPlus dual with a rail-to-rail, high-drive output. diyAudio calls it "a 21st-century NE5532" and TI lists it as an OPA2604/OPA2134 successor.*

**Technology:** CMOS-input (per discovery notes) SoundPlus audio op-amp. 36 V single supply (±18 V), 10 MHz GBW, rail-to-rail output, marketed as "Low Distortion High Drive". SMD only: SOIC-8 confirmed via the OPA1688ID/IDR orderables.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

It is not a socket-rolling legend because it is SMD-only. It is well regarded as an inexpensive, rugged all-rounder. A diyAudio member calls it 'a very good all-round opamp at a reasonable price, like a 21st century version of the NE5532', with modestly high 8 nV/√Hz noise. It is valued for 36 V operation, rail-to-rail high-current output and built-in short-circuit protection, so it drives headphones directly. It appears as the final amplification stage of the Topping NX4 DSD portable headphone amp (per a GitHub modder), and one owner built an adapter to swap it for the INA1620. It is used in DIY headphone amps (HiFiVision build, diyAudio OPA1688/OPA1622 thread) and DJ-mixer cue outputs. It also appears in DAC reconstruction filters and in retro-computer audio boards (ZX-Evolution rev D+ switched its op-amp recommendation to OPA1688 in June 2026). TI and the community point to it, alongside OPA1656, as a replacement for the discontinued OPA2604AP and a successor to OPA2134. No Audio Science Review or Samuel Groner measurements were found in this pass.

**How people describe the sound:** very good all-rounder, like a 21st-century NE5532

**Typical uses:** Headphone driver / output stage (Topping NX4 DSD final stage per modder; HiFiVision DIY headphone amp; DJ mixer cue output), Line-level buffers, line-level converters and headphone pre-amps (synth DIY), DAC output / reconstruction filter (FPGA sigma-delta DAC project), SMD replacement for OPA2604 / OPA2134 in new designs, Retro-computer and sound-card audio outputs (ZX-Evolution rev D+, Mac NuBus PAS16 daughterboard)

**Caveats:**

- Voltage noise (~8 nV/√Hz) is modest compared with OPA1656 (2.9 nV/√Hz), OPA1612 or OPA1602. It is not ideal for low-level or low-impedance sources such as phono or microphone inputs.
- SMD only (SOIC-8). DIP socket rolling needs an adapter, and no DIP version exists.
- Output power into low-impedance headphones is limited. One design note found OPA1688 current insufficient for a 250 mW / 18 Ω target (AarnimM22/HD800-wireless). Owners of the Topping NX4 swapped in the INA1620 for more drive.
- Little independent measurement coverage (no ASR or Groner data found), so the praise is mostly spec- and value-based.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remarking reports specific to the OPA1688 were found. It comes only in SMD packages (SOIC-8) and is inexpensive, so it is a far less attractive target than the DIP "upgrade" parts TI suggests it replaces (OPA2604AP and OPA2134PA), which are heavily faked. The usual marketplace risk still applies: 2025 Chinese BOMs source OPA1688IDR from Taobao sellers. Buy from TI or authorized distributors (Mouser 595-OPA1688ID, Digi-Key, Arrow). Top-marking details were not retrieved. One third-party catalog lists "OPA1688AIDR", which does not match the orderables seen elsewhere (OPA1688ID/IDR). Treat such part strings with suspicion until checked against TI's orderable list.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA1656 (TI's 'next-generation OPA2134 and OPA2604 replacement'. CMOS, 2.9 nV/√Hz, 53 MHz, 100 mA output, 36 V; the closest higher-performance successor), OPA1678 / OPA1679 (low-cost CMOS SoundPlus, 4.5 nV/√Hz, -120 dB, about 2 mA/ch), OPA1652 / OPA1654 (CMOS SoundPlus, 4.5 nV/√Hz, 0.00005% THD+N), OPA1622 (bipolar headphone driver, 2.8 nV/√Hz, +145/-130 mA; for when OPA1688 drive is insufficient), INA1620 (used by modders to replace OPA1688 in the Topping NX4 via an adapter), OPA1602 / OPA1604 (bipolar, 2.5 nV/√Hz, ±30 mA), NE5532 (the classic it is compared to), LM4562 / LME49720 (DIP option when a through-hole OPA2604 substitute is needed)

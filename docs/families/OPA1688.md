# OPA1688 / OPA1689

**CMOS-input 36 V audio op-amp, dual / quad** · Texas Instruments (2015–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Cheap, rugged 36 V TI SoundPlus CMOS dual with rail-to-rail high-drive output: 'a 21st-century NE5532'.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 nV/√Hz | 10 MHz | −109 dB | 4.5 V (±2.25 V) to 36 V (±18 V) | 1.6 mA per amplifier |

## Why it's rated

SMD-only, so not a socket-rolling legend, but liked as a cheap, rugged all-rounder. A diyAudio member called it 'a 21st century version of the NE5532'. Builders value 36 V operation, rail-to-rail high-drive output and short-circuit protection for direct headphone drive. Forums suggest it as an SMD OPA2604AP substitute. No ASR or Samuel Groner measurements known.

**Described as:** very good all-rounder, 21st-century NE5532

**Used in:** Headphone output stage (reportedly Topping NX4 DSD; DIY amps), Line buffers, converters and headphone pre-amps (synth DIY), DAC reconstruction filters; DJ-mixer cue outputs, SMD substitute for OPA2604 / OPA2134 in new designs, Retro-computer and sound-card audio (ZX-Evolution, Mac NuBus PAS16)

## Watch out

- 8 nV/√Hz is modest versus OPA1656 (2.9 nV/√Hz), OPA1612 or OPA1602; not ideal for phono or mic.
- SMD only (SOIC-8, WSON-8; quad SOIC-14, VQFN-16). Rolling needs an adapter; WSON is hard to adapt.
- Limited drive for low-impedance headphones: short of a 250 mW / 18 Ω target; NX4 modders swapped in INA1620.
- Little independent measurement; praise rests mostly on specs and value.
- TI's 'next-generation OPA2134/OPA2604 replacement' FAQ concerns OPA1656, not OPA1688.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific fake reports; cheap SMD parts are poor targets. Marketplace risk remains (Amazon 'OPA1688IDRGR … OP1688 WSON8', Taobao). 'OP1688' may be the WSON top mark (unconfirmed). 'OPA1688AIDR' is not a TI orderable; treat it with suspicion.

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

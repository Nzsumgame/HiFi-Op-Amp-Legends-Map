# OPA1688 / OPA1689 (TI SoundPlus OPA168x: 36 V, 10 MHz CMOS audio op-amp with a rail-to-rail high-drive output; dual / quad)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

*Cheap, rugged 36 V TI SoundPlus CMOS dual with a rail-to-rail high-drive output. A diyAudio member called it 'a 21st-century NE5532'.*

**Technology:** CMOS-input SoundPlus audio op-amp (input bias ±10 pA typ). Runs from 4.5 V (±2.25 V) to 36 V (±18 V), 10 MHz GBW, rail-to-rail output, marketed as 'Low Distortion High Drive'. SMD only. OPA1688 (dual) comes in SOIC-8 (D) and WSON-8 (DRG). OPA1689 (quad) comes in SOIC-14 and VQFN-16, per distributor listings.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

It is not a socket-rolling legend because it is SMD-only. It is well regarded as an inexpensive, rugged all-rounder. A diyAudio member called it 'a very good all-round opamp at a reasonable price, like a 21st century version of the NE5532', with modest 8 nV/√Hz noise. Builders value its 36 V operation, rail-to-rail high-drive output and short-circuit protection, which let it drive headphones directly. One GitHub modder reports it as the final stage of the Topping NX4 DSD, and built an adapter to swap in the INA1620. Other reported uses include DIY headphone amps (HiFiVision build, diyAudio OPA1688/OPA1622 thread), DJ-mixer cue outputs, DAC reconstruction filters and retro-computer audio boards. Forum threads (TI E2E, AudioKarma, Head-Fi) suggest it as an SMD substitute for the discontinued OPA2604AP. TI's own 'next-generation OPA2134/OPA2604 replacement' FAQ is about the OPA1656. No Audio Science Review or Samuel Groner measurements were found.

**How people describe the sound:** very good all-rounder, like a 21st-century NE5532 (one diyAudio member)

**Typical uses:** Headphone driver / output stage (Topping NX4 DSD final stage per one modder; HiFiVision DIY headphone amp; DJ mixer cue output), Line-level buffers, line-level converters and headphone pre-amps (synth DIY), DAC output / reconstruction filter (FPGA sigma-delta DAC project), SMD substitute for OPA2604 / OPA2134 in new designs, Retro-computer and sound-card audio outputs (ZX-Evolution rev D+, Mac NuBus PAS16 daughterboard)

**Caveats:**

- Voltage noise (8 nV/√Hz) is modest next to OPA1656 (2.9 nV/√Hz), OPA1612 or OPA1602. It is not ideal for low-level sources such as phono or microphone inputs.
- SMD only: OPA1688 in SOIC-8 or WSON-8, OPA1689 in SOIC-14 or VQFN-16. There is no DIP version, so socket rolling needs an adapter (WSON is hard to adapt).
- Output power into low-impedance headphones is limited. One design note found OPA1688 current insufficient for a 250 mW / 18 Ω target (AarnimM22/HD800-wireless), and Topping NX4 owners swapped in the INA1620 for more drive.
- Little independent measurement coverage, so the praise rests mostly on specs and value.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remarking reports specific to the OPA1688 were found. It is SMD-only (SOIC-8 / WSON-8) and inexpensive, so it is a far less attractive target than the DIP parts it substitutes for (OPA2604AP, OPA2134PA), which are heavily faked. Marketplace risk still applies: Amazon lists '1PCS OPA1688IDRGR OPA1688IDRGT OPA1688 OP1688 WSON8' and 2025 Chinese BOMs source OPA1688IDR from Taobao. 'OP1688' may be the WSON top mark (unconfirmed). Buy from TI or authorized distributors. One third-party catalog lists 'OPA1688AIDR', which does not match TI orderables (OPA1688ID, IDR, IDRGR, IDRGT), so treat such strings with suspicion.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA1656 (TI's 'next-generation OPA2134 and OPA2604 replacement'. CMOS, 2.9 nV/√Hz, 53 MHz, 100 mA output, 36 V), OPA1678 / OPA1679 (low-cost CMOS SoundPlus, 4.5 nV/√Hz), OPA1652 / OPA1654 (CMOS SoundPlus, 4.5 nV/√Hz), OPA1622 (bipolar headphone driver, 2.8 nV/√Hz; for when OPA1688 drive is insufficient), INA1620 (used by modders to replace OPA1688 in the Topping NX4 via an adapter), OPA1602 / OPA1604 (bipolar, 2.5 nV/√Hz), NE5532 (the classic it is compared to), LM4562 / LME49720 (DIP option when a through-hole OPA2604 substitute is needed)

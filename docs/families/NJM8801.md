# NJM8801

**Bipolar audio dual, SMD only** · New JRC (by 2014-11 – 2021) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2022–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*New JRC's "mass-production MUSES": MUSES know-how on a volume process, ranked between NJM4580 and MUSES8820.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5 nV/√Hz typ | 15 MHz typ | 5 V/µs typ | 0.0005 % typ (THD) | ±2 V to ±18 V | 6 mA typ |

## Why it's rated

Niche, Japan-centric part. New JRC markets it as MUSES know-how on a mass-production process; Japanese sellers call it "MUSESの量産型" (mass-production MUSES). A Zigsow review titled "NJM4580以上MUSES8820未満" finds it close to the MUSES8820 but slightly less clear. Covered by stereo.jp. No Western bench measurements exist.

**Described as:** close to MUSES8820, slightly less clear, better than NJM4580

**Used in:** Preamps, reference amps, active filters, line amps (vendor list), Pioneer OEM audio; sold as service replacement part, Hobby upgrade over NJM4580 via SOP8-to-DIP8 adapter

## Watch out

- Sound claims rest on one Japanese user review plus vendor marketing.
- Surface-mount only (SOP8, SSOP8). Socket rolling needs an adapter, which can affect stability.
- Stability and output-drive limits not checked against the datasheet.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit reports found, though none were specifically sought. A low-volume, SMD-only Japan-market part, so fakes seem unlikely. Buy from DigiKey, Mouser or established Japanese retailers such as Kyohritsu eleshop.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

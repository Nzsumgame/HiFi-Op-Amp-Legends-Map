# NJM8801 (New JRC / Nisshinbo high-quality audio bipolar dual, 'mass-production MUSES')

New Japan Radio Co., Ltd. (New JRC) (by Nov 2014 (Japanese datasheet Ver.2014-11-10) – 2021) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices Inc. (2022 – present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*New JRC's bipolar audio dual that applies MUSES know-how to a mass-production process. A Japanese reviewer ranked it above the NJM4580 and below the MUSES8820.*

| Slew rate | Supply current |
|---|---|
| 5 V/µs typ | 6 mA typ |

## Why it's rated

Niche, Japan-centric reputation. New JRC markets it as MUSES know-how moved to a mass-production process, and Japanese sellers call it 'MUSESの量産型' (mass-production MUSES). A Zigsow user review is titled 'NJM4580以上MUSES8820未満' (above NJM4580, below MUSES8820): voicing close to the MUSES8820 but slightly less clear. It also appears in stereo.jp coverage of New JRC. No Western bench measurements were found.

**Described as:** voicing similar to MUSES8820 (one Zigsow reviewer), slightly clouded / less clear than MUSES8820 (same reviewer), better than NJM4580 (same reviewer)

**Used in:** Vendor-listed applications: audio preamplifiers, reference amplifiers, active filters, line amplifiers, OEM consumer audio: used in Pioneer equipment, sold as a service replacement part (soundservice.gr), Hobby op-amp swaps as a step up from NJM4580-class parts. Needs an SOP8-to-DIP8 adapter because no DIP version exists.

## Watch out

- The sound claims come from a single Japanese user review plus vendor marketing, so confidence is low.
- Surface-mount only (SOP8 'E', SSOP8 'VA3'). Socket rolling needs an adapter, and an adapter can affect stability.
- Stability and output-drive limits were not checked against the datasheet.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeit reports were found, but no dedicated counterfeit search was run. It is a low-volume, SMD-only, Japan-market part with little grey-market pull, so fakes seem unlikely but are not ruled out. Buy through Nisshinbo-authorized distributors (DigiKey, Mouser) or established Japanese retailers such as Kyohritsu eleshop.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

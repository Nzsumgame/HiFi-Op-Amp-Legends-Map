# NJM8801 (New JRC / Nisshinbo entry-level audio dual op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*New JRC's entry-level dual audio op-amp said to use MUSES technology. A Japanese reviewer placed its sound above the NJM4580 and below the MUSES8820.*

**Technology:** Dual bipolar op-amp in New JRC's entry high-quality audio line, reportedly based on MUSES technology (per the discovery notes). This run did not check the input-stage type, process or compensation against the datasheet.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Niche, Japan-centric reputation. Discovery notes describe it as New JRC's entry-level part built on MUSES technology. A Zigsow (Japan) review is titled 'Above NJM4580, below MUSES8820': similar voicing to the MUSES8820 but slightly clouded. It also appears in Japanese YouTube 10-op-amp shoot-outs and in stereo.jp coverage. No Western measurements were found (no ASR, no Samuel Groner). All of this comes from the discovery notes; none of it was re-checked in this run.

**How people describe the sound:** voicing similar to MUSES8820, slightly clouded / less clear than MUSES8820, better than NJM4580

**Typical uses:** Op-amp swapping in hobby audio gear as a step up from NJM4580-class parts (from the Japanese reviewer's comparison context), Cheaper alternative to MUSES8820 in DIY DAC or headphone-amp builds (inferred from the comparison, not stated by a source)

**Caveats:**

- Evidence is one Japanese user review plus video shoot-outs, so confidence is low.
- Packages were not checked. If the part only comes as NJM8801E (SOP-8/EMP8), socket rolling needs a DIP adapter.
- No stability, output-drive or supply-range caveats could be checked in this run.

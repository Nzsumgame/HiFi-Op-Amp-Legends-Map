# LT1360 / LT1361 / LT1362 (50MHz, 800V/us C-Load bipolar single/dual/quad)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*Linear Tech's 50MHz/800V/us C-Load bipolar op amp. Sound-card op-amp swappers rate it for DAC I/V stages and describe it as 'extremely clear and airy'.*

**Technology:** A voltage-feedback op amp built on Linear Technology's complementary bipolar process. The inputs are matched and high-impedance, and the design slews like a current-feedback amplifier. It belongs to LTC's C-Load high-speed family (LT1354 to LT1365), is unity-gain stable and is meant to drive capacitive loads. The family description comes from the LT1364/LT1365 datasheet (13645fa Rev A). That datasheet places the LT1360 to LT1362 in the same topology family as the 50MHz, 4mA/amp members.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche favourite of sound-card op-amp swappers (mostly Asus Xonar Essence ST/STX), used mainly in the I/V stage after current-output DACs. With an LME49720 elsewhere in the chain, users describe the LT1361 as giving an 'extremely clear and airy sound'. Xonar discussions rank it among the best I/V op amps, alongside the LT1028, OPA627 and OPA827. Head-Fi posts list it with the LT1122, LT1364 and LT1469 as alternatives to the LME49720. Users call it forgiving of poor bypassing and tolerant of a wide supply range, so it can stand in for most bipolar duals. No bench measurements were found (Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner or similar). All praise is subjective forum opinion.

**How people describe the sound:** extremely clear, airy

**Typical uses:** I/V conversion after current-output DACs (Asus Xonar Essence ST/STX), DAC output buffer / filter stage, sound-card op-amp swapping, video / cable drivers, buffers, active filters (vendor applications)

**Caveats:**

- Forum posts say the dual comes only in SOIC-8, so DIP-8 sockets need an adapter. A DIP-8 orderable, LT1361CN8#PBF, does appear in a part sitemap.
- Bipolar inputs with fairly high bias current (LCSC parametric: 300 nA) and about 1 mV offset: expect DC offset with high source or feedback impedances.
- A 50MHz, 800V/us part: needs a tight layout and short decoupling. Socket adapters add parasitics, although the C-Load design tolerates capacitive loading.
- Supply current is about 4 mA per amplifier. Distributor output-current data (34 mA) points to limited ability to drive low-impedance headphones directly.
- Descriptors come from a small number of subjective posts; no measurements were found.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remarking reports specific to the LT1360/LT1361/LT1362 were found; web searches could not be run for this family. General advice: buy from ADI or authorized distributors, because pulled or grey-market high-speed LTC parts are common on marketplace sites. A DIP-8 LT1361 is not automatically fake even though forums say the dual is SOIC-8 only: an LT1361CN8#PBF orderable appears in a part-number sitemap.

## Related parts and alternatives

LT1364/LT1365 (same topology, 70MHz, 1000V/us, 7.5mA max/amp; also rated by the audio community), LT1358/LT1359 (same family, lower power: 25MHz, 600V/us, 2mA/amp; some users prefer its sound), LT1363 (single 70MHz, 1000V/us), LT1813 (dual 100MHz, 700V/us, 3mA/amp, low-voltage), LT1469 (dual 90MHz precision; an audio-community favourite for I/V)

# LT1360 / LT1361 / LT1362 (50MHz, 800V/us C-Load bipolar single/dual/quad)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*Linear Tech's 50MHz/800V/us C-Load bipolar op amp. Sound-card op-amp swappers rate it for DAC I/V stages and describe it as 'extremely clear and airy'.*

**Technology:** A voltage-feedback op amp built on Linear Technology's complementary bipolar process. It is unity-gain stable and belongs to LTC's C-Load high-speed family (LT1354 to LT1365). The C-Load design keeps it stable with any capacitive load: it senses the pole the load puts on the output and adds compensation at the gain node. The input stage is matched and high-impedance and the part slews like a current-feedback amplifier; that part of the description comes from the family text in the LT1364/LT1365 datasheet.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche favourite of sound-card op-amp swappers (mostly Asus Xonar Essence ST/STX), used mainly in the I/V stage after current-output DACs. One user paired it with an LME49720 and described the result as an 'extremely clear and airy sound'. Some Xonar/Head-Fi threads list it with the LT1028, OPA627, OPA827, LT1364 and LT1469 as I/V or DAC-output choices. Users also call it forgiving of poor bypassing. All of this is subjective forum opinion from a handful of posts; no bench measurements (Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner or similar) were found.

**How people describe the sound:** extremely clear, airy

**Typical uses:** I/V conversion after current-output DACs (Asus Xonar Essence ST/STX), DAC output buffer / filter stage, sound-card op-amp swapping, wideband amplifiers, buffers, active filters, cable drivers (vendor applications)

**Caveats:**

- Forum posts say the dual comes only in SOIC-8, so DIP-8 sockets need an adapter. A DIP-8 LT1361CN8#PBF appears only in a part sitemap and is unconfirmed.
- Bipolar inputs with fairly high bias current (LCSC parametric: 300 nA) and up to 1 mV offset: expect DC offset with high source or feedback impedances.
- A 50MHz, 800V/us part: needs a tight layout and short decoupling. Socket adapters add parasitics, although the C-Load design is stable with capacitive loads.
- Up to 5 mA per amplifier. The 34 mA output-current figure (distributor data) suggests it should not drive low-impedance headphones directly.
- Descriptors come from a small number of subjective posts; no measurements were found.

### 1. Package / assembly: Analog Devices (Linear Technology), PCN 21_0060, 11 Nov 2021 (Rev -, Rev A and Rev B exist)

Marking only, not a die change. On legacy Linear 8-SOICN products the top mark changed from ink to laser. This entry records both marking styles as genuine. The completeness-sweep mention of 21_0060 is merged here.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remarking reports specific to the LT1360/LT1361/LT1362 were found. Genuine LT1361CS8 parts come in several marking styles: an ink top mark on older parts, a laser top mark after ADI PCN 21_0060, and, after PCN 24_0236 (Oct 2024), the trace code on top instead of the bottom. A marking-style difference alone does not prove a fake. Buy from ADI or authorized distributors; pulled or grey-market high-speed LTC parts are common on marketplace sites. A DIP-8 'LT1361' deserves scrutiny: the DIP-8 orderable LT1361CN8#PBF appears only in a part sitemap and is unconfirmed.

# LT1360 / LT1361 / LT1362

**Bipolar C-Load high-speed op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · Linear Technology (c. 1994–2017) → Analog Devices (2017–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Linear Tech's 50 MHz, 800 V/µs C-Load op-amp; sound-card swappers call it 'extremely clear and airy' for DAC I/V.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 nV/√Hz typ | 50 MHz typ (37 MHz at ±5 V, 32 MHz at ±2.5 V) | 800 V/µs typ | ±2.5 V to ±15 V | 5 mA max, about 4 mA typ per amplifier |

## Why it's rated

A niche favourite of sound-card op-amp swappers (mostly Asus Xonar Essence ST/STX) for the I/V stage after current-output DACs. One user paired it with an LME49720 for an 'extremely clear and airy sound'. Threads list it with the LT1028, OPA627, OPA827, LT1364 and LT1469 as I/V choices, and call it forgiving of poor bypassing. No bench measurements found.

**Described as:** extremely clear, airy

**Used in:** I/V after current-output DACs (Xonar Essence ST/STX), DAC output buffer and filter stage, Sound-card op-amp swapping, Wideband amps, buffers, active filters, cable drivers

## Watch out

- Dual reportedly SOIC-8 only, so DIP-8 sockets need an adapter; the DIP-8 LT1361CN8#PBF is unconfirmed.
- Bipolar inputs with about 300 nA bias and up to 1 mV offset: expect DC offset with high impedances.
- 50 MHz, 800 V/µs: needs tight layout and short decoupling; socket adapters add parasitics.
- Up to 5 mA per amp and about 34 mA output; not for driving low-impedance headphones directly.
- Sound descriptions come from a few subjective posts; no measurements found.
- **Counterfeits:** No family-specific counterfeit reports. Genuine LT1361CS8 parts carry ink top marks (older), laser marks (after PCN 21_0060), or top-side trace codes (after PCN 24_0236), so marking style alone proves nothing. Treat DIP-8 'LT1361' parts and grey-market pulls with suspicion.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

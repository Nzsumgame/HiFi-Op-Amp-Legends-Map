# AD828 (ADI low-power dual video op-amp)

Analog Devices (1990s (introduction year unconfirmed) to present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*ADI's 130 MHz, 450 V/µs dual video op-amp; Chinese sound-card and car-audio modders rate it above the AD827 for dynamics and treble extension.*

| Slew rate | Supply current |
|---|---|
| 450 V/µs | 15 mA |

## Why it's rated

The praise comes from Chinese DIY sources. A CSDN listening ranking of 22 classic op-amps credits the AD828AN with 'jump, grandeur, extension' but a somewhat rough midrange, and ranks it above the AD827. An Elecfans topic page says the AD828AR beats the AD827JN and calls it neutral, with good mid resolution, strong bass dynamics and clear, extended highs; it recommends the part for sound-card and DVD output stages. Car-audio modders on Autohome fit it to head units. One Bilibili creator found a cheap AD828 board disappointing. There is no Western audiophile or measurement-based consensus.

**Described as:** dynamic / 'jump' (CSDN), grand, large-scale (CSDN), extended (CSDN, Elecfans), somewhat rough midrange (CSDN), neutral full-range tone (Elecfans), strong bass dynamics (Elecfans), clear highs (Elecfans)

**Used in:** Op-amp swaps in sound-card outputs, DVD player output stages, Op-amp swaps in car head units, Vendor-intended use: video line and cable drivers at G >= +2

## Watch out

- This is a video op-amp that ADI optimises for gains of +2 or more. In unity-gain buffer or socket-swap positions it may ring or oscillate, so check stability and decoupling.
- The praise is subjective and comes mostly from Chinese forums and marketing-style pages. One Bilibili review was negative.
- The PDIP AD828AN appears to be discontinued, so DIP parts sold on marketplaces may be new-old stock or remarked.
- **Counterfeits:** No AD828-specific counterfeit reports were found. The PDIP AD828AN appears in ADI PDN 10_0081 Rev. B (discontinued), so DIP-8 'AD828AN' parts and cheap pre-built AD828 boards on marketplaces may be new-old stock, pulls or remarked parts. Buy the SOIC AD828ARZ from authorised distributors.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

**Confirmed:**

- The AD828 is ADI's 'Dual, Low Power Video Op Amp', a low-cost dual optimised for gains of +2 or more and cable driving (https://www.analog.com/en/products/ad828.html).
- Specs: 130 MHz -3 dB bandwidth at G = +2, 450 V/µs slew, 0.1 dB flatness to 40 MHz, differential gain/phase 0.01%/0.05°, 15 mA supply current, 50 mA minimum output per amplifier (https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/AD828.pdf).

# AD828 (ADI low-power dual video op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

**Technology:** Bipolar voltage-feedback, low-cost dual video op-amp. ADI optimises it for gains of +2 or more with high output drive (cable driving). It is a separate die from the covered AD826 and AD827/AD847.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The CSDN classic-audio-op-amp ranking (listening order of 22 parts) rates the AD828AN as having 'jump, grandeur, extension' but a somewhat rough midrange, and places it above the AD827. Elecfans' 'ad828运放音质好吗' page says the AD828AR beats the famous AD827JN, with a neutral full-range tone, good mid-range resolution, excellent bass dynamics and clear, extended highs, and recommends it for sound-card and DVD output stages. Car-audio modders on Autohome install it in head units. One Bilibili creator found a cheap AD828 board disappointing.

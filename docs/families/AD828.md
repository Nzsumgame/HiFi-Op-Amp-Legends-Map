# AD828

**Bipolar VFB dual video op-amp, optimised for G ≥ +2** · Analog Devices (1990s (year unconfirmed)–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*ADI's 130 MHz, 450 V/µs dual video op-amp; Chinese sound-card and car-audio modders rate it above the AD827.*

| GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|
| 130 MHz −3 dB bandwidth; 0.1 dB flat to 40 MHz | 450 V/µs | Not specified; video diff gain/phase 0.01% / 0.05° | 15 mA (max vs typ unconfirmed) |

## Why it's rated

Praise comes from Chinese DIY sources. A CSDN ranking of 22 op-amps credits the AD828AN with 'jump, grandeur, extension' but a slightly rough midrange, above the AD827. Elecfans calls the AD828AR neutral, with strong bass dynamics and clear, extended highs. Autohome car-audio modders use it; one Bilibili review was negative. No Western or measurement-based consensus.

**Described as:** dynamic, 'jumpy', grand, large-scale, extended, slightly rough midrange, neutral, strong bass dynamics, clear highs

**Used in:** Sound-card output op-amp swaps, DVD player output stages, Car head-unit op-amp swaps, Vendor intent: video line and cable drivers, G ≥ +2

## Watch out

- Optimised for G ≥ +2; may ring or oscillate as a unity-gain buffer. Check stability and decoupling.
- Praise is subjective, mostly from Chinese forums and marketing-style pages; one Bilibili review was negative.
- PDIP AD828AN appears discontinued; marketplace DIP parts may be new-old stock or remarked.
- **Counterfeits:** No AD828-specific counterfeit reports. The PDIP AD828AN is in ADI PDN 10_0081 Rev. B, so DIP-8 parts and cheap AD828 boards may be new-old stock, pulls or remarks. Buy SOIC AD828ARZ from authorised distributors.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

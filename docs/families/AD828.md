# AD828 (ADI low-power dual video op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*ADI's 130 MHz, 450 V/µs dual video op-amp; Chinese sound-card and car-audio modders rate it above the AD827 for dynamics and treble extension.*

**Technology:** Bipolar voltage-feedback dual video op-amp (process details not confirmed this pass). ADI says it is a low-cost dual optimised for video applications that need gains of +2 or more and high output drive, such as cable driving. Its relation to the AD826 / AD827 / AD847 dies is unconfirmed.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The praise comes from Chinese DIY sources. A CSDN listening ranking of 22 classic op-amps credits the AD828AN with 'jump, grandeur, extension' but a somewhat rough midrange, and ranks it above the AD827. An Elecfans topic page says the AD828AR beats the AD827JN and calls it neutral, with good mid resolution, strong bass dynamics and clear, extended highs; it recommends the part for sound-card and DVD output stages. Car-audio modders on Autohome fit it to head units. One Bilibili creator found a cheap AD828 board disappointing. There is no Western audiophile or measurement-based consensus.

**How people describe the sound:** dynamic / 'jump' (CSDN), grand, large-scale (CSDN), extended (CSDN, Elecfans), somewhat rough midrange (CSDN), neutral full-range tone (Elecfans), strong bass dynamics (Elecfans), clear highs (Elecfans)

**Typical uses:** Op-amp swaps in sound-card outputs, DVD player output stages, Op-amp swaps in car head units, Vendor-intended use: video line and cable drivers at G >= +2

**Caveats:**

- This is a video op-amp that ADI optimises for gains of +2 or more. In unity-gain buffer or socket-swap positions it may ring or oscillate, so check stability and decoupling.
- The praise is subjective and comes mostly from Chinese forums and marketing-style pages. One Bilibili review was negative.
- The PDIP AD828AN appears to be discontinued, so DIP parts sold on marketplaces may be new-old stock or remarked.

## Counterfeits

No AD828-specific counterfeit reports were found. The PDIP AD828AN appears in ADI PDN 10_0081 Rev. B (discontinued), so DIP-8 'AD828AN' parts and cheap pre-built AD828 boards on marketplaces may be new-old stock, pulls or remarked parts. Buy the SOIC AD828ARZ from authorised distributors.

## Open questions

- Rev. C print date and change list, and whether Rev. A/B copies survive (which mirror holds Rev. B?).
- Exact lifecycle of AD828AR (leaded SOIC) and the reel variants, plus the scope and date of PDN 10_0081 for AD828AN.
- Specs not confirmed this pass: input voltage noise, settling time, offset voltage, supply range (±5 V / ±15 V operation is expected), output swing into 150 Ω, and whether 15 mA is max or typ.
- Whether the part is unity-gain stable (ADI only says it is optimised for G >= +2).
- Whether any die or fab change occurred. No PCN about a silicon change was found.

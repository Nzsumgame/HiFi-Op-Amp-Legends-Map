# AD8022 (ADI dual high-speed, low-noise op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

**Technology:** Dual high-speed, low-noise bipolar op-amp (datasheet title: 'Dual High Speed, Low Noise Op Amp'), high output current

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Posters in Head-Fi's 'The Opamp thread' say replacing the NJM4580 with the AD8022 improved resolution and timbre accuracy, and that it sounds close to the AD797ANZ. They describe it as spatial, clear and very transparent ('pure music'), and say it can drive headphones directly. Caveats: it needs careful decoupling or it oscillates, and its high input bias current makes it poor for high-impedance inputs.

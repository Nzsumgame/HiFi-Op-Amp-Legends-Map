# AD8022 (ADI dual high-speed, low-noise op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*ADI's 130 MHz, 2.5 nV/√Hz XFCB bipolar dual; Head-Fi op-amp rollers say it beats NJM4580 in resolution and comes close to an AD797.*

**Technology:** Dual voltage-feedback bipolar op-amp on ADI's high-voltage XFCB (complementary bipolar) process. Datasheet title: 'Dual High Speed, Low Noise Op Amp'. High output current, built to stay stable into capacitive loads.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Fab / process transfer).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Posters in Head-Fi's 'The Opamp thread' say replacing the NJM4580 with the AD8022 improved resolution and timbre accuracy, and that it sounds close to the AD797ANZ. They describe it as spatial, clear and very transparent ('pure music'), and say it can drive headphones directly. Caveats: it needs careful decoupling or it oscillates, and its high input bias current makes it poor for high-impedance inputs. This is a niche reputation from forum op-amp rolling, not a widely cited audio standard.

**How people describe the sound:** resolving, transparent, spatial, clear, accurate timbre (per Head-Fi posters)

**Typical uses:** op-amp rolling in NJM4580/NE5532 sockets (DACs, portable players, sound cards), direct headphone drive at modest levels (per forum posters), low-impedance, low-noise preamp and ADC-driver stages (datasheet design intent)

**Caveats:**

- 130 MHz bandwidth: needs tight decoupling and short layout, and can oscillate in socket adapters
- bipolar input with relatively high input bias current: poor choice behind high-impedance sources or large feedback resistors
- SOIC/MSOP only, so it needs a DIP-8 adapter for socket rolling

- **How to tell old from new:** None on the marking. Trace by ADI lot or date code against the PCN effective date.

- **Drop-in risk:** low - same design and data sheet under ADI's stated equivalence; the listing is unconfirmed

- **Verification:** New item found during verification: one search summary named AD8022ARMZ as affected by PCN 26_0028. A second, targeted search ('PCN 26_0028' AD8022) confirmed the PCN scope but not the AD8022 listing, so it is unconfirmed.

ASE Chungli, Taiwan was qualified and added as an assembly subcontractor for 8- and 10-lead MSOP devices to secure supply. AD8022 MSOP variants are listed per the search summary. The die and data sheet are unchanged.

- **When:** 2014 (PCN 14_0246; revised as Rev. A and Rev. B)
- **Affected:** AD8022ARMZ, AD8022ARMZ-REEL / -REEL7 (if listed)
- **How to tell old from new:** Devices assembled at ASE Chungli have a tool mark or laser mark on the backside giving the country of origin, TAIWAN. Otherwise, use a date code after the PCN effective date.
- **Audio impact:** None expected (same die).
- **Drop-in risk:** low - assembly subcontractor added; same die and data sheet
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Verification:** A search confirmed the PCN content (ASE Chungli for 8L/10L MSOP, backside TAIWAN country-of-origin mark) and the Rev. -, A and B forms. The AD8022ARMZ listing is from a search summary.

### Revision chain status

Analog Devices AD8022 is the only document. The part is single-source, and no acquirer re-issue or second source was found. Known chain, oldest to newest: Rev 0 (initial release, date still unknown, before 9/02) -> Rev A (9/02) -> Rev B (5/05) -> Rev C (8/11, current; ADI product page content last updated 02/23/2017).  Copies holding each revision: - Rev 0: none found. This is the missing link, and its date is unconfirmed. The alldatasheet id 48381 copy and the digchip copy (digchip.com/.../041/AD8022.php, with an unusual title 'Dual Low Noise, Low Distortion, High ...') are candidates, but no revision marking is shown. - Rev A: the University of Chicago EDG copy. The search snippet for edg.uchicago.edu/~bogdan/ADC_QUIET/DOC/parts/AD8022.pdf literally shows 'REV. A Information furnished by Analog Devices...', so its confidence can be raised to high. Its title uses the plural 'Dual High Speed Low Noise Op Amps'. - Rev B: no copy confirmed. The search engine's AI summary called the datasheet.su copy (pdf2.datasheet.su/international%20rectifier/auir2085s.pdf, already listed, singular title without 'Data Sheet') Rev B, but no page text showed a Rev B marking, so this is unverified and stays low confidence. - Rev C: analog.com (upper- and lowercase PDF URLs), icbase.com ADI13561611.pdf, manuallib / archive.org 2588286, and RadioLocman di=121365 (.de and .com).  Still missing: 1. A copy of Rev 0 and its release date. The AD8022 appeared around 1999-2000, but this is not confirmed. 2. A confirmed Rev B (5/05) copy. 3. Per-revision change descriptions from the Rev C revision-history table. The search summaries did not show them. 4. A legacy http://www.analog.com/static/imported-files/data_sheets/ URL. None was found, so none is recorded, but a Wayback lookup of that path could yield Rev B.  All 4 searches were used.

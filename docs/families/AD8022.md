# AD8022

**High-speed, low-noise bipolar op-amp, dual** · Analog Devices (c. 2000–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** PCN 26_0028 qualifies ADI Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) as a wafer fab for XF26/XF18/XF12/XF8 process products. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*ADI's 130 MHz, 2.5 nV/√Hz XFCB bipolar dual. Head-Fi rollers say it out-resolves NJM4580 and nears an AD797.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.5 nV/√Hz | 130 MHz (−3 dB bandwidth) | 50 V/µs; settles 68 ns to 0.1% | +5 V single, or ±2.5 V to ±12 V | 4.0 mA per amplifier |

## Why it's rated

Head-Fi 'Opamp thread' posters say swapping NJM4580 for AD8022 improved resolution and timbre, coming close to an AD797ANZ. They call it spatial, clear and transparent ('pure music') and say it can drive headphones directly. It needs careful decoupling, and high bias current suits low-impedance sources only. A niche forum reputation.

**Described as:** resolving, transparent, spatial, clear, accurate timbre

**Used in:** Rolling in NJM4580 / NE5532 sockets, Direct headphone drive at modest levels (forum reports), Low-impedance, low-noise preamp and ADC-driver stages

## Watch out

- 130 MHz bandwidth: needs tight decoupling and short layout; can oscillate in socket adapters.
- Relatively high input bias current: poor behind high-impedance sources or large feedback resistors.
- SOIC / MSOP only; needs a DIP-8 adapter for socket rolling.
- **Counterfeits:** No documented AD8022 counterfeits. Cheap multi-pack listings (e.g. Amazon 'AD8022ARZ AD8022AR SOP-8') are of unknown origin. Buy from authorised distributors; remarked SOIC audio duals are common.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2026 · PCN 26_0028 Rev. -

PCN 26_0028 qualifies ADI Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) as a wafer fab for XF26/XF18/XF12/XF8 process products. One source listed AD8022ARMZ and reel variants; a second check did not confirm it.

- **Affected:** AD8022ARMZ, AD8022ARMZ-REEL, AD8022ARMZ-REEL7 (reported once; unconfirmed)
- **How to tell:** No marking change. Trace by ADI lot or date code against the PCN effective date.
- **Audio impact:** None documented; ADI states no fit, form, function or reliability impact.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same design and datasheet per ADI; AD8022 listing unconfirmed.
- **Confidence:** low

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab site | previous fab for XF-process products (not named) | ADI Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) qualified |

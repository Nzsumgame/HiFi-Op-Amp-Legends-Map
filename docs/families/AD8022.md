# AD8022 (ADI dual high-speed, low-noise op-amp)

Analog Devices (Rev 0 datasheet (date not confirmed) to present; Rev A 9/02, Rev B 5/05, Rev C 8/11) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** ADI PCN 26_0028 qualifies ADI Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) as a wafer-fab site for XF26/XF18/XF12/XF8 process products. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*ADI's 130 MHz, 2.5 nV/√Hz XFCB bipolar dual; Head-Fi op-amp rollers say it beats NJM4580 in resolution and comes close to an AD797.*

| Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|
| 50 V/µs | +5 V single, or ±2.5 V to ±12 V |

## Why it's rated

Posters in Head-Fi's 'The Opamp thread' say replacing the NJM4580 with the AD8022 improved resolution and timbre accuracy, and that it sounds close to the AD797ANZ. They describe it as spatial, clear and very transparent ('pure music'), and say it can drive headphones directly. Caveats: it needs careful decoupling or it oscillates, and its high input bias current makes it poor for high-impedance inputs. This is a niche reputation from forum op-amp rolling, not a widely cited audio standard.

**Described as:** resolving, transparent, spatial, clear, accurate timbre (per Head-Fi posters)

**Used in:** op-amp rolling in NJM4580/NE5532 sockets (DACs, portable players, sound cards), direct headphone drive at modest levels (per forum posters), low-impedance, low-noise preamp and ADC-driver stages (datasheet design intent)

## Watch out

- 130 MHz bandwidth: needs tight decoupling and short layout, and can oscillate in socket adapters
- bipolar input with relatively high input bias current: poor choice behind high-impedance sources or large feedback resistors
- SOIC/MSOP only, so it needs a DIP-8 adapter for socket rolling
- **Counterfeits:** No documented AD8022 counterfeit reports found in this pass. Low-cost multi-pack marketplace listings (e.g. Amazon 'AD8022ARZ AD8022AR SOP-8') come from unknown sources. Buy from authorized distributors, because remarked parts are common for SOIC audio duals.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2026 (PCN 26_0028 Rev. -; dates not reliably captured)

ADI PCN 26_0028 qualifies ADI Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) as a wafer-fab site for XF26/XF18/XF12/XF8 process products. One search summary listed AD8022ARMZ and its reel variants as affected. A second search did not confirm the listing.

- **Affected:** AD8022ARMZ, AD8022ARMZ-REEL, AD8022ARMZ-REEL7 (per one search summary; unconfirmed)
- **How to tell:** None on the marking. Trace by ADI lot or date code against the PCN effective date.
- **Audio impact:** None documented. ADI states no fit/form/function or reliability impact.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - same design and data sheet under ADI's stated equivalence; the listing is unconfirmed
- **Confidence:** low

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab site | previous fab for XF-process products (not named) | ADI Limerick, Ireland (ADLK) qualified |

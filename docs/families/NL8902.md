# NL8902 (Nisshinbo J-FET-input dual audio op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*Nisshinbo's 2025-era J-FET-input dual built on MUSES know-how. One noted Japanese DIY blogger ranked it above the MUSES8920 and NL8802 in listening tests.*

**Technology:** J-FET-input dual audio op-amp. It is the J-FET counterpart of the bipolar-input NL8802, in Nisshinbo's 'NL' line that applies MUSES sound technology to a more productive process (vendor wording seen for NL8802). No die, process or fab details have been verified.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A very new part that is little known outside Japan. Its praise comes almost entirely from new_western_elec (nw-electric.way-nifty.com), a respected Japanese DIY-audio blogger. In a Sept 2025 A/B session covering MUSES8820 vs NL8802 and MUSES8920 vs NL8902, he ranked OPA2604 < MUSES8820 < MUSES8920 < NL8802 < NL8902. He described NL8902 as having J-FET input like MUSES8920 with more high-frequency extension and more information and detail. He went on to compare NL8902 with Nisshinbo's new MUSES8921 (Oct 2025 post; MUSES8921 audition Apr 2026). NS-kobo's phono-EQ op-amp comparison page also comes up in NL8802/NL8902 searches, but what it says about NL8902 was not captured. No Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner or other measured data was found.

**How people describe the sound:** more high-frequency extension than MUSES8920, more information/detail than MUSES8920 and NL8802 (ranking order), J-FET character like MUSES8920

**Typical uses:** op-amp rolling in DAC output and line stages (new_western_elec audition context), J-FET audio duties similar to MUSES8920/8921, such as line amps, active filters and I/V stages (inferred from positioning, not vendor-stated for NL8902)

**Caveats:**

- The praise comes from essentially one listener. There are no independent measurements.
- No datasheet was checked this session, so noise, GBW, slew rate, supply range, Iq and output current are unknown.
- Package availability for socket rolling (DIP-8 vs SMD only) is unconfirmed.
- Supply risk: new_western_elec has reported worry about Nisshinbo HD restructuring (Dec 2025) and earlier MUSES discontinuations (MUSES03, MUSES05). Long-term availability of niche audio parts is uncertain (blog opinion, low confidence).
- Do not confuse it with MUSES8920/8920A/8921. Those are separate part numbers, not revisions of NL8902.

## Counterfeits

No reports of NL8902 counterfeits were found. Fake MUSES-branded parts are widely reported (e.g. MUSES01 fakes with white-ink markings, misplaced pin-1 dots and stiff leads, sold on Amazon JP, Yahoo Auctions and AliExpress). The NL line is cheaper and not MUSES-branded, so it may be less attractive to counterfeiters (inference). Buy from authorized Japanese distributors such as Kyohritsu eleshop, Akizuki or Marutsu, or from Nisshinbo's distributor channel.

## Related parts and alternatives

NL8802 (Nisshinbo): bipolar-input sibling, ranked just below NL8902 by new_western_elec, MUSES8921 (Nisshinbo, Oct 2025): J-FET dual re-layout of MUSES8920A; press/vendor figures are 8.0 nV/rtHz, 11 MHz GBW, 25 V/us, 0.0004% THD, 5 pA Ib, MUSES8920A / MUSES8920AE (Nisshinbo): replaced MUSES8920 with no change in electrical characteristics, per the vendor, MUSES01 (Nisshinbo/JRC): flagship J-FET dual, NJM8901 (JRC/Nisshinbo): older BiFET 'entry' MUSES-technology dual, TI OPA1642 / OPA2604 / OPA1656: Western FET/CMOS-input audio duals

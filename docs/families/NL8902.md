# NL8902 (Nisshinbo JFET-input dual audio op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*Nisshinbo's May 2025 JFET-input 'entry' dual built on MUSES know-how. A Japanese DIY blogger ranked it above MUSES8920 and NL8802 in listening tests.*

**Technology:** JFET-input dual audio op-amp. Nisshinbo describes it as MUSES sound technology with improved productivity. Headline claims: unity-gain stable, EMI-hardened input (EMIRR 82 dB at 1.8 GHz), 1.3 mA per channel. It is the JFET counterpart of the bipolar NL8802. Packages are SMD only: EMP-8 (AN) and DFN4035-8 (GR). No die, process or fab details are published.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A new part with little coverage outside Japan. Most of the praise comes from new_western_elec (nw-electric.way-nifty.com), a Japanese DIY-audio blogger. In a Sept 2025 A/B session he ranked OPA2604 < MUSES8820 < MUSES8920 < NL8802 < NL8902, and he heard more high-frequency extension and more detail than MUSES8920. His Oct 2025 post compares NL8902 with the new MUSES8921. NS-kobo's phono-EQ listening comparison reportedly found NL8902AN better than OPA2604AP, with richer musical reproduction (known only from a search summary; the attribution is not fully certain). The distributor Macnica pitches it as entry-level with sound 'on par with higher-end models'. That is marketing. No measurements from Audio Science Review or other independent sources were found.

**How people describe the sound:** more high-frequency extension than MUSES8920 (new_western_elec), more information/detail than MUSES8920 and NL8802 (new_western_elec ranking), richer musical reproduction than OPA2604AP in a phono EQ (NS-kobo, via search summary), JFET character like MUSES8920

**Typical uses:** op-amp rolling in DAC output, line and phono-EQ stages on EMP8-to-DIP8 adapters (community use), vendor-listed uses: preamplifiers, active filters, line amplifiers, I/V conversion amplifiers

**Caveats:**

- The praise comes from one or two Japanese hobbyist listeners. There are no independent measurements.
- SMD only (EMP-8 / DFN4035-8). DIP sockets need an adapter board, and the adapter and layout can colour comparisons.
- Only press-release typical figures are known. The datasheet min/max tables and the datasheet version were not checked.
- Supply risk: new_western_elec has voiced worry about Nisshinbo restructuring and past MUSES discontinuations (blog opinion, low confidence).
- Do not confuse it with MUSES8920/8920A/8921. Those are separate part numbers, not revisions of NL8902.

## Counterfeits

No reports of NL8902 counterfeits were found. Fake MUSES-branded parts are widely reported (e.g. MUSES01 fakes). NL parts are cheaper and not MUSES-branded, so they may be less attractive to counterfeiters (inference). Nisshinbo makes the NL8902 only in EMP-8 and DFN4035-8, so any 'NL8902' sold as a native DIP-8 part should be treated as suspect. Retailers' DIP adapter modules are legitimate. Buy from Akizuki, Kyohritsu eleshop, Marutsu, Macnica or other authorized channels.

## Related parts and alternatives

NL8802 (Nisshinbo): bipolar-input sibling, ranked just below NL8902 by new_western_elec, MUSES8921 (Nisshinbo, Oct 2025): JFET dual; press figures 8.0 nV/rtHz, 11 MHz GBW, 25 V/us, 0.0004% THD, 5 pA Ib (not rechecked this session), MUSES8920A / MUSES8920AE (Nisshinbo): replaced MUSES8920 with no change in electrical characteristics, per the vendor, MUSES01 (Nisshinbo/JRC): flagship JFET dual, NJM8901 (JRC/Nisshinbo): older BiFET 'entry' MUSES-technology dual, TI OPA1642 / OPA2604 / OPA1656: Western FET/CMOS-input audio duals

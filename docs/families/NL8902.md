# NL8902 / NL8902AN / NL8902GR

**JFET-input audio dual, unity-gain stable, SMD only** · Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2025–present) → New Japan Radio (JRC) / Nisshinbo MUSES line (2010s–2020s) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Nisshinbo's 2025 'entry' JFET dual built on MUSES know-how; one Japanese DIYer ranks it above MUSES8920 and NL8802.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 nV/√Hz typ; 1.4 µVrms (20 Hz–20 kHz) | 9 MHz typ | 20 V/µs typ | 0.0004% THD typ | ±4.0 V to ±16 V; abs. max 18 V per rail (36 V) | 1.3 mA per channel typ |

## Why it's rated

Little coverage outside Japan. Blogger new_western_elec ranked it top in a Sept 2025 A/B (OPA2604 < MUSES8820 < MUSES8920 < NL8802 < NL8902), hearing more treble extension and detail than MUSES8920. NS-kobo's phono-EQ test reportedly preferred it to OPA2604AP. Macnica's 'on par with higher-end models' is marketing. No ASR or other independent measurements exist.

**Described as:** extended highs, detailed, rich, musical (reportedly), MUSES8920-like JFET character

**Used in:** DAC output, line and phono-EQ stages via EMP8-to-DIP8 adapter, Preamplifiers and line amplifiers, Active filters, I/V conversion amplifiers

## Watch out

- Praise comes from one or two Japanese hobbyist listeners; no independent measurements.
- SMD only (EMP-8 / DFN4035-8). DIP sockets need an adapter, which can colour comparisons.
- Only press-release typical figures are known; datasheet min/max tables and version unchecked.
- new_western_elec worries about supply, citing Nisshinbo restructuring and past MUSES discontinuations (opinion).
- Not a revision of MUSES8920/8920A/8921; those are separate parts.
- **Counterfeits:** No NL8902 fakes reported. Cheaper non-MUSES branding may deter counterfeiters, unlike faked MUSES01 (inference). It ships only in EMP-8 and DFN4035-8, so a native DIP-8 'NL8902' is suspect; retailer DIP adapters are fine. Buy via Akizuki, Kyohritsu, Marutsu or Macnica.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

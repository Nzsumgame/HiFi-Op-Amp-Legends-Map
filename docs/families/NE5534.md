# NE5534 / NE5534A / SA5534 / SE5534 (incl. NJM5534) - single low-noise decompensated bipolar

**Tier** A: legend / hazard · **Category** Bipolar classics

*Signetics' 1979 low-noise decompensated single and the 5532's forerunner: about 3.5 nV/rtHz, 10 MHz and 600-ohm drive. A staple of consoles, CD/DAC stages and MM/mic preamps.*

**Technology:** Bipolar single op amp, decompensated. Community notes and the Signetics-derived datasheet description say it is internally compensated only for gains of about 3 or more. An external capacitor between COMP and COMP/BAL (pins 5 and 8) is needed for unity or low gain, and also optimises response. The Signetics-derived description ('Compared to other operational amplifiers such as TL083 they show better noise...') is confirmed in onsemi NE5534/D. Its presence in TI's 1990 edition comes from the original research session only. Dave Jones (EEVblog 1752) notes that the NE5532 and NE5534 'haven't always been exactly the same design' and that there are subtle differences.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (2 recorded).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The single-channel, decompensated forerunner of the NE5532. It is lower in noise than the 5532 and drives 600-ohm loads. Douglas Self covers it alongside the 5532 in his books ('The NE5532 and 5534 Opamps'), and it is widely used in MM phono, mic and line stages of consoles, CD players, DAC filter stages and ADC drivers (for example the AKM AK5383 reference circuit uses NJM5534). Dave Jones (EEVblog) treats it as the low-noise audio default, citing about 4 nV/rtHz and wishing a TL074-based design had used 5534s. Posts on diyAudio (not re-verified) say it sounds good when correctly compensated, and it is the usual baseline that parts such as the OPA627 are compared against. Soomal (China) treats it as an old-school, mid-grade part. In the Creative AE-9 teardown it calls the socketed TI NE5534 clearly lower grade than the LM49710 used before, and in listening found AD797 and OPA1611 more dynamic and resolving but 'harder' and less pleasing. It also notes Matrix 'boldly' using the 'old-flavour' NE5534 in the HPA-2 Classic DAC low-pass filter. In Japan, vintage Signetics/Philips NOS parts have a cult following, and bloggers rank them above TI and JRC versions (anecdotal).

**How people describe the sound:** neutral / clean when properly compensated (diyAudio, anecdotal), 'old-flavour' classic (Soomal, Matrix HPA-2 Classic teardown), softer and less 'hard' than AD797/OPA1611, but less dynamic and resolving (Soomal, AE-9 op-amp roll), vintage Signetics/Philips NOS described as an upgrade over TI/JRC (Japanese blogs, anecdotal)

**Typical uses:** MM phono and microphone preamps, mixing-console line and mic stages, CD-player / DAC I/V and low-pass filter stages (e.g. Matrix M-Stage HPA-2 Classic LPF), ADC input drivers (AKM AK5383 reference circuit uses NJM5534), socketed op-amp positions in sound cards (Creative AE-9 ships TI NE5534)

**Caveats:**

- Decompensated: in a unity-gain or low-gain socket it needs the external COMP to COMP/BAL capacitor. Dropping it into a unity-gain buffer position can oscillate.
- Second-source parts (Signetics/Philips, TI, onsemi, JRC) may be separate layouts. Vendor-specific SPICE models exist, but compensation-margin differences are community reports only, so verify stability when swapping brands.
- The TI NE5532 and NE5534 are not guaranteed identical designs (EEVblog 1752). Don't assume 5532 behaviour carries over to the 5534, or the reverse.
- TI NE5534AD (SOIC tube) is reported EOL under PDN 20230522005.3 (TI E2E; not re-verified). Order a reel or G4 variant.
- TI's NE5532 redesign (abs-max supply cut from +/-22 V to +/-18 V, slew-rate change; PCN with first ship 2024) does NOT apply to the NE5534 as of June 2026 per EEVblog 1752. Re-check for any future SLOS070 revision or PCN.
- The legacy supply range is +/-3 to +/-20 V (abs max +/-22 V), and the 600-ohm swing is specified at +/-18 V.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Signetics / Philips: c.2000 (claimed)

Japanese blogs (radiokits.jp / takinx.dcnblog.jp) claim the Signetics masters were lost in the 2000 Philips Albuquerque fab fire and that Philips redrew the layout, changing input-protection behaviour. No vendor document, PCN or datasheet change supports this, and no independent source was found in this check.

- **When:** c.2000 (claimed)
- **Affected:** NE5534, NE5534A, NE5532
- **How to tell old from new:** Claimed: older parts carry pad-printed (tampo) Signetics marking; later parts are laser-marked Philips. This identification method is folklore, not vendor-documented.
- **Audio impact:** Anecdotal only: Japanese listeners prefer older pad-printed Signetics NOS. No measurements were found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - no documented datasheet change; the claim is unverified folklore
- **Confidence:** low

### 2. Multiple second sources…: 1980s-present

Each vendor sells its own part under the same generic number. Community sources (GroupDIY 'different brands of ne5534', Japanese blogs) report behavioural and sonic differences. The existence of separate vendor-specific SPICE models (NJM5534, NE5534ON, NE5534PH and NE5534TI in Nazar Shtybel's library) suggests measurable differences. Datasheet-level deltas between vendors were not retrieved.

- **When:** 1980s-present
- **Affected:** NE5534, NE5534A, SA5534, NJM5534
- **How to tell old from new:** Manufacturer logo and prefix on the package (Signetics/Philips 'N' suffix, TI logo, ON logo, JRC 'NJM').
- **Audio impact:** Community reports rank Signetics/Philips above TI/JRC (anecdotal). Stability margins in low-gain positions may differ.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - check stability and compensation when swapping brands in unity or low-gain sockets
- **Confidence:** low

## Counterfeits

Discovery notes report remarked 'Philips/Signetics NOS' NE5534 on marketplaces; this was not independently verified. Japanese blogs say NOS NE5534 sells above NE5532, which would make relabelling attractive (anecdotal). Genuine OEM house-numbered Signetics NE5534s also exist (for example HP custom part numbers, per EEVblog), so an odd marking is not proof of a fake. Precautions: be sceptical of large 'NOS Signetics' lots, check that the marking style and date code fit the claimed era, and buy current production from authorised TI or onsemi distributors. Bench-check noise and stability: a decompensated part used at unity gain without the cap may oscillate, which can mask other differences.

NE5532 / NE5532A (dual, internally compensated sibling; note TI's redesigned NE5532, whose datasheet was revised Dec 2025, with abs-max supply of +/-18 V), NJM2114 (JRC dual low-noise part; used as the front buffer in Creative's AE-9), LME49710 (single, low distortion; Soomal rates it a grade above the NE5534 fitted in AE-9 sockets), LME49990 (single; Soomal's top pick for AE-9 direct mode), OPA1611 (single, 1.1 nV/rtHz bipolar), AD797 (single, ultra-low noise and distortion), LT1115 / LT1028 (ultra-low-noise bipolar singles for phono and mic stages)

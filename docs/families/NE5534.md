# NE5534 / NE5534A / SA5534 / SE5534 (incl. NJM5534) - single low-noise decompensated bipolar

**Tier** A: legend / hazard · **Category** Bipolar classics

*Signetics' 1979 low-noise decompensated single and the 5532's forerunner: about 3.5 nV/rtHz, 10 MHz and 600-ohm drive. A staple of consoles, CD/DAC stages and MM/mic preamps.*

**Technology:** Bipolar single op amp, decompensated: internally compensated for gains of about 3 or more. For unity or low gain, an external capacitor goes between COMP and COMP/BAL (pins 5 and 8). The TI datasheet headline figures of 13 V/us and 10 MHz UGBW were confirmed by web search. onsemi NE5534/D carries the Signetics-derived wording ('Compared to other operational amplifiers such as TL083...'). Dave Jones (EEVblog 1752) notes that the TI NE5532 and NE5534 'haven't always been exactly the same design'.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (3 recorded: Unclassified).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The single-channel, decompensated forerunner of the NE5532. It is lower in noise than the 5532 and drives 600-ohm loads. Douglas Self covers it alongside the 5532 in his books, and it is widely used in MM phono, mic and line stages of consoles, CD players, DAC filter stages and ADC drivers (the AKM AK5383 reference circuit uses NJM5534). Dave Jones (EEVblog) treats it as a low-noise audio default. diyAudio posters (not re-verified) say it sounds good when correctly compensated, and it is a common baseline that upgrades such as the OPA627 are compared against. Soomal (China) calls the TI NE5534 fitted in the Creative AE-9 clearly lower grade than the LM49710 it replaced. It also notes Matrix using the 'old-flavour' NE5534 in the HPA-2 Classic low-pass filter. In Japan, vintage Signetics/Philips NOS parts have a following, and bloggers rank them above TI and JRC versions (anecdotal).

**How people describe the sound:** neutral / clean when properly compensated (diyAudio, anecdotal), 'old-flavour' classic (Soomal, Matrix HPA-2 Classic teardown), softer and less 'hard' than AD797/OPA1611, but less dynamic and resolving (Soomal, AE-9 op-amp roll), vintage Signetics/Philips NOS described as an upgrade over TI/JRC (Japanese blogs, anecdotal)

**Typical uses:** MM phono and microphone preamps, mixing-console line and mic stages, CD-player / DAC I/V and low-pass filter stages (e.g. Matrix M-Stage HPA-2 Classic LPF), ADC input drivers (AKM AK5383 reference circuit uses NJM5534), socketed op-amp positions in sound cards (Creative AE-9 ships TI NE5534)

**Caveats:**

- Decompensated: in a unity-gain or low-gain socket it needs the external COMP to COMP/BAL capacitor. Dropping it into a unity-gain buffer position can oscillate.
- Second-source parts (Signetics/Philips, TI, onsemi, JRC) are separate vendor products. Differences in compensation margin are community reports only, so verify stability when swapping brands.
- The TI NE5532 and NE5534 are not guaranteed identical designs (EEVblog 1752). Don't assume 5532 behaviour carries over to the 5534, or the reverse.
- TI NE5534AD (SOIC tube) is reported EOL; TI points to NE5534ADRG4. Distributors have at times shown NE5534 variants as obsolete although TI lists them as active.
- TI's NE5532 redesign (PCN20231114002.1: abs-max supply +/-22 V to +/-18 V, ESD 2 kV to 1 kV) does NOT apply to the NE5534 as of Sep 2026. SLOS070D (Nov 2014) is still current. Re-check for any SLOS070E or NE5534 PCN.
- JRC NJM5534D/M are obsolete; the current JRC part is NJM5534DD.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Unclassified: Texas Instruments (sibling NE5532…, NE5532 process change PCN20231114002.1 (15 Nov 2023) and…

TI moved the NE5532 to a new process: abs-max supply dropped from +/-22 V to +/-18 V, ESD from 2 kV to 1 kV, and the slew rate changed (Hackaday, June 2026). Hackaday's headline says 'NE5532 and others', but EEVblog 1752 and the unchanged SLOS070D show the NE5534 is not included. A search summary also claimed that PCN 20231114002 declares the NE5534 EOL. That was not substantiated: the TI NE5534 and NE5534A product pages are live and active.

- **When:** NE5532 process change PCN20231114002.1 (15 Nov 2023) and NE5532x/SA5532x datasheet PCN20260429001.1 (30 Apr 2026); no NE5534 equivalent as of Sep 2026
- **Affected:** none in this family (NE5532x/SA5532x only)
- **How to tell old from new:** Not applicable to the NE5534. The TI NE5534 datasheet is still SLOS070D (Nov 2014), with abs max +/-22 V. If a SLOS070E or NE5534 PCN appears, compare abs-max supply, ESD and slew rate.
- **Audio impact:** None for the NE5534 at present.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - no NE5534 change documented. Risk only if TI later extends the NE5532 process change to SLOS070 parts.
- **Confidence:** medium

### 2. Unclassified: Signetics / Philips, c.2000 (claimed)

Japanese blogs (radiokits.jp / takinx.dcnblog.jp) claim the Signetics masters were lost in the 2000 Philips Albuquerque fab fire and that Philips redrew the layout. Web search confirmed that Signetics had Albuquerque fabs (FAB22/FAB23) but found no source linking a fire to an NE5534 redesign. A conflicting community claim says a fire at NXP's Caen, France fab ended NXP's 553x production. No vendor PCN or datasheet supports either claim.

- **When:** c.2000 (claimed)
- **Affected:** NE5534, NE5534A, NE5532
- **How to tell old from new:** Claimed: older parts carry pad-printed (tampo) Signetics marking; later parts are laser-marked Philips. This is folklore, not vendor-documented.
- **Audio impact:** Anecdotal only: Japanese listeners prefer older pad-printed Signetics NOS. No measurements were found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - no documented datasheet change; the claim is unverified folklore
- **Confidence:** low

### 3. Unclassified: Multiple second sources…, 1980s-present

Each vendor sells its own part under the same generic number. GroupDIY posters say each company supposedly uses its own production mask. GroupDIY and Head-Fi threads and Japanese blogs report behavioural and sonic differences. Separate vendor-specific SPICE models (NJM5534, NE5534ON, NE5534PH, NE5534TI) exist. Datasheet-level deltas between vendors were not retrieved.

- **When:** 1980s-present
- **Affected:** NE5534, NE5534A, SA5534, NJM5534
- **How to tell old from new:** Manufacturer logo and prefix on the package (Signetics/Philips 'N' suffix, TI logo, ON logo, JRC 'NJM').
- **Audio impact:** Community reports rank Signetics/Philips above TI/JRC (anecdotal). Stability margins in low-gain positions may differ.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - check stability and compensation when swapping brands in unity or low-gain sockets
- **Confidence:** low

## Counterfeits

Marketplaces sell 'Signetics / former Philips NE5534N' NOS (e.g. eBay listings); remarked fakes are reported in discovery notes but not independently verified. Japanese blogs say NOS NE5534 sells above NE5532, which would make relabelling attractive (anecdotal). Genuine OEM house-numbered Signetics NE5534s also exist (HP custom part numbers, per EEVblog), so an odd marking is not proof of a fake. Precautions: be sceptical of large 'NOS Signetics' lots, check that the marking style and date code fit the claimed era, and buy current production (TI NE5534P/NE5534AP/ADRG4, onsemi, NJM5534DD) from authorised distributors. Bench-check noise and stability: a decompensated part at unity gain without the cap may oscillate, which can mask other differences.

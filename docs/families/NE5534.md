# NE5534 / NE5534A / SA5534 / SE5534 / NJM5534

**Bipolar low-noise decompensated single op-amp** · Signetics (1979–1990s) → Philips Semiconductors / NXP (1990s–2000s) · second sources: Texas Instruments, onsemi, JRC / New Japan Radio / Nisshinbo Micro Devices, STMicroelectronics · Tier A (legend / revision hazard)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** Each vendor sells its own part under the generic number; GroupDIY posters say each uses its own mask. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*Signetics' 1979 low-noise single, forerunner of the 5532: ~3.5 nV/√Hz, 10 MHz, drives 600 Ω. Console and preamp staple.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.5 nV/√Hz typ | 10 MHz unity-gain bandwidth typ | 13 V/µs typ (NE5534, SA5534) | ±3 V to ±20 V; abs max ±22 V; 30 V headline |

## Why it's rated

The single, decompensated forerunner of the NE5532: quieter, and it drives 600 Ω. Douglas Self covers it, Dave Jones (EEVblog) treats it as a low-noise default, and it fills console, phono, mic, CD and DAC stages. diyAudio says it sounds good when compensated. Soomal rates the Creative AE-9's TI NE5534 below the LM49710. Japanese fans prize Signetics/Philips NOS.

**Described as:** neutral, clean, 'old-flavour' classic, softer than AD797/OPA1611, less dynamic and resolving, vintage NOS preferred (Japan)

**Used in:** MM phono and microphone preamps, Mixing-console line and mic stages, CD/DAC I/V and LPF (Matrix HPA-2 Classic), ADC drivers (AKM AK5383 reference uses NJM5534), Sound-card op-amp sockets (Creative AE-9 ships TI NE5534)

## Watch out

- Decompensated: low-gain or unity-gain sockets need the COMP to COMP/BAL capacitor, or it may oscillate.
- Brands (Signetics/Philips, TI, onsemi, JRC) are separate products; verify stability when swapping. TI's 5532 and 5534 differ.
- TI NE5534AD (SOIC tube) is EOL; use NE5534ADRG4. Distributors sometimes show active variants as obsolete.
- TI's NE5532 redesign (PCN20231114002.1: ±22 V→±18 V abs max, 2 kV→1 kV ESD) does not cover NE5534 yet.
- JRC NJM5534D/M are obsolete; the current JRC part is NJM5534DD.
- **Counterfeits:** Marketplaces sell 'Signetics / former Philips NE5534N' NOS; remarked fakes are reported but unverified. Genuine HP house-numbered Signetics parts exist. Check marking era and date code, buy current production from authorised distributors, and bench-check noise and stability.

## Silicon changes

### Second-source difference: Multiple second sources (Signetics/Philips vs TI vs onsemi vs JRC NJM5534), 1980s to present

Each vendor sells its own part under the generic number; GroupDIY posters say each uses its own mask. Forums and Japanese blogs report behavioural and sonic differences. Vendor-specific SPICE models exist (NJM5534, NE5534ON, NE5534PH, NE5534TI). Datasheet deltas between vendors were not retrieved.

- **Affected:** NE5534, NE5534A, SA5534, NJM5534
- **How to tell:** Manufacturer logo and prefix on the package (Signetics/Philips 'N' suffix, TI logo, ON logo, JRC 'NJM').
- **Audio impact:** Community reports rank Signetics/Philips above TI/JRC (anecdotal). Stability margins in low-gain positions may differ.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: check stability and compensation when swapping brands in low-gain sockets
- **Confidence:** low

# NE5534 / NE5534A / SA5534 / SE5534 (incl. NJM5534) - single low-noise decompensated bipolar

Signetics (1979 onward) → Philips Semiconductors / NXP (c.1990s-2000s (Philips); NXP from 2006) · second sources: Texas Instruments, ON Semiconductor / onsemi, JRC / New Japan Radio / Nisshinbo Micro Devices, STMicroelectronics · Tier A (legend / revision hazard)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** Each vendor sells its own part under the same generic number; [Details](#silicon-changes)

*Signetics' 1979 low-noise decompensated single and the 5532's forerunner: about 3.5 nV/rtHz, 10 MHz and 600-ohm drive. A staple of consoles, CD/DAC stages and MM/mic preamps.*

## Why it's rated

The single-channel, decompensated forerunner of the NE5532. It is lower in noise than the 5532 and drives 600-ohm loads. Douglas Self covers it alongside the 5532 in his books, and it is widely used in MM phono, mic and line stages of consoles, CD players, DAC filter stages and ADC drivers (the AKM AK5383 reference circuit uses NJM5534). Dave Jones (EEVblog) treats it as a low-noise audio default. diyAudio posters (not re-verified) say it sounds good when correctly compensated, and it is a common baseline that upgrades such as the OPA627 are compared against. Soomal (China) calls the TI NE5534 fitted in the Creative AE-9 clearly lower grade than the LM49710 it replaced. It also notes Matrix using the 'old-flavour' NE5534 in the HPA-2 Classic low-pass filter. In Japan, vintage Signetics/Philips NOS parts have a following, and bloggers rank them above TI and JRC versions (anecdotal).

**Described as:** neutral / clean when properly compensated (diyAudio, anecdotal), 'old-flavour' classic (Soomal, Matrix HPA-2 Classic teardown), softer and less 'hard' than AD797/OPA1611, but less dynamic and resolving (Soomal, AE-9 op-amp roll), vintage Signetics/Philips NOS described as an upgrade over TI/JRC (Japanese blogs, anecdotal)

**Used in:** MM phono and microphone preamps, mixing-console line and mic stages, CD-player / DAC I/V and low-pass filter stages (e.g. Matrix M-Stage HPA-2 Classic LPF), ADC input drivers (AKM AK5383 reference circuit uses NJM5534), socketed op-amp positions in sound cards (Creative AE-9 ships TI NE5534)

## Watch out

- Decompensated: in a unity-gain or low-gain socket it needs the external COMP to COMP/BAL capacitor. Dropping it into a unity-gain buffer position can oscillate.
- Second-source parts (Signetics/Philips, TI, onsemi, JRC) are separate vendor products. Differences in compensation margin are community reports only, so verify stability when swapping brands.
- The TI NE5532 and NE5534 are not guaranteed identical designs (EEVblog 1752). Don't assume 5532 behaviour carries over to the 5534, or the reverse.
- TI NE5534AD (SOIC tube) is reported EOL; TI points to NE5534ADRG4. Distributors have at times shown NE5534 variants as obsolete although TI lists them as active.
- TI's NE5532 redesign (PCN20231114002.1: abs-max supply +/-22 V to +/-18 V, ESD 2 kV to 1 kV) does NOT apply to the NE5534 as of Sep 2026. SLOS070D (Nov 2014) is still current. Re-check for any SLOS070E or NE5534 PCN.
- JRC NJM5534D/M are obsolete; the current JRC part is NJM5534DD.
- **Counterfeits:** Marketplaces sell 'Signetics / former Philips NE5534N' NOS (e.g. eBay listings); remarked fakes are reported in discovery notes but not independently verified. Japanese blogs say NOS NE5534 sells above NE5532, which would make relabelling attractive (anecdotal). Genuine OEM house-numbered Signetics NE5534s also exist (HP custom part numbers, per EEVblog), so an odd marking is not proof of a fake. Precautions: be sceptical of large 'NOS Signetics' lots, check that the marking style and date code fit the claimed era, and buy current production (TI NE5534P/NE5534AP/ADRG4, onsemi, NJM5534DD) from authorised distributors. Bench-check noise and stability: a decompensated part at unity gain without the cap may oscillate, which can mask other differences.

## Silicon changes

### Second-source difference: Multiple second sources (Signetics/Philips vs TI vs onsemi vs JRC NJM5534), 1980s to present

Each vendor sells its own part under the same generic number; GroupDIY posters say each company uses its own production mask. GroupDIY and Head-Fi threads and Japanese blogs report behavioural and sonic differences. Separate vendor-specific SPICE models exist (NJM5534, NE5534ON, NE5534PH, NE5534TI). Datasheet-level deltas between vendors were not retrieved.

- **Affected:** NE5534, NE5534A, SA5534, NJM5534
- **How to tell:** Manufacturer logo and prefix on the package (Signetics/Philips 'N' suffix, TI logo, ON logo, JRC 'NJM').
- **Audio impact:** Community reports rank Signetics/Philips above TI/JRC (anecdotal). Stability margins in low-gain positions may differ.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - check stability and compensation when swapping brands in unity-gain or low-gain sockets.
- **Confidence:** low

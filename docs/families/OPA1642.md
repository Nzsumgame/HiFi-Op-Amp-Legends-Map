# OPA1641 / OPA1642 / OPA1644

**JFET-input audio op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · Texas Instruments (2009–present) → Texas Instruments (2017–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*TI's 2009 SoundPlus JFET op-amp: 5.1 nV/√Hz, −126 dB THD+N, flat input capacitance. A common DAC filter/buffer part.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 nV/√Hz (5.8 @100 Hz, 8 @10 Hz); 4.3 µVpp audio band | 11 MHz; full-power bandwidth 3.2 MHz (1 Vp) | 20 V/µs; overload recovery 600 ns (G = −10) | 0.00005% (−126 dB); IMD −122 to −128 dB | 4.5–36 V (±2.25 V to ±18 V); 40 V absolute max | 1.8 mA typ / 2.3 mA max per amplifier |

## Why it's rated

Pitched at OPA2134-class uses with far lower distortion. Soomal describes a 'typical OPA1642 style': refined and balanced, mild delicate mids, relaxed bass; with OPA1662 (FiiO E17K) a warm 'BB' character. Used as DAC filter or buffer in FiiO, Lotoo, Astell&Kern, HiBy, Apogee and EarMen gear, and in the Hackaday 'OPA Alice' mic.

**Described as:** refined, delicate (细腻), balanced (均衡), mild, fine mids, relaxed bass, full dynamics, warm (偏暖), Burr-Brown house sound

**Used in:** DAC low-pass filter and I/V stages in DAPs, USB DACs, Line-out buffers; active filters, tone controls, mixing consoles, Phantom-powered electret mic preamps (OPA Alice; low quiescent current), Phono (RIAA) preamps; piezo and guitar buffers, DIY DACs valuing voltage-independent input capacitance

## Watch out

- SMD only (SOIC, VSSOP, TSSOP); DIP-8 rolling needs an adapter (e.g. Audiophonics).
- Input range ends 3.5 V below V+; below ±3.5 V it excludes midsupply.
- Not a headphone or 600 Ω driver: keep output under ~13 mA (2.8 kΩ min at 36 V).
- Use 24–51 Ω output isolation for capacitive loads; offset up to 3.5 mV may need a servo.
- VSSOP θJA is 180 °C/W; a shorted output at ±18 V dissipates over 600 mW.
- **Counterfeits:** No family-specific fakes reported. TI never made a DIP version, so a one-piece DIP-8 'OPA1642' is fake; genuine DIP forms are SOIC on adapters. Genuine marks: O1641A/O1642A/O1644A (SOIC, TSSOP), 1641/1642 (VSSOP). Beware remarked JFET duals on adapters.

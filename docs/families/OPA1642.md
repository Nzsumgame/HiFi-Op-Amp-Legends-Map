# OPA1641 / OPA1642 / OPA1644 (TI SoundPlus JFET-input single/dual/quad)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** JFET-input

*TI's 2009 SoundPlus JFET-input op-amp (5.1 nV/rtHz, -126 dB THD+N, flat input capacitance), a common DAC low-pass/buffer part in DAPs and DACs.*

**Technology:** True JFET input stage with a rail-to-rail output stage. Internally compensated and unity-gain stable. Rated 4.5-36 V (±2.25 to ±18 V). Internal phase-reversal protection. Input capacitance stays flat with common-mode voltage. Copper leadframe. TI SoundPlus audio line; the pre-2015 datasheet pages carry the Burr-Brown Audio logo. Surface-mount only: SOIC-8, VSSOP-8 (formerly called MSOP-8), SOIC-14, TSSOP-14.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

TI's JFET-input SoundPlus family, pitched at OPA2134-class uses but with far lower datasheet distortion. Soomal describes a 'typical OPA1642 style': the whole sound is refined and balanced, with mild, delicate mids and relaxed bass. Paired with the OPA1662 (FiiO E17K) Soomal hears the 'BB' character: relaxed, grand, full dynamics, warm overall. Review sites list it as the DAC low-pass-filter or line buffer in many portable and desktop products (e.g. FiiO X3K/X3 II/X5 III/M11/E17K/K13 R2R, Lotoo PAW Gold, Astell&Kern AK380, HiBy RS6, Apogee Element 24/Groove, EarMen CH-Amp). In DIY it is the core of the 'OPA Alice' phantom-powered electret mic (Hackaday 2020), where its low quiescent current is the selling point. DIY DAC builders also pick it for its voltage-independent input capacitance. No Audio Science Review or Samuel Groner measurement of the bare op-amp was checked.

**How people describe the sound:** refined / delicate (细腻), balanced (均衡), mild, fine mids (中频温和而细腻), relaxed bass (低频比较松弛), relaxed, grand, full dynamics, warm overall (松弛大气, 动态饱满, 偏暖; FiiO E17K with OPA1662), Burr-Brown house sound (Soomal)

**Typical uses:** DAC post-filter (LPF) and I/V stages in DAPs and USB DACs, line-out buffers, phantom-powered electret/condenser mic preamps (OPA Alice mic), phono (RIAA) preamps, high-impedance piezo/guitar buffers, active filters, tone controls, mixing consoles (TI target applications)

**Caveats:**

- Surface-mount only (SOIC, VSSOP, TSSOP). Op-amp rolling into DIP-8 sockets needs an adapter board (e.g. the Audiophonics OPA1642 DIP-8 adapter).
- The common-mode input range stops 3.5 V below V+. Below ±3.5 V supplies it does not include midsupply, which limits low-voltage single-supply unity-gain buffers.
- Not a headphone or 600 Ω driver. TI warns that 600 Ω at full supply can trip the current limit (ISC +36/-30 mA) and advises keeping output current under about 13 mA (2.8 kΩ minimum at 36 V single supply).
- Capacitive-load overshoot: the datasheet recommends 24-51 Ω output isolation (Figs 19-20).
- Offset is 1 mV typ / 3.5 mV max, higher than precision parts. DC-coupled outputs may need trimming or a servo.
- VSSOP-8 θJA is 180 °C/W. A shorted output at ±18 V dissipates over 600 mW.
- Soomal's sound descriptions are of whole devices, not the op-amp in isolation.

## Counterfeits

This run's limited searches found no OPA164x-specific counterfeit reports. Two facts from the datasheet help screen parts. First, TI never offered the family in DIP, only SOIC-8 (D), VSSOP-8 (DGK), SOIC-14 and TSSOP-14, so any one-piece DIP-8 body marked OPA1642 is not a genuine TI package. Legitimate DIP-8 versions are SOIC parts on adapter boards, such as the one Audiophonics sells. Second, the genuine top markings per the 2026 TI addendum are O1641A, O1642A and O1644A on SOIC and TSSOP, and 1641 or 1642 on VSSOP, plus the TI logo and lot code. The realistic risk is remarked generic JFET duals sold on DIP adapters. Buy from authorized distributors.

- PCN status is unresolved. Searches for OPA164x PCNs surfaced only generic TI RFAB fab-site / die-revision PCNs (e.g. 20230713001, 20230906001, 20230814006, 20240806004, 20250212004), and no web result confirmed that any of them lists OPA1641/1642/1644. - The exact revision held by each third-party/distributor mirror is not confirmed. Kyohritsu probably holds Rev B (Aug 2010); octopart 62126618 may hold Rev C (Dec 2015); the Original (Dec 2009) and Rev A (Apr 2010) copies have not been identified. They are needed to quantify the Rev D edits to 10 Hz/100 Hz e_n, 20 Hz-20 kHz noise, input bias current, VO test conditions and temperature range.
- The URLs opa1644.pdf symlink, focus.ti.com symlinks, sbos484/sbos484a/b/c/d and sbos791/sbos791a revision-specific paths are inferred from TI URL patterns and were not seen. They appear only in archive_seed_urls and need a Wayback check.
- The meaning of the '.B' orderable duplicates and the mixed VSSOP lead finishes in the 2026 addendum is undocumented (alternate assembly/test site?). It is not known whether they indicate any die or fab change.
- It is unverified whether OPA164x-Q1 (SBOS791A) uses the same die as the commercial parts, and what changed between SBOS791 Original and A. The -Q1 lifecycle status was not confirmed.
- The suspected close relationship between OPA164x and OPA140/OPA2140/OPA4140 is unverified.
- No independent bench measurements of the bare op-amp (ASR, Samuel Groner, Japanese blogs) were checked, and no forum reports of socket or adapter oscillation were found.

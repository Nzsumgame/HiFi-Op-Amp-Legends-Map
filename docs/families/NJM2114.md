# NJM2114 / JRC2114

**Bipolar low-noise audio dual, NPN input, 5532-type** · New Japan Radio (JRC / NJR) (?–2021) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2022–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*JRC's faster, lower-offset NJM5532 replacement; stock socketed part on Creative X-Fi/ZxR/AE-9 and ASUS Essence STX.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.9 µVrms typ (equivalent input noise) | 13 MHz typ | 15 V/µs typ | ±3.0 V to ±22.0 V |

## Why it's rated

A mid-tier Japanese audio dual sold and perceived as an improved NJM5532. JP reviews of the DD grade are mostly positive (firm bass, more clarity than NJM2068); one ranks it below NE5532. Best known abroad as the stock JRC2114 in Creative and ASUS sound cards; Soomal calls it a neutral buffer/I-V part. No independent measurements.

**Described as:** clear, firm bass, subdued highs, drier than OPA2134, less bass-heavy than NJM5532, clearer than NJM2068, neutral (对声音影响较小)

**Used in:** Creative X-Fi Titanium HD, Zx, ZxR, X7, AE-9 (with LME49710/NE5534), ASUS Xonar Essence STX I/V converter, E-MU 0202, Gigabyte Z170X-Gaming G1, TempoTec DAII Plus, Drop-in NJM5532/NJM2068/NJM4580 swap in JP DIY gear, Reportedly I/V in some Philips/Marantz CD players (unverified)

## Watch out

- Soomal (AE-9, 2020): swapping JRC2114 brings little; upgrade the NE5534 singles instead (e.g. LME49710).
- Mixed JP rankings: one comparison (likely nabe.adiary.jp) rates it C-rank, below NE5532, LM4562 and OPA2134.
- Maintenance status (2023), Marutsu sales ended, NJM2114L obsolete: expect shrinking stock and more grey-market parts.
- 5532-class bipolar input: bias current and source impedance matter; 13 MHz GBW needs sensible decoupling.
- Zigsow DD review: 'same price class as NE5532 but better sound'.
- **Counterfeits:** No NJM2114-specific counterfeit reports; 'JRC2114D' is just the genuine marking. Grey-market risk rises as stock shrinks; marketplace lots use every alias. Buy from Mouser (513-NJM2114D / 513-NJM2114M), DigiKey, Akizuki or Keisei; check marking and date code match the DD grade.

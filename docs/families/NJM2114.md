# NJM2114 / JRC2114 (dual low-noise bipolar, improved 5532-type)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*JRC's 'improved NJM5532' dual. It is the stock I/V and filter op-amp in Creative X-Fi Titanium HD, ZxR, X7 and AE-9 and in the ASUS Essence STX, and a cheap swap in Japanese DIY.*

**Technology:** Bipolar dual op-amp. It is internally compensated: the standard 8-pin dual pinout leaves no compensation pins. A JP search summary says it has an NPN input stage and is NJM5532-compatible, with higher slew rate, wider bandwidth and lower offset. Datasheet title per the KiCad library: 'Dual High Performance Low Noise Operational Amplifier'. Numeric specs were not verified in this run.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A mid-tier Japanese audio dual, marketed and perceived as JRC's improved NJM5532. JP DIY reviews of NJM2114DD are mostly positive: a Zigsow review is titled 'same price class as NE5532 but better sound', and reviewers describe clear, firm bass and more clarity than NJM2068. Opinion is not unanimous: one JP comparison ranks NJM2114 (with NJM4580) C-rank, below NE5532, LM4562 and OPA2134 (B-rank). Internationally it is best known as the stock JRC2114(D) in the Creative X-Fi Titanium HD, Zx, ZxR, X7 and AE-9, the ASUS Xonar Essence STX, the Gigabyte Z170X-Gaming G1 and the TempoTec DAII Plus. Soomal considers it a neutral buffer/I-V part. No independent measurements were found.

**How people describe the sound:** 'Same price class as NE5532 but better sound' (Zigsow NJM2114DD review title), clear, firm bass with relatively subdued highs (JP NJM2114DD reviews, via search summary), not bass-heavy like NJM5532; drier than OPA2134; slightly more coloured than LME49860 (JP reviews, via search summary), 'higher-performance 2068: wins on information and clarity' (JP comparison, via search summary), 'JRC2114对声音影响较小': has relatively little effect on the sound (Soomal AE-9 review, 2020)

**Typical uses:** Creative Sound Blaster X-Fi Titanium HD (2010): socketed JRC2114D, presumed filter and I/V, with LME49710 singles (Soomal), Creative Sound Blaster ZxR (2013): socketed NJM2114D + LME49710 on front channels, SMD JRC2114 on others. Creative spec: 'Headphone Op-Amps (Swappable) NJM2114D', TPA6120A2 driver., Creative Sound Blaster Zx (2013) and SoundBlaster X7 (2015, JRC2114D + LME49710) (Soomal), Creative Sound Blaster AE-9 (2020): socketed JRC2114 buffer with NE5534 singles; SMD JRC2114 + L49720 on sub-channels (Soomal), ASUS Xonar Essence STX (2009): socketed 2114D used as the I/V converter (Soomal), E-MU 0202 USB (Soomal teardown, 2010); Gigabyte Z170X-Gaming G1 onboard audio (2015); TempoTec Fantasia DAII Plus DAC, two 2114D as I/V (2017), I/V stage in some Philips / Marantz CD players (head-fi opamp thread p.113; models unverified, low confidence), Drop-in NJM5532 / NJM2068 / NJM4580 swap in Japanese headphone amps, USB-DAC filters and cassette decks

**Caveats:**

- Soomal's AE-9 review (2020-04-15) says op-amps on Creative's EARLIER cards were already good, so swapping brought almost no improvement. It calls the AE-9's stock NE5534 singles puzzling, and advises that users wanting a different sound should upgrade the two singles (LME49710 for continuity) rather than the JRC2114.
- Mixed JP rankings: one comparison surfaced by search ranks it C-rank, below NE5532. The page was not pinned; the candidate is nabe.adiary.jp/opamp-compare.
- Availability risk: a 2023 blog reports Nisshinbo made it a maintenance (pre-EOL) product. Not confirmed on Nisshinbo's list, and the affected packages are unknown.
- No objective measurements (ASR, Groner, jh4vaj) were found. Stability, output drive and supply limits are unverified. Treat it as a 5532-class bipolar-input part: bias current and source impedance matter.

## Counterfeits

No NJM2114-specific counterfeit or remark reports were found. 'Clones and counterfeits of JRC parts are common' is an unsourced generic remark. 'JRC2114D' is the normal name derived from the genuine part's JRC-logo + '2114D' marking and does not by itself mean a clone. Secondary-market lots exist (e.g. a Reverb listing 'JRC NJM2114D Dual Audio Op Amp, Improved NE5532, DIP, Lot of 6'). Prefer authorised channels (Mouser 513-NJM2114D / 513-NJM2114M, Akizuki, Marutsu, Keisei), especially if the maintenance status is confirmed.

## Related parts and alternatives

NJM5532 / NE5532 (the baseline it improves on; JRC's own version is NJM5532), NJM2068 / NJM2068DD (JRC sibling low-noise dual, PNP input), NJM4580 (JRC standard audio dual), MUSES8820 / MUSES8920 (Nisshinbo audio-grade duals), LM4562 / LME49720 (modern low-distortion bipolar dual; LME49710 is the single partner in Creative cards), OPA1612 / OPA1602 (modern low-noise bipolar duals)

# AD8671 / AD8672 / AD8674

**Precision very-low-noise op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · Analog Devices (c.2002–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*ADI's 2.8 nV/√Hz, 10 MHz precision part; Head-Fi rollers found it cleaner and more detailed than NJM2068 and AD8620.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.8 nV/√Hz typ; 77 nV p-p (0.1–10 Hz) | 10 MHz | 4 V/µs | ±5 V to ±15 V | about 3 mA per amplifier (unconfirmed) |

## Why it's rated

Its audio reputation rests mainly on one Head-Fi thread, 'AD8672 - a nice audio opamp?'. One listener found it cleaner than the NJM2068, more detailed than the NJM2068 and AD8620, and harsher than the mellow AD8620 ('not to say piercing'), and less relaxing because of the detail. ADI markets it as a precision part, not an audio part.

**Described as:** clean, detailed, forward, somewhat harsh, not relaxing

**Used in:** Op-amp rolling in DIY headphone amps and DAC outputs, Precision low-noise signal conditioning (ADI's intended use)

## Watch out

- Praise is thin: mostly one subjective Head-Fi thread.
- Needs at least ±5 V (10 V total); unsuitable for low-voltage or USB-powered gear.
- SOIC/MSOP/TSSOP only, so rolling needs an adapter.
- Bipolar input with up to 12 nA bias: keep source impedances low.
- **Counterfeits:** No part-specific counterfeit reports. Buy from authorized distributors, as with other popular SMD precision op-amps. Leaded non-Z codes (AD8672ARM, AD8674ARU) are old stock; treat loose market lots with caution.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

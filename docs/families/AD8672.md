# AD8671 / AD8672 / AD8674 (ADI very-high-precision low-noise single/dual/quad)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

**Technology:** Very-high-precision, low-noise, low-offset op-amp, 10 MHz bandwidth (bipolar)

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The Head-Fi thread 'AD8672 - a nice audio opamp?' has listening comparisons that call it cleaner than the NJM2068 and more detailed than both the NJM2068 and the AD8620, but harsher ('not to say piercing') than the mellow AD8620.

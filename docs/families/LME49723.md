# LME49723 (National / TI dual bipolar audio op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*Little-known National/TI LME-series dual audio op-amp. One experienced diyAudio listener ranks it among the best-sounding VFB parts, with fuller bass than the LM4562.*

**Technology:** Dual voltage-feedback audio op-amp with a bipolar input (per community sources), from National Semiconductor's LME49xxx 'high-fidelity audio' line (datasheet title: 'Dual High Fidelity Audio Operational Amplifier'). Split supply ±2.5 V to ±17 V. The sources found do not describe the process or topology.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche cult part. In the diyAudio thread 'The best sounding audio integrated opamps', one experienced listener names the LME49723 among the 'absolute best sounding' VFB op-amps (with LT1028, OPA132UA, LME49720HA) and his favourite plastic-DIP op-amp, reporting fuller bass and more detail than the LM4562/LME49720 but sometimes too much clarity and thin body on high violin notes. A Head-Fi comment (attribution unconfirmed) says it differs from the LM4562/LME49720/LME49860 more than they differ from each other. AudioKarma members included it in CD-player op-amp rolls where the OPA2604 and a Sparkos discrete were preferred. No objective measurement review was found.

**How people describe the sound:** fuller, more convincing bass dynamics and body (one listener), more detail than the LM4562/LME49720 (one listener), on some recordings too much clarity, thin body on high violin notes (one listener), sounds more different from the LM4562/LME49720/LME49860 than they do from each other (unconfirmed attribution)

**Typical uses:** DIP-8 op-amp rolling in CD players, DACs and preamps, Op-amp upgrades in headphone amps and line stages, SOIC-8 audio stages in DIY designs, e.g. Rezzonics NuTube overdrive and NuTubeAmp (LME49723MA/NOPB in BOMs)

**Caveats:**

- TI lists the device as NRND.
- The DIP version is reportedly scarce and was not found at distributors in 2026 searches; most supply is SOIC-8, needing an adapter for socket rolling.
- Praise comes from a handful of listeners; no published measurements found.
- AudioKarma advises careful decoupling against oscillation for the sister LME49720; no LME49723-specific report found.
- Split-supply part (±2.5 V minimum), not a low-voltage single-supply op-amp; one hobby project replaced it with an MCP6001 for that reason.

### Revision chain status

TI SNAS429 (the National Semiconductor datasheet taken over by TI), oldest to newest: (1) the National-format original, now counted as SNAS429A. Search summaries give it as "SNAS429A, February 11, 2008", and the TI header gives the original date as January 2008. Copies are at octopart 10848611, alldatasheet 216563 and datasheet4u/datasheetspdf id 624470. (2) SNAS429B, "JANUARY 2008 - REVISED APRIL 2013", the TI-format reissue at ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lme49723.pdf and octopart 47564704. No revision C or later was found, and the product is NRND. Still missing: (a) the National DS/DSxxxxxx document number, which no summary showed. (b) Whether a National edition existed before SNAS429A. Under TI's lettering, the first release would normally have no letter, so a pre-Feb-2008 or January 2008 initial edition may have existed. It was not confirmed. (c) The SNAS429B "Changes from Revision A" list, which no summary gave. (d) Any National edition from 2009-2011, none found. No second-source vendors exist for LME49723. The new mirror copies added here (datasheetspdf, digchip, datasheetdir, aipcba, electronicsdatasheets) do not show a revision footer in their summaries and are marked low confidence. The archive seed URLs follow the standard National and TI URL patterns (national.com/ds/LM/, focus.ti.com symlink, ti.com/lit/ds/snas429x/). They are for Wayback lookup and were not seen in any search result.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit report specific to the LME49723 was found. Sister LME parts are widely faked (remarked 'LME49710' documented by tinytransistors.net 2022; fake 'LME49720NA' on diyAudio). With the DIP NA version scarce and the part NRND, treat cheap marketplace 'LME49723NA' DIPs with suspicion and prefer LME49723MA/NOPB from authorised distributors (TI, Mouser, DigiKey). General caution, not a documented LME49723 case (confidence low).

LME49720 / LM4562 (same National LME audio family, standard dual pinout), LME49860 (high-voltage member of the same family), OPA1612 / OPA1602 (modern TI bipolar audio duals), NE5532 / NJM5532 (classic bipolar dual), OPA1656 / OPA1678 (current TI audio duals)

- Input bias current, offset, Iq per channel, open-loop gain and i_n still to be taken from the datasheet.

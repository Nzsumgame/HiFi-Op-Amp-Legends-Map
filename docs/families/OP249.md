# OP249

**Precision high-speed JFET-input op-amp, dual** · PMI (late 1980s–c. 1990) → Analog Devices (c. 1990–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** PCN 10_0062 'OP249 Data sheet and Die Changes': minor mask change for diode performance and stability, and lower-stress passivation on ceramic DIP to improve Vos. (+1 more) [Details](#silicon-changes)

*PMI/ADI's fast (22 V/µs) precision JFET dual, 'highly recommended' in the 1993 rec.audio FAQ.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17 nV/√Hz (26 at 100 Hz, 75 at 10 Hz) | 4.7 MHz | 22 V/µs | 5.6 mA typ / 7.0 mA max |

## Why it's rated

Thin, mostly historical. Bob Neidorff's 1993 rec.audio Audio FAQ lists OP249 among 'highly recommended' audio duals with OPA2604, AD797, LT1115 and HA5112, noting FET parts suit line-level, high-impedance signals. Fosi Audio lists it as a DIP-8 rolling option for the ZA3. A SOWA synth mixer chose it for 'low offset and good dynamic parameters'. No measured reviews.

**Used in:** Line-level audio stages and buffers, DIP-8 op-amp rolling (e.g. Fosi Audio ZA3), DC-coupled CV/audio mixers in synth DIY (SOWA)

## Watch out

- A faster op-amp swapped into existing gear may oscillate (general FAQ warning).
- 17 nV/√Hz is noisier than bipolar audio duals; output drive unconfirmed for headphone duty.
- Reputation rests on a 1990s FAQ and vendor lists; no modern measurements.
- Parts from before and after the c. 2010 fab transfer and mask change may differ in offset.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific fake reports (not searched in depth). Leaded GP/GS and old ceramic parts are mostly broker stock. Check logo, marking, date code; measure supply current (~5.6 mA), offset, slew (22 V/µs). Genuine parts differ around 2010 (PCN 10_0062).

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, before 2010 · PCN 10_0062

PCN 10_0062 says OP249 moved to ADI's ADWIL (Wilmington) wafer fab and then showed more Vos variation. That led to the mask and passivation fixes in the die-redesign entry. Transfer date not known.

- **Affected:** OP249 (all grades)
- **How to tell:** Not documented; only lot or date code could separate pre- and post-transfer wafers.
- **Audio impact:** Probably negligible: the effect is on DC offset variation; no AC change reported.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same design and pinout; wider offset spread on post-transfer lots before the fix.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab | earlier fab (not named in the sources seen) | ADI Wilmington (ADWIL) |
| Vos variation (observed) | baseline | increased (per PCN 10_0062 background) |

### Die redesign: Analog Devices, c. 2010 · PCN 10_0062 (dates inferred)

PCN 10_0062 'OP249 Data sheet and Die Changes': minor mask change for diode performance and stability, and lower-stress passivation on ceramic DIP to improve Vos. Both pre-qualified on the military version. Includes Group C data and old-versus-new spec comparison.

- **Affected:** OP249 (all grades), OP249AZ, OP249FZ, OP249GPZ, OP249GSZ
- **How to tell:** No marking change documented; use date code versus PCN effective date. Passivation change applies to CERDIP (OP249FZ, probably OP249AZ).
- **Audio impact:** Probably negligible: targets offset stability; slew, GBW and noise not reported changed.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same pinout; only Vos/TCVos specs revised, relevant to DC-precision circuits.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Die mask | original diode layout | minor mask change to improve diode performance and stability |
| CERDIP passivation | previous passivation | lower-stress passivation |
| Offset voltage and Vos temperature coefficient limits (data sheet Table 4) | earlier limits (values not captured) | changed (the Rev. I history lists 'Changes to Offset Voltage Parameter and Offset Voltage Temperature Coefficient Parameter, Table 4'); linking this to the PCN is an inference |
| OP249F columns (data sheet Table 3) | present | deleted (per the revision history; timing relative to the PCN not verified) |

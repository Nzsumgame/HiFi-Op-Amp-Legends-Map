# OP249 (PMI / Analog Devices dual precision high-speed JFET)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*PMI/ADI's fast (22 V/µs) precision JFET dual. The 1993 rec.audio FAQ rated it 'highly recommended', and Fosi still lists it as a DIP-8 rolling option.*

**Technology:** Dual precision high-speed op amp with a P-channel JFET input pair. The input type comes from the PMI SPICE macromodel (JX = PJF), not the datasheet. Designed by Precision Monolithics (PMI) and continued by Analog Devices.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Evidence is thin and mostly historical. Bob Neidorff's rec.audio Audio FAQ (part 2, v1.5, last modified 1993-06-04) lists OP249 among its 'highly recommended' audio duals, alongside OPA2604, AD797, LT1115 and HA5112. The FAQ also says FET op amps such as OPA2604 suit line-level, high-source-impedance signals. Today Fosi Audio's ZA3 marketing lists OP249 among compatible DIP-8 duals for op-amp rolling. Synth DIYers (the SOWA 6-input CV/audio mixer) picked it for 'low offset and good dynamic parameters'. Bordodynov's LTspice collection files it under 'AudioOpamp/AD'. No measurement-based audio reviews (Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner) and no community sound descriptors were found in this run.

**Typical uses:** Line-level audio stages and buffers (FET-input use case described in the 1993 rec.audio FAQ), Op-amp rolling in DIP-8 sockets (for example the Fosi Audio ZA3), DC-coupled CV/audio mixers in synth DIY (SOWA mixer)

**Caveats:**

- The rec.audio FAQ warns that faster op amps swapped into existing gear may oscillate, depending on design and layout. This is a general warning, not specific to OP249.
- No datasheet noise, supply-current or output-drive figures were verified in this run, so check the ADI datasheet before using it for headphone drive or low-noise duty.
- Reputation rests on a 1990s FAQ plus vendor marketing lists; there is no modern measured evidence of audio superiority.

## Counterfeits

No OP249-specific counterfeit or remarking reports were found. This run was limited to GitHub-hosted sources, because web search was exhausted and other hosts are blocked. OP249 is a legacy PMI/ADI precision part that appears on marketplace and IC-listing sites (for example OP249GPZ/OP249GSZ pages on ic.123408.xyz). Treat cheap marketplace or pulled lots with the usual care: compare the logo, top marking and date-code format with genuine ADI parts, and measure supply current, offset and slew rate against the ADI datasheet. Suspect any 'OP249' in unusual packages or at very low prices.

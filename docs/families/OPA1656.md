# OPA1655 / OPA1656 (single/dual CMOS FET-input, Burr-Brown audio)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** CMOS-input

*TI's 2019 Burr-Brown FET-input audio op-amp: -129 dB THD+N at 20 kHz, fA-level current noise, 100 mA output; TI pitches it as the OPA2134/OPA2604 successor.*

**Technology:** CMOS FET-input. The datasheet says "FET-input architecture" and describes three gain stages with a feedforward path and a high-current rail-to-rail output stage. Supply is 4.5-36 V (±2.25 to ±18 V). The input common-mode range runs from V- to (V+) - 2.25 V, so the input is not rail-to-rail.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

TI's own E2E FAQ presents the OPA1656 as the next-generation replacement for the OPA2134 and OPA2604 and calls it the lowest-voltage-noise high-voltage CMOS op-amp on the market (vendor claim). Hi-Fi and DIY users treat it as the modern, transparent, low-distortion FET-input Burr-Brown part. Community reports from the discovery sweep (not re-checked this run): in an ASR input-shorted phono noise test the roughly €5 OPA1656 matched the roughly €33 MUSES02; a Fosi ZA3 user said it 'basically wipes the floor' with Burson V7 Classic modules; Japanese listeners call it clean and balanced, with less colouration than the OPA2134; one SQ5J DAC test picked it for the I/V stage for a 'very modern' presentation; diyAudio and AMB threads discuss it as an OPA1612 substitute.

**How people describe the sound:** transparent, clean, balanced / flat, low colouration and less harsh than OPA2134, modern presentation, good spatial / soundstage rendering, OPA1655: high fidelity, smooth voices and instruments, slightly treble-leaning, good value (Japanese listening summary, low confidence)

**Typical uses:** DAC I/V and output/filter stages, headphone-amp gain and line stages (100 mA output; TI FAQ recommends it as a headphone driver), high-gain microphone preamps (TI FAQ), MM phono preamps (datasheet application 8.2.1; fA-level current noise suits high source impedance), op-amp rolling on SOIC-to-DIP8 adapters (Fosi and similar), measurement and ADC front-end buffers, active tone controls and guitar/XLR interfaces (datasheet examples)

**Caveats:**

- Packages are SOIC-8 only for the OPA1656 (SOIC-8 and SOT-23-5 for the OPA1655). There is no DIP, so rolling needs third-party adapter boards of variable quality and unknown origin.
- It is fast (53 MHz GBW, 20 MHz unity-gain bandwidth), so socket and adapter parasitics and poor decoupling can cause ringing or oscillation. The datasheet rates capacitive load drive at 100 pF and shows RISO of 25-50 Ω taming overshoot. The oscillation risk in sockets is an engineering inference, not a documented incident.
- The marketed 2.9 nV/rtHz is measured at 10 kHz. The datasheet gives 4.3 nV/rtHz at 1 kHz and 11.8 nV/rtHz at 100 Hz (CMOS 1/f noise), so bipolar parts are quieter in low-impedance circuits.
- Maximum supply is 36 V (±18 V; absolute max 40 V), so it is not a drop-in for OPA2604 circuits running ±20-24 V rails.
- The input is not rail-to-rail: VCM tops out at (V+) - 2.25 V, which limits follower headroom on low rails.
- Iq is 3.9 mA per channel typical (4.6 mA max), about 7.8 mA per dual package.

## Counterfeits

No vendor or forensic counterfeit report was found. A Sep 2026 web search returned a general summary that AliExpress OPA1656s may be fake or may not work, and that some sellers ship genuine parts in small orders and fakes in larger ones. That summary could not be tied to a specific OPA1656 thread; the related threads are diyAudio 'OPA1612 from Aliexpress, no counterfeit but why so cheap?' and the Super-Freq 'Fake op-amps' page. Treat it as low confidence. AliExpress listings such as 'New Original Non counterfeit OPA1656IDR' exist. Vendor-documented identifiers from the SBOS901C addendum: the OPA1656 exists only in SOIC-8 (D), marked 'OP1656' plus the TI logo and lot-trace code. The OPA1655 is marked 'OP1655' in SOIC and '2R8Q' in SOT-23-5. TI makes no DIP-8 package, so every DIP 'OPA1656' is a third-party adapter; inspect the SOIC on it. Suggested plausibility checks (engineering inference): about 7.8 mA supply current per dual package at ±15-18 V; pA-level input bias current, where a relabelled bipolar part would show nA or more; 20 MHz unity-gain bandwidth and 24 V/µs slew rate.

# OPA1655 / OPA1656 (single/dual CMOS FET-input, Burr-Brown audio)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** CMOS-input

*TI's 2019 Burr-Brown FET-input audio op-amp. It has -129 dB THD+N at 20 kHz, fA-level current noise and 100 mA output, and TI positions it as the successor to the OPA2134 and OPA2604.*

**Technology:** CMOS FET-input. The datasheet says "FET-input architecture" and describes three gain stages with a feedforward path and a high-current rail-to-rail output stage. Supply is 4.5-36 V. The input common-mode range runs from V- to (V+) - 2.25 V, so the input is not rail-to-rail.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

TI's E2E FAQ presents the OPA1656 as the next-generation replacement for the OPA2134 and OPA2604. Hi-Fi and DIY users treat it as the modern, transparent, low-distortion FET-input Burr-Brown part. In an ASR input-shorted phono noise test, the roughly €5 OPA1656 matched the roughly €33 MUSES02. A Fosi ZA3 user said it 'basically wipes the floor' with Burson V7 Classic modules. Japanese listeners call it clean and balanced, with less colouration and harshness than the OPA2134. In a four-way SQ5J DAC test it was picked as the I/V stage (with OPA2211 in the differential stage) for a 'very modern' presentation with good spatial character. diyAudio and AMB threads discuss it as an OPA1612 substitute. These community claims come from the discovery notes and were not re-checked this run, because the web-search budget was exhausted.

**How people describe the sound:** transparent, clean, balanced / flat, low colouration and less harsh than OPA2134, modern presentation, good spatial / soundstage rendering, OPA1655: high fidelity, smooth voices and instruments, slightly treble-leaning, good value (Japanese listening summary, low confidence)

**Typical uses:** DAC I/V and output/filter stages, headphone-amp gain and line stages (100 mA output), MM phono preamps (datasheet application 8.2.1; fA-level current noise suits high source impedance), op-amp rolling on SOIC-to-DIP8 adapters (Fosi and similar), measurement and ADC front-end buffers, active tone controls and guitar/XLR interfaces (datasheet examples)

**Caveats:**

- Packages are SOIC-8 only for the OPA1656 (SOIC-8 and SOT-23-5 for the OPA1655). There is no DIP, so rolling needs third-party adapter boards of variable quality and unknown origin.
- It is fast (53 MHz GBW, 20 MHz unity-gain bandwidth), so socket and adapter parasitics and poor decoupling can cause ringing or oscillation. The datasheet rates capacitive load drive at 100 pF and shows RISO of 25-50 Ω taming overshoot. Use a series output resistor when driving cables. The oscillation risk in sockets is an engineering inference, not a documented incident.
- The marketed 2.9 nV/rtHz is measured at 10 kHz. The datasheet gives 4.3 nV/rtHz at 1 kHz and 11.8 nV/rtHz at 100 Hz (CMOS 1/f noise), so bipolar parts are quieter in low-impedance circuits.
- Maximum supply is 36 V (±18 V; absolute max 40 V), so it is not a drop-in for OPA2604 circuits running ±20-24 V rails.
- The input is not rail-to-rail: VCM tops out at (V+) - 2.25 V, which limits follower headroom on low rails.
- Iq is 3.9 mA per channel typical (4.6 mA max), about 7.8 mA per dual package.

## Counterfeits

No vendor or forensic counterfeit report was found; this was not searched this run. The discovery notes mention suspected fake OPA1656s from AliExpress, which is unverified (low confidence). Vendor-documented identifiers from the SBOS901C addendum: the OPA1656 exists only in SOIC-8 (D), marked 'OP1656' plus the TI logo and lot-trace code. The OPA1655 is marked 'OP1655' in SOIC and '2R8Q' in SOT-23-5. A chip body in DIP-8 marked OPA1656 or OPA1655 is therefore not a TI package: every DIP 'OPA1656' is a third-party adapter, so inspect the SOIC on it. Suggested plausibility checks (engineering inference): supply current of about 7.8 mA per dual package at ±15-18 V (3.9 mA per channel typ, 4.6 max); pA-level input bias current, where a relabelled bipolar part would show nA or more; and 20 MHz unity-gain bandwidth / 24 V/µs slew rate.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA1612 (TI bipolar; 1.1 nV/rtHz, better with low source impedance, higher current noise), OPA1642 / OPA1641 (TI JFET-input SoundPlus), OPA1652 / OPA1654 (older, cheaper TI FET-input SoundPlus, SBOS477; different die despite the 'OPA165x' name), OPA2156 (TI 36 V CMOS), OPA828 (TI JFET, high slew rate), OPA1688 (named by TI, per discovery notes, as an OPA2604 replacement alongside the OPA1656), OPA2134 / OPA2604 (the predecessors it replaces), MUSES02 (Nisshinbo; matched on noise in an ASR phono test)

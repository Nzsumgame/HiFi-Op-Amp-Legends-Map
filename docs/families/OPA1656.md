# OPA1655 / OPA1656

**CMOS FET-input audio op-amp, 1/2 channels** · Texas Instruments (Burr-Brown brand) (2019–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*TI's 2019 Burr-Brown FET-input part: −129 dB THD+N at 20 kHz, fA current noise, 100 mA; OPA2134/OPA2604 successor.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.3 nV/√Hz (2.9 at 10 kHz; 11.8 at 100 Hz) | 53 MHz (G = 100); 20 MHz unity-gain bandwidth | 24 V/µs | −131 dB 1 kHz; −129 dB (2 kΩ), −120 dB (600 Ω) 20 kHz | ±2.25 V to ±18 V (4.5–36 V); abs. max 40 V | 3.9 mA/ch typ, 4.6 mA max (5.0 mA max, −40 to 125 °C) |

## Why it's rated

The modern, transparent, low-distortion Burr-Brown FET part. TI calls it the lowest-noise high-voltage CMOS op-amp (vendor claim). Reportedly: in an ASR phono noise test the €5 OPA1656 matched the €33 MUSES02; a Fosi ZA3 user said it 'wipes the floor' with Burson V7 Classic; Japanese listeners find it cleaner than OPA2134. diyAudio and AMB discuss it as an OPA1612 substitute.

**Described as:** transparent, clean, balanced, flat, less coloured than OPA2134, modern presentation, good soundstage, OPA1655: smooth, slightly bright

**Used in:** DAC I/V, output and filter stages, Headphone amp gain and line stages (100 mA out), Mic preamps and MM phono preamps (fA current noise), Op-amp rolling on SOIC-to-DIP8 adapters (Fosi etc.), Measurement buffers, tone controls, guitar/XLR interfaces

## Watch out

- SOIC-8 only (OPA1655 also SOT-23-5); DIP rolling needs third-party adapters of variable quality.
- Fast (53 MHz GBW, Zo 26 Ω at 1 MHz): adapters or weak decoupling may ring; RISO 25–50 Ω helps.
- Marketed 2.9 nV/√Hz is at 10 kHz; 4.3 at 1 kHz, 11.8 at 100 Hz. Bipolars win at low impedance.
- 36 V maximum supply (40 V abs.): not a drop-in for OPA2604 circuits on ±20–24 V rails.
- Input not rail-to-rail: VCM tops out at (V+) − 2.25 V, limiting follower headroom on low rails.
- **Counterfeits:** No forensic reports. AliExpress parts are reportedly sometimes fake (low confidence). Genuine: SOIC-8 marked OP1656 (OPA1655: OP1655, SOT-23 2R8Q); every DIP is an adapter. Check about 7.8 mA per dual, pA-level bias (bipolar fakes show nA), 20 MHz, 24 V/µs.

# OP275

**Bipolar/JFET 'Butler' audio op-amp, dual** · Precision Monolithics (PMI), within Analog Devices (c. 1992) → Analog Devices (1992–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*ADI's 'Butler' bipolar+JFET audio dual: China's classic 'warm, tube-like' op-amp and Musiland's house part.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 nV/√Hz typ | 9 MHz typ | 22 V/µs typ | 0.0006% typ | ±4.5 V to ±22 V (9–44 V total) | ~2 mA/amp typ; 2.5 mA/amp (5 mA/pkg), likely max |

## Why it's rated

ADI's audio dual, pitched on JFET 'sound quality'; ADI's AN-1429 says it is 'reputable with music fans'. On Soomal it is the textbook 'warm, tube-like' op-amp and Musiland's signature part (2007–2014). Soomal's RMAA plots show many low-level harmonics, possibly the source of the warmth (unproven). OEM uses include Denon DVD-A1, Yamaha DME32 and Apogee Mini-DAC.

**Described as:** warm (偏暖 / 暖色调), tube-like (胆味), rich, mellow (醇厚柔和), musical (很有音乐味道), warm, treble intact, 'straight', balanced (JP, unconfirmed)

**Used in:** Musiland (2007–2014): Long March II, Monitor, MD, HP series, Gain stage before LME49600/TPA6120 buffers (HP11, TempoTec DAII Plus), DVD-Audio/SACD players (Denon DVD-A1, DVD-2900); HiFiMAN HM-602 filter, Pro audio: Yamaha DME32, Apogee Mini-DAC, Socket rolling in sound cards; MXR MC402 pedal (reportedly)

## Watch out

- Needs at least ±4.5 V (9 V total); unsuited to low-voltage circuits.
- Weak headphone driver alone: Soomal says it cannot drive a 300 Ω HD580 with authority.
- Many low-level harmonics (Soomal RMAA); not a lowest-distortion choice by modern standards.
- 'Warm' is subjective and implementation-dependent; Soomal found some OP275 cards neutral.
- Pricey vs NE5532/4580; Musiland later used CLC2059 (01 Mini, 2009) and MC33079 (03 US/PS, 2011).
- **Counterfeits:** No confirmed counterfeit report, though a diyAudio thread alleges Chinese fakes. Its premium price (~20× an NE5532 in 2009) invites remarking. Genuine parts read 'OP275G'; check ~2–2.5 mA/amp supply current, 22 V/µs and 9 MHz. Buy OP275GPZ/GSZ from authorized distributors.

# TPA6120A2 (TI dual current-feedback headphone amplifier)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Headphone drivers

*TI's 2004 stereo current-feedback headphone driver, rated at 128 dB dynamic range and 1300 V/µs. Its current drive powered the Xonar Essence STX and many DAC/amps.*

**Technology:** Dual (stereo) current-feedback amplifier (CFA) headphone driver. Split supply of ±5 V to ±15 V; TI's product page gives the total supply range as 9 V to 33 V. PowerPAD packages: 20-pin SOIC (DWP, listed by TI as HSOIC) and 14-pin VQFN (RGY). Surface-mount only, with no DIP version.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

TI's ready-made current-feedback headphone driver was widely used in the 2009-2016 PC-audio and Chinese DAC/amp boom. Examples: the Asus Xonar Essence STX, Musiland Monitor sound cards, FiiO E9, and Sony PHA-1/PHA-2. It remains in current DAC/amps (SMSL, Sabaj, Aiyima, WiiM, FiiO, Shanling). Enthusiasts value its strong current drive, very low datasheet noise and distortion, and low cost. DIYers often use it as the output stage of an op-amp + CFA composite loop, e.g. OPA1602/OPA1612 + TPA6120A2 on diyAudio. Soomal tested 10-16 products using it and stresses that results depend heavily on the implementation. Measured results are mixed: designs with series output resistors show about 10 Ω output impedance (ASR on Sony PHA-1; Roland UA-M10), while well-executed composite designs measure very well.

**How people describe the sound:** powerful drive, including 600 Ω headphones (Soomal), very low noise (spec-driven reputation), thick, weighty presentation (Soomal, Sabaj A20d), coarse or lacking finesse in a high-output-impedance implementation (Soomal, Sabaj A20d 2022), strongly implementation- and supply-dependent (Soomal)

**Typical uses:** Sound-card headphone outputs: Asus Xonar Essence STX/ST, Creative Sound Blaster ZxR / E5 / X7 / BlasterX G5, Musiland Monitor series, Portable amps and DAPs: Sony PHA-1 / PHA-2, OPPO HA-2, FiiO E9 and X7 AM5 module, Topping NX3, Shanling M5 Ultra, Desktop DAC/amps: SMSL SAP-9 / M20 / DO400, Sabaj A20d, Aiyima DAC-A10, WiiM Ultra, Roland UA-M10, Rupert Neve RNHP, Current buffer inside a VFA-CFA composite loop (OPA1602/OPA1612, OPA2134, NE5532), DIY headphone amp boards (pavouk.org, Cocoacrumbs, Head-Fi and AMB threads)

**Caveats:**

- TI's typical application uses series output resistors, so many products have high output impedance, e.g. about 10 Ω on Sony PHA-1 (ASR) and Roland UA-M10.
- Very high drive can damage low-impedance headphones or IEMs if gain is not limited (Soomal warning on the Musiland Monitor 01 PS).
- Very wide-bandwidth CFA: sensitive to layout, feedback-resistor value and capacitive loads. Composite loops need careful compensation. Gain-setting questions are common on TI E2E.
- The PowerPAD must be soldered to a thermal via array.
- Surface-mount only (PowerPAD SOIC-20 / VQFN-14), so it cannot be swapped in a DIP socket.
- Needs rails of ±5 V to ±15 V. Portable designs need boost converters, and Soomal reports it performs better at higher supply voltage.

## Counterfeits

No documented counterfeit or remarked TPA6120A2 reports were found. Loose chips are sold mostly through AliExpress/Taobao modules and kits, so their supply chain cannot be verified. Buy from TI or authorized distributors (Mouser 595-TPA6120A2DWPR / 595-TPA6120A2RGYT, DigiKey, LCSC C70439). The practical checks are a genuine TI top mark with date code and an intact exposed PowerPAD.

## Related parts and alternatives

TI OPA1622: modern high-output-current audio op-amp for headphone drive, TI BUF634A: high-current buffer used inside an OPA1612 composite loop, TI THS6012: related wide-band CFA line driver that DIYers use interchangeably in VFA-CFA composites (no vendor statement of equivalence), ADI AD8397: high-output-current rail-to-rail op-amp (used in the FiiO X7 AM1), LME49600: high-current audio buffer (current lifecycle status not checked)

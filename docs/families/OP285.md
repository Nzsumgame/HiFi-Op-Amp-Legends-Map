# OP285 (ADI/PMI dual 9 MHz precision 'Butler' bipolar/JFET audio op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Bipolar classics

**Technology:** Dual voltage-feedback precision op-amp with ADI's 'Butler Amplifier' front end, combining bipolar and JFET transistors. It is the faster sibling of the OP275 (about 9 MHz GBW) and is a separate die from it, not a grade of the OP275.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A long-standing Chinese upgrade classic that is always named next to the OP275. The widely reprinted CN op-amp guides (CSDN '常用运放的介绍', ZOL '主流音频运放芯片分析', PCPOP '摩机常识 运放芯片篇', 21ic '常用运放概述') say OP275/OP285 have a 'tube-like soft, moist midrange (中频具有胆机柔美润泽的特点) with intimate vocals', clear outlines and high resolution. These guides are cited in searches for 胆味运放. One comparison says the OP285G sounds like the OP275G but slightly better, with more colour saturation and a wider soundstage, though slightly less bass. Car-audio upgrade shops (sounting.com) list it among 'fever-grade' audio op-amps. / Head-Fi has a dedicated thread, 'I like the OP285'. It describes very good resolution with a refined but resolving treble, and a balance similar to the AD746 but more colourful and perhaps clearer at 12 V DC. Posters say it sounds 'right' overall but needs care in the circuit to sound its best. diyAudio's 'Best Sounding DUAL opamps' thread names it as a budget alternative worth trying alongside the OPA627 and OPA637.

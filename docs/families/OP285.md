# OP285 / OP285GP / OP285GS / OP285GSZ

**Bipolar/JFET 'Butler' precision dual, 9 MHz** · Precision Monolithics (PMI) (1990s) → Analog Devices (1990s–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*The laser-trimmed precision twin of the OP275, praised in Chinese upgrade guides for a sweet, resolving midrange.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 nV/√Hz typ | 9 MHz | 22 V/µs | 0.0006% (THD) | ±22 V abs. max (supply or input unconfirmed) | 5 mA |

## Why it's rated

A long-time Chinese upgrade favourite, usually named with the OP275. CN guides credit it with a soft, tube-like midrange, intimate vocals and high resolution. Head-Fi posters like its resolving treble but say it needs care in the circuit. diyAudio suggests it as a budget alternative to the OPA627/OPA637. All opinion, not measurement.

**Described as:** soft, tube-like midrange, intimate vocals, high resolution, refined, resolving treble, wider soundstage than OP275, slightly less bass

**Used in:** Op-amp rolling in DIY and Chinese DACs and preamps, Headphone amp upgrades, Car-audio head-unit and DSP upgrades, I/V and output stages; precision alternative to OP275

## Watch out

- Praise comes from reprinted CN guides and forum anecdotes, not measurements.
- Head-Fi posters say it needs careful decoupling and loading to sound its best.
- DIP-8 OP285GP reportedly obsolete since about 2002; socket rolling today means SOIC on adapter, old stock or remarks.
- Headline specs match the OP275; it is not 'faster' than the OP275.
- **Counterfeits:** No ADI advisory found. Marketplace listings lumping OP285G/GS/GSZ/GSZ-REEL into one product suggest mixed or remarked stock. Fresh-looking DIP OP285GP parts are likely old stock or remarks. Buy OP285GSZ from authorized distributors such as Arrow.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

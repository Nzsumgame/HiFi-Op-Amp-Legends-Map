# OPA1602 / OPA1604 (TI SoundPlus dual/quad bipolar-input, 2.5 nV/√Hz)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*TI's 2011 SoundPlus bipolar dual/quad with 2.5 nV/√Hz noise and 0.00003% THD+N. TI named it the replacement for the LME49720, and it is widely used for OEM I/V and line stages.*

**Technology:** Bipolar-input, rail-to-rail-output, unity-gain-stable voltage-feedback op-amp (TI SoundPlus audio line). Surface-mount only: SOIC-8/MSOP-8 (dual), SOIC-14/TSSOP-14 (quad).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This is an OEM and engineering favourite, not an op-amp-rolling legend: it comes only in SMD packages, so enthusiasts rarely swap it. It pairs low noise and distortion with modest current draw (2.6 mA/ch). TI named it the drop-in-class replacement for the EOL LME49720, and designers use it in portable players and dongles. The iBasso DX90 uses a pair of OPA1602 per channel as the DAC I/V stage, with OPA1611 for the filter and gain stages and a BUF634 output. The Cayin RU3 dongle uses two OPA1602 plus two SGM8262 in parallel drive. DIY projects use it as a transformerless balanced line driver, and the quad OPA1604 appears in multichannel mixers and lab front-ends. No op-amp-specific listening tests or Audio Science Review / Samuel Groner measurements were found this run.

**How people describe the sound:** neutral, restrained, relaxed, slightly soft mids: Soomal's description of the whole iBasso DX90 player, not of the op-amp alone

**Typical uses:** DAC I/V conversion (iBasso DX90), Portable DAC/headphone dongle output stage in parallel drive (Cayin RU3), Replacement for the LME49720/LM4562 in new designs (TI recommendation), Transformerless balanced line driver (DIY XPS project), Multichannel line mixers and filter banks (OPA1604)

**Caveats:**

- SMD only (SOIC/MSOP/TSSOP). Rolling it into a DIP-8 socket needs an adapter board.
- Output drive of about ±30 mA suits line level but not low-impedance headphones on its own. The designs above add BUF634s or parallel sections.
- Bipolar input brings nA-level bias current, a general bipolar-input caveat with high source impedance (not an OPA1602-specific report).
- No op-amp-specific stability or oscillation reports were found this run.

## Counterfeits

No counterfeit or remark reports specific to OPA1602/OPA1604 were found this run. As an SMD part it is often sold on marketplaces pre-mounted on DIP-8 adapter boards, where the chip's origin cannot be checked. Buy from authorized distributors (TI, DigiKey, Mouser) and check the TI top-side marking and lot trace code against TI's marking lookup.

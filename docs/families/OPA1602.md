# OPA1602 / OPA1604

**Bipolar-input SoundPlus audio op-amp, RRO, 2/4 channels** · Texas Instruments (2011–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*TI's 2011 SoundPlus bipolar: 2.5 nV/√Hz, 0.00003% THD+N at 2.6 mA/ch. A go-to for OEM DAC I/V and line stages.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.5 nV/√Hz typ | 35 MHz typ | 20 V/µs typ | 0.00003% typ | ±2.25 V to ±18 V (4.5 V to 36 V) | 2.6 mA per channel typ |

## Why it's rated

An OEM and engineering favourite rather than a rolling legend, since it is SMD only. Low noise and distortion at modest current. Reported design wins (Soomal, Frieve reviews; maker pages): iBasso DX90 DAC I/V (with OPA1611 and BUF634) and Cayin RU3 dongle (with SGM8262). No op-amp-specific listening tests or ASR measurements found.

**Described as:** neutral (DX90 as whole), restrained, relaxed, slightly soft mids

**Used in:** DAC I/V conversion (iBasso DX90, per Soomal), Parallel-drive dongle output stage (Cayin RU3), LME49720/LM4562 replacement in new designs (reportedly TI-suggested), Transformerless balanced line driver (DIY XPS project), Multichannel mixers and filter banks (OPA1604)

## Watch out

- SMD only (SOIC/MSOP/TSSOP); a DIP-8 socket needs an adapter board.
- About ±30 mA drive suits line level; headphone designs add BUF634s or parallel sections.
- 1.8 pA/√Hz current noise raises noise with high source impedances.
- Absolute max allows a short to VS/2 for only one amplifier per package.
- No op-amp-specific stability or oscillation reports found.
- **Counterfeits:** No OPA1602/OPA1604-specific counterfeit reports. Marketplace parts often come pre-mounted on DIP-8 adapters of unknown origin. Buy from TI, DigiKey or Mouser and check the marking and lot code with TI's marking lookup.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

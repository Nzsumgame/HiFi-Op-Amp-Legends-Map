# AD845

**JFET-input (CBFET) precision op-amp, single** · Analog Devices (by 1990-12–present) → Analog Devices (c. 2010–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*ADI's 16 MHz, 100 V/µs JFET single from c. 1990. One headphone survey found it smooth and slightly valve-like.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 nV/√Hz; 18 at 10 kHz; 4 µV p-p 0.1–10 Hz | 16 MHz typ, 12.8 MHz min; AOL 250 V/mV min | 100 V/µs typ; settles 350 ns to 0.01% | ±4.75 V to ±18 V | 10 mA typ, 12 mA max (reviewer: about 10 mA) |

## Why it's rated

Thin enthusiast coverage. The only listening report is a 2005 DiyEden survey translated on Soomal. It calls the AD845 smooth and slightly valve-like with ADI punch, looser and 'slower' than Burr-Brown. It ranks resolution above OPA132/134 and AD823, below AD843 and OPA627, and suits mains-powered amps. A 'cult following' for DAC I/V is unsourced.

**Described as:** smooth, slightly valve-like, keeps ADI punch, looser, softer, 'slower', less detail than AD843

**Used in:** Mains-powered headphone amp gain stage, Line stage or buffer, DAC I/V stage (claimed, unconfirmed)

## Watch out

- All listening impressions come from one translated reviewer's survey.
- Single only; SOIC is 16-lead wide body (AD845JR-16), not SOIC-8.
- About 10 mA quiescent current (reviewer): poor for battery amps.
- Needs ±4.75 V minimum; reviewer measured 8.7 V (0.5 V into 33 Ω), 9.0 V (2 V into 330 Ω).
- Fast part (100 V/µs, 16 MHz): normal high-speed layout and decoupling care applies.
- **Counterfeits:** No AD845 fakes reported. Check logo, grade letter, date code. SOIC-8 'AD845' is suspect (real SOIC is 16-lead). Expect ~10 mA, ~100 V/µs, ≥12.8 MHz; a relabelled TL071 draws far less. Non-Z JN/KN carry pre-2010 codes.

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

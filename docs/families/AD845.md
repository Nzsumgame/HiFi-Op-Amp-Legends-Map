# AD845 (ADI 16 MHz CBFET JFET-input single, J/K/A/B/S grades)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*ADI's 16 MHz, 100 V/us JFET-input CBFET single, around since at least 1990. One headphone-amp survey called it smooth and slightly valve-like while keeping ADI's punch.*

**Technology:** N-channel JFET-input monolithic op-amp built on ADI's complementary bipolar (CB) process, which is where the 'CBFET' in the datasheet title ('Precision, 16 MHz CBFET Op Amp') comes from. ADI's 12/90 Rev. A SPICE macro-model also models the input as an N-channel JFET pair.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche ADI JFET single with thin enthusiast coverage. The only located listening report is a Chinese translation (DiyEden, on Soomal, 2005) of a headphone-amp op-amp survey. That one reviewer calls the AD845 smooth, with less detail than the AD843 and a slightly valve-like feel while keeping ADI's punch. It sounds looser, softer and 'slower' than Burr-Brown parts. The reviewer ranks its resolution above OPA132/134 and AD823 and below AD843/OPA627, and recommends it for mains-powered amps. A claimed 'cult following' for DAC I/V stages remains unsourced.

**How people describe the sound:** smooth, slightly valve/tube-like, keeps ADI punch/impact, looser, softer, 'slower' than Burr-Brown, resolution above OPA134/AD823, below AD843/OPA627 (one reviewer), less detail than AD843

**Typical uses:** headphone amplifier gain stage (mains-powered), line stage / buffer, DAC I/V stage (claimed, unverified)

**Caveats:**

- All listening impressions come from one reviewer's survey (via translation).
- Single channel only: dual-socket rolling needs an adapter.
- The SOIC version is a 16-lead wide-body package (AD845JR-16), not SOIC-8.
- High quiescent current (about 10 mA, per the reviewer): poor for battery amps.
- Minimum supply is +/-4.75 V (9.5 V total), which the reviewer also noted.
- Fast part (100 V/us, 16 MHz): normal high-speed layout and decoupling care applies.

## Counterfeits

No AD845-specific counterfeit reports were found. General checks: (1) ADI logo, full part number with grade letter, and a plausible date code. (2) The SOIC version is a 16-lead wide body (JR-16/JRZ-16), so an 'AD845' marked on an SOIC-8 body is suspect (inferred). (3) Supply current should be about 10 mA (reviewer figure); a relabelled low-power JFET part such as a TL071 draws far less (inferred). (4) Check for slew near 100 V/us and unity-gain bandwidth of at least 12.8 MHz (datasheet). (5) Non-Z JN/KN parts reportedly went out of production around 2010, so they should carry older date codes.

## Verification notes

Status: **partially-verified**

**Refuted or corrected during verification:**

- The previous note that the 'CBFET' process name was unverified: the datasheet title is 'Precision, 16 MHz CBFET Op Amp', and the part is built on ADI's complementary bipolar (CB) process.
- The previous note that non-Z AD845KN was not seen: AD845KN datasheet listings exist on alldatasheet, and a search summary ties AD845KN to a PDN naming AD845KNZ as its replacement.
- The search-summary inference that the whole AD845 series is discontinued is not supported. Evidence shows only that non-Z JN/KN were replaced by Z parts; RS UK and Digi-Key still list the part.

**Corrections applied:**

- Technology: confirmed CBFET, an N-JFET input on ADI's complementary bipolar process.
- Datasheet: current revision is Rev. E; title and vendor PDF URL confirmed. Product page confirmed and raised to high confidence. Added 14 distributor and third-party mirrors, with older-revision candidates marked low confidence.
- Key specs: GBW is 16 MHz typ with 12.8 MHz min; slew 100 V/us confirmed. Added 350 ns settling to 0.01%, 250 V/mV min AOL, 4 uV p-p 0.1-10 Hz noise, +/-4.75 to +/-18 V supply, 50 mA output and grade temperature ranges.
- Grades: J/K 0 to 70 C, A/B -40 to 85 C, S -55 to 125 C (datasheet).
- The reviewer's '9.5 V minimum supply' matches the datasheet +/-4.75 V minimum.
- Lineage and status: added the reported PDN 10_0081 discontinuance of non-Z JN/KN (replacement JNZ/KNZ, about 2010) at medium-low confidence. Family status is still unconfirmed.
- Discovery hint 'check obsolete/LTB status': partially addressed. Leaded non-Z parts were discontinued; no family-wide obsolescence notice was found.
- No silicon change, die revision or fab-transfer PCN was found; silicon_changes stays empty.

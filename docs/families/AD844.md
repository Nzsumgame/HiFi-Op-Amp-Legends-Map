# AD844 (Analog Devices current-feedback op-amp, used as open-loop DAC I/V)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

**Technology:** Bipolar current-feedback op-amp (current conveyor with an accessible TZ node), designed by Barrie Gilbert

## Why enthusiasts rate it

DAC DIY builders praise it as an I/V converter, usually run without global feedback from the TZ node. Posts in the diyAudio thread 'Using the AD844 as an I/V' (more than 120 pages) call it the 'best opamp based I/V so far by a long way', say it beat the OPA627, and describe stacking 2 or 3 AD844s per channel with an OPA627 as the buffer.

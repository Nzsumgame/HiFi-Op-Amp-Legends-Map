# OPA128 (Burr-Brown Difet electrometer-grade op-amp, TO-99 metal can)

Burr-Brown (until 2000) → Texas Instruments (2000 to c.2020) → Analog Devices (pre-OPA128) → Texas Instruments (Burr-Brown) (later) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Burr-Brown's TO-99 Difet electrometer amp (75 fA max bias). Chinese DIYers collect OPA128SM cans for preamps and praise its resolution despite its 1 MHz GBW.*

| Slew rate |
|---|
| 3 V/us |

## Why it's rated

Its audio praise is niche and mostly Chinese. On a CSDN listening ranking of classic audio op-amps, 'OPA128SM x2' sits just below the OPA1612 and is credited with good overtone rendering and high resolution. CN DIYers collect military-pulled OPA128SM/883 gold cans as '发烧运放' (audiophile op-amps) for preamps, sometimes selling them alongside the OPA627. The same sources warn that its low GBW and slew rate make it a poor general-purpose audio op-amp, though some value it for DAC I/V stages. It was designed as an electrometer (fA-level input) amplifier, not an audio part.

**Described as:** good overtone rendering (CSDN), high resolution (CSDN)

**Used in:** preamp line stage in CN DIY builds (with a TO-99 adapter), DAC I/V conversion (per CN forum claims), design intent: electrometer, photodiode / ionization-chamber / charge amplifiers

## Watch out

- Only 1 MHz GBW and 3 V/us slew, so it is not a general-purpose audio op-amp.
- Single channel in TO-99, so dual-socket audio use needs two cans and an adapter.
- Discontinued (TI LTB 2020), so most available stock is pulled or surplus.
- The praise comes from a small number of Chinese enthusiast sources.
- **Counterfeits:** No OPA128-specific counterfeit report was found. The part has been discontinued since the 2020 lifetime buy, and stock circulating in CN markets is described as military-pulled 'OPA128SM/883' cans, so treat any 'new' stock with suspicion. Remarking of TO-99 Difet parts is plausible. A genuine part's fA-level bias current can only be confirmed with an electrometer-grade test setup. Checking supply current, GBW and slew against the datasheet is a quicker sanity check.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

<summary>Verification and open questions</summary>

Status: **verified-with-corrections**, 5 web searches.

**Confirmed:**

- The OPA128 datasheet has TI literature number SBOS148 (https://www.radiolocman.com/datasheet/pdf.html?di=83971, https://www.alldatasheet.com/datasheet-pdf/pdf/56727/BURR-BROWN/OPA128.html).
- The datasheet copy is Burr-Brown PDS-653E, dated May 1995 (https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/405/opa128-445402.pdf).
- Headline specs are 75 fA max bias, 500 uV max offset, 5 uV/C max drift, 110 dB min Aol and 90 dB min CMRR. It is an improved pin-for-pin replacement for the AD515 (https://www.alldatasheet.com/html-pdf/56727/BURR-BROWN/OPA128/500/1/OPA128.html).
- GBW is 1 MHz and slew rate is 3 V/us (https://edg.uchicago.edu/~tang/SNFactory/opa128.pdf).
- OPA128JM reached end of life with an LTB of 05-14-2020 (https://www.digikey.com/product-detail/en/texas-instruments/OPA128JM/OPA128JM-ND/301117).
- OPA128SM is obsolete, and the variants are listed NRND/discontinued (https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/texas-instruments/OPA128SM/301120, https://octopart.com/opa128sm-texas+instruments-420293).
- The OPA129 is a related part, discussed against the OPA128 on TI E2E (https://e2e.ti.com/support/amplifiers-group/amplifiers/f/amplifiers-forum/712204/opa129-differences-from-opa128).

- Added the confirmed headline specs: 75 fA max Ib, 500 uV Vos, 5 uV/C drift, 110 dB Aol, 90 dB CMRR, 1 MHz GBW, 3 V/us slew.
- Added the AD515 pin-for-pin replacement lineage and the OPA129 sibling.

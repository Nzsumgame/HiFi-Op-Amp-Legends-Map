# OPA128 (Burr-Brown Difet electrometer-grade op-amp, TO-99 metal can)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*Burr-Brown's TO-99 Difet electrometer amp (75 fA max bias). Chinese DIYers collect OPA128SM cans for preamps and praise its resolution despite its 1 MHz GBW.*

**Technology:** Single-channel Difet (dielectrically isolated FET-input) electrometer-grade op-amp in a TO-99 metal can. The best grade has 75 fA max bias current, 500 uV max offset and 5 uV/C max drift. It is slow, at about 1 MHz GBW and 3 V/us. Marketed as an improved pin-for-pin replacement for the ADI AD515.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Its audio praise is niche and mostly Chinese. On a CSDN listening ranking of classic audio op-amps, 'OPA128SM x2' sits just below the OPA1612 and is credited with good overtone rendering and high resolution. CN DIYers collect military-pulled OPA128SM/883 gold cans as '发烧运放' (audiophile op-amps) for preamps, sometimes selling them alongside the OPA627. The same sources warn that its low GBW and slew rate make it a poor general-purpose audio op-amp, though some value it for DAC I/V stages. It was designed as an electrometer (fA-level input) amplifier, not an audio part.

**How people describe the sound:** good overtone rendering (CSDN), high resolution (CSDN)

**Typical uses:** preamp line stage in CN DIY builds (with a TO-99 adapter), DAC I/V conversion (per CN forum claims), design intent: electrometer, photodiode / ionization-chamber / charge amplifiers

**Caveats:**

- Only 1 MHz GBW and 3 V/us slew, so it is not a general-purpose audio op-amp.
- Single channel in TO-99, so dual-socket audio use needs two cans and an adapter.
- Discontinued (TI LTB 2020), so most available stock is pulled or surplus.
- The praise comes from a small number of Chinese enthusiast sources.

## Counterfeits

No OPA128-specific counterfeit report was found. The part has been discontinued since the 2020 lifetime buy, and stock circulating in CN markets is described as military-pulled 'OPA128SM/883' cans, so treat any 'new' stock with suspicion. Remarking of TO-99 Difet parts is plausible. A genuine part's fA-level bias current can only be confirmed with an electrometer-grade test setup. Checking supply current, GBW and slew against the datasheet is a quicker sanity check.

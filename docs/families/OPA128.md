# OPA128 (Burr-Brown Difet electrometer-grade op-amp, TO-99 metal can)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*Burr-Brown's femtoamp-bias Difet electrometer op-amp in a TO-99 can, collected by Chinese DIYers for preamps and DAC I/V stages despite its slow speed.*

**Technology:** Single Difet (dielectrically isolated JFET-input) electrometer-grade op-amp with ultra-low bias current (tens of fA) in a TO-99 metal can. It is slow, at about 1 MHz GBW and 3 V/us (draft values, not web-confirmed).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This comes from Chinese DIY sources found in an earlier sweep and has not been re-checked. A CSDN listening ranking places 'OPA128SM x2' just below the OPA1612 and credits it with good overtone rendering and high resolution. Kechuang.org and erji.net posts show military-pulled OPA128SM/883 cans traded as audiophile op-amps for preamps. The same sources note that its low GBW and slew rate make it a poor general-purpose audio op-amp, while its ultra-low bias current is valued for DAC I/V conversion.

**How people describe the sound:** good overtone rendering (CSDN ranking, attributed), high resolution (CSDN ranking, attributed)

**Typical uses:** line/preamp stages (CN DIY reports), DAC I/V conversion (CN DIY reports), electrometer / photodiode / high-impedance sensor front ends (design intent)

**Caveats:**

- Low GBW (~1 MHz) and slew (~3 V/us) limit it as a general audio op-amp (per CN sources).
- Single op-amp in TO-99; use in DIP-8 sockets requires lead forming/adapter and checking the case-pin connection (pinout not web-confirmed).
- Much of the trade is in pulled military parts of unknown provenance.

## Counterfeits

Not web-confirmed. Most hobbyist supply is pulled or surplus OPA128SM(/883) gold cans sold on CN forums/marketplaces, so re-marked or out-of-spec parts are a plausible risk. Check the bias current (fA-level leakage is easy to measure with a high-value feedback resistor) before trusting a part.

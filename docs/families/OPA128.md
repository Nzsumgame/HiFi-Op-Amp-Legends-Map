# OPA128 (Burr-Brown Difet electrometer-grade op-amp, TO-99 metal can)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

**Technology:** Single Difet (dielectrically isolated JFET-input) electrometer-grade op-amp with ultra-low bias current (tens of fA) in a TO-99 metal can. It is slow, at about 1 MHz GBW and 3 V/us.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

On the CSDN classic-audio-op-amp listening ranking it sits just below the OPA1612, where the 'OPA128SM x2' is described as having good overtone rendering and high resolution. Chinese DIYers trade and collect military-pulled OPA128SM/883 gold cans as '发烧运放' for preamps, as the kechuang.org posts and an erji.net sale post bundled with the OPA627 show. The same CN sources caution that its low GBW and slew rate make it ill-suited as a general audio op-amp, but its ultra-low bias current is valued for DAC I/V conversion.

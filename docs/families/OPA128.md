# OPA128

**Difet electrometer-grade op-amp, single, TO-99 can** · Burr-Brown (until 2000) → Texas Instruments (2000–c.2020) → Analog Devices (pre-OPA128) → Texas Instruments (Burr-Brown) (later) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Burr-Brown's 75 fA electrometer can, collected by Chinese DIYers for preamps despite only 1 MHz GBW.*

| GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|
| 1 MHz | 3 V/µs | up to ±18 V |

## Why it's rated

Niche, mostly Chinese audio praise. A CSDN listening ranking puts 'OPA128SM x2' just below the OPA1612 for overtones and resolution. CN DIYers collect military-pulled OPA128SM/883 gold cans as '发烧运放' for preamps, often beside the OPA627. Low GBW and slew make it a poor general-purpose audio part, though some like it for DAC I/V. It was designed as an electrometer.

**Described as:** good overtone rendering, high resolution

**Used in:** Preamp line stage with TO-99 adapter (CN DIY), DAC I/V conversion (CN forum claims), Electrometer, photodiode, ionization-chamber, charge amplifiers

## Watch out

- Only 1 MHz GBW and 3 V/µs slew; not a general-purpose audio op-amp.
- Single channel in TO-99: dual-socket use needs two cans and an adapter.
- Discontinued (TI LTB 2020); most stock is pulled or surplus.
- Praise comes from a small number of Chinese enthusiast sources.
- **Counterfeits:** No OPA128-specific fake reports. Discontinued since 2020; CN stock is sold as pulled 'OPA128SM/883' cans, so distrust 'new' stock. Remarking is plausible. fA bias needs an electrometer setup to confirm; supply current, GBW and slew are quicker checks.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

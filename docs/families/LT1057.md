# LT1057 / LT1058 (Linear Technology dual/quad JFET-input precision high-speed op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

**Technology:** Dual (LT1057) or quad (LT1058) matched JFET-input precision high-speed op-amp. It is quoted in CN sources at about 5 MHz GBW, 14 V/us slew and 60 pA bias, and is a drop-in upgrade for the LF412A and OP-215.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

New evidence from CN and KR since the earlier exclusion. The Baidu Tieba '40款音频运算放大器主观分析评价' lists the LT1057ACN with good high and low extension, speed and dynamics, but a cool tone and a slightly recessed midrange. The HIFIDIY '各种运放的个人意见和评价' thread describes the gold-can LT1057 as having both-end extension, speed, dynamics and resolution but a 'cold, otherworldly' colour, and users roll it next to the OPA2111HK gold can. The EET-China '话说烧级运放' blog classes it as the 'cold and elegant' (冷艳) type with high dynamics, resolution and layering. The Korean digipine tone summary repeats the same characterisation, and car-audio shop sounting.com lists it as a fever-grade op-amp.

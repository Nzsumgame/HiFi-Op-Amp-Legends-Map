# LT1057 / LT1058 (Linear Technology dual/quad JFET-input precision high-speed op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*Linear Tech's matched-JFET precision dual and quad. Chinese and Korean op-amp rollers call it cold, fast and highly resolving, especially the gold TO-5 can.*

**Technology:** Matched JFET-input precision op-amp. The LT1057 is a dual in the industry-standard 8-pin pinout. The LT1058 is a quad in the standard 14-pin pinout, and its datasheet calls it the lowest-offset quad JFET op-amp in that pinout. Datasheet headline figures: 14 V/us slew (10 V/us min), 5 MHz GBW, 1.3 us settling to 0.02%, 13 nV/rtHz at 1 kHz, and 50 pA bias current at 70 C. CN hobby sources quote about 60 pA bias. The draft's claim that it is positioned as an LF412A/OP-215 upgrade was not confirmed in this pass.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Praise comes mainly from Chinese and Korean op-amp-rolling communities. The Baidu Tieba '40款音频运算放大器主观分析评价' roundup credits the LT1057ACN with good extension at both ends, speed and dynamics, but a cool tone and a slightly recessed midrange. HIFIDIY users describe the gold-can LT1057 as extended, fast, dynamic and resolving but 'cold' in colour, and roll it next to the OPA2111HK. An EET-China blog puts it in the 'cold and elegant' (冷艳) class. The Korean digipine summary repeats this, and the car-audio shop sounting.com lists it as a 'fever' (烧级) op-amp. These are subjective forum opinions and were not re-verified in this pass.

**How people describe the sound:** cool / cold tone, wide extension at both ends, fast and dynamic, high resolution and layering, slightly recessed midrange

**Typical uses:** op-amp rolling in DAC I/V and output stages (DIY), headphone amplifier and preamp socket upgrades, car-audio 'fever' op-amp swaps

**Caveats:**

- Praise is regional (CN/KR forums) and subjective, with no Western-forum consensus found.
- The 'cold' voicing may not suit systems that are already bright.
- The metal-can versions sought by rollers are old or NOS stock, which carries a counterfeit and remark risk.
- JFET-input bias current rises with temperature (datasheet specifies 50 pA at 70 C).

## Counterfeits

No LT1057-specific counterfeit reports were found in this pass. The metal-can (TO-5) and early DIP LT1057/LT1058 that rollers seek are now old or NOS stock, sold largely through Taobao, eBay and secondary brokers. Treat unusually cheap 'gold can' LT1057s with the usual suspicion of remarked parts, and check marking style, date codes and DC parameters (offset voltage, bias current) against the datasheet.

## Related parts and alternatives

OPA2111 (metal-can JFET dual that HIFIDIY users roll next to the LT1057), LF412A / OP-215 (older JFET duals the LT1057 is said to upgrade; this positioning is unconfirmed)

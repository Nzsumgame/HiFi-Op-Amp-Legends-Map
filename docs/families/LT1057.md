# LT1057 / LT1058 (Linear Technology dual/quad JFET-input precision high-speed op-amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*Linear Tech's matched-JFET precision dual, rolled by CN/KR DIY listeners (often in the gold TO-5 can) for a fast, extended, detailed but cool sound.*

**Technology:** Dual (LT1057) or quad (LT1058) matched JFET-input precision high-speed op-amp. CN sources quote about 5 MHz GBW, 14 V/us slew and 60 pA bias, and describe it as a drop-in upgrade for the LF412A and OP-215. These figures and the drop-in claim were not checked against the vendor datasheet in this pass.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Praise comes from CN and KR hobby forums, not from measurements. The Baidu Tieba '40款音频运算放大器主观分析评价' lists the LT1057ACN with good extension at both ends, speed and dynamics, but a cool tone and a slightly recessed midrange. The HIFIDIY '各种运放的个人意见和评价' thread says the gold-can LT1057 has extension at both ends, speed, dynamics and resolution but a 'cold, otherworldly' colour, and users roll it next to the OPA2111HK gold can. The EET-China '话说烧级运放' blog puts it in the 'cold and elegant' (冷艳) group, with high dynamics, resolution and layering. The Korean digipine tone summary repeats the same description, and the car-audio shop sounting.com lists it as a fever-grade (烧级) op-amp.

**How people describe the sound:** extended highs and lows, fast, dynamic, high resolution, good layering, cool / cold tonal balance, slightly recessed midrange

**Typical uses:** op-amp rolling in DIY DAC and preamp stages, swapped against the OPA2111HK gold can, listed as an upgrade op-amp for car audio (sounting.com)

**Caveats:**

- All the sound descriptions are subjective listening impressions from CN/KR forums and blogs. No measurements back them.
- Several sources describe the tone as cool or cold with a slightly recessed midrange, so it may not suit systems that are already bright.
- The TO-5 metal-can version does not fit an 8-pin DIP socket directly (its pinout and adapter needs are not confirmed here).
- The spec figures and the drop-in claim for LF412A/OP-215 were not checked against the vendor datasheet.

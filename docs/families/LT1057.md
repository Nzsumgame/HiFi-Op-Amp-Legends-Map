# LT1057 / LT1058

**Matched-JFET precision op-amp, dual / quad** · Linear Technology (1980s–2017) → Analog Devices (2017–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Linear Tech's fast, precise JFET dual and quad. Chinese and Korean rollers call it cold, fast and resolving.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate |
|---|---|---|
| 13 nV/√Hz (26 nV/√Hz at 10 Hz) | 5 MHz | 14 V/µs typ, 10 V/µs min; settles 1.3 µs to 0.02% |

## Why it's rated

Praise comes mainly from Chinese and Korean op-amp rollers. Baidu Tieba's 40-op-amp roundup credits it with extension, speed and dynamics but a cool tone and slightly recessed mids. HIFIDIY users call the gold-can version resolving but 'cold' and roll it beside the OPA2111HK. EET-China, digipine and sounting.com echo this. All subjective.

**Described as:** cool / cold (冷艳), wide extension, fast and dynamic, high resolution, slightly recessed midrange

**Used in:** DAC I/V and output stage rolling (DIY), Headphone amp and preamp socket upgrades, Car-audio 'fever' (烧级) op-amp swaps

## Watch out

- Praise is regional (CN/KR forums) and subjective; no Western consensus found.
- The 'cold' voicing may not suit systems that are already bright.
- Metal-can versions are old or NOS stock, with counterfeit and remark risk.
- JFET bias current rises with temperature (50 pA specified at 70 °C).
- **Counterfeits:** No LT1057-specific fake reports known. Sought-after TO-5 and early DIP parts are NOS, sold via Taobao, eBay and brokers. Distrust cheap 'gold cans'; check marking, date codes, offset and bias current against the datasheet.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

## Part numbers

| LT1057IS8 (#PBF) | 2 | unknown | SO-8, -40 °C to 85 °C (I grade). |
| LT1057 (TO-5 metal can) | 2 | unknown | 'Gold can' prized by HIFIDIY users. Codes LT1057ACH/CH/AMH unconfirmed; likely NOS only. |
| LT1058 | 4 | unknown | Quad, standard 14-pin pinout. ADI product page live. |

| LT1058S | 4 | unknown | 0 °C to 70 °C grade beside LT1058AC / LT1058C; probably wide SO (unconfirmed). |

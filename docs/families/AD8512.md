# AD8510 / AD8512 (ADI precision JFET-input dual)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*ADI's 8 MHz JFET-input dual with 8 nV/√Hz noise and 0.0005% THD+N. A Chinese DIY forum post compares its sound to the AD823 and says it suits headphone amps.*

**Technology:** Precision JFET-input op-amp: very low noise (8 nV/√Hz at 1 kHz), low input bias current, 8 MHz GBW, 20 V/µs slew, dual supply ±5 V to ±15 V. Comes as single (AD8510), dual (AD8512) and quad (AD8513).

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Fab / process transfer).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

This is a niche, tier-C reputation. The main praise found is a single post in a Chinese erji.net thread ('谈谈自己用过的一些运放', op-amps I have used). The poster says it sounds almost the same as the AD823, with more output current and lower supply current. They call it slightly harsh but 'not unpleasant and equally appealing', and think it works in headphone amps. No wider consensus in Western audio forums was confirmed in this pass.

**How people describe the sound:** close to AD823 (per one erji.net poster), slightly harsh / forward but not unpleasant (same source)

**Typical uses:** op-amp swaps in DIY headphone amplifiers (erji.net report), general low-noise JFET-input audio gain stages (inferred from specs)

**Caveats:**

- Praise comes from a single forum source and should not be generalised.
- Needs at least ±5 V (about 10 V total). The output is not rail-to-rail.
- Sold only in SMD packages (SOIC/MSOP/TSSOP), so DIP sockets need an adapter.
- JFET input bias current roughly doubles for every 10 °C rise, so it is best kept away from hot spots.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2025 (PCN 25_0156 Rev. -)

Wafer-fab change: production of the listed AD8510/AD8512/AD8513 part numbers uses an existing qualified process at ADI's Camas, WA fab to ensure continuous supply. ADI says it expects no impact on fit, form, function or reliability. The datasheet was not revised (still Rev. K).

- **When:** 2025 (PCN 25_0156 Rev. -)
- **Affected:** AD8510, AD8512, AD8513
- **How to tell old from new:** Not established. The search summaries did not show a date-code or trace-code cutover, so check the PCN's effective date and date codes.
- **Audio impact:** None claimed by ADI; datasheet limits are unchanged. As with any fab or process move, typical noise, bias-current and offset distributions could shift within those limits, which is not verified.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same part numbers and datasheet limits, and the vendor states no fit/form/function change
- **Confidence:** medium

### Revision chain status

Analog Devices AD8510_8512 / AD8510_8512_8513, known so far (oldest to newest): Rev. 0 (date unknown, no copy found). Rev. A (date unknown, early 2000s; AD8510/AD8512 only). The RS Components mirror docs.rs-online.com/17b1/0900766b801ce1ae.pdf, already listed as 'unknown', holds Rev. A: its search result title shows the 'REV. A' footer. Revs. B through J are missing: no dates, copies or change lists were found. The AD8513 quad was added somewhere in B-J, and the revision that added it is unknown. Rev. K is dated 2010-01-21 (analog.com current, radiolocman di=170505). New mirrors whose revision was not shown: alldatasheet 83235 (AD8510, a sibling of 83236 and probably early), alldatasheet 1957163 (a later upload), radiolocman data.html?di=350333, datasheetbank and datasheetall. A separate document, AD8512-EP, has Rev. 0 (date unknown) on Mouser. Later EP revisions are unknown. Archive seeds: the uppercase analog.com media URL was seen in search summaries. The http://www.analog.com/static/imported-files/data_sheets/AD8510_8512_8513.pdf seed was built from ADI's standard legacy path pattern and was not seen in results, so treat it as unverified. Still missing: Rev. 0 and Revs. B-J (dates, change lists, copies), the revision that first added the AD8513, the Rev. J to K change summary, and any pre-2004 'UploadedFiles' URLs on analog.com. There were no second sources or acquirer re-issues: ADI is the sole source.

## Counterfeits

No specific counterfeit reports for this family were found in this pass. It is a mid-price SMD-only ADI JFET part, so relabelled or reclaimed units on marketplace listings are a generic risk. Buy from authorised distributors and check the ADI marking and date codes.

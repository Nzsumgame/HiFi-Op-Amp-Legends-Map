# AD8510 / AD8512 (ADI precision JFET-input dual)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

**Technology:** Low-noise, low-bias, wide-bandwidth precision JFET-input dual

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A Chinese enthusiast thread on erji.net ('谈谈自己用过的一些运放', op-amps I have used) describes it as sounding almost identical to the AD823, with more output current and lower supply current. The poster finds it slightly harsh but 'not unpleasant and equally appealing', and considers it viable for headphone amps.

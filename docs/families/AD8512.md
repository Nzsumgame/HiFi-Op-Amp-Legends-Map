# AD8510 / AD8512 / AD8513

**Precision JFET-input op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · Analog Devices (early 2000s–present) → Analog Devices (2025–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** Wafer-fab change: listed AD8510/AD8512/AD8513 part numbers move to an existing qualified process at ADI's Camas, WA fab to ensure supply. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*ADI's 8 MHz JFET dual with 8 nV/√Hz and 0.0005% THD+N; one Chinese DIY report likens it to the AD823 in headphone amps.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 nV/√Hz typ (7.6 nV/√Hz at 10 kHz) | 8 MHz typ | 20 V/µs typ; settling 500 ns to 0.1% | 0.0005% | ±5 V to ±15 V | 2.5 mA per amplifier |

## Why it's rated

A niche reputation. The main praise is one post in an erji.net thread on op-amps the poster has used: it sounds almost like the AD823, with more output current and lower supply current, slightly harsh but 'not unpleasant', and suits headphone amps. No wider consensus in Western forums is known.

**Described as:** close to AD823, slightly harsh / forward, not unpleasant

**Used in:** Op-amp swaps in DIY headphone amplifiers, Low-noise JFET-input audio gain stages (inferred from specs)

## Watch out

- Praise comes from a single forum post; do not generalise.
- Needs at least ±5 V (about 10 V total); output is not rail-to-rail.
- SMD only (SOIC/MSOP/TSSOP); DIP sockets need an adapter.
- JFET bias current roughly doubles every 10 °C; keep it away from hot spots.
- **Counterfeits:** No family-specific counterfeit reports. As a mid-price SMD-only ADI JFET part, relabelled or reclaimed marketplace units are a generic risk. Buy from authorised distributors and check ADI marking and date codes.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2025 · PCN 25_0156 Rev. -

Wafer-fab change: listed AD8510/AD8512/AD8513 part numbers move to an existing qualified process at ADI's Camas, WA fab to ensure supply. ADI expects no fit, form, function or reliability impact. Previous fab not stated. Datasheet unchanged (Rev. K).

- **Affected:** AD8510, AD8512, AD8513
- **How to tell:** No date-code or trace-code cutover known; check the PCN's effective date and date codes.
- **Audio impact:** None claimed; datasheet limits unchanged. Typical noise, bias and offset distributions could shift within limits (unverified).
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same part numbers and datasheet limits; vendor states no fit/form/function change
- **Confidence:** medium

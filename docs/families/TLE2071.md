# TLE2071 / TLE2072 / TLE2074 (TI Excalibur low-noise high-speed JFET-input)

Texas Instruments (2003 to at least 2004 (current status unknown)) · second sources: Texas Instruments · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** TI PCN 20231219013.1 ('Qualification of RFAB using ...') moves TLE207x orderables to the RFAB wafer fab with a die revision and an assembly-site change. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*A fast TI Excalibur JFET op-amp (10 MHz, 40 V/us). One Head-Fi CMoy builder tried about 20 chips and rated it the best cheap choice for 9 V headphone amps.*

| Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range |
|---|---|---|
| 40 V/us (TI product page); the datasheet feature bullet says '3x the slew rate of TL07x (45 V/us)' | not verified | 38 V max total (+/-19 V); the 5 V minimum is from partinfo and unconfirmed |

## Why it's rated

A niche budget favourite, not a legend. In a Head-Fi CMoy thread (c. 2007-2009), one user who tried about 20 op-amps rated the TLE2072 the best cheap op-amp for a 9 V CMoy, ahead of the OPA2132 and OPA2134. Other posts rate it above the OPA2132, OPA2134 and OPA2227 at low supply voltage, and say it draws less battery current. Sound descriptions (forward, lively midrange, textured bass, strong cymbals) are individual forum impressions. Forum lore says the TLE2072 has the better DC specs and the TLE2082 the better AC specs, although TI's macromodels for the two are identical. The open-hardware ohdsp ADC/DAC boards (2016) use the TLE2071/2072 and name the NE5532, OPA1652 and OPA1642 as alternatives. No ASR, Samuel Groner or other distortion measurement was found.

**Described as:** forward presentation, tipped-up midrange, lively, bass with weight and texture, treble that pulls off cymbals with conviction, detailed

**Used in:** 9 V battery CMoy headphone amplifiers (op-amp rolling), Audio ADC input filter/driver (ohdsp ADC-PCM4202 boards), DAC differential-to-single-ended output buffers (ohdsp BUF-DiffToSE-ADAU1966), Low-noise JFET charge preamps on 9 V batteries (CERN DIY particle detector, TLE2072CP/ACP), Upgrade for TL071/TL072/TL081 sockets (TI markets it as a direct upgrade to TL05x/TL07x/TL08x)

## Watch out

- The praise rests on a handful of Head-Fi CMoy posts. The sound descriptors are forum folklore and no published measurements were found.
- JFET voltage noise of about 11.6 nV/rtHz typ (17 max) is well above bipolar audio parts. This matters in low-impedance, high-gain stages.
- Output current is modest (about 25 mA per LISO), so low-impedance headphones need a buffer.
- The input range includes V+ but stops several volts above V-. Keep inputs away from the negative rail.
- Per partinfo (unconfirmed), the offset-null pins are bonded out only on the ceramic JG package; on plastic DIP/SOIC, pins 1, 5 and 8 are NC.
- At 10 MHz and 40-45 V/us it is faster than the TL07x it replaces. Decouple well and watch capacitive loads (general engineering caution).
- **Counterfeits:** No TLE207x-specific counterfeit or remarking reports are known, but no dedicated search was run. It is an inexpensive, low-volume TI part and an unlikely main target. Treat marketplace 'TLE2072' DIPs as unverified and prefer authorized distributors (Digi-Key 296-10426-5-ND, Mouser 595-TLE2072CP / 595-TLE2072IDR). Bench check: slew rate should be about 40-45 V/us and input bias current in the tens of pA at room temperature. A remarked TL072/TL082 slews only about 13 V/us.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments, PCN 20231219013.1 (19 Dec 2023; the Mouser copy is dated 22 Dec 2023); first-ship and effective dates not retrieved. Current datasheet retitled later (revision letter and date not retrieved).

TI PCN 20231219013.1 ('Qualification of RFAB using ...') moves TLE207x orderables to the RFAB wafer fab with a die revision and an assembly-site change. The summary says the die was changed as a result of the process change and that thermal characteristics were updated. This is another Excalibur/BiFET part moved out of a legacy fab onto a revised die under the same part number, following the TL07x and LF353. The datasheet has since been retitled without 'JFET-Input', as TI did with the TL07x ('FET-input') and TLE208x. No electrical deltas were retrieved.

- **Affected:** TLE2071CP, TLE2071IDR, TLE2071ACDR, TLE2072ACP, TLE2072CP, TLE2072CDR
- **How to tell:** The part number is unchanged. Summaries of the PCN show the die-revision field changing from A/B/C/H to A, with RFAB (Richardson, USA) as the chip site, plus an assembly-site change. Identification is by date code or lot after the (unretrieved) first-ship date. The current TI datasheet (tle2074a.pdf) is retitled 'TLE207x, TLE207xA Excalibur Low-Noise High-Speed Operational Amplifiers', dropping 'JFET-Input'. Older revisions (SLOS181x) are titled '...EXCALIBUR LOW-NOISE HIGH-SPEED JFET-INPUT...'. Whether the TLE2074 quad is on the PCN is unknown.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown in detail. The TLE207x is chosen for its low noise and 9-10 MHz speed. A revised die may change noise, slew, output behaviour and stability margin even with unchanged limits, as happened with the LF353 and TL07x.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: the die revision is PCN-documented, but no electrical deltas were retrieved; pre- and post-2024 lots may differ under the same part number.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab | legacy fab (not named in the retrieved summary) | RFAB, Richardson USA |
| Die revision (PCN field) | A, B, C, H | A |
| Assembly site | existing site | changed/added (per PCN summary; site not retrieved) |
| Thermal characteristics | per the existing datasheet | updated values in the PCN (numbers not retrieved) |
| Datasheet title | TLE207x, TLE207xA EXCALIBUR LOW-NOISE HIGH-SPEED JFET-INPUT OPERATIONAL AMPLIFIERS (SLOS181) | TLE207x, TLE207xA Excalibur Low-Noise High-Speed Operational Amplifiers (current tle2074a.pdf) |

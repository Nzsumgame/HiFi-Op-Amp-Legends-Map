# TLE2071 / TLE2072 / TLE2074 (A grades)

**Low-noise high-speed JFET-input op-amp, 1/2/4 ch** · Texas Instruments (c. 1991–present) → Texas Instruments (2003–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*TI's fast Excalibur JFET op-amp (10 MHz, 40 V/µs); one Head-Fi CMoy builder rated it the best cheap 9 V pick of ~20.*

## Why it's rated

A niche budget favourite, not a legend. On Head-Fi (c. 2007–2009) one user who tried about 20 op-amps rated the TLE2072 the best cheap choice for a 9 V CMoy, ahead of OPA2132 and OPA2134; others agreed at low supply and noted lower battery drain. The ohdsp open-hardware ADC/DAC boards use it. No ASR or Samuel Groner measurements exist.

**Described as:** forward, tipped-up midrange, lively, weighty, textured bass, convincing cymbals, detailed

**Used in:** 9 V battery CMoy headphone amplifiers, Audio ADC input filter/driver (ohdsp ADC-PCM4202), DAC differential-to-single-ended buffers (ohdsp BUF-DiffToSE-ADAU1966), 9 V JFET charge preamps (CERN DIY particle detector), Upgrade for TL05x/TL07x/TL08x sockets

## Watch out

- Praise rests on a few Head-Fi posts. Lore: TLE2072 has better DC, TLE2082 better AC specs; TI's models match.
- JFET noise (~11.6 nV/√Hz typ, 17 max) is well above bipolar audio parts; matters in low-impedance, high-gain stages.
- Modest output current (~25 mA); buffer low-impedance headphones.
- Input range includes V+ but stops volts above V-; offset-null pins reportedly only on ceramic JG (plastic pins 1/5/8 NC).
- Faster than the TL07x it replaces (10 MHz, 40–45 V/µs); decouple well and watch capacitive loads.
- **Counterfeits:** No TLE207x fakes reported. Treat marketplace 'TLE2072' DIPs as unverified; buy from Digi-Key (296-10426-5-ND) or Mouser (595-TLE2072CP / 595-TLE2072IDR). Bench check: about 40–45 V/µs slew and tens of pA bias; a remarked TL072/TL082 slews only ~13 V/µs.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments, 2023-12 · PCN 20231219013.1

PCN 20231219013.1 ('Qualification of RFAB using ...') moves TLE207x orderables to TI's RFAB wafer fab with a die revision, assembly-site change and updated thermal data, following the TL07x and LF353. The datasheet was later retitled without 'JFET-Input'. No electrical deltas known. Mouser copy dated 22 Dec 2023.

- **Affected:** TLE2071CP, TLE2071IDR, TLE2071ACDR, TLE2072ACP, TLE2072CP, TLE2072CDR
- **How to tell:** Same part number. Die-revision field A/B/C/H → A; chip site RFAB (Richardson, USA). Identify by date code or lot after first ship (date unknown). TLE2074 coverage unknown.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. A revised die may change noise, slew, output behaviour and stability margin under unchanged limits, as with LF353 and TL07x.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: die revision documented but no electrical deltas; pre- and post-2024 lots may differ
- **Confidence:** medium

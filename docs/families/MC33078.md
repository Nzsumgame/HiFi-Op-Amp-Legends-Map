# MC33078 / MC33079 (NCV33078 / NCV33079)

**Bipolar low-noise op-amp, dual/quad** · Motorola (pre-1999) → onsemi (1999–present) · second sources: Texas Instruments, STMicroelectronics, Unisonic Technologies (UTC) · Tier A (legend / revision hazard)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** ON PCN 11528 (19 July 2001) moved devices from Motorola's BMC fab in Mesa, AZ to ON's Tesla fab in Roznov, Czech Republic. (+2 more) [Details](#silicon-changes)

*Motorola's low-noise bipolar dual/quad (4.5 nV/√Hz, 16 MHz, 7 V/µs); Musiland chose it over the OP275 for its DACs.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5 nV/√Hz typ | 16 MHz typ (onsemi, TI); 15 MHz typ (ST) | 7 V/µs typ | 0.002% THD typ | ±5 V to ±18 V (36 V max VCC to VEE) | 4.1 mA (MC33078), 8.4 mA (MC33079) typ total |

## Why it's rated

A budget low-noise part praised mainly by Soomal. Musiland dropped the OP275 for the MC33079 in its Monitor 03 US/PS (2011), then used it in the 06 MX, 10 and 06 Plus. Soomal heard better treble detail, finer resolution and slightly brighter sound, but judged whole products and called it not top-tier Hi-Fi. No ASR or Self measurements.

**Described as:** better treble detail, a little brighter, finer resolution, 'very nice highs' (folklore), clean, slightly bright (unsourced)

**Used in:** I/V for current-output DACs (Musiland PCM1798 cards), Balanced I/V after ES9028Q2M (Monitor 06 Plus), Post-DAC filter and gain stage (Monitor 10, AK4490), Headphone-amp gain stage, Preamps, filters, pedals; TL07x/NE5532 upgrade rolling

## Watch out

- Needs at least ±5 V (10 V total); unsuitable for USB-bus designs without a boost converter.
- Bipolar input with about 300 nA bias, so source impedances and DC paths matter.
- TI's die differs from onsemi's and is now shared with NE5532, LM833, RC4580; same number, different silicon.
- ST specifies 15 MHz versus 16 MHz; no cross-vendor listening or measurement comparison found.
- Soomal praise is whole-product; reviews mislabel brands (an onsemi MC33079DG called 'Motorola').
- **Counterfeits:** No specific counterfeit reports. Risk lies in unbranded lots (eBay '50/10 pcs MC33078DR SOP-8 MC33078D'). TI mark reportedly 'M33078'. Distrust 'Motorola' parts dated well after 2001, when ON-branded MC33078/D Rev. 1 appeared. Buy authorized; check logo and date code.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments, 2023-11 · PCN 20231114002.1; E2E 2026

TI unified the NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 dies, keeping part numbers (E2E 1652528). MC33078DR went through the Nov 2023 SFAB 150 mm → RFAB PCN with NE5532DR and LM833DR. MC33078 is probably the donor design; any circuit change unconfirmed. Hackaday (3 Jun 2026) and Headphonesty (Jul 2026) covered it.

- **Affected:** MC33078DR (listed in PCN 20231114002.1), MC33078D, MC33078P, MC33078DGKR, MC33078DGKT (other TI MC33078 orderables; covered by the E2E die-merge statement, PCN listing not seen)
- **How to tell:** No marker; datasheet still SLLS633C and no datasheet PCN. TI date codes from 2024 are probably new silicon. onsemi, ST, UTC and Motorola parts unaffected.
- **Audio impact:** Probably small: SLLS633C still says 7 V/µs, 4.5 nV/√Hz. Large deltas (9→5 V/µs, diodes removed, ±22→±18 V) are NE5532's (SLOS075K).
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: pin-compatible, limits unchanged, but process migration confirmed; re-qualify new TI lots for overload or protection behaviour.
- **Confidence:** medium

### Second-source difference: Texas Instruments and STMicroelectronics vs onsemi (Motorola original design), 2004-10 (TI); SGS-Thomson era (ST)

Same part number, different silicon. TI's die is believed identical to its LM833 (E2E 1443925) and is now the shared NE5532/LM833/RC4580 die. onsemi continues Motorola's design. ST's GBP spec differs, suggesting an independent die. No vendor-to-vendor comparison found.

- **Affected:** MC33078 (TI), MC33078 (ST), MC33079 (ST), MC33078 / MC33079 (onsemi)
- **How to tell:** Check logo and top mark (TI reportedly 'M33078'). ST gives 15 MHz GBP versus 16 MHz. TI's MC33079 appears only in its early Rev. A datasheet.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown; headline noise and slew match, but internal circuits may differ, so reviews may not carry across vendors.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: pinout and headline specs match; differences are second-order (GBW, possibly input protection).
- **Confidence:** medium

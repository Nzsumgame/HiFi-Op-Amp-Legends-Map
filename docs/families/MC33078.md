# MC33078 / MC33079 (low-noise bipolar dual/quad)

**Tier** A: legend / hazard · **Category** Bipolar classics

*Motorola's low-noise bipolar dual/quad (4.5 nV/√Hz, 16 MHz, 7 V/µs). Musiland used the MC33079 instead of the OP275 in its sound cards, and Soomal credited it with better treble detail.*

**Technology:** Bipolar, internally compensated, low-noise voltage-feedback op-amp. MC33078 is the dual and MC33079 the quad. The datasheets specify ±5 V to ±18 V supplies. The polarity of the input pair is unconfirmed. Motorola's SPICE library (MOTOR.LIB) tags the MC33078 model 'PNP Input', but that model is a generic Boyle template with an N-channel JFET input pair, so it is no evidence either way. onsemi lists input bias current as +300 nA, the same sign it gives the NPN-input NE5532, which hints at NPN. This is an unchecked inference. Three makers sell the part under the same number. onsemi's part is Motorola's original design. TI has made its own die since Oct 2004, and a TI engineer believes it is the same die as TI's LM833. ST's version is specified at 15 MHz rather than 16 MHz.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (4 recorded: Unclassified).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A budget low-noise bipolar dual/quad whose best-documented enthusiast praise comes from Soomal (China). Musiland dropped its usual OP275 for the MC33079 in the Monitor 03 US and 03 PS (PCM1798, 2011). It then used the part in the 06 MX (a TI part, 2013), the Monitor 10 (MC33079DG after an AK4490, 2016) and the 06 Plus (onsemi MC33079DG as a balanced I/V stage after an ES9028Q2M, 2017). Soomal said the 03 US had 'better treble detail' than the OP275-based 02 US. It heard the 03 PS as a little brighter, with finer resolution and better HF transients, but called the gain incremental. Both are whole-product judgements. In the 06 MX review Soomal also said the op-amp was not a top-tier Hi-Fi part. One EDAboard thread weighs it as a TL074 upgrade, and a guitar-pedal swap table says the TI MC33078 has 'very nice highs'. No ASR, Samuel Groner or Douglas Self measurements were found.

**How people describe the sound:** better treble detail (Soomal, Monitor 03 US vs OP275-based 02 US; product-level), a little brighter than the OP275-based 01 PS (Soomal, 03 PS; product-level), finer resolution, clearly improved HF transients (Soomal, 03 PS; product-level), 'very nice highs' (guitar-pedal op-amp swap table, TI MC33078; folklore), clean, slightly bright (op-amp-roller folklore; no source found)

**Typical uses:** I/V stage for differential current-output DACs (PCM1798 in Musiland Monitor 03 US / 03 PS / 06 MX), balanced I/V stage after an ESS ES9028Q2M (Musiland Monitor 06 Plus, onsemi MC33079DG), post-DAC filter/gain stage (Musiland Monitor 10 with AK4490), headphone-amp gain stage (Soomal 'E-brand F-type' headphone amp, MC33078, 2010), general consumer/pro audio preamps, active filters, guitar pedals, and op-amp rolling in place of TL07x/NE5532

**Caveats:**

- Needs at least ±5 V (10 V total), so it does not suit low-voltage or USB-bus designs without a boost converter.
- Bipolar input with about 300 nA typ bias current, so source impedances and DC paths matter.
- TI's MC33078 is TI's own die. A TI engineer believes it is the same die as TI's LM833, and TI now says it shares one die with the NE5532, LM833 and RC4580 (E2E, 2026). TI and onsemi parts may therefore sound or measure differently despite the same number.
- Several makers (onsemi, TI, ST, UTC) sell parts under the same number, and ST specifies 15 MHz GBW against 16 MHz for the others. No ON-vs-TI-vs-ST listening or measurement comparison was found.
- All the Soomal praise is whole-product judgement, and Soomal itself called the MC33079 not a top-tier Hi-Fi part. Brand attribution in reviews is loose: Soomal called an onsemi MC33079DG 'Motorola'.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Unclassified: Texas Instruments, Confirmed on TI E2E in 2026 (thread 1652528, around June…

TI E2E thread 1652528, 'NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 all now the same die', reports that TI has unified the four dies while keeping the part numbers. Search summaries describe it as the NE5532 moving onto the MC33078/RC4580-class die. In an earlier E2E thread (1443925), a TI engineer already believed the MC33078 and LM833 were the same die because their specs and graphs are identical. So the TI MC33078 may be the donor die rather than a changed part. Whether TI MC33078 silicon itself changed is unconfirmed. Headphonesty (July 2026) and Hackaday (June 2026) covered the consolidation.

- **When:** Confirmed on TI E2E in 2026 (thread 1652528, around June 2026; Hackaday coverage 3 June 2026). It is linked to the NE5532 transition (SLOS075K, Dec 2025).
- **Affected:** MC33078 (TI orderables: MC33078D, MC33078DR, MC33078DGKR, MC33078DGKT, MC33078P, MC33078MDREP)
- **How to tell old from new:** No MC33078-specific marker. TI's MC33078 datasheet is still SLLS633C (Nov 2006), and no PCN was found. The NE5532 change is marked by SLOS075K. onsemi, ST, UTC and Motorola-marked parts are unaffected.
- **Audio impact:** Probably small or none for TI MC33078 if it was the donor die: SLLS633C still specifies 7 V/µs and 4.5 nV/√Hz. The large deltas (NE5532 slew 9→5 V/µs, input protection diodes removed, maximum supply ±22→±18 V) apply to the NE5532, not to MC33078 as documented.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: no MC33078 datasheet change or PCN was found, and the evidence points to NE5532 moving onto the MC33078/LM833 die. Re-qualify new TI lots only if a design depends on undocumented behaviour.
- **Confidence:** medium

### 2. Unclassified: Texas Instruments / STMicroelectronics…, TI since October 2004;

Same part number, different silicon across vendors. TI's MC33078 is a TI second-source die, and a TI engineer believes it is identical to TI's LM833 (E2E 1443925). onsemi continues Motorola's original design. ST's version has a different GBP specification, which suggests an independent die. No measurement comparison between vendors was found.

- **When:** TI since October 2004; ST since the SGS-Thomson era
- **Affected:** MC33078 (TI), MC33079 (TI), MC33078 (ST)
- **How to tell old from new:** Check the logo and top mark: TI's mark is reportedly 'M33078', and onsemi and ST parts carry their own logos. ST's datasheet specifies 15 MHz GBP where onsemi and TI specify 16 MHz.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. The headline noise and slew figures match (4.5 nV/√Hz, 7 V/µs), but the internal circuits may differ, so reviews of one vendor's part may not transfer to another's.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: the pinout and headline specs match. Differences are second-order (GBW, possibly the input stage and protection).
- **Confidence:** low

### 3. Unclassified: onsemi (ON Semiconductor, ex-Motorola), Initial PCN 11528 issued 19 July 2001.

ON Semiconductor PCN 11528 announced the transfer and qualification of devices then made in the Motorola BMC wafer fab in Mesa, AZ to ON's Tesla wafer fab in Roznov, Czech Republic, which became the primary fab. The listed products include MC33078D/P and MC33079D/P. BMC products that were not transferred went to End of Life under PCN 11785. This is a same-part-number wafer-fab move of the original Motorola MC33078/MC33079 die. It is separate from the known TI-versus-onsemi second-source difference.

- **When:** Initial PCN 11528 issued 19 July 2001. A companion discontinuance notice, PCN 11785, was issued 27 September 2001 for the BMC products that were not transferred.
- **Affected:** MC33078D, MC33078P, MC33079D, MC33079P
- **How to tell old from new:** Not stated in the retrieved summary. By inference, Motorola/ON parts with pre-2001/2002 date codes would carry Mesa BMC wafers and later ones Roznov wafers, but no marking rule was found.
- **Audio impact:** None documented. It was a transfer of the same design to another fab, and no before/after noise or THD comparison was found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - same design and part numbers; any difference would be process-spread within the data sheet limits.
- **Confidence:** medium

### 4. Unclassified: Texas Instruments, 2025-2026, the same timeframe as the NE5532 die merge…

TI's MC33078 (dual) is one of the four parts TI confirmed now share a single die with the NE5532, LM833 and RC4580. The MC33079 quad is not in TI's portfolio, so it is not part of the change. I did not determine the specific changes to MC33078 specs.

- **When:** 2025-2026, the same timeframe as the NE5532 die merge (confirmed on the TI E2E thread 'NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 all now the same die').
- **Affected:** MC33078D, MC33078DR, MC33078P, MC33078DGKR
- **How to tell old from new:** The part number stays the same. The TI MC33078 datasheet I found is Rev C. I found no MC33078-specific PCN number or datasheet delta. TI does not appear to sell the MC33079 (quad): ti.com only returns MC33078 (dual), and the MC33079 is an onsemi part, so onsemi MC33079 and NCV33079 are not affected by TI's merge.
- **Audio impact:** TI MC33078s built after the merge are RC4580-type silicon, so their behaviour may differ from the Motorola/onsemi-lineage MC33078 that audio users know.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - The part is pin compatible. Supply-limit, slew and topology differences like those reported for the NE5532 may apply but were not verified for the MC33078.
- **Confidence:** medium

## Counterfeits

No MC33078/MC33079-specific counterfeit reports were found. Unbranded marketplace lots are the usual risk, for example eBay listings of '50/10 pcs MC33078DR SOP-8 MC33078D' with no maker stated. Alldatasheet gives the TI top mark as 'M33078' (not re-confirmed); the onsemi and ST markings were not captured. Folklore prefers Motorola-marked parts, so relabelled 'Motorola' vintage parts are plausible. Treat 'Motorola' parts with date codes well after 2001, when ON-branded MC33078/D Rev. 1 appeared, with suspicion. Buy from authorized distributors and check the logo, date code and marking.

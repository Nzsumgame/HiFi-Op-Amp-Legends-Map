# MC33078 / MC33079 (low-noise bipolar dual/quad)

Motorola (pre-1999) → ON Semiconductor / onsemi (1999-present) · second sources: Texas Instruments, SGS-Thomson / STMicroelectronics, Unisonic Technologies (UTC) · Tier A (legend / revision hazard)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** ON Semiconductor PCN 11528 announced the transfer and qualification of devices made in the Motorola BMC wafer fab in Mesa, AZ to ON's Tesla wafer fab in Roznov, Czech Republic,… (+2 more) [Details](#silicon-changes)

*Motorola's low-noise bipolar dual/quad (4.5 nV/√Hz, 16 MHz, 7 V/µs). Musiland used the MC33079 instead of the OP275 in its sound cards, and Soomal credited it with better treble detail.*

| Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|
| 7 V/µs typ | ±5 V to ±18 V (dual supply) | 4.1 mA typ total (MC33078); 8.4 mA typ total (MC33079); about 4 mA typ total (ST MC33078) |

## Why it's rated

A budget low-noise bipolar dual/quad whose best-documented enthusiast praise comes from Soomal (China). Musiland dropped its usual OP275 for the MC33079 in the Monitor 03 US and 03 PS (PCM1798, 2011). It then used the part in the 06 MX (a TI part, 2013), the Monitor 10 (MC33079DG after an AK4490, 2016) and the 06 Plus (onsemi MC33079DG as a balanced I/V stage after an ES9028Q2M, 2017). Soomal said the 03 US had 'better treble detail' than the OP275-based 02 US. It heard the 03 PS as a little brighter, with finer resolution and better HF transients, but called the gain incremental. Both are whole-product judgements. In the 06 MX review Soomal also said the op-amp was not a top-tier Hi-Fi part. One EDAboard thread weighs it as a TL074 upgrade, and a guitar-pedal swap table says the TI MC33078 has 'very nice highs'. No ASR, Samuel Groner or Douglas Self measurements were found.

**Described as:** better treble detail (Soomal, Monitor 03 US vs OP275-based 02 US; product-level), a little brighter than the OP275-based 01 PS (Soomal, 03 PS; product-level), finer resolution, clearly improved HF transients (Soomal, 03 PS; product-level), 'very nice highs' (guitar-pedal op-amp swap table, TI MC33078; folklore), clean, slightly bright (op-amp-roller folklore; no source found)

**Used in:** I/V stage for differential current-output DACs (PCM1798 in Musiland Monitor 03 US / 03 PS / 06 MX), balanced I/V stage after an ESS ES9028Q2M (Musiland Monitor 06 Plus, onsemi MC33079DG), post-DAC filter/gain stage (Musiland Monitor 10 with AK4490), headphone-amp gain stage (Soomal 'E-brand F-type' headphone amp, MC33078, 2010), general consumer/pro audio preamps, active filters, guitar pedals, and op-amp rolling in place of TL07x/NE5532

## Watch out

- Needs at least ±5 V (10 V total), so it does not suit low-voltage or USB-bus designs without a boost converter.
- Bipolar input with about 300 nA typ bias current, so source impedances and DC paths matter.
- TI's MC33078 is TI's own die. A TI engineer believes it is the same die as TI's LM833, and TI now says it shares one die with the NE5532, LM833 and RC4580 (E2E, 2026). TI and onsemi parts may therefore sound or measure differently despite the same number.
- Several makers (onsemi, TI, ST, UTC) sell parts under the same number, and ST specifies 15 MHz GBW against 16 MHz for the others. No ON-vs-TI-vs-ST listening or measurement comparison was found.
- All the Soomal praise is whole-product judgement, and Soomal itself called the MC33079 not a top-tier Hi-Fi part. Brand attribution in reviews is loose: Soomal called an onsemi MC33079DG 'Motorola'.
- **Counterfeits:** No MC33078/MC33079-specific counterfeit reports were found. Unbranded marketplace lots are the usual risk, for example eBay listings of '50/10 pcs MC33078DR SOP-8 MC33078D' with no maker stated. Alldatasheet gives the TI top mark as 'M33078' (not re-confirmed); the onsemi and ST markings were not captured. Folklore prefers Motorola-marked parts, so relabelled 'Motorola' vintage parts are plausible. Treat 'Motorola' parts with date codes well after 2001, when ON-branded MC33078/D Rev. 1 appeared, with suspicion. Buy from authorized distributors and check the logo, date code and marking.

## Silicon changes

### Die redesign: Texas Instruments, Process-migration PCN 20231114002.1 dated 15 Nov 2023; search summaries say it lists MC33078DR. TI confirmed the die merge on E2E (thread 1652528) in 2026. Hackaday covered it on 3 Jun 2026 and Headphonesty in Jul 2026. It is linked to the NE5532 re-spec (SLOS075K, Dec 2025).

TI E2E thread 1652528 ('NE5532, LM833, RC4580 and MC33078 all now the same die') reports that TI unified the four dies while keeping the part numbers. Search summaries describe the NE5532 moving onto the MC33078/RC4580-class die, and Hackaday quotes Rich Cabot identifying it as an RC4580. TI's MC33078DR went through the same November 2023 process-migration PCN (SFAB 150 mm to RFAB) as NE5532DR and LM833DR, so TI MC33078 silicon was at least re-fabricated on a new process. In the earlier E2E thread 1443925 a TI engineer already believed MC33078 and LM833 were the same die. TI's MC33078 may therefore be close to the donor design, and whether its circuit changed is unconfirmed. Its datasheet limits are unchanged.

- **Affected:** MC33078DR (listed in PCN 20231114002.1), MC33078D, MC33078P, MC33078DGKR, MC33078DGKT (other TI MC33078 orderables; covered by the E2E die-merge statement, PCN listing not seen)
- **How to tell:** There is no MC33078-specific marker. TI's datasheet is still SLLS633C (Oct 2004, revised Nov 2006), and no MC33078 datasheet PCN was found. PCN 20231114002.1 lists MC33078DR, so TI date codes from 2024 on are probably new silicon (inference). TI's current MC33078 page covers only the dual. onsemi, ST, UTC and Motorola-marked MC33078/MC33079 and NCV parts are not affected.
- **Audio impact:** Probably small if TI's MC33078 was already the donor design: SLLS633C still specifies 7 V/µs and 4.5 nV/√Hz. The large deltas (slew 9 to 5 V/µs, input diodes removed, ±22 to ±18 V) are documented for NE5532, not MC33078. Behaviour may differ from the Motorola/onsemi-lineage MC33078 that audio users know.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium - pin-compatible and SLLS633C limits unchanged, but a process migration is confirmed by PCN and TI says the die is now shared with RC4580, NE5532 and LM833. Re-qualify new TI lots where a design depends on undocumented behaviour such as overload recovery, input protection or capacitive-load stability.
- **Confidence:** medium

### Second-source difference: Texas Instruments and STMicroelectronics vs onsemi (Motorola original design), TI since October 2004 (SLLS633); ST since the SGS-Thomson era; Motorola/onsemi original

The same part number is built on different vendors' silicon. TI's MC33078 is a TI second-source die that a TI engineer believes is identical to TI's LM833 (E2E 1443925); it is now also the shared NE5532/LM833/RC4580 die (see the TI die-redesign entry). onsemi continues Motorola's original design. ST's MC33078/MC33079 have a different GBP specification, which suggests an independent die. No vendor-to-vendor measurement comparison was found.

- **Affected:** MC33078 (TI), MC33078 (ST), MC33079 (ST), MC33078 / MC33079 (onsemi)
- **How to tell:** Check the logo and top mark (TI's mark is reportedly 'M33078'); onsemi and ST parts carry their own logos. ST datasheets give 15 MHz GBP, while onsemi and TI give 16 MHz. TI's quad MC33079 appears only in an early TI 'MC33078, MC33079 (Rev. A)' datasheet, and TI's current product line lists only the MC33078.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. The headline noise and slew figures match (4.5 nV/√Hz, 7 V/µs), but the internal circuits may differ, so a review of one vendor's part may not carry over to another's.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - pinout and headline specs match. Differences are second-order (GBW, possibly input stage and protection).
- **Confidence:** medium

### Fab / process transfer: onsemi (ON Semiconductor, ex-Motorola), Initial PCN 11528 issued 19 July 2001. A companion discontinuance notice, PCN 11785, was issued 27 September 2001 for the BMC products that were not transferred.

ON Semiconductor PCN 11528 announced the transfer and qualification of devices made in the Motorola BMC wafer fab in Mesa, AZ to ON's Tesla wafer fab in Roznov, Czech Republic, which became the primary fab. MC33078D/P are listed, and MC33079D/P are listed per the completeness sweep. BMC products that were not transferred went end-of-life under PCN 11785. This is a same-part-number fab move of the original Motorola MC33078/MC33079 die, separate from the TI-versus-onsemi second-source difference.

- **Affected:** MC33078D, MC33078P, MC33079D, MC33079P
- **How to tell:** No marking rule was found. Motorola/ON parts with pre-2001/2002 date codes would carry Mesa BMC wafers and later ones Roznov wafers (inference).
- **Audio impact:** None documented. The same design moved to another fab, and no before/after noise or THD comparison was found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - same design and part numbers; any difference would be process spread within datasheet limits.
- **Confidence:** high

# AD8655 / AD8656 (ADI low-noise precision CMOS, rail-to-rail, 5 V)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

*Low-noise (2.7 nV/√Hz) rail-to-rail CMOS op-amp for 2.7–5.5 V. Builders of portable and USB-powered headphone amps use it where ±15 V parts can't run.*

**Technology:** Single-supply CMOS (2.7–5.5 V), rail-to-rail input and output, unity-gain stable. Offset is trimmed with ADI DigiTrim. ADI PCN 23_0068 places the AD8656 among its '0.6 µm CMOS amplifier products' built at TSMC Fab 9/2A/2B, with ADI Limerick added as an alternate wafer fab from 2023. Datasheet figures: 2.7 nV/√Hz at 10 kHz, 28 MHz bandwidth, 220 mA output current. Distributor listings give about 1 pA bias current.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Fab / process transfer).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The part has a niche following among builders of portable and low-voltage (5 V / USB-powered) headphone amps. Head-Fi posters call it a 'nifty low-voltage rail-to-rail dual' that 'sounds quite nice driving headphones directly'. In AD8656-vs-AD8620 threads on Head-Fi and diyAudio, posters rate it below the AD8620 when other things are equal. It is not an 'op-amp rolling' legend in ±15 V gear.

**How people describe the sound:** 'sounds quite nice driving headphones directly' (Head-Fi forum post)

**Typical uses:** Portable and battery headphone amplifiers, USB-bus-powered / single 5 V audio stages (DAC output buffers, headphone drivers), Low-voltage preamp and buffer stages that need rail-to-rail swing

**Caveats:**

- Absolute maximum supply is about 5.5 V (2.7–5.5 V operating), so it will be destroyed in a ±12 V/±15 V op-amp socket.
- Sold only in surface-mount packages (MSOP-8 and SOIC-8 per the datasheet summary). Rolling it into DIP-8 sockets needs an adapter.
- Forum comparisons rate it below the AD8620. Those are forum opinions, not measurements.
- Supply current is fairly high for a 5 V part (3.7 mA per amp typ), which matters for battery life in portable builds.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, PCN 23_0068 Rev. - published 8 May 2023, effective 10 Aug…

ADI added ADI Limerick (ADLK) as an alternate wafer fab to TSMC Fab 9, Fab 2A and Fab 2B for its 0.6 µm CMOS amplifier products. For a limited number of parts, wafer diameter also changed from 6 to 8 inches. ADI states no impact to fit, form, function or reliability, and has published qualification reports. Whether the AD8655 is listed was not confirmed.

- **When:** PCN 23_0068 Rev. - published 8 May 2023, effective 10 Aug 2023; Rev. A published 6 Feb 2024 (models added), effective 10 May 2024
- **Affected:** AD8656 (package/grade variants per PCN list), AD8655 (not confirmed)
- **How to tell old from new:** Not visible from the part marking. Wafer-fab origin can be traced only through ADI lot or date-code records. Parts made after Aug 2023 may come from TSMC or ADI Limerick wafers.
- **Audio impact:** None expected. ADI says data-sheet limits are unchanged, and no measurements comparing fabs are known. Parameters without guaranteed limits, such as the 1/f noise corner, could in principle differ slightly.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - same part number and data-sheet limits; ADI states no change to fit, form or function.
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Verification:** A search confirmed the PCN scope (ADLK as an alternate to TSMC Fab 9/2A/2B for 0.6 µm CMOS amplifiers, no fit/form/function impact) and found the Rev. - and Rev. A qualification reports. The summary did not itemize the AD8656 or AD8655, so the listing rests on the earlier pass.

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab | TSMC Fab 9 / Fab 2A / Fab 2B (0.6 µm CMOS) | TSMC or ADI Limerick (ADLK) |
| Wafer diameter (limited parts) | 6 inch | 8 inch |

- **When:** PCN 07_0024 (originally 2007; the form is at Rev. E)
- **Affected:** AD8655, AD8656
- **How to tell old from new:** Unknown. Use a date code after the PCN effective date.
- **Audio impact:** None expected (package materials only).
- **Drop-in risk:** low - package-material change; no data-sheet change is associated with it.
- **Confidence:** low
- **Verification:** Reclassified from 'nature unknown' to package-or-assembly, because the OP275 and AD8610 passes identify PCN 07_0024 as the Sumitomo mold-compound change. A combined search did not show the AD8655/AD8656 rows.

## Counterfeits

This pass found no specific counterfeit reports. ADI offers only surface-mount packages (MSOP-8 and SOIC-8), so any 'AD8656' sold as a DIP-8 is either an adapter board or suspect. Buy from authorized distributors.

# AD8655 / AD8656 (ADI low-noise precision CMOS, rail-to-rail, 5 V)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

**Technology:** Low-voltage (5.5 V max) rail-to-rail CMOS dual. Search results give 2.7 nV/√Hz noise, about 220 mA output current and 1 pA bias.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Portable and low-voltage headphone-amp builders discuss it. Head-Fi posts call it a 'nifty low-voltage rail-to-rail dual' that 'sounds quite nice driving headphones directly'. Comparison threads on Head-Fi and diyAudio (AD8656 vs AD8620) rate it below the AD8620 when other things are equal.

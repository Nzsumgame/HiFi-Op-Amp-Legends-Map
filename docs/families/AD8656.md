# AD8655 / AD8656

**Low-noise precision CMOS, rail-to-rail I/O, 5 V, 1/2 ch** · Analog Devices (c. 2005–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** ADI added ADI Limerick (ADLK) as an alternate fab to TSMC Fab 9/2A/2B for 0.6 µm CMOS amps; some parts moved from 6- to 8-inch wafers. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*2.7 nV/√Hz rail-to-rail CMOS for 2.7–5.5 V: the pick for portable and USB-powered headphone amps.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.7 nV/√Hz | 28 MHz | 11 V/µs (8.5 V/µs also quoted) | 0.0007% | 2.7 V to 5.5 V single supply | 3.7 mA typ, 4.5 mA max per amplifier |

## Why it's rated

A niche favourite for portable and 5 V / USB-powered headphone amps. Head-Fi posters call it a 'nifty low-voltage rail-to-rail dual' that 'sounds quite nice driving headphones directly'. In Head-Fi and diyAudio comparisons it ranks below the AD8620, other things equal. Not a rolling legend in ±15 V gear.

**Described as:** nice driving headphones directly

**Used in:** Portable and battery headphone amplifiers, USB-powered 5 V DAC buffers and headphone drivers, Low-voltage rail-to-rail preamp and buffer stages

## Watch out

- Maximum supply about 5.5 V; a ±12 V/±15 V op-amp socket will destroy it.
- Surface-mount only (MSOP-8, SOIC-8); DIP-8 sockets need an adapter.
- Forum comparisons rate it below the AD8620; opinions, not measurements.
- 3.7 mA per amp typ is fairly high for a 5 V part, hurting battery life.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific counterfeit reports found. ADI offers only MSOP-8 and SOIC-8, so any DIP-8 'AD8656' is an adapter board or suspect. Buy from authorized distributors.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2023-08 · PCN 23_0068 (Rev. A 2024-05)

ADI added ADI Limerick (ADLK) as an alternate fab to TSMC Fab 9/2A/2B for 0.6 µm CMOS amps; some parts moved from 6- to 8-inch wafers. Rev. - published 8 May 2023, effective 10 Aug 2023; Rev. A (more models) published 6 Feb 2024, effective 10 May 2024.

- **Affected:** AD8656 (package/grade variants per PCN list), AD8655 (not confirmed)
- **How to tell:** Not visible in the marking. Fab origin is traceable only via ADI lot or date-code records; post-Aug 2023 parts may be TSMC or Limerick wafers.
- **Audio impact:** None expected; data-sheet limits unchanged. Unguaranteed parameters such as the 1/f noise corner could in principle differ slightly.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same part number and limits; ADI states no fit, form or function change.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab | TSMC Fab 9 / Fab 2A / Fab 2B (0.6 µm CMOS) | TSMC or ADI Limerick (ADLK) |
| Wafer diameter (limited parts) | 6 inch | 8 inch |

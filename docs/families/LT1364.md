# LT1363 / LT1364 / LT1365

**Bipolar C-Load VFB op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · Linear Technology (1994–2017) → Analog Devices (2017–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*Linear's 1994 70 MHz, 1000 V/µs C-Load dual: a cheap Japanese favourite, fast and transparent; English forums split.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≈9 nV/√Hz (9 typ at 10 kHz; 15–20 at 100 Hz, graph) | 70 MHz typ (50 min; 44 min 0–70 °C) ±15 V; 50 MHz ±5 V | 1000 V/µs (750 min) ±15 V; 450 V/µs (300 min) ±5 V | ≈0.00065% (AV=+1), 0.00075% (AV=−1) to 1.3 kHz (graph) | Specified at ±2.5 V, ±5 V, ±15 V; abs. max 36 V total | 6.3 mA typ / 7.5 mA max per amp (±15 V) |

## Why it's rated

Mixed, based on listening reports; no ASR or Samuel Groner measurements. Head-Fi users find it as quiet as OPA2134 with more transparency and less grain, and faster than OPA6x7. diyAudio suggests it for delta-sigma DAC filters, but critics call it coloured, able to 'kill nuances'. Japanese bloggers (Akizuki sells it) praise fast, uncoloured, 'tight, lean-muscled' sound.

**Described as:** transparent, less grain, fast, detailed, dynamic, involving, pinpoint imaging, tight, lean-muscled, clean treble, lighter bass than MUSE8820, coloured, euphonic (critics)

**Used in:** Delta-sigma DAC I/V, filter and buffer stages, Op-amp rolling: DACs, headphone amps, Xonar/Prodigy, TubeMagic D1, Low-impedance headphone drive (diyAudio suggestion), Line buffers and cable drivers, Video/RF and fast data-acquisition buffers

## Watch out

- Runs hot: 6.3 mA per amp; a DIP-8 dual at ±15 V dissipates ~0.38 W, ~50 °C rise.
- 70 MHz: layout-, socket- and decoupling-sensitive. Bypass 0.01–0.1 µF close; add CF when RF > 5 kΩ.
- High bias current (up to 2 µA): ~200 mV offset with 100 kΩ. Balance impedances or AC-couple.
- 9 nV/√Hz is noisier than NE5532/LM4562: poor for phono or high-gain stages.
- No comparator use or sustained differential input. C grade guaranteed only 0–70 °C; I/military grades by consultation.
- **Counterfeits:** No LT136x-specific fakes reported; the 'white print' tip concerns other op-amps. Checks: SO-8 marked '1364'; ~12.6 mA per dual at ±15 V; ~1000 V/µs at AV = −2. Ink and laser marks are both genuine (PCN 21_0060).

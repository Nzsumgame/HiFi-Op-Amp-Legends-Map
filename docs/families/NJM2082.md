# NJM2082 / NJM2082DD (New JRC / Nisshinbo JFET-input dual, improved NJM082)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** JFET-input

*New JRC's faster (20 V/us) improved NJM082 JFET dual; Japanese DIY listeners rate it a fuller, more composed swap for the TL072/NJM072.*

**Technology:** Dual J-FET-input op-amp. The datasheet calls it an improved version of the NJM082 (TL082-class), with low input offset and bias current, 10^12 ohm input resistance, 20 V/us slew and a +/-4 V to +/-18 V supply range. Intended uses: fast integrators, DACs, sample-and-hold and audio. NJM2082DD is sold by Japanese retailers as a noise-screened DIP grade.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The praise comes from a small number of Japanese hobbyist sources, which this pass did not re-check. The C-Area wiki (higemouse) found the NJM2082D heavier and more composed than the TL072CP, with better low- and high-frequency extension. A zigsow review ('072改', 'improved 072') describes the NJM2082DD as slightly warm like the NJM072 but more neutral, and suited to classical and clean-tone music. The dad8893 blog includes it in a JFET op-amp listening comparison (TL072 / NJM072 / NJM2082 / OPA2134 / MUSES8920).

**How people describe the sound:** heavier and more composed than TL072CP (C-Area wiki), extended lows and highs versus TL072CP (C-Area wiki), slightly warm like NJM072 but more neutral (zigsow review, DD grade)

**Typical uses:** op-amp rolling in DIY headphone amps and preamps, as a replacement in TL072/NJM072 sockets, JFET-input audio buffers and filters

**Caveats:**

- The impressions are subjective, come from a few Japanese sources, and were not re-verified in this pass.
- The DD grade's 'noise-screened' status comes from a retailer and has no vendor specification behind it.
- A JFET-input dual has higher voltage noise than bipolar audio duals such as the NJM4580 or NE5532.

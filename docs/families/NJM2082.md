# NJM2082 / NJM2082D / NJM2082DD / NJM2082M / NJM2082V / NJM2082L

**JFET-input dual op-amp, improved NJM082** · New Japan Radio (New JRC / NJR) (by 2003–2021) → Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2022–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*New JRC's faster (20 V/µs) take on the NJM082; Japanese DIYers call it a fuller, calmer TL072/NJM072 swap.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 nV/√Hz typ | 5 MHz typ | 20 V/µs typ | ±4 V to ±18 V | 4 mA typ |

## Why it's rated

Praise comes from a few Japanese hobbyist sources. The C-Area wiki found the NJM2082D heavier and more composed than the TL072CP, with better extension at both ends. A zigsow review calls the DD grade an 'improved 072': slightly warm like the NJM072 but more neutral. The dad8893 blog includes it in a JFET listening comparison.

**Described as:** heavier than TL072CP, more composed, extended lows and highs, slightly warm, more neutral than NJM072

**Used in:** Drop-in for TL072/NJM072 in DIY headphone amps, preamps, JFET-input audio buffers and filters, Fast integrators, DACs, sample-and-hold

## Watch out

- Impressions are subjective and come from a few Japanese sources.
- The DD 'noise-screened' grade is a retailer claim with no vendor specification behind it.
- As a JFET dual it has more voltage noise than bipolar duals like NJM4580 or NE5532.
- **Counterfeits:** No NJM2082-specific counterfeit or remarking reports are known. Take care with marketplace 'DD' parts: the noise screening is a sales grade, and only the suffix marking shows it.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

# THS4631 (single high-voltage, high-slew-rate, wideband JFET-input op amp)

Texas Instruments (December 2004 – present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** Rev C re-characterises the THS4631 on 'new silicon data', and on E2E TI says the performance changes come from a new manufacturing process. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*A ±15 V JFET-input op amp with 210 MHz GBW and 1000 V/µs slew. It is a cult DAC I/V and buffer part in Japan, praised as airy and transparent once it is stable.*

| Slew rate |
|---|
| 550 V/µs (2 V step, G=2); 900 V/µs (10 V step, G=2); 1000 V/µs (10 V step, G=5) |

## Why it's rated

A niche cult part in the Japanese DIY and op-amp-rolling scene. It is mostly used for I/V after current-output DACs, and in headphone amps and buffers via DIP-8 conversion boards. One Ameblo blog title calls it 'the rumoured op-amp' (噂のオペアンプ). Shirokuma sells it pre-mounted on a dual 8-pin DIP board. Users report extended highs, air and transparency when it is not oscillating. A Zigsow review calls it a 'tsundere honours-student character' (ツンデレ優等生キャラ). TI's datasheet lists 'Current DAC output buffer' as an application. No Audio Science Review or other audio-band measurements are known to this record.

**Described as:** airy, transparent, extended highs, expressive, 'tsundere honours student' (ツンデレ優等生): excellent once stable

**Used in:** I/V conversion after current-output DACs, DIP-8 op-amp rolling via conversion boards (e.g. Shirokuma dual board), Headphone amp op-amp swap (e.g. Elekit TU-HP01, per the xgender-isao blog), High-impedance wideband buffer / line driver

## Watch out

- Oscillates easily in rolling and adapter use. Japanese users report needing an output Zobel network. TI's datasheet warns that large feedback resistors oscillate with the input capacitance and recommends a feedback capacitor, which matters for I/V stages.
- Runs hot: 12.5 mA typ and 14.5 mA max quiescent current per channel in Rev C (11.5 typ / 13 max in Rev B), about 0.375 W idle at ±15 V. A dual adapter carries two chips.
- The thermal pad connection is ambiguous. The current datasheet's layout text says the PowerPAD is electrically isolated, recommends ground and allows VS− to VS+. Rev C's pin table says the pad is internally connected to V−. Tying the pad to V− satisfies both readings.
- SMD only, so socket use needs an adapter. It is a single, so it replaces a dual only on a dual-conversion board.
- Noise is specified only above 10 kHz, and there is no audio-band THD+N spec.
- Parts shipped after the March 2025 datasheet (Rev C) may be re-characterised silicon. The listening impressions (c. 2018–2020) predate this.
- **Counterfeits:** No THS4631-specific counterfeit reports are known; this pass did not search for them specifically. Genuine markings are '4631' on SOIC-8 (D) and HSOIC-8 PowerPAD (DDA), and 'ADK' on HVSSOP-8 PowerPAD (DGN). A DDA part must have an exposed pad underneath. The THS4631D and THS4631DGN tube SKUs have been obsolete since PDN20230907001 (Sep 2023), so 'new' stock under those part numbers from brokers is old or suspect. Hobbyists usually buy the part pre-mounted on DIP adapters (e.g. Shirokuma) or as bare DDA parts (e.g. eleshop.jp). Prefer authorized distributors. The marking cannot tell pre- and post-2025 silicon apart.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Texas Instruments, Published in SLOS451C (March 2025). Rev B (Aug 2011) was still current in 2019, and TI's PSpice model dated 2020-11-02 still uses Rev B Iq and Isc values. No PCN was located, and the physical change date is unknown. The Sep 2023 PDN20230907001 discontinued only the D and DGN tube SKUs; that is a SKU action, not evidence of a die change.

Rev C re-characterises the THS4631 on 'new silicon data', and on E2E TI says the performance changes come from a new manufacturing process. TI recommends omitting the feedback capacitor in new designs that use the new silicon. Output current rises sharply (98 to 180 mA typ), quiescent current rises (11.5 to 12.5 mA typ; 13 to 14.5 mA max), and 0.1 dB flatness with CF = 8.2 pF is re-specified from 38 to 6 MHz. The new pin table ties the PowerPAD to V-, while the layout text still says isolated. Headline specs are unchanged: 7 nV/rtHz, 210 MHz GBW, 1000 V/us, +/-500 uV Vos max, 100 pA Ib max. Whether the die was also redesigned is not stated.

- **Affected:** THS4631DDA, THS4631DR, THS4631DGNR
- **How to tell:** By datasheet only. SLOS451C (Rev C, March 2025) says 'Updated graphs with new silicon data' and carries new limits; SLOS451B (Aug 2011) describes the legacy part. TI E2E thread 1514297 ('THS4631: Revision C - Relevant to all IC's or only to newly ordered ones') attributes the changes to a new manufacturing process. The top marking is unchanged ('4631' on D/DDA, 'ADK' on DGN in both the 2017 and 2025 addenda), and no date-code cutoff or PCN was found. The '.A'/'.B' orderable suffixes are TI-wide and are not evidence of a die change.
- **Audio impact:** No listening or measurement comparison of old and new lots was found. Expect more idle heat, stronger static drive and a different response with feedback capacitance. The part is already oscillation-prone in I/V stages, so Zobel and CF values tuned on old parts may need re-checking; TI now advises leaving out CF in new designs. The Japanese impressions (c. 2018-2020) predate Rev C.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: pinout, package and headline AC specs are unchanged. But higher Iq, a changed compensated-loop response and the ambiguous pad potential can upset marginal thermal or stability designs, or adapters that ground the pad. Tie the pad to V-.
- **Confidence:** medium

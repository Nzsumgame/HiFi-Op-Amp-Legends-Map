# THS4631 (single high-voltage, high-slew-rate, wideband JFET-input op amp)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*A ±15 V JFET-input op amp with 210 MHz GBW and 1000 V/µs slew. It is a cult DAC I/V and buffer part in Japan, praised as airy and transparent once it is stable.*

**Technology:** TI's JFET-input high-speed voltage-feedback op amp; the datasheet calls the input stage JFET. Single channel, unity-gain stable: 325 MHz bandwidth at G=1, 210 MHz GBW. Runs on ±5 to ±15 V (10–30 V single supply). SMD only: SOIC-8 (D), HSOIC-8 PowerPAD (DDA; 'SOIC-PP' in Rev B) and HVSSOP-8 PowerPAD (DGN; 'MSOP-PP' in Rev B). Standard single-op-amp pinout: 2 = IN−, 3 = IN+, 4 = V−, 6 = OUT, 7 = V+; 1, 5 and 8 are NC. TI's documents disagree about the thermal pad. Rev B, and the layout text of the current Rev C, say the PowerPAD is 'electrically isolated from all pins and the active circuitry', recommend grounding it, and allow any potential from VS− to VS+. The new Pin Functions table in Rev C says the pad is 'internally connected to V−'.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Unclassified).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche cult part in the Japanese DIY and op-amp-rolling scene. It is mostly used for I/V after current-output DACs, and in headphone amps and buffers via DIP-8 conversion boards. One Ameblo blog title calls it 'the rumoured op-amp' (噂のオペアンプ). Shirokuma sells it pre-mounted on a dual 8-pin DIP board. Users report extended highs, air and transparency when it is not oscillating. A Zigsow review calls it a 'tsundere honours-student character' (ツンデレ優等生キャラ). TI's datasheet lists 'Current DAC output buffer' as an application. No Audio Science Review or other audio-band measurements are known to this record.

**How people describe the sound:** airy, transparent, extended highs, expressive, 'tsundere honours student' (ツンデレ優等生): excellent once stable

**Typical uses:** I/V conversion after current-output DACs, DIP-8 op-amp rolling via conversion boards (e.g. Shirokuma dual board), Headphone amp op-amp swap (e.g. Elekit TU-HP01, per the xgender-isao blog), High-impedance wideband buffer / line driver

**Caveats:**

- Oscillates easily in rolling and adapter use. Japanese users report needing an output Zobel network. TI's datasheet warns that large feedback resistors oscillate with the input capacitance and recommends a feedback capacitor, which matters for I/V stages.
- Runs hot: 12.5 mA typ and 14.5 mA max quiescent current per channel in Rev C (11.5 typ / 13 max in Rev B), about 0.375 W idle at ±15 V. A dual adapter carries two chips.
- The thermal pad connection is ambiguous. The current datasheet's layout text says the PowerPAD is electrically isolated, recommends ground and allows VS− to VS+. Rev C's pin table says the pad is internally connected to V−. Tying the pad to V− satisfies both readings.
- SMD only, so socket use needs an adapter. It is a single, so it replaces a dual only on a dual-conversion board.
- Noise is specified only above 10 kHz, and there is no audio-band THD+N spec.
- Parts shipped after the March 2025 datasheet (Rev C) may be re-characterised silicon. The listening impressions (c. 2018–2020) predate this.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Unclassified: Texas Instruments, Published in SLOS451C (March 2025).

Rev C re-characterises the THS4631 on 'new silicon data'. Output current rises sharply (98 to 180 mA typ), quiescent current rises (11.5 to 12.5 mA typ; 13 to 14.5 mA max), and 0.1 dB flatness with CF=8.2 pF is re-specified from 38 to 6 MHz. The new pin table ties the PowerPAD to V−, while the layout text still says isolated. Headline specs are unchanged: 7 nV/√Hz, 210 MHz GBW, 1000 V/µs, ±500 µV Vos max, 100 pA Ib max. A die, fab or process change is an inference; TI does not state it.

- **When:** Published in SLOS451C (March 2025). Rev B (Aug 2011) was still current in 2019, and TI's PSpice model dated 2020-11-02 still uses the Rev B Iq and Isc values. No PCN was located, and the physical change date is unknown. The Sep-2023 PDN20230907001 discontinued only the D and DGN tube SKUs; it is a SKU action, not evidence of a die change.
- **Affected:** THS4631DDA, THS4631DR, THS4631DGNR
- **How to tell old from new:** By datasheet only. SLOS451C (Rev C, March 2025) says 'Updated graphs with new silicon data' and carries new limits; SLOS451B (Aug 2011) describes the legacy part. The top marking is unchanged ('4631' on D/DDA, 'ADK' on DGN in both the 2017 and 2025 addenda), and no date-code cutoff or PCN was found. The '.A'/'.B' orderable suffixes are TI-wide and not evidence of a die change.
- **Audio impact:** No listening or measurement comparison of old and new lots was found. Expect more idle heat, stronger static drive and a different response with feedback capacitance. The part is already oscillation-prone in I/V stages, so Zobel and CF values tuned on old parts may need re-checking. The Japanese impressions (c. 2018–2020) predate Rev C.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: pinout, package and headline AC specs are unchanged. But higher Iq, a changed compensated-loop response and the ambiguous pad potential can upset marginal thermal or stability designs, or adapters that ground the pad. Tie the pad to V−.
- **Confidence:** medium

## Counterfeits

No THS4631-specific counterfeit reports are known; this pass did not search for them specifically. Genuine markings are '4631' on SOIC-8 (D) and HSOIC-8 PowerPAD (DDA), and 'ADK' on HVSSOP-8 PowerPAD (DGN). A DDA part must have an exposed pad underneath. The THS4631D and THS4631DGN tube SKUs have been obsolete since PDN20230907001 (Sep 2023), so 'new' stock under those part numbers from brokers is old or suspect. Hobbyists usually buy the part pre-mounted on DIP adapters (e.g. Shirokuma) or as bare DDA parts (e.g. eleshop.jp). Prefer authorized distributors. The marking cannot tell pre- and post-2025 silicon apart.

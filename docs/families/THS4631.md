# THS4631

**JFET-input wideband op-amp, single, ±15 V** · Texas Instruments (2004–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** Rev C re-characterises the part on 'new silicon data'; TI (E2E) cites a new process, advising no CF in new designs. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*A ±15 V JFET-input op-amp with 210 MHz GBW and 1000 V/µs; a Japanese cult I/V part, airy and transparent once stable.*

## Why it's rated

A niche cult part in Japan's DIY scene, mostly for I/V after current-output DACs and in headphone amps via DIP-8 adapters. An Ameblo blog calls it 'the rumoured op-amp' (噂のオペアンプ); Shirokuma sells it pre-mounted. Users report extended highs, air and transparency when it is not oscillating. TI lists 'current DAC output buffer' as an application. No audio-band measurements known.

**Described as:** airy, transparent, extended highs, expressive, tsundere honours student (ツンデレ)

**Used in:** I/V conversion after current-output DACs, DIP-8 rolling via conversion boards (Shirokuma dual board), Headphone amp swap (Elekit TU-HP01, per xgender-isao blog), High-impedance wideband buffer / line driver

## Watch out

- Oscillates easily on adapters; users add an output Zobel. TI warns large feedback resistors oscillate; use a feedback capacitor.
- Runs hot: 12.5 mA typ per channel (Rev C), about 0.375 W idle at ±15 V; dual adapters carry two.
- Thermal pad ambiguous: layout text says isolated (ground it), Rev C pin table says V−. Tie it to V−.
- SMD single only; needs an adapter, dual-conversion board for dual sockets. Noise specified only above 10 kHz.
- Parts shipped after Rev C (March 2025) may be new silicon; listening reports (c. 2018–2020) predate it.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific reports. Genuine marks: '4631' on D/DDA, 'ADK' on DGN; DDA has an exposed pad. 'New' THS4631D/DGN tube stock (obsolete since 2023) is suspect. Buy authorized or Shirokuma/eleshop.jp boards. Markings cannot distinguish pre/post-2025 silicon.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Texas Instruments, 2025-03 · SLOS451C (no PCN found)

Rev C re-characterises the part on 'new silicon data'; TI (E2E) cites a new process, advising no CF in new designs. Output current, Iq, flatness changed; headlines (7 nV/√Hz, 210 MHz, 1000 V/µs, ±500 µV, 100 pA) did not. Rev B was current in 2019; TI's 2020-11-02 model used it.

- **Affected:** THS4631DDA, THS4631DR, THS4631DGNR
- **How to tell:** Datasheet only: SLOS451C (Mar 2025) vs SLOS451B (Aug 2011). Markings unchanged ('4631', 'ADK'); no date-code cutoff. PDN20230907001 (Sep 2023) only dropped tube SKUs; '.A'/'.B' suffixes prove nothing.
- **Audio impact:** Untested by listeners. Expect more idle heat, stronger drive and different response with CF; recheck Zobel and CF values tuned on old parts.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: same pinout and AC specs, but higher Iq, changed loop response, pad potential; tie pad to V−.
- **Confidence:** medium

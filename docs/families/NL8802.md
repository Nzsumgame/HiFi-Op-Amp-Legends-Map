# NL8802

**Bipolar-input audio op-amp, dual** · Nisshinbo Micro Devices (2023–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Nisshinbo's 2023 entry-level MUSES-derived bipolar dual: 5.5 nV/√Hz, 45 MHz, ±22 V. Japanese hobbyists rate it highly.*

## Why it's rated

A niche Japanese part with a small hobbyist following, marketed as the entry-level MUSES-technology model. Blogger new_western_elec (2025) preferred MUSES8820 for loud rock and NL8802 for recent anime music. One Japanese ranking places it above MUSES8820 and MUSES8920, below NL8902. No objective third-party measurements are known.

**Described as:** suits recent anime/pop, clearer bass (unconfirmed), ranked above MUSES8820

**Used in:** Audio preamplifiers, active filters, line amplifiers, Home, pro, car and portable audio, Swaps for MUSES8820 / NJM4580 duals via SOP-DIP adapter

## Watch out

- Praise rests on a few Japanese listening tests; one verdict depended on genre.
- SMD only (EMP-8; DFN3030-8 announced). Stability of a 45 MHz part on adapters is unconfirmed.
- Only datasheet Ver. 1.0 seen; output drive, capacitive-load stability and full THD+N conditions unchecked.
- Attribution of the hobbyist ranking (nw-electric or ns-kobo) is unclear.
- **Counterfeits:** No counterfeits known. It is recent and SMD only, so any 'NL8802 DIP-8' listing is suspect. Buy from authorised distributors (Mouser, Profusion, Japanese shops) and check the top marking against the datasheet.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

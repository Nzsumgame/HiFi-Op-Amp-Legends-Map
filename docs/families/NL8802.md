# NL8802 (Nisshinbo bipolar-input dual audio op-amp, MUSES-derived)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*A 2025 Nisshinbo bipolar dual that puts MUSES sound technology on a cheaper, higher-volume process; one Japanese listening test preferred it to the MUSES8820.*

**Technology:** Bipolar-input dual op-amp. Per the discovery notes, a productised audio op-amp that applies Nisshinbo/JRC MUSES technology to a more productive process. Die, process and compensation details were not verified in this pass.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche new Japanese part, known almost entirely from one blogger. new_western_elec (nw-electric.way-nifty.com) ran a listening comparison in September 2025. It preferred NL8802 to MUSES8820D, hearing clearer bass definition and more extension at both frequency extremes. The overall ranking was OPA2604 < MUSES8820 < MUSES8920 < NL8802 < NL8902, with the JFET sibling NL8902 on top. The ns-kobo op-amp comparison page also lists it. No objective measurements were found in this pass (Audio Science Review, Samuel Groner, THD+N sweeps).

**How people describe the sound:** clearer bass definition than MUSES8820D, more extension at both frequency extremes, ranked above MUSES8920 but below NL8902 (new_western_elec, 2025)

**Typical uses:** Socketed op-amp swapping in dual-op-amp audio positions currently fitted with MUSES8820 / NJM4580-class parts (community comparison context), DAC and headphone-amp op-amp comparisons by Japanese DIY bloggers

**Caveats:**

- Very new (2025): little independent listening or measurement data. The praise rests mainly on a single blogger's subjective test.
- No datasheet was read in this pass. Supply range, output drive, quiescent current and stability with capacitive loads or in adapter sockets are unverified.
- The package or packages were not confirmed, so check that a DIP-8 version exists before planning a socket swap.

## Counterfeits

No NL8802 counterfeits or remarked parts were found; it is a new 2025 part. Its MUSES relatives, especially MUSES8820, are widely faked in cheap marketplace listings, so the same risk should be expected once NL8802 becomes popular. Buy from authorised Japanese distributors (for example Marutsu eleshop, https://eleshop.jp/shop/g/gOBU121/, which the discovery lens linked to NL8802) or major global distributors. Check the top marking against the datasheet's marking specification.

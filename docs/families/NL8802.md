# NL8802 (Nisshinbo bipolar dual audio op-amp, entry-level MUSES-derived)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Japanese audio series (New JRC / Nisshinbo)

*Nisshinbo's Nov 2023 entry-level MUSES-derived bipolar dual (5.5 nV/√Hz, 45 MHz, ±22 V). Japanese hobbyist listening tests rank it with or above MUSES8820.*

**Technology:** Bipolar-input dual op-amp on a bipolar process (vendor: 'bipolar input and bipolar technology'). Nisshinbo says it applies the 'MUSES' high-quality-sound technology to a more productive process and calls it the entry-level model that carries MUSES technology. Die, topology and compensation are not disclosed.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche Japanese part with a small hobbyist following. Nisshinbo markets it as the entry-level model carrying MUSES sound technology, balancing sound, performance and cost. The blogger new_western_elec compared it with MUSES8820 in September 2025 ('MUSES8820とNL8802、MUSES8920とNL8902を聴く'). Per the search summary the verdict depended on genre: MUSES8820 for loud rock, NL8802 for recent anime music. A ranking of OPA2604 < MUSES8820 < MUSES8920 < NL8802 < NL8902 appears in the Japanese comparisons found, but it is unclear whether it comes from nw-electric or from ns-kobo's NS phonoEQ 2 op-amp comparison. No objective third-party measurements were found (Audio Science Review or similar).

**How people describe the sound:** suited to recent anime/pop more than MUSES8820, which suits loud rock (new_western_elec, 2025), ranked above MUSES8820 and MUSES8920 but below NL8902 in one Japanese hobbyist ranking (attribution unclear), clearer bass definition than MUSES8820D (earlier draft claim; unconfirmed)

**Typical uses:** Audio preamplifiers, active filters and line amplifiers (vendor), Home, professional, car and portable audio (vendor target markets), Hobbyist op-amp swaps in place of MUSES8820 / NJM4580-class duals, needing an SOP-to-DIP adapter because no DIP-8 version was seen

**Caveats:**

- The praise rests on a few Japanese hobbyist listening tests, and one of them gave a genre-dependent verdict rather than a clear win over MUSES8820.
- The package is SMD only (EMP-8; DFN3030-8 announced). Socket swaps need adapter boards, and stability of a 45 MHz GBW part on adapters is unverified.
- Only a Ver.1.0 datasheet (NL8802_S_E, Mouser mirror) was seen. Output drive, capacitive-load stability and full THD+N conditions were not checked in this pass.

## Counterfeits

No NL8802 counterfeits or remarked parts were found. The part is recent (2023) and SMD only, so the DIP-8 fakes that plague MUSES8820 / MUSES8920 do not apply directly. Any 'NL8802 DIP-8' listing should be treated as suspect, because Nisshinbo lists only EMP-8-AN and DFN3030-8-GQ. Buy from authorised distributors (Mouser, Profusion, Japanese authorised shops) and check the top marking against the datasheet.

**Corrections applied:**

- Tagline, lineage period and status changed from 2025 to a Nov 2023 launch.

The ranking OPA2604 < ... < NL8902 is found in the Japanese comparisons, but its attribution to nw-electric is unclear (possibly ns-kobo). The 'clearer bass than MUSES8820D' descriptor is marked unconfirmed.

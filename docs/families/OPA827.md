# OPA827 / OPA827AID

**JFET-input precision single, low-noise** · Burr-Brown (c. 1989–) → Texas Instruments (2006–present) → Texas Instruments (2018–present) · Tier B (widely praised)

*TI's 2006 JFET single (4 nV/√Hz, 22 MHz) that DIYers treat as the modern OPA627, a DAC I/V favourite.*

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Why it's rated

DIYers treat it as the cheaper modern OPA627, mostly as a DAC I/V converter and in socket-rolled stages on adapters. Xonar Essence threads rank it with LT1361, LT1028 and OPA627 for I/V. Impressions conflict: neutral, clean and more dynamic than OPA627, or dark, warm and bass-centric (diyAudio). Zigsow reviews call it 'Successor to the legendary stone'. No ASR measurement found.

**Described as:** almost neutral, clean, clear, fluid, more dynamic than OPA627, bass-centric, dark, warm, impressive soundstage, transparent low-mids (Zigsow), assertive low end

**Used in:** DAC I/V stage (Asus Xonar Essence ST/STX), Portable DAC/amp swaps on DIP-8 adapters (Fostex HP-A4), Line-level buffers and preamps, 16–18-bit ADC drivers, transimpedance amps, professional audio

## Watch out

- SMD single only; DIP-8 sockets need an adapter, dual sockets two parts on a 2-in-1 board.
- Not rail-to-rail: input and output reach only within 3 V of each rail (about ±2 V on ±5 V).
- Needs at least ±4 V and draws 4.8 mA; keep decoupling at the chip on adapters.
- DC precision: non-symmetric INL as ADC buffer (PatrickBaus thesis); higher near-DC 1/f noise than OPA227.
- JFET bias rises steeply with heat (±5 nA at 125 °C). Costs more than common audio duals.
- **Counterfeits:** No OPA827-specific fake reports. Genuine marks: SOIC 'OPA / 827 / A', VSSOP 'NSP'. TI never made DIP-8 or shipped OPA2827, so both are suspect. Buy from authorised distributors; check Iq (4.8 mA) and slew (≥20 V/µs).

## Silicon changes

### Datasheet respec: Texas Instruments, 2012-02 · SBOS376G (Rev F was 2009-03)

Rev G moved from Mixed Status to Production Data, deleted the planned high grade, revised bias and drift text, set offset/drift/bias limits, and added SR and short-circuit minimums. Likely final characterisation; datasheet change only, no PCN or die change found.

- **Affected:** OPA827AID, OPA827AIDR, OPA827AIDGKR, OPA827AIDGKT
- **How to tell:** None on the part. Pre-2012 parts were sold against Rev F (Mixed Status) and earlier specs.
- **Audio impact:** Negligible for audio; matters only for DC-precision designs relying on preview limits or the dropped high grade.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same pinout and packages; no known die change.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Product status | Mixed Status (preview grade included) | Production Data |
| High-grade ordering option | listed in Package/Ordering table (Rev F) | deleted (Rev G) |
| Input offset voltage / drift / Ib limits | Rev F values (not retrieved); Features bullets differed | Rev G+: 150 uV max, 2.0 uV/°C max, ±10 pA max at 25 °C |
| Slew rate minimum | not specified | 20 V/us |
| Short-circuit current minimum | not specified | ±55 mA |

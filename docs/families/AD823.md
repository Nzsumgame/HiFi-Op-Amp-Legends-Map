## Why it's rated

A 2000s favourite for battery CMoy portables. Tangent found it punchier and more detailed than the OPA134/132, ideal on 3 V, but too aggressive with forward headphones and short on current. Soomal cites a warm, dynamic reputation and found it in early Graham Slee SOLO amps and the Colorfly C4 Pro and Hidizs AP100 DAPs. No ASR or Groner measurements.

**Described as:** more bass impact, more detailed, aggressive / forward (冲), warm (偏暖), good dynamics, emotional (Head-Fi, unverified)

**Used in:** Battery CMoy / portable headphone amps on 3 V, Graham Slee SOLO (earlier versions, per Soomal), DAPs: Colorfly C4 Pro; Hidizs AP100 (role unconfirmed), Op-amp rolling in DIY and commercial DAC/amps, ADI 3 V headphone circuit (−62 dB THD+N, 32 Ω)

## Watch out

- Low output current (15–17 mA, 0.5 V from rails); weak into low-impedance headphones without a buffer.
- Offset much higher on ±15 V (3.5 mV max vs 0.8 mV at 5 V).
- Inputs beyond +VS by 300 mV or with supplies off need a series resistor (1 kΩ withstands 10 V).
- About 20° phase margin into 470 pF as follower; add a series output resistor. Only AD823ANZ comes in DIP.
- Post-2026 stock comes from Camas fab; not guaranteed identical silicon. Reputation is mostly subjective.
- **Counterfeits:** No AD823 counterfeit reports found. Inferences: recent-date-code 'AD823AN' DIPs deserve scrutiny; an 'AD823A' DIP is mislabelled or fake; check full order code (AD823ARZ vs AD823AARZ) and datasheet (D00901 vs D09439). Post-2026 Camas parts may differ slightly yet be genuine.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Analog Devices, 2026 · PCN 26_0029 Rev. -

PCN 26_0029, 'Qualification of ADI Camas Wafer Fab CB Processes', moves Complementary Bipolar production to the existing qualified Camas, WA process, with 'no expected impact to fit, form, function or reliability'. AD823ANZ and AD823ARZ are listed. Same part numbers; no die-layout change stated. Issue and implementation dates unconfirmed.

- **Affected:** AD823ANZ, AD823ARZ
- **How to tell:** Not confirmed; ADI usually separates stock by date code or lot. Part number, marking and datasheet (Rev. E) are unchanged.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown; datasheet limits unchanged and no comparisons exist. Record date codes if you prize older AD823ANZ stock.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: no fit/form/function impact stated, datasheet unchanged; matters mainly for matched pairs.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wafer fab | previous ADI fab (not named in the sources seen) | ADI Camas, WA fab (existing qualified CB process) |

B 6/12: ADI introduced a materially different die under a near-identical name, alongside rather than replacing the original.

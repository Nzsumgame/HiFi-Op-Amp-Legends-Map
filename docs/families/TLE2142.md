# TLE2141 / TLE2142 / TLE2144

**Complementary-bipolar low-noise op-amp, 1/2/4 channels** · Texas Instruments (early 1990s–present) → Texas Instruments (2025–) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** TI added RFAB (Richardson, TIB process) as a fab alongside DL-LIN (Dallas): die revision B to C, datasheet SLOS183D to SLOS183E, new assembly BOM options. [Details](#silicon-changes)

*TI Excalibur part: 10.5 nV/√Hz, 5.9 MHz, up to ±22 V, drives 10 nF; found in some high-end DAC output stages.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10.5 nV/√Hz (15 nV/√Hz at 10 Hz) | 5.9 MHz | 27 V/µs min | ±2 V to ±22 V |

## Why it's rated

Niche and lightly discussed. A Head-Fi poster says a 4300-euro Acoustic Arts DAC uses eight TLE2142s in its output stage (unverified). One user liked its tonality, body and bass; another preferred the LM4562. There is no broad consensus.

**Described as:** good tonality, body, good bass

**Used in:** DAC output stages (Acoustic Arts DAC, reportedly), Op-amp rolling in headphone amps and DACs, High-rail line stages up to ±22 V

## Watch out

- Praise is sparse and mixed; one Head-Fi user preferred the LM4562.
- Parts from late October 2025 may be die rev C (RFAB); no comparisons with die B yet.
- The Acoustic Arts DAC claim is second-hand from a forum post.
- **Counterfeits:** No specific counterfeit reports. Buy from authorized distributors, as with other TI DIP op-amps sold on marketplaces. Genuine stock from late October 2025 on may be die revision C from RFAB.

## Silicon changes

### Fab / process transfer: Texas Instruments, 2025-07-31 · PCN20250730004.1

TI added RFAB (Richardson, TIB process) as a fab alongside DL-LIN (Dallas): die revision B to C, datasheet SLOS183D to SLOS183E, new assembly BOM options. Part of TI's move off 200 mm fabs. First ship 29 Oct 2025. TI expects no form, fit, function or reliability impact.

- **Affected:** TLE2141 (confirmed in PCN listing), TLE2142 (likely; same SLOS183 datasheet, not explicitly confirmed), TLE2144 (likely; not explicitly confirmed)
- **How to tell:** Die rev C and RFAB fab codes on reel/box labels; date codes after late October 2025; datasheet SLOS183E. Dallas die-B stock may ship in parallel.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. A new die on a different process could change noise, distortion or stability. No measurements or listening comparisons found.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: new die and process under same part number; SLOS183E tables not yet compared with rev D.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Die revision | B | C |
| Wafer fab | DL-LIN (Dallas) | DL-LIN or RFAB (Richardson, TIB process) |
| Datasheet | SLOS183D (Oct 2012) | SLOS183E (Jul 2025) |

# TLE2141 / TLE2142 / TLE2144 (TI Excalibur low-noise high-speed bipolar)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** Modern low-noise / ultra-low-distortion bipolar

*TI Excalibur complementary-bipolar amp: 10.5 nV/rtHz, 5.9 MHz, runs on up to ±22 V and drives 10 nF loads. A niche pick found in some high-end DAC output stages.*

**Technology:** Complementary bipolar (TI Excalibur process), internally compensated; single (TLE2141), dual (TLE2142), quad (TLE2144); A-suffix = tighter-precision grade

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (1 recorded: Fab / process transfer).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

A niche, lightly discussed part among hi-fi hobbyists. In Head-Fi op-amp rolling threads, one poster says a 4300-euro Acoustic Arts DAC uses eight TLE2142s in its output stage. One user liked its tonality, body and bass. Another was not impressed compared with the LM4562. There is no broad consensus.

**How people describe the sound:** good tonality (one Head-Fi user), body (one Head-Fi user), good bass (one Head-Fi user)

**Typical uses:** DAC output stages (per a Head-Fi report on an Acoustic Arts DAC), op-amp rolling in headphone amps / DACs, high-rail (up to ±22 V) line stages

**Caveats:**

- Praise is sparse and mixed: one Head-Fi user preferred the LM4562.
- Parts made from late October 2025 may be die revision C (RFAB, TIB process). No listening or measurement comparisons with older die B stock have been found.
- The Acoustic Arts DAC claim is second-hand from a forum post and has not been independently verified.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Fab / process transfer: Texas Instruments, PCN20250730004.1 issued 31 Jul 2025; proposed first ship…

TI qualified RFAB (Richardson) using TIB process technology as an extra fab alongside DL-LIN (Dallas), with a die revision from B to C, a datasheet update from SLOS183D to SLOS183E, and new assembly BOM options. This is part of TI's multiyear move off 200 mm factories. TI states there is no anticipated impact on form, fit, function, quality or reliability.

- **When:** PCN20250730004.1 issued 31 Jul 2025; proposed first ship 29 Oct 2025
- **Affected:** TLE2141 (confirmed in PCN listing), TLE2142 (likely; same SLOS183 datasheet, not explicitly confirmed), TLE2144 (likely; not explicitly confirmed)
- **How to tell old from new:** Die revision C (was B) and RFAB fab-site codes on reel/box labels. Date codes after late October 2025. Datasheet SLOS183E instead of SLOS183D. Existing DL-LIN (Dallas) die-B material may ship in parallel.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. A new die on a different process under the same part number could change noise, distortion or stability details. No measurements or listening comparisons found.
- **Drop-in risk:** medium: new die and process under an unchanged part number. TI claims no form/fit/function change, but the SLOS183E spec tables have not been compared with rev D.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Die revision | B | C |
| Wafer fab | DL-LIN (Dallas) | DL-LIN or RFAB (Richardson, TIB process) |
| Datasheet | SLOS183D (Oct 2012) | SLOS183E (Jul 2025) |

### Revision chain status

TI is the only vendor found. No second sources or acquirer re-issues turned up for TLE2141/2142/2144.  Revision chain, oldest to newest: (1) Pre-1997 per-device datasheets. The dual sheet is 'TLE2142, TLE2142A, TLE2142Y EXCALIBUR LOW-NOISE HIGH-SPEED PRECISION OPERATIONAL AMPLIFIER' (icbase TTI20590106.pdf, already listed). Singular-title copies that probably belong to this generation are datasheet4u/datasheetspdf 1442138 (TLE2142) and 1442136 (TLE2141), and semiconductors.es. Their literature number(s) and dates are still MISSING, as are any separate TLE2141 and TLE2144 standalone lit numbers. (2) SLOS183 original, February 1997: the combined TLE214x/TLE214xA sheet. No copy has been identified as holding it. (3) SLOS183A, B and C: dates and copies still MISSING. None of the 4 searches surfaced these letters. Likely holders are older aggregator scrapes (digchip, datasheetarchive, alldatasheet 28934 TLE214X / 228137 / 201893), but none showed a header. (4) SLOS183D, 'FEBRUARY 1997 - REVISED OCTOBER 2012': confirmed again from the header in the search summary (radiolocman di=330795). (5) SLOS183E, July 2025: new-format sheet on ti.com, tied to PCN20250730004 (RFAB fab, die B to C).  A possible text change between the old per-device edition and later SLOS183 text was seen: 'patent-pending' input stage with a 6-Hz 1/f corner versus 10-Hz. It needs checking. The derivative datasheets (TLE2141-EP, TLE2142-EP, TLE2144-EP, TLE2142-Q1) have unknown lit numbers and revision chains.  The archive seed URLs follow TI's standard legacy URL patterns (focus.ti.com symlink, and a slos183d revision-specific path modelled on the confirmed slos183e path). They were NOT seen in search results and should be treated as candidate Wayback lookups only.

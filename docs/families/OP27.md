# OP27 / OP37 (PMI/ADI low-noise precision bipolar; OP37 = decompensated, gain ≥5)

Precision Monolithics Inc. (PMI) (c.1980 to c.1990) → Analog Devices (c.1990 to present) · second sources: Linear Technology, Texas Instruments · Tier B (widely praised)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** TI makes its own OP27A/OP27C (SLOS100, Rev. (+1 more) [Details](#silicon-changes)

*PMI's c.1980 3 nV/√Hz zener-trimmed precision bipolar op-amp, long used in phono, tape-head and mic preamps; ADI's datasheet still carries those audio circuits.*

| GBW | Slew rate | Supply range |
|---|---|---|
| OP27: 8 MHz typ (5 min). OP37: 63 MHz typ (45 min) @10 kHz, 40 MHz typ @1 MHz | OP27: 2.8 V/µs typ (1.7 min). OP37: 17 V/µs typ (11 min) | PSRR specified from ±4 V to ±18 V. Absolute max ±22 V |

## Why it's rated

The reputation is mostly pro-audio and engineering, not 'op-amp rolling'. PMI and ADI pitched the OP27/OP37 at professional audio, with ±10 V into 600 Ω and application circuits for RIAA phono, NAB tape-head and mic preamps. A 1990s hobbyist audio FAQ says 'The OP-27, OP-37, LT1028, and LT1115 are very well received for phono preamps, head amplifiers, and microphone preamplifiers'. Douglas Self's 'The Design of Active Crossovers' (2nd ed.) has a section titled 'The OP27 Opamp'; its content was not read. The diyAudio 'a classic' quotes come from discovery notes and were not re-checked by web search. No ASR or Groner measurements were verified.

**Described as:** 'a classic' (diyAudio, via discovery notes; unverified), 'nice forgotten opamp' for phono (diyAudio, via discovery notes; unverified), 'very well received' for phono, head-amp and mic preamps (1990s Atari ST diskmag audio FAQ)

**Used in:** MM phono RIAA preamps (datasheet Figure 41), Microphone preamps, transformerless (OP37) or transformer-coupled (OP27), Tape-head (NAB) playback preamps, Low-noise gain stages from low source impedance (<1–3 kΩ), Precision instrumentation and ADC drivers

## Watch out

- OP37 is decompensated and stable only at gain ≥5. An unplugged mic input (100% feedback) can oscillate.
- Bias-current cancellation raises current noise, so the noise advantage disappears with high source resistance. The OP07 is better above about 3 kΩ at low frequencies.
- OP27 slew rate is low (2.8 V/µs), and the 8 MHz GBW causes RIAA errors at gains above 40 dB (datasheet).
- Unprotected input diodes conduct above 0.7 V differential. Use Rf ≥ 500 Ω in fast unity-gain buffers.
- Best linearity with peak output current below ±10 mA (OP37 datasheet).
- Single channel only; about 3 mA per amplifier.
- Same part number from ADI (PMI line), ADI/LTC (OP27-LTC) and TI, with different grade sets. Specs and sound are not guaranteed identical across vendors.
- **Counterfeits:** No OP27/OP37-specific counterfeit reports were verified. Several legitimate logos exist (PMI, ADI, LTC, TI), so a non-ADI logo alone does not mean a fake. Don't confuse OP27/OP37 with the Burr-Brown/TI OPA27/OPA37, which are different parts. Treat cheap marketplace A-grade CERDIP or TO-99 parts with suspicion. A quick check: about 3 mA supply current at ±15 V and a noise floor near 3 nV/√Hz with low source impedance.

## Silicon changes

### Second-source difference: Texas Instruments (second source) vs PMI/ADI, from Feb 1989 (SLOS100) to at least 2010 (Rev. E)

TI makes its own OP27A/OP27C (SLOS100, Rev. E Feb 2010) and an OP37 under the same generic numbers as PMI/ADI. No document says whether TI's die is its own design or licensed. The headline noise specs match ADI's (3 nV/√Hz, 2.7 Hz 1/f corner). TI's OP27C (100 µV max Vos) is not the same grade as the discontinued PMI OP27C.

- **Affected:** OP27A (TI), OP27C (TI), OP37G (TI)
- **How to tell:** TI logo and TI ordering codes; the TI data sheet is SLOS100. TI's OP27 grades are A and C only, while ADI's current grades are A/E/G.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. No measurement comparisons were found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - same pinout, sockets and headline specs; any differences would be in unspecified parameters.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Grade set | PMI/ADI: A/E/G (older B/C/F) | TI: A/C |
| Vos max, lower grade | ADI OP27G: 100 µV (Rev F) | TI OP27C: 100 µV |

### Second-source difference: Linear Technology (now ADI 'OP27-LTC') vs PMI/ADI, late 1980s onward; ADI lists both lines since the 2017 LTC merger

Two OP27 product lines now sit under ADI: the PMI-heritage OP27 and the Linear Technology OP27 (op27-ltc.html). The LTC data sheets are mirrored on alldatasheet. Whether the LTC die is an independent design is unconfirmed, and the shared LTC macromodel template is not evidence either way.

- **Affected:** OP-27A/C/E/G (LTC), OP-37A/C/E/G (LTC), OP-227/OP-237 (LTC)
- **How to tell:** LTC logo; ADI's separate 'OP27-LTC' product page; LTC's A/C/E/G grade set, versus A/E/G on the PMI-heritage ADI line.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. No measurements found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low - pin-compatible and specified to the same OP-27/OP-37 grade names; unspecified parameters may differ.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Grade set | PMI/ADI: A/E/G | LTC: A/C/E/G |

# OP27 / OP37

**Low-noise precision bipolar single; OP37 decompensated** · Precision Monolithics (PMI) (c. 1980–1990) → Analog Devices (c. 1990–present) · second sources: Linear Technology, Texas Instruments · Tier B (widely praised)

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon:** TI makes its own OP27A/OP27C (SLOS100, Rev. E Feb 2010) and an OP37 under PMI/ADI's numbers. (+1 more) [Details](#silicon-changes)

*PMI's c. 1980 3 nV/√Hz zener-trimmed precision op-amp, long the pro-audio choice for phono, tape-head and mic preamps.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.0 nV/√Hz typ, 3.8 max (A/E); 3.2 / 4.5 (G) | OP27: 8 MHz (5 min); OP37: 63 MHz (45 min) | OP27: 2.8 V/µs (1.7 min); OP37: 17 V/µs (11 min) | Not specified; RIAA preamp <0.01% up to 7 V rms | PSRR specified ±4 V to ±18 V; absolute max ±22 V | ≈3 mA typ (Pd 90 mW typ / 140 max, A/E) |

## Why it's rated

A pro-audio and engineering classic rather than a rolling favourite. PMI and ADI aimed it at professional audio (±10 V into 600 Ω) with RIAA, NAB tape-head and mic preamp circuits. A 1990s audio FAQ calls it 'very well received' for phono, head-amp and mic preamps; Douglas Self covers it in 'The Design of Active Crossovers'. No ASR measurements verified.

**Described as:** 'a classic' (diyAudio), 'nice forgotten opamp' (diyAudio), 'very well received'

**Used in:** MM phono RIAA preamps (datasheet Figure 41), Mic preamps: transformerless (OP37) or transformer-coupled (OP27), Tape-head (NAB) playback preamps, Low-noise gain from low source impedance (<1–3 kΩ), Precision instrumentation and ADC drivers

## Watch out

- OP37 is stable only at gain ≥5; an unplugged mic input (100% feedback) can oscillate.
- Bias-current cancellation raises current noise: above about 3 kΩ source, the OP07 is quieter at low frequencies.
- OP27's 2.8 V/µs and 8 MHz cause RIAA errors above 40 dB gain; best linearity below ±10 mA output.
- Input diodes conduct above 0.7 V differential; use Rf ≥ 500 Ω in fast unity-gain buffers.
- ADI, ADI/LTC and TI sell it with different grade sets; specs and sound may differ by vendor.
- **Counterfeits:** No verified OP27/OP37 counterfeit reports. PMI, ADI, LTC and TI logos are all legitimate. OPA27/OPA37 are different parts. Distrust cheap A-grade CERDIP or TO-99 offers; check ≈3 mA at ±15 V and a ≈3 nV/√Hz noise floor.

## Silicon changes

### Second-source difference: Texas Instruments (second source) vs PMI/ADI, 1989-02 (SLOS100) to at least 2010

TI makes its own OP27A/OP27C (SLOS100, Rev. E Feb 2010) and an OP37 under PMI/ADI's numbers. Whether TI's die is its own or licensed is undocumented. Headline noise matches ADI (3 nV/√Hz, 2.7 Hz corner). TI's OP27C differs from the old PMI OP27C.

- **Affected:** OP27A (TI), OP27C (TI), OP37G (TI)
- **How to tell:** TI logo and ordering codes; datasheet SLOS100. TI grades A and C only; ADI grades A/E/G.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown; no measurement comparisons found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: same pinout, sockets and headline specs; differences only in unspecified parameters.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Grade set | PMI/ADI: A/E/G (older B/C/F) | TI: A/C |
| Vos max, lower grade | ADI OP27G: 100 µV (Rev F) | TI OP27C: 100 µV |

### Second-source difference: Linear Technology (now ADI 'OP27-LTC') vs PMI/ADI, late 1980s on; both at ADI since 2017

Two OP27 lines now sit under ADI: the PMI-heritage OP27 and the Linear Technology OP27 (op27-ltc.html). Whether the LTC die is an independent design is unconfirmed.

- **Affected:** OP-27A/C/E/G (LTC), OP-37A/C/E/G (LTC), OP-227/OP-237 (LTC)
- **How to tell:** LTC logo; ADI's separate OP27-LTC page; LTC A/C/E/G grade set versus ADI A/E/G.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown; no measurements found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low: pin-compatible, same grade names; unspecified parameters may differ.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Grade set | PMI/ADI: A/E/G | LTC: A/C/E/G |

# OP27 / OP37 (PMI/ADI low-noise precision bipolar; OP37 = decompensated, gain ≥5)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** Bipolar classics

*PMI's c.1980 3 nV/√Hz zener-trimmed precision bipolar op-amp, long used in phono, tape-head and mic preamps; ADI's datasheet still carries those audio circuits.*

**Technology:** Monolithic bipolar, single channel (PMI/ADI design described here). NPN input stage at high quiescent current for low e_n, input bias-current cancellation, back-to-back input clamp diodes without series resistors, and zener-zap offset trim at wafer test. OP27 is unity-gain stable (8 MHz, 2.8 V/µs). OP37 is the same design decompensated for closed-loop gain ≥5 (63 MHz, 17 V/µs). Linear Technology and Texas Instruments sold their own OP27/OP37 under the same part numbers; their die designs are not documented.

> ⚠ **Same part number, different silicon.** See [silicon changes](#silicon-changes-under-the-same-part-number) (3 recorded: Unclassified).

## Why enthusiasts rate it

The reputation is mostly pro-audio and engineering, not 'op-amp rolling'. PMI and ADI pitched the OP27/OP37 at professional audio, with ±10 V into 600 Ω and application circuits for RIAA phono, NAB tape-head and mic preamps. A 1990s hobbyist audio FAQ says 'The OP-27, OP-37, LT1028, and LT1115 are very well received for phono preamps, head amplifiers, and microphone preamplifiers'. Douglas Self's 'The Design of Active Crossovers' (2nd ed.) has a section titled 'The OP27 Opamp'; its content was not read. The diyAudio 'a classic' quotes come from discovery notes and were not re-checked by web search. No ASR or Groner measurements were verified.

**How people describe the sound:** 'a classic' (diyAudio, via discovery notes; unverified), 'nice forgotten opamp' for phono (diyAudio, via discovery notes; unverified), 'very well received' for phono, head-amp and mic preamps (1990s Atari ST diskmag audio FAQ)

**Typical uses:** MM phono RIAA preamps (datasheet Figure 41), Microphone preamps, transformerless (OP37) or transformer-coupled (OP27), Tape-head (NAB) playback preamps, Low-noise gain stages from low source impedance (<1–3 kΩ), Precision instrumentation and ADC drivers

**Caveats:**

- OP37 is decompensated and stable only at gain ≥5. An unplugged mic input (100% feedback) can oscillate.
- Bias-current cancellation raises current noise, so the noise advantage disappears with high source resistance. The OP07 is better above about 3 kΩ at low frequencies.
- OP27 slew rate is low (2.8 V/µs), and the 8 MHz GBW causes RIAA errors at gains above 40 dB (datasheet).
- Unprotected input diodes conduct above 0.7 V differential. Use Rf ≥ 500 Ω in fast unity-gain buffers.
- Best linearity with peak output current below ±10 mA (OP37 datasheet).
- Single channel only; about 3 mA per amplifier.
- Same part number from ADI (PMI line), ADI/LTC (OP27-LTC) and TI, with different grade sets. Specs and sound are not guaranteed identical across vendors.

## Silicon changes under the same part number

### 1. Unclassified: Texas Instruments (second source) vs…, from Feb 1989 (SLOS100) to at least 2010

TI makes its own OP27A/OP27C (SLOS100, Rev E Feb 2010) and an OP37, under the same part numbers as PMI/ADI. Whether TI's die is its own design or licensed is not documented. The headline noise specs match ADI's (3 nV/√Hz, 2.7 Hz corner). TI's OP27C (100 µV max Vos) is not the same grade as the discontinued PMI OP27C.

- **When:** from Feb 1989 (SLOS100) to at least 2010
- **Affected:** OP27A (TI), OP27C (TI), OP37G (TI)
- **How to tell old from new:** TI logo and TI ordering codes; the TI datasheet is SLOS100. TI's OP27 grades are A and C only, while ADI's current grades are A/E/G.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. No measurement comparisons were found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low – same pinout, sockets and headline specs; differences would be in unspecified parameters.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Grade set | PMI/ADI: A/E/G (older B/C/F) | TI: A/C |
| Vos max, lower grade | ADI OP27G: 100 µV (Rev F) | TI OP27C: 100 µV |

### 2. Unclassified: Linear Technology (now ADI 'OP27-LTC')…, late 1980s onward; ADI lists both after the 2017 LTC merger

Two OP27 product lines now sit under ADI: the PMI-heritage OP27 and the Linear Technology OP27 (op27-ltc.html). The LTC datasheets are mirrored on alldatasheet. Whether the LTC die is an independent design is unconfirmed, and the matching LTC macromodel template is not evidence either way.

- **When:** late 1980s onward; ADI lists both after the 2017 LTC merger
- **Affected:** OP-27A/C/E/G (LTC), OP-37A/C/E/G (LTC), OP-227/OP-237 (LTC)
- **How to tell old from new:** LTC logo. ADI's separate 'OP27-LTC' product page. LTC's A/C/E/G grade set, whereas ADI (PMI line) uses A/E/G.
- **Audio impact:** Unknown. No measurements found.
- **Drop-in risk:** low – pin-compatible and specified to the same OP-27/OP-37 grade names; unspecified parameters may differ.
- **Confidence:** medium

| Parameter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Grade set | PMI/ADI: A/E/G | LTC: A/C/E/G |

## Counterfeits

No OP27/OP37-specific counterfeit reports were verified. Several legitimate logos exist (PMI, ADI, LTC, TI), so a non-ADI logo alone does not mean a fake. Don't confuse OP27/OP37 with the Burr-Brown/TI OPA27/OPA37, which are different parts. Treat cheap marketplace A-grade CERDIP or TO-99 parts with suspicion. A quick check: about 3 mA supply current at ±15 V and a noise floor near 3 nV/√Hz with low source impedance.

## Related parts and alternatives

LT1007 / LT1037 (LTC/ADI): improved pin-compatible OP27/OP37-class parts, LT1028 / LT1128: 'may be inserted directly into OP-07, OP-27, OP-37, LT1007 and LT1037 sockets' (LT1028 datasheet), OPA27 / OPA37 (Burr-Brown/TI): similarly named pin-compatible low-noise parts of a different design, OPA227 and AD797: named as OP27 replacements by a 2026 obsolescence article (theobsolescencebrief.com), OP227 / OP237 (dual OP27 / OP37, if available), LT1115: named with the OP-27 in 1990s audio guides for phono and mic use

## Open questions

- The OP27 Rev G and Rev H dates and change lists were not seen, so it is not known whether either changed specs or the die. Check the Rev H revision history.
- OP37 Rev B (12/02) is still ADI's current sheet, and Pb-free OP37 codes are not in it. The current OP37 ordering codes and lifecycle are unverified.
- ADI OP27 lifecycle: NRND is reported only by a third-party 2026 article and is unconfirmed on analog.com.
- The die origin of the TI OP27 (SLOS100) and the LTC OP-27 (own design or PMI-licensed) is unknown. So is whether they differ from PMI/ADI in unspecified parameters (THD, 1/f, input stage).
- The identity of analog.com OP27-OP37.pdf ('OP27/OP37 DS') as LTC's combined sheet is inferred.
- The TI OP37 literature number was not seen, and SLOS100 revisions C and D are undated.
- The meaning of ADI's OP27S/OP37S is unverified.
- The OP27 Rev A→B entry is missing from ADI's history. Original PMI 1980s datasheets have not been located.
- The OP37 die diagram text '1427U' / '1990' is unexplained, and no fab-transfer PCN was found.
- The diyAudio and phonoclone reputation pages and Self's 'The OP27 Opamp' section were not read. No ASR or Groner measurements were found.

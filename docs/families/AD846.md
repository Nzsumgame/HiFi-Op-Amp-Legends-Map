# AD846

**Bipolar current-feedback op-amp, single** · Analog Devices (c. 1990–obsolete (dates unconfirmed)) · Tier B (widely praised)

*Obsolete ADI 450 V/µs current-feedback amp with 0.01% settling, still prized by DIYers for DAC I/V stages.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 nV/√Hz | 80 MHz −3 dB bandwidth (current feedback) | 450 V/µs; settles to 0.01% in 110 ns | ±18 V | 6.5 mA max |

## Why it's rated

A small diyAudio niche calls it 'great sounding' for DAC I/V conversion; one thread asks if there is a better-sounding op-amp, and another hunts for a replacement since it went obsolete. ADI's own AD1862 DAC datasheet names the NE5534 or AD846 for the I/V stage. The praise is niche, not wide consensus.

**Described as:** 'great sounding', 'very good sounding'

**Used in:** DAC current-to-voltage (I/V) stage, e.g. after the AD1862, Output and line stages in DIY DACs and CD-player mods

## Watch out

- Obsolete: any stock sold today is old or of unknown origin.
- Current feedback: the feedback resistor sets bandwidth and stability, so voltage-feedback parts may not drop in.
- A diyAudio poster notes it was 'only 12 bit accurate' (0.01%) and saw little commercial success.
- **Counterfeits:** No documented counterfeits. Long obsolete, so treat 'new' AN/BN/AQ/BQ marketplace stock as suspect (possibly remarked AD844/AD811 or pulls). Check quiescent current (6.5 mA max) and that bandwidth tracks the feedback resistor.

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

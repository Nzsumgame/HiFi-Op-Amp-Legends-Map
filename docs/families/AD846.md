# AD846 (Analog Devices 450 V/us precision current-feedback op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*Obsolete ADI current-feedback amp with 12-bit (0.01%) settling. The AD1862 DAC datasheet suggested it for I/V, and diyAudio users still rate it for DAC I/V stages.*

**Technology:** Single monolithic bipolar current-feedback (transimpedance) op amp: 450 V/us, 80 MHz (-3 dB, G=-1), 110 ns settling to 0.01%, 200 MOhm open-loop transresistance into 500 Ohm. Obsolete.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

On diyAudio, posters call it 'great sounding' and 'very good sounding' for DAC I/V conversion. One thread is titled 'AD 846 AN is there a better sounding opamp out there', and an 'AD 846 replacement' thread began after it went obsolete. ADI's AD1862 datasheet names the NE5534 or AD846 for the current-to-voltage stage. The praise comes from a small niche of forum users, not wide consensus.

**How people describe the sound:** 'great sounding' (diyAudio posters), 'very good sounding' (diyAudio posters)

**Typical uses:** DAC current-to-voltage (I/V) converter, e.g. after the AD1862, Output/line stages in DIY DACs and CD-player mods

**Caveats:**

- Obsolete. Stock sold today is old or of unknown origin.
- It is a current-feedback amp, so the feedback resistor sets bandwidth and stability. Forum posters warn that a voltage-feedback op amp may not drop in as a replacement.
- A diyAudio poster notes that it was 'only 12 bit accurate' (the datasheet's 0.01% precision claim) and had little commercial success.

## Counterfeits

No documented AD846 counterfeit cases were found. The part has long been obsolete, though, so any 'new' AN/BN/AQ/BQ stock from marketplace sellers should be treated as suspect (possibly remarked AD844/AD811 or pulls). To check a part, compare its quiescent current (6.5 mA max) and verify it behaves as a current-feedback amp: bandwidth should depend on the feedback-resistor value.

## Related parts and alternatives

AD811 (ADI current-feedback; ADI replacement suggestion according to a diyAudio post; runs warm at ~16 mA Iq), AD8001 (ADI current-feedback; ADI replacement suggestion according to a diyAudio post), AD844 (ADI current-feedback, popular for DAC I/V), NE5534 (named with the AD846 in the AD1862 datasheet for I/V; voltage-feedback)

# AD846 (Analog Devices 450 V/us precision current-feedback op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

*Obsolete ADI precision current-feedback op-amp that diyAudio builders rate highly for DAC I/V stages; the AD1862 DAC datasheet reportedly suggested it for I/V.*

**Technology:** Single-channel bipolar current-feedback op-amp, 450 V/us slew (per datasheet title); obsolete (ADI datasheet sits in its obsolete-data-sheets library). Earlier draft wording 'stable in open loop' could not be checked and was removed.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

On diyAudio, posters call it a 'great sounding' and 'very good sounding' op-amp for DAC I/V conversion, saying 'you could do a lot worse by random substitution'. One thread is titled 'AD 846 AN is there a better sounding opamp out there'. After the part went obsolete, users started an 'AD 846 replacement' thread. It also comes up in 'Best opamp for I/V conversion?'. The earlier sweep reported that the AD1862 DAC datasheet recommended it for I/V; this has not been re-checked. The praise is anecdotal forum opinion.

**How people describe the sound:** 'great sounding' (diyAudio poster), 'very good sounding' (diyAudio poster)

**Typical uses:** DAC current-to-voltage (I/V) conversion, e.g. after the AD1862

**Caveats:**

- Obsolete: new supply is gone and parts now come from old stock or marketplaces
- Current-feedback topology: feedback resistor value sets stability and bandwidth, so it is not a drop-in swap for voltage-feedback op-amps in every circuit
- Sonic praise comes from anecdotal forum posts

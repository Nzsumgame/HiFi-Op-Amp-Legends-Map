# AD846 (Analog Devices 450 V/us precision current-feedback op-amp)

**Tier** B: widely praised · **Category** High-speed / video crossovers

**Technology:** Single precision bipolar current-feedback op-amp, 450 V/us, stable in open loop; obsolete

## Why enthusiasts rate it

diyAudio posters call it a 'great sounding' and 'very good sounding' op-amp for DAC I/V conversion, saying 'you could do a lot worse by random substitution'. One thread is titled 'AD 846 AN is there a better sounding opamp out there', and after it went obsolete users started an 'AD 846 replacement' thread. It is also discussed in 'Best opamp for I/V conversion?'. The AD1862 DAC datasheet recommended it for I/V.

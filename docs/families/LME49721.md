# LME49721

**Rail-to-rail I/O audio dual, 2.2–5.5 V, CMOS-type input** · National Semiconductor (2007–2011) → Texas Instruments (2011–present) · Tier C (niche / baseline)

*Rare audio-grade rail-to-rail dual for 5 V rails: 4 nV/√Hz, 0.00008% THD+N. A Japanese low-voltage headphone-amp pick.*

| Input noise (1 kHz) | GBW | Slew rate | THD+N | Supply range | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 nV/√Hz typ | 20 MHz typ | ±8.5 V/µs typ | 0.00008% typ | 2.2 V to 5.5 V | 2.15 mA typ |

## Why it's rated

A standard pick among Japanese builders of low-voltage and portable headphone amps. nabe's blog recommends it with the AD8656 after low-voltage tests; a zigsow review calls it "really excellent"; Akizuki sells it as a full-swing HiFi op-amp. Praise comes mostly from these Japanese hobbyist sources, not Western forums.

**Used in:** Portable / battery-powered headphone amplifiers, USB-bus-powered (5 V) buffers and preamps, Low-voltage single-supply paths needing rail-to-rail swing

## Watch out

- 5.5 V total supply limit: ±12 V or ±15 V NE5532/OPA2134 sockets will destroy it.
- Output current only about ±9.7 mA, limiting direct drive of low-impedance headphones.
- SOIC-8 only; DIP-8 sockets need an adapter.
- Praise rests on a small number of Japanese hobbyist sources.
- **Counterfeits:** No LME49721-specific counterfeit reports found. Only SOIC-8 (MA) codes exist, so a native DIP-8 'LME49721' is an adapter board or suspect. Buy from authorized distributors (TI, DigiKey, Mouser).

## Silicon changes

None documented. Compare datasheet revisions before assuming old and new parts are identical.

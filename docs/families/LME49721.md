# LME49721 (National / TI low-voltage rail-to-rail I/O audio dual)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

*A rare audio-grade rail-to-rail I/O dual for 2.2-5.5 V supplies (4 nV/rtHz, 0.00008% THD+N). Japanese low-voltage headphone-amp builders often choose it.*

**Technology:** Dual op-amp with CMOS-type input (input bias current 40 fA typ), rail-to-rail input and output, 2.2-5.5 V single supply, 20 MHz GBW, +/-8.5 V/us slew, +/-9.7 mA output current. Only SOIC-8 (MA) ordering codes are listed.

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Among Japanese builders of low-voltage and portable headphone amps it is treated as a standard choice. nabe's blog (高品質な低電圧ヘッドホンアンプ) recommends it after low-voltage testing, as one of two parts alongside the AD8656. A zigsow user review is titled '高性能な低電圧駆動可能なオペアンプ…らしい。実際優秀。' ('Supposedly a high-performance low-voltage op-amp... and it really is excellent'). Akizuki sells it as a '2回路フルスイングHiFiオペアンプ' ('2-circuit full-swing HiFi op-amp'). The praise comes mostly from these Japanese hobbyist sources and is not widespread in Western audiophile forums.

**Typical uses:** Portable / battery-powered headphone amplifiers, USB-bus-powered (5 V) audio buffers and preamps, Low-voltage single-supply audio signal paths needing rail-to-rail swing

**Caveats:**

- The supply is limited to 5.5 V total, so it cannot go into +/-15 V or +/-12 V sockets meant for NE5532 or OPA2134 class parts, which would destroy it.
- Output current is only about +/-9.7 mA, which limits direct drive of low-impedance headphones.
- Only SOIC-8 ordering codes are listed, so DIP-8 use needs an adapter.
- Praise is attributed to a small number of Japanese hobbyist sources.

## Counterfeits

No LME49721-specific counterfeit or remark reports were found. TI lists only SOIC-8 (MA) ordering codes, so any 'LME49721' sold as a native DIP-8 is either an adapter board or suspect. Buy from authorized distributors (TI, DigiKey, Mouser).

## Related parts and alternatives

AD8656 (recommended alongside it on nabe's low-voltage headphone-amp tests), OPA2365 (TI 2.2-5.5 V rail-to-rail I/O dual; editorial suggestion), LME49720 (higher-voltage sibling from the same line; not rail-to-rail, needs larger supplies)

# LME49721 (National / TI low-voltage rail-to-rail I/O audio dual)

**Tier** C: niche / baseline · **Category** CMOS-input

**Technology:** CMOS input (bias current in the fA range), rail-to-rail input and output, 2.2-5.5 V single supply, 20 MHz, +/-8.5 V/us

## Why enthusiasts rate it

Japanese low-voltage and portable headphone-amp builders treat it as the standard choice. On nabe's blog (高品質な低電圧ヘッドホンアンプ), it is one of two parts recommended after low-voltage testing, alongside the AD8656. A zigsow user review is titled '高性能な低電圧駆動可能なオペアンプ…らしい。実際優秀。' ('Supposedly a high-performance low-voltage op-amp... and it really is excellent'). Akizuki stocks it as '2回路フルスイングHiFiオペアンプ' ('2-circuit full-swing HiFi op-amp').
